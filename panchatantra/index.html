The Panchatantra

हि

  1. This is the Panchatantra

    Five books of stories from ancient India. Mostly animals, all about people.

  2. Why it was told

    A teacher promised to make a king’s three sons wise in six months. He did it with these stories.

  3. Stories inside stories

    Characters stop to tell stories. Tap a door to step inside, then come back.

  4. Two ways to read

    In a hurry, flip through the cards. When you have time, read the full story.
