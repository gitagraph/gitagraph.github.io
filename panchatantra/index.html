This is the Panchatantra

The name means five books: pancha is five, and a tantra here is a book or treatise. It is a collection of stories from ancient India, first told in Sanskrit more than 1500 years ago. Most of its characters are animals, and all of it is about how people behave. It has been retold in more than fifty languages.

Why it was told

A king had three sons who would not learn. A teacher named Vishnusharman promised to make them wise in six months, and staked his name on it. He did it with stories. These are those stories.

How it is built

There are five books. Each is one long story, and its characters stop to tell other stories. Wherever one of them begins a tale, a door appears. Step inside, then step back out to where you were.
