Ego and pride

The Gita distinguishes pride and arrogance from the mistaken sense of being the sole doer. The Ashtavakra Gita challenges that sense of doership and even warns against pride in liberation.

गीता घमंड और अभिमान के साथ-साथ 'मैं ही कर्ता हूँ' की धारणा पर भी प्रश्न उठाती है। अष्टावक्र गीता कर्तापन की भावना को बंधन से जोड़ती है और मुक्ति के अभिमान की भी आलोचना करती है।

Ashtavakra Gita

The Ashtavakra Gita calls the thought 'I am the doer' a snake bite, and says one can take pride even in liberation.

  1. 1.8

    You have been bitten by the great black snake of the ego that says, 'I am the doer.' Drink the nectar of the conviction 'I am not the doer,' and be happy.

  2. 16.10

    One who takes pride even in liberation and still claims the body as his own is neither a knower nor a yogi; he simply has his share of suffering.

  1. 3.27
