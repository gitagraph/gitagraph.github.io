Never satisfied, always wanting more

The Gita describes desire as difficult to satisfy, while the Ashtavakra Gita connects craving with bondage. Nachiketa and Maitreyi each question what wealth and pleasure can ultimately provide.

गीता कामना की तुलना ऐसी आग से करती है जो कभी तृप्त नहीं होती। अष्टावक्र गीता तृष्णा को बंधन से जोड़ती है। नचिकेता और मैत्रेयी की कथाएँ पूछती हैं कि धन और सुख से आख़िर क्या मिल सकता है।

Ashtavakra Gita

The Ashtavakra Gita is blunt: wherever there is craving, there is bondage, and its ending is called liberation.

  1. 10.3

    Wherever there is craving, know that the world of becoming is right there. Taking firm hold of mature dispassion, be free of craving and be happy.

  2. 10.4

    Bondage is made of craving alone, and its ending is called liberation. Simply by not clinging to the world, one finds contentment again and again.

  3. 9.8

    Latent desires are themselves the world of becoming, so let go of them all. Dropping them is dropping the world; then remain, from now on, however you happen to be.

Bhagavad Gita

  1. 3.37

    काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।
    महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥ ३.३७ ॥

    It is desire, it is anger, born of the quality of passion; it devours without end and is the great evil. Know it as the enemy here.

    यह काम है, यही क्रोध है, जो रजोगुण से उत्पन्न होता है। यह कभी न भरने वाला और बड़ा पापी है; इसे ही इस संसार में शत्रु जानो।

  2. 3.39

    आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
    कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥ ३.३९ ॥

    Son of Kunti, knowledge is veiled by this constant enemy of the wise, which takes the form of desire, never satisfied, like fire.

  3. 2.70

    आपूर्यमाणमचलप्रतिष्ठं समुद्रमापः प्रविशन्ति यद्वत् ।
    तद्वत्कामा यं प्रविशन्ति सर्वे स शान्तिमाप्नोति न कामकामी ॥ २.७० ॥

    As waters flow into the ocean, which is ever being filled yet stays steady within its bounds, so one into whom all desires enter in the same way attains peace — not one who chases desires.

    जैसे नदियों का पानी सदा भरते रहने पर भी अपनी मर्यादा में अचल रहने वाले समुद्र में समा जाता है, वैसे ही जिसमें सारी कामनाएँ समा जाती हैं, वही शांति पाता है — कामनाओं के पीछे भागने वाला नहीं।

  4. 2.71

    विहाय कामान्यः सर्वान् पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः ।
    निर्ममो निरहंकारः स शान्तिमधिगच्छति ॥ २.७१ ॥

    The person who gives up all desires and moves about free from longing, without any sense of 'mine' and without ego, attains peace.
