Who am I?

The Gita separates the body, which it calls the field, from the one who knows it. That knower is described as unborn, beyond harm, and part of something larger.

गीता शरीर, जिसे वह क्षेत्र कहती है, और उसे जानने वाले को अलग करती है। उस जानने वाले को अजन्मा, अविनाशी और किसी बड़े का अंश बताया गया है।

Bhagavad Gita

  1. 13.1

    इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।
    एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः ॥ १३.१ ॥

    This body, Kaunteya, is called the field; and the one who knows it is called the knower of the field by those who understand these matters.

    हे कौंतेय, यह शरीर 'क्षेत्र' कहलाता है; और जो इसे जानता है, उसे इस विषय के जानकार 'क्षेत्रज्ञ' कहते हैं।

  2. 2.20

    न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः ।
    अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥ २.२० ॥

    This self is not born and does not die. It did not begin at some point, and it will never come to an end. Without birth, lasting for ever, unchanging and older than time, it is not destroyed when the body is destroyed.

    यह आत्मा न कभी जन्म लेती है, न कभी मरती है; ऐसा भी नहीं कि यह एक बार होकर फिर न रहे। यह अजन्मी, नित्य, शाश्वत और पुरातन है; शरीर का नाश होने पर भी इसका नाश नहीं होता।

  3. 2.23

    नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावकः ।
    न चैनं क्लेदयन्त्यापो न शोषयति मारुतः ॥ २.२३ ॥

    Weapons cannot cut it, fire cannot burn it, water cannot wet it, and wind cannot dry it.

    इसे शस्त्र काट नहीं सकते, आग जला नहीं सकती, पानी गीला नहीं कर सकता और हवा सुखा नहीं सकती।

  4. 13.22

    उपद्रष्टानुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः ।
    परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः ॥ १३.२२ ॥

    The supreme Purusha in this body is also called the witness, the one who consents, the sustainer, the experiencer, the great Lord and the supreme Self.

    इस शरीर में जो परम पुरुष है, उसे साक्षी, अनुमति देने वाला, पालन करने वाला, भोक्ता, महेश्वर और परमात्मा भी कहा जाता है।

  5. 15.7

    ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।
    मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥ १५.७ ॥

    A part of my very own self, eternal, becomes the living soul in this world of living beings; it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

    इस जीव-लोक में जो सनातन जीवात्मा है, वह मेरा ही अंश है। वही प्रकृति में स्थित मन सहित छह इंद्रियों (पाँच इंद्रियाँ और छठा मन) को अपनी ओर खींचती है।

Ashtavakra Gita

This is the Ashtavakra Gita's central question: it answers that you are not the body or the mind but the witnessing awareness, already free.

  1. 1.3

    You are not earth, nor water, nor fire, nor air, nor space. To be free, know yourself as the Self, the witness of all these, whose nature is consciousness.

  2. 1.7

    You are the one seer of everything, and you are always essentially free. Your only bondage is this: you see the seer as something other than yourself.

  3. 1.12

    The Self is the witness: all-pervading, complete, one, free, consciousness itself, without action, unattached, without desire, at peace. Only through delusion does it seem caught up in worldly life.

    Read in context →

  4. 15.4

    You are not the body, nor is the body yours; you are neither the doer nor the enjoyer. You are consciousness itself, ever the witness, needing nothing. Go about happily.

  5. 15.13

    In you, the one, the imperishable, the peaceful, the stainless sky of consciousness, where is birth, where is action, where is even the ego?
