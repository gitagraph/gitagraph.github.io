How should I live?

Speak the truth, give, hold yourself back, be kind, enjoy without grabbing: the Upanishads give short, practical answers alongside the deep ones.

सच बोलो, दो, संयम रखो, दयालु बनो, बिना पकड़े भोगो: उपनिषद् गहरे उत्तरों के साथ छोटे, व्यावहारिक उत्तर भी देते हैं।

  1. 16.1

    अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।

  4. 17.15

    अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् ।
    स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते ॥ १७.१५ ॥

    Speech that causes no distress, that is true, pleasant and beneficial, together with regular recitation of scripture: this is called austerity of speech.

    ऐसी वाणी जो किसी को उद्वेग न पहुँचाए, जो सच्ची, प्रिय और हितकारी हो, और साथ में शास्त्रों का नियमित पाठ — यह वाणी का तप कहलाता है।

Ashtavakra Gita

The Ashtavakra Gita's answer is simple: live with patience, honesty, kindness and contentment, and let ordinary life be lived at ease rather than chased.

  1. 1.2

    If you want liberation, child, drop the objects of the senses as you would poison. Take up patience, honesty, kindness, contentment and truth as you would nectar.

  2. 10.4

    Bondage is made of craving alone, and its ending is called liberation. Simply by not clinging to the world, one finds contentment again and again.
