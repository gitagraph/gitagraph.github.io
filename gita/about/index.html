About this project

For someone opening the Gita for the first time, and for someone who has read it many times.

Sanskrit text: Sanskrit Wikisource (sa.wikisource.org, "भगवद्गीता"), used under CC BY-SA 4.0, checked verse by verse against the GRETIL e-text (Göttingen). The transliteration is generated from that Devanagari. The English and Hindi translations were written fresh for this project with AI assistance. 700 verses, as in Wikisource and GRETIL.

The translations are new and written to be easy to read. Suggestions and corrections are welcome. Tell us on GitHub.
