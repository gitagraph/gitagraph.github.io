Narada

Narada

नारद

Divine sage

Named among Krishna's glories and as a witness to his divinity (10.13, 10.26).

कृष्ण की विभूतियों में और उनकी दिव्यता के साक्षी के रूप में नामित (10.13, 10.26)।

2 verses

  1. 10.13

    So all the sages describe you, and so do the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa; and now you yourself are telling me.

  2. 10.26

    Among all trees I am the sacred fig; among the divine sages, Narada; among the Gandharvas, Chitraratha; and among the perfected ones, the sage Kapila.
