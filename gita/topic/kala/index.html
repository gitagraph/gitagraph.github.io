काल

Time

Revealed as Krishna's destroying aspect: 'I am Time' (11.32).

कृष्ण का संहारक रूप: 'मैं काल हूँ' (11.32)।

9 verses

  1. 7.26

    Arjuna, I know the beings of the past, the present and the future; but no one knows me.

  2. 8.17

    Those who know that a day of Brahma lasts a thousand ages, and that his night likewise ends after a thousand ages, truly understand day and night.

  3. 8.18

    At the coming of that day, all manifest things emerge from the unmanifest; at the coming of night they dissolve back into that very thing called the unmanifest.

  4. 8.19

    This same multitude of beings, coming into existence again and again, dissolves helplessly when night falls, Partha, and springs forth again when day dawns.

  5. 8.23

    Now, best of the Bharatas, I will tell you the times at which yogis who depart either do not return or do return.

  6. 9.7

    At the end of a cosmic age, Kaunteya, all beings pass back into my own nature; at the start of the next age I send them forth again.

  7. 10.30

    Among the Daityas I am Prahlada; among those who count, I am Time; among beasts, the lion; and among birds, Garuda, son of Vinata.

  8. 10.33

    Among letters I am the letter A, and among compounds, the copulative compound. I alone am unending Time, and I am the sustainer whose face is turned everywhere.

  9. 11.32

    I am Time, grown mighty, the destroyer of worlds, now set to sweep these people away. Even without you, none of the warriors drawn up in the opposing ranks will survive.
