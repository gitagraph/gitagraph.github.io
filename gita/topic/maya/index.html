Maya

Maya

माया

Divine illusion

Krishna's power, made of the three gunas, that veils him; hard to cross except by refuge in him (7.14).

कृष्ण की तीन गुणों वाली शक्ति, जो उन्हें ढक लेती है; उनकी शरण के बिना इसे पार करना कठिन है (7.14)।

9 verses

  1. 4.6

    Though I am unborn and my being never perishes, and though I am the Lord of all beings, I take birth by governing my own nature through my own power of maya.

  2. 5.15

    The all-pervading one takes on no one's sin and no one's good deed either. Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and that is why beings are deluded.

  3. 7.13

    Deluded by these states made of the three gunas, this whole world fails to recognise me, who stand beyond them, imperishable.

  4. 7.14

    This divine maya of mine, made of the gunas, is very hard to get past; but those who take refuge in me alone cross beyond it.

  5. 7.15

    Evildoers, the deluded and the lowest of people do not turn to me — their understanding stolen by maya, they have taken on a demonic disposition.

  6. 7.25

    Veiled by my yogamaya, I am not revealed to everyone. This deluded world does not recognise me as unborn and imperishable.

  7. 7.27

    Through the delusion of opposites that arises from liking and aversion, Bharata, all beings fall into bewilderment at birth, scorcher of foes.

  8. 9.5

    And yet beings do not rest in me either: see my divine power. My Self sustains all beings and brings them into existence, yet it does not dwell in them.

  9. 18.61

    The Lord dwells in the heart of all beings, Arjuna, turning them all by his maya as if they were mounted on a machine.
