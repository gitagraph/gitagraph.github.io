Drona

Drona

द्रोण

Teacher of arms

Taught archery to both the Kauravas and the Pandavas, Arjuna above all. Arjuna names him in his grief (2.4).

कौरवों और पांडवों दोनों को, और सबसे बढ़कर अर्जुन को, धनुर्विद्या सिखाई। अर्जुन अपने शोक में उनका नाम लेते हैं (2.4)।

8 verses

  1. 1.2

    Seeing the Pandava army drawn up in battle order, King Duryodhana then went up to his teacher and spoke these words.

  2. 1.3

    Duryodhana said: Teacher, look at this vast army of the sons of Pandu, arrayed by the son of Drupada, your own gifted pupil.

  3. 1.7

    Now hear, best of the twice-born, about the most distinguished on our side, the leaders of my army. I name them for you so you have them in mind.

  4. 1.8

    Yourself, Bhishma, Karna, and Kripa, ever victorious in battle; Ashvatthama, Vikarna, and the son of Somadatta as well.

  5. 1.25

    facing Bhishma, Drona and all the rulers of the earth, and said: Partha, see these Kurus assembled here.

  6. 2.4

    Madhusudana, slayer of enemies, how can I fight back with arrows in battle against Bhishma and Drona, who deserve my reverence?

  7. 11.26

    And all these sons of Dhritarashtra, along with the hosts of kings, Bhishma, Drona and that charioteer's son Karna too, together with the chief warriors on our side as well,

  8. 11.34

    Drona and Bhishma, Jayadratha and Karna, and the other heroic warriors too, already slain by me, you must kill. Do not be troubled. Fight: you will conquer your rivals in battle.
