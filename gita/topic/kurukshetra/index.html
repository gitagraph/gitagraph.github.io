Kurukshetra

कुरुक्षेत्र

The battlefield

Called dharmakshetra, the field of dharma, in the first verse. The setting of the whole dialogue.

पहले श्लोक में इसे धर्मक्षेत्र, यानी धर्म का क्षेत्र, कहा गया है। पूरे संवाद की पृष्ठभूमि।

1 verses

  1. 1.1

    On the field of dharma, at Kurukshetra, my sons and the sons of Pandu gathered there eager to fight. What did they do, Sanjaya?
