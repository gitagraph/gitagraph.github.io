Asuri Sampad

आसुरीसम्पद्

Demonic qualities

Hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness, ignorance. They lead to bondage (16.4–5).

दंभ, अहंकार, अभिमान, क्रोध, कठोरता, अज्ञान। ये बंधन की ओर ले जाते हैं (16.4–5)।

20 verses

  1. 7.15

    Evildoers, the deluded and the lowest of people do not turn to me — their understanding stolen by maya, they have taken on a demonic disposition.

  2. 9.12

    Their hopes are empty, their actions futile and their knowledge useless; senseless, they cling to the deluding nature of fiends and demons.

  3. 16.4

    Hypocrisy, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance, Partha, belong to one born with the demonic nature.

  4. 16.5

    The divine nature is held to lead to liberation, and the demonic to bondage. Do not grieve, Pandava; you were born with the divine nature.

  5. 16.6

    There are two kinds of beings created in this world: the divine and the demonic. The divine has been described at length; now, Partha, hear from me about the demonic.

  6. 16.7

    People of demonic nature do not understand what to take up and what to turn away from. Neither purity, nor right conduct, nor truthfulness is found in them.

  7. 16.8

    They say the world is without truth, without foundation and without God; that it has come about through the union of male and female and nothing else, with desire as its only cause.

  8. 16.9

    Clinging to this view, these lost souls of small understanding and cruel deeds rise up as enemies of the world, bent on its destruction.

  9. 16.10

    Giving themselves to desire that can never be filled, full of hypocrisy, pride and intoxication, they seize false notions out of delusion and pursue their aims with impure resolves.

  10. 16.11

    Weighed down by endless worries that last until death, treating the enjoyment of desires as the highest goal and convinced that this is all there is…

  11. 16.13

    'This I have gained today; that wish I will fulfil next. This wealth is mine, and more will be mine again.'

  12. 16.14

    'That enemy I have killed, and I will kill the others too. I am the master, I am the enjoyer; I am successful, strong and happy.'

  13. 16.15

    'I am rich and well born; who else is my equal? I will perform sacrifices, I will give gifts, I will enjoy myself.' So they speak, led astray by ignorance.

  14. 16.16

    Confused by countless thoughts, caught in a net of delusion and addicted to the enjoyment of desires, they fall into a foul hell.

  15. 16.17

    Self-admiring, stubborn, drunk on wealth and status, they perform sacrifices in name only, out of show and without following the prescribed rules.

  16. 16.18

    Clinging to ego, force, arrogance, lust and anger, these envious people hate me, who dwell in their own bodies and in the bodies of others.

  17. 16.19

    These hateful, cruel, inauspicious people, the lowest of humans, I hurl again and again into demonic wombs within the cycles of birth and death.

  18. 16.20

    Having entered demonic wombs, deluded birth after birth, Kaunteya, they never reach me and sink from there to an even lower state.

  19. 17.5

    Those people who undergo harsh austerities not sanctioned by scripture, driven by hypocrisy and ego, and carried along by the force of desire and attachment…

  20. 17.6

    …senselessly tormenting the elements that make up the body, and me as well, who dwell within the body: know them to be of demonic resolve.
