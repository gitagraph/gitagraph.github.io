Jiva

Jiva

जीव

Individual soul

The embodied soul, an eternal fragment of Krishna (15.7), which carries the senses from body to body like wind carries scent.

देहधारी आत्मा, कृष्ण का सनातन अंश (15.7), जो इंद्रियों को एक शरीर से दूसरे में वैसे ले जाता है जैसे वायु गंध को।

6 verses

  1. 5.15

    The all-pervading one takes on no one's sin and no one's good deed either. Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and that is why beings are deluded.

  2. 7.5

    This is my lower nature. But know, mighty-armed one, that beyond it I have another, higher nature — the living principle by which this world is upheld.

  3. 15.7

    A part of my very own self, eternal, becomes the living soul in this world of living beings; it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

  4. 15.8

    Whenever this lord of the body takes up a body or leaves one, it carries these along with it, the way the wind carries scents away from their source.

  5. 15.9

    Presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste and smell, and over the mind as well, it enjoys the objects of the senses.

  6. 15.10

    Whether it is leaving the body, staying in it, or enjoying experiences while joined with the gunas, the deluded do not perceive it; those with the eye of knowledge do.
