Vyasa

व्यास

Author of the Mahabharata

The sage credited with the Mahabharata, of which the Gita is part. Sanjaya credits Vyasa's grace for hearing the dialogue (18.75).

महाभारत के रचयिता माने जाने वाले ऋषि; गीता उसी का अंग है। संजय संवाद सुन पाने का श्रेय व्यास की कृपा को देते हैं (18.75)।

3 verses

  1. 10.13

    So all the sages describe you, and so do the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa; and now you yourself are telling me.

  2. 10.37

    Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva (Krishna); among the Pandavas, Dhananjaya (Arjuna); among sages I am Vyasa; and among poets, the poet Ushana (Shukracharya).

  3. 18.75

    By the grace of Vyasa I heard this supreme secret, this yoga, directly from Krishna himself, the lord of yoga, as he spoke it.
