Prakriti

प्रकृति

Nature, matter

Material nature, the source of the body, mind and the three gunas. Eightfold in its lower form (7.4).

भौतिक प्रकृति: शरीर, मन और तीन गुणों का स्रोत। अपने अपरा रूप में आठ प्रकार की (7.4)।

29 verses

  1. 3.5

    No one can stay without acting even for a moment; every being is driven to act, whether it wants to or not, by the qualities (gunas) born of nature (Prakriti).

  2. 3.27

    All actions are carried out entirely by the qualities of nature, but one whose mind is clouded by ego thinks, "I am the doer."

  3. 3.28

    But one who knows the truth about how the qualities and their actions are divided, mighty-armed one, understands that it is qualities acting upon qualities, and does not become attached.

  4. 3.29

    Those deluded by the qualities of nature cling to the workings of those qualities. One who knows the whole should not unsettle such slow people who know only in part.

  5. 3.33

    Even a knowing person acts in keeping with their own nature. All beings follow their nature; what can forcing it achieve?

  6. 4.6

    Though I am unborn and my being never perishes, and though I am the Lord of all beings, I take birth by governing my own nature through my own power of maya.

  7. 5.8

    One who is steady and knows the truth thinks, "I do nothing at all," even while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, walking, sleeping and breathing…

  8. 5.9

    …while speaking, letting go, taking hold, opening and closing the eyes, holding firmly that it is only the senses moving among their objects.

  9. 5.14

    The Lord does not create agency for the world, nor actions, nor the link between action and its fruit; it is nature that carries all this on.

  10. 7.4

    Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and the sense of 'I' — this is my nature, divided eightfold.

  11. 7.5

    This is my lower nature. But know, mighty-armed one, that beyond it I have another, higher nature — the living principle by which this world is upheld.

  12. 7.6

    Understand that all beings have these two as their source. I am the origin of the whole world and also its dissolution.

  13. 7.13

    Deluded by these states made of the three gunas, this whole world fails to recognise me, who stand beyond them, imperishable.

  14. 7.20

    Those whose understanding has been carried off by this or that desire turn to other gods, following one observance or another, driven by their own nature.

  15. 8.18

    At the coming of that day, all manifest things emerge from the unmanifest; at the coming of night they dissolve back into that very thing called the unmanifest.

  16. 9.7

    At the end of a cosmic age, Kaunteya, all beings pass back into my own nature; at the start of the next age I send them forth again.

  17. 9.8

    Taking hold of my own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, which is helpless under the sway of nature.

  18. 9.10

    With me as overseer, nature gives birth to all that moves and all that is still; for this reason, Kaunteya, the world keeps turning.

  19. 13.5

    The great elements, the ego-sense, the intellect and the unmanifest; the ten senses and the one mind, and the five domains of the senses…

  20. 13.19

    Know that Prakriti and Purusha are both without beginning, and know that the changes of form and the gunas arise from Prakriti.

  21. 13.20

    Prakriti is said to be the cause behind effect, instrument and agency; Purusha is said to be the cause behind the experiencing of pleasure and pain.

  22. 13.23

    One who knows Purusha and Prakriti together with the gunas in this way is not born again, however he may be living.

  23. 13.29

    One who sees that all actions are carried out entirely by Prakriti alone, and that the Self is not the doer, truly sees.

  24. 14.3

    My womb is the great Brahman — primal Prakriti — and in it I place the seed; from that, Bharata, all beings come into being.

  25. 14.4

    Whatever forms are born in any womb, Kaunteya, the great Brahman is their womb, and I am the father who gives the seed.

  26. 15.7

    A part of my very own self, eternal, becomes the living soul in this world of living beings; it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

  27. 18.40

    There is no being on earth, nor among the gods in heaven, that is free of these three gunas born of Prakriti.

  28. 18.59

    If, relying on ego, you think 'I will not fight', that resolve of yours is futile; your own nature will compel you.

  29. 18.60

    Kaunteya, bound by your own work born of your nature, you will do, even against your will, what you now refuse to do out of delusion.
