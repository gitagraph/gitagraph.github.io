ज्ञानयोग

Path of knowledge

Liberation through discrimination between the Self and what is not the Self; 'there is no purifier like knowledge' (4.38).

आत्मा और अनात्मा के विवेक से मुक्ति; 'ज्ञान के समान पवित्र करने वाला कुछ नहीं' (4.38)।

39 verses

  1. 2.39

    This understanding has been explained to you in terms of Sankhya; now hear it in terms of yoga. Joined with this understanding, Partha, you will throw off the bondage of action.

  2. 3.1

    Janardana, if you hold that understanding is higher than action, then why, Keshava, do you push me into this terrible deed?

  3. 3.3

    Sinless one, long ago I taught that in this world there are two ways of steady practice: the path of knowledge for those given to reflection, and the path of action for those given to yoga.

  4. 4.19

    One whose every undertaking is free of desire and selfish intention, whose actions are burned up in the fire of knowledge, is called learned by the wise.

  5. 4.23

    For one who is unattached and free, whose mind is settled in knowledge, and who acts for the sake of sacrifice, all action melts away completely.

  6. 4.33

    Scorcher of foes, the sacrifice of knowledge is better than any sacrifice of material things; for all action, Partha, finds its completion in knowledge.

  7. 4.34

    Learn it by bowing down, by sincere questioning, and by service. The wise who have seen the truth will teach you that knowledge.

  8. 4.35

    Knowing that, Pandava, you will not fall into such confusion again, and through it you will see all beings without exception in yourself, and then in me.

  9. 4.36

    Even if you were the worst of all sinners, you would cross over every wrong by the boat of knowledge alone.

  10. 4.37

    Arjuna, as a blazing fire turns firewood to ash, so the fire of knowledge turns all actions to ash.

  11. 4.38

    Truly, nothing in this world purifies like knowledge. One who is perfected in yoga finds it in oneself, in time.

  12. 4.39

    One who has faith, who is devoted to it and has mastered the senses, gains knowledge; and having gained it, soon reaches supreme peace.

  13. 4.41

    Actions do not bind one who has given up action through yoga, whose doubts are cut away by knowledge, and who is established in the Self, Dhananjaya.

  14. 4.42

    So, Bharata, cut through this doubt in your heart, born of ignorance, with the sword of knowledge of the Self. Take refuge in yoga, and stand up.

  15. 5.4

    Only the naive, not the wise, speak of the path of knowledge and the path of yoga as different. One who is truly settled in either one gains the fruit of both.

  16. 5.5

    The state reached by the followers of knowledge is reached by the followers of yoga as well. One who sees knowledge and yoga as one truly sees.

  17. 5.16

    But for those whose ignorance has been destroyed by knowledge of the Self, that knowledge shines like the sun and reveals the Supreme.

  18. 5.17

    With their intellect on That, their self in That, their devotion in That and That as their final goal, their impurities shaken off by knowledge, they go to the state from which there is no return.

  19. 7.2

    I will explain to you in full this knowledge together with its realisation; once you know it, nothing further remains to be known here.

  20. 7.3

    Among thousands of people, hardly one strives for perfection; and even among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows me as I truly am.

  21. 7.17

    Of these the wise one, ever united and devoted to the One alone, stands out. For I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and the wise one is dear to me.

  22. 7.18

    All of these are noble, but the wise one I consider my very Self; for, steadfast in mind, he is anchored in me alone as the highest goal.

  23. 7.19

    Only after many births does the one who truly knows take refuge in me, seeing that Vasudeva is all there is. A great soul like that is hard indeed to find.

  24. 9.1

    Because you do not find fault, I will now tell you the most hidden knowledge of all, together with its practical realisation; once you know it, you will be set free from what is harmful.

  25. 9.2

    This is the king of all knowledge and the king of all secrets, supremely purifying. It can be known by direct experience, it is in keeping with dharma, it is very easy to practise, and it never perishes.

  26. 9.15

    Others worship me by offering the sacrifice of knowledge, seeing me as one, as distinct, and in many ways as the one whose faces are everywhere.

  27. 10.11

    Out of compassion for them alone, dwelling in their hearts, I destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge.

  28. 12.1

    Of those devotees who, ever joined in yoga, worship you in this way, and those who worship the imperishable, unmanifest reality, which have the better grasp of yoga?

  29. 12.3

    But those who worship the imperishable — that which cannot be defined, is unmanifest, reaches everywhere, is beyond thought, unchanging at the core, unmoving and constant…

  30. 12.4

    …keeping the whole host of senses in check, even-minded toward everything, and devoted to the welfare of all beings — they too reach me alone.

  31. 12.5

    The struggle is far greater for those whose minds cling to the unmanifest, for the path to the unmanifest is hard for embodied beings to travel.

  32. 13.11

    …constancy in knowledge of the Self, and keeping in view the purpose of knowing the truth — this is declared to be knowledge; whatever is contrary to it is ignorance.

  33. 13.24

    Some see the Self within themselves by their own self through meditation; others by the path of Sankhya; and still others by the yoga of action.

  34. 14.1

    I will again set out the supreme knowledge, the finest of all knowledge, by knowing which all the sages passed from this world to the highest perfection.

  35. 15.10

    Whether it is leaving the body, staying in it, or enjoying experiences while joined with the gunas, the deluded do not perceive it; those with the eye of knowledge do.

  36. 15.20

    Sinless one, I have now told you this most secret teaching. Understanding it, Bharata, a person becomes truly wise and has done all that needs to be done.

  37. 18.13

    Mighty-armed one, learn from me these five causes for the accomplishment of every action, as set out in the Sankhya teaching that brings action to its end.

  38. 18.50

    Learn from me in brief, Kaunteya, how one who has attained that perfection also attains Brahman, the highest culmination of knowledge.

  39. 18.70

    And whoever studies this sacred dialogue of ours, by him I will have been worshipped through the sacrifice of knowledge; that is my view.
