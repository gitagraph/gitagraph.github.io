Jayadratha

जयद्रथ

Kaurava warrior

King of Sindhu; in the cosmic form Arjuna sees him already slain (11.34).

सिंधु के राजा; विश्वरूप में अर्जुन उन्हें पहले से मारा हुआ देखते हैं (11.34)।

1 verses

  1. 11.34

    Drona and Bhishma, Jayadratha and Karna, and the other heroic warriors too, already slain by me, you must kill. Do not be troubled. Fight: you will conquer your rivals in battle.
