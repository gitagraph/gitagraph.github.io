Vivasvan

विवस्वान्

The sun god

First to receive this yoga from Krishna, long ago (4.1).

बहुत पहले कृष्ण से यह योग पाने वाले पहले (4.1)।

2 verses

  1. 4.1

    I taught this imperishable yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan passed it to Manu, and Manu told it to Ikshvaku.

  2. 4.4

    Your birth is recent, and Vivasvan's birth was long ago. How am I to understand that you taught it at the beginning?
