अवतार

Divine descent

God taking birth in the world to protect the good and restore dharma (4.7–8).

सज्जनों की रक्षा और धर्म की पुनर्स्थापना के लिए ईश्वर का संसार में जन्म लेना (4.7–8)।

7 verses

  1. 4.5

    Many births of mine have passed, Arjuna, and many of yours too. I know them all, but you do not, scorcher of foes.

  2. 4.6

    Though I am unborn and my being never perishes, and though I am the Lord of all beings, I take birth by governing my own nature through my own power of maya.

  3. 4.7

    Whenever righteousness declines, Bharata, and unrighteousness rises, then I bring myself forth.

  4. 4.8

    To protect the good, to destroy those who do evil, and to establish righteousness firmly again, I come into being age after age.

  5. 4.9

    One who truly understands my divine birth and action in this way is not born again on leaving the body, but comes to me, Arjuna.

  6. 7.24

    The undiscerning think of me, the unmanifest, as having taken on a manifest form, not knowing my higher being, which is imperishable and unsurpassed.

  7. 9.11

    Deluded people look down on me when I take on a human body, not knowing my higher nature as the great Lord of all beings.
