शरणागति

Surrender

Taking refuge in the Lord alone (18.66).

केवल ईश्वर की शरण लेना (18.66)।

18 verses

  1. 2.7

    My nature is overcome by the flaw of faint-heartedness, and my mind is confused about dharma. I ask you: tell me clearly what is truly good for me. I am your disciple; I have come to you for refuge — guide me.

  2. 3.30

    Hand over all your actions to me, with your mind fixed on the Self; free of craving and of the sense of "mine", and with your fever gone, fight.

  3. 4.10

    Free from craving, fear and anger, absorbed in me and taking refuge in me, many, purified by the discipline of knowledge, have reached my state of being.

  4. 7.14

    This divine maya of mine, made of the gunas, is very hard to get past; but those who take refuge in me alone cross beyond it.

  5. 7.19

    Only after many births does the one who truly knows take refuge in me, seeing that Vasudeva is all there is. A great soul like that is hard indeed to find.

  6. 9.18

    I am the goal, the sustainer, the master and the witness; the home, the refuge and the friend; the origin and the dissolution, the ground, the storehouse and the seed that never decays.

  7. 9.22

    But those who think of me alone and worship me with no other thought, ever devoted to me: I myself take on their welfare, bringing them what they need and keeping safe what they have.

  8. 9.32

    For, Partha, those who take refuge in me, even those of lowly birth, women, Vaishyas and Shudras too, reach the highest goal.

  9. 9.34

    Fix your mind on me, be devoted to me, offer worship to me and bow down to me. Joining yourself to me in this way, with me as your highest aim, you will come to me.

  10. 11.44

    So, bowing down and laying my body at your feet, I seek your grace, adorable Lord. As a father bears with his son, a friend with a friend, a lover with the beloved, please bear with me, O God.

  11. 12.6

    But those who lay down all their actions in me, hold me as their highest goal, and worship me, meditating on me with undivided yoga…

  12. 12.14

    …ever content, a yogi who is self-controlled and firm in resolve, whose mind and understanding are offered to me — that devotee of mine is dear to me.

  13. 15.4

    …one should then seek out that state from which those who reach it never return, thinking: 'I take refuge in that primal Person from whom this ancient flow of creation has streamed forth.'

  14. 18.56

    Though always performing all actions, one who takes refuge in me reaches, by my grace, the eternal, imperishable abode.

  15. 18.58

    With your mind on me, you will cross every obstacle by my grace. But if, out of ego, you will not listen, you will be lost.

  16. 18.62

    Take refuge in him alone with your whole being, Bharata. By his grace you will attain supreme peace and the eternal abode.

  17. 18.66

    Letting go of all dharmas, come to me alone for refuge. I will free you from all sins; do not grieve.

  18. 18.73

    My delusion is gone; by your grace, Achyuta, my memory has been restored. I stand firm, my doubts are cleared, and I will do as you say.
