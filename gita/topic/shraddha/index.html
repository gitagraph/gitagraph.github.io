Shraddha

Shraddha

श्रद्धा

Faith

A person is made of their faith; as is their faith, so are they (17.3).

मनुष्य श्रद्धामय है; जैसी उसकी श्रद्धा, वैसा ही वह (17.3)।

25 verses

  1. 3.31

    Those who always follow this teaching of mine with faith and without finding fault are also released from the bonds of action.

  2. 3.32

    But those who scoff at it and do not follow my teaching, know them to be confused about all knowledge, senseless and lost.

  3. 4.34

    Learn it by bowing down, by sincere questioning, and by service. The wise who have seen the truth will teach you that knowledge.

  4. 4.39

    One who has faith, who is devoted to it and has mastered the senses, gains knowledge; and having gained it, soon reaches supreme peace.

  5. 4.40

    But one who is ignorant, without faith and full of doubt is ruined. For the doubter there is neither this world nor the next, nor any happiness.

  6. 6.37

    Krishna, what becomes of the person who has faith but lacks self-control, whose mind slips away from yoga, and who fails to reach its perfection?

  7. 6.47

    And of all yogis, the one who, full of faith, worships me with his innermost self absorbed in me — that one I regard as the most closely united.

  8. 7.21

    Whatever form a devotee wishes to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his unwavering.

  9. 7.22

    Endowed with that faith he seeks to worship that form, and from it he obtains his desires — yet those are in truth granted by me alone.

  10. 9.3

    People who have no faith in this teaching, Parantapa, do not reach me; they turn back onto the road of death and rebirth.

  11. 9.23

    Even those who are devoted to other gods and worship them with faith, Kaunteya, are in fact worshipping me alone, though not in the proper way.

  12. 9.25

    Those devoted to the gods go to the gods, those devoted to the ancestors go to the ancestors, those who worship spirits go to the spirits, and those who worship me come to me.

  13. 12.2

    Those who fix their minds on me and worship me, always united with me and filled with the highest faith, I consider the most perfectly joined in yoga.

  14. 12.20

    But those who, full of faith and holding me as supreme, devotedly follow this immortal teaching of dharma just as I have given it — those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.

  15. 13.25

    Still others, not knowing this themselves, worship after hearing it from others; they too, devoted to what they have heard, cross beyond death.

  16. 17.1

    Krishna, what about those who set aside the rules of scripture yet worship with faith? What is their standing: sattva, rajas or tamas?

  17. 17.2

    The faith of embodied beings is of three kinds, born of each one's own nature: sattvic, rajasic and tamasic. Hear about it.

  18. 17.3

    Everyone's faith takes the shape of their inner nature, Bharata. A person is made of faith: whatever one's faith is, that is what one is.

  19. 17.4

    Sattvic people worship the gods; rajasic people worship yakshas and rakshasas; and tamasic people worship ghosts and hosts of spirits.

  20. 17.13

    Sacrifice that ignores the rules, distributes no food, lacks mantras, gives no fee to the priests and is devoid of faith is called tamasic.

  21. 17.17

    When this threefold austerity is practised with the highest faith by disciplined people who seek no reward, it is called sattvic.

  22. 17.28

    Whatever is offered, given, practised as austerity or done without faith is called 'asat', Partha; it is of no value, either after death or here.

  23. 18.42

    Calm, self-restraint, austerity, purity, forbearance, straightforwardness, knowledge, realised wisdom and faith in the sacred are the natural work of a brahmin.

  24. 18.67

    Never speak of this to one without austerity, nor to one without devotion, nor to one who will not listen, nor to one who finds fault with me.

  25. 18.71

    And a person who simply listens to it with faith and without finding fault, he too will be freed and will reach the auspicious worlds of those whose deeds are pure.
