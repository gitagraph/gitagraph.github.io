Purushottama

पुरुषोत्तम

The Supreme Person

Beyond the perishable (kshara) and the imperishable (akshara) stands the highest Person (15.16–18).

क्षर और अक्षर से परे सर्वोच्च पुरुष (15.16–18)।

14 verses

  1. 8.8

    With a mind trained by the yoga of practice and straying nowhere else, contemplating constantly, Partha, one reaches the supreme divine Person.

  2. 8.9

    One who meditates on the seer, the ancient one, the ruler, subtler than the subtlest, the sustainer of all, of inconceivable form, radiant as the sun and beyond all darkness...

  3. 8.10

    ...at the time of departure, with an unwavering mind, filled with devotion and by the strength of yoga, drawing the life-breath fully between the eyebrows — he reaches that supreme divine Person.

  4. 8.22

    That supreme Person, Partha, within whom all beings dwell and by whom all this is pervaded, can be reached through undivided devotion.

  5. 10.15

    Only you know yourself through yourself, Supreme Person, source of beings, lord of beings, god of gods, master of the world.

  6. 13.22

    The supreme Purusha in this body is also called the witness, the one who consents, the sustainer, the experiencer, the great Lord and the supreme Self.

  7. 14.27

    For I am the foundation of Brahman — of the immortal and imperishable, of eternal dharma, and of absolute bliss.

  8. 15.15

    I am seated in the heart of everyone; from me come memory, knowledge and their loss. I alone am what all the Vedas seek to make known; I am the author of Vedanta and the knower of the Vedas.

  9. 15.16

    There are two kinds of persons in this world, the perishable and the imperishable. All beings are the perishable; the unchanging one, standing firm at the core, is called the imperishable.

  10. 15.17

    But there is another, the highest Person, called the Supreme Self, the unchanging Lord who pervades the three worlds and sustains them.

  11. 15.18

    Because I am beyond the perishable and higher even than the imperishable, I am celebrated in the world and in the Veda as Purushottama, the Supreme Person.

  12. 15.19

    Whoever, free of confusion, knows me in this way as the Supreme Person knows everything, Bharata, and worships me with their whole being.

  13. 15.20

    Sinless one, I have now told you this most secret teaching. Understanding it, Bharata, a person becomes truly wise and has done all that needs to be done.

  14. 18.61

    The Lord dwells in the heart of all beings, Arjuna, turning them all by his maya as if they were mounted on a machine.
