लोभ

Greed

Third gate to hell (16.21).

नरक का तीसरा द्वार (16.21)।

7 verses

  1. 1.38

    Even if they, their minds overpowered by greed, do not see the evil in destroying a family or the sin in betraying friends,

  2. 1.45

    Alas, what a great sin we have resolved to commit, setting out to kill our own people out of greed for the pleasures of a kingdom!

  3. 14.12

    Greed, busy activity, the launching of ventures, restlessness and longing — these arise when rajas has grown strong, best of the Bharatas.

  4. 16.12

    …bound by hundreds of ropes of hope, given over to lust and anger, they strive to pile up wealth by unjust means for the sake of enjoying their desires.

  5. 16.13

    'This I have gained today; that wish I will fulfil next. This wealth is mine, and more will be mine again.'

  6. 16.21

    Lust, anger and greed: this is the threefold gate of hell that ruins the self. Therefore one should give up all three.

  7. 18.27

    A doer who is driven by passion, eager for the fruits of action, greedy, harmful by nature, impure, and swept by joy and grief, is called rajasic.
