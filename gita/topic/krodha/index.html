Krodha

Krodha

क्रोध

Anger

Desire thwarted becomes anger (2.62). Second gate to hell.

बाधित कामना क्रोध बन जाती है (2.62)। नरक का दूसरा द्वार।

12 verses

  1. 2.56

    One whose mind is not shaken in sorrow, who does not crave pleasures, who is free from passion, fear and anger — such a sage is called steady of mind.

  2. 2.62

    When a person dwells on sense objects, attachment to them arises; from attachment comes desire, and from desire comes anger.

  3. 2.63

    Anger breeds delusion, and delusion confuses memory; when memory fails, understanding is ruined, and when understanding is ruined, a person is lost.

  4. 3.37

    It is desire, it is anger, born of the quality of passion; it devours without end and is the great evil. Know it as the enemy here.

  5. 5.23

    One who can withstand, right here before leaving the body, the surge that comes from desire and anger is steady in yoga; that person is happy.

  6. 5.26

    For the self-disciplined who are free of desire and anger, who have mastered their thoughts and know the Self, the peace of Brahman is near on every side.

  7. 16.4

    Hypocrisy, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance, Partha, belong to one born with the demonic nature.

  8. 16.12

    …bound by hundreds of ropes of hope, given over to lust and anger, they strive to pile up wealth by unjust means for the sake of enjoying their desires.

  9. 16.18

    Clinging to ego, force, arrogance, lust and anger, these envious people hate me, who dwell in their own bodies and in the bodies of others.

  10. 16.21

    Lust, anger and greed: this is the threefold gate of hell that ruins the self. Therefore one should give up all three.

  11. 16.22

    A person released from these three gates of darkness, Kaunteya, does what is truly good for the self, and so reaches the highest goal.

  12. 18.53

    …letting go of ego, force, arrogance, desire, anger and possessiveness, free of the sense of 'mine' and at peace, such a person is fit to become Brahman.
