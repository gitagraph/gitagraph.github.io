Pandavas

पाण्डव

The Pandava side

The five sons of Pandu and their allies.

पांडु के पाँच पुत्र और उनके सहयोगी।

6 verses

  1. 1.1

    On the field of dharma, at Kurukshetra, my sons and the sons of Pandu gathered there eager to fight. What did they do, Sanjaya?

  2. 1.2

    Seeing the Pandava army drawn up in battle order, King Duryodhana then went up to his teacher and spoke these words.

  3. 1.3

    Teacher, look at this vast army of the sons of Pandu, arrayed by the son of Drupada, your own gifted pupil.

  4. 1.10

    That strength of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is beyond measure, while their strength, guarded by Bhima, is limited.

  5. 10.37

    Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas, Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; and among poets, the poet Ushana.

  6. 11.26

    And all these sons of Dhritarashtra, along with the hosts of kings, Bhishma, Drona and that charioteer's son too, together with the chief warriors on our side as well,
