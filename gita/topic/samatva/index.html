Equanimity

Evenness of mind in success and failure. 'Equanimity is called yoga' (2.48).

सफलता और असफलता में मन की समता। 'समत्वं योग उच्यते' (2.48)।

36 verses

  1. 2.14

    Contacts of the senses with their objects, son of Kunti, give rise to cold and heat, pleasure and pain. They come and go and do not last; bear them patiently, Bharata.

  2. 2.15

    The steady person whom these do not disturb, finest of men, who stays the same in pain and pleasure, is fit for immortality.
