Bhurishrava

भूरिश्रवा

Kaurava warrior

Son of Somadatta, named among the Kaurava warriors (1.8).

सोमदत्त के पुत्र, कौरव योद्धाओं में गिने गए (1.8)।

1 verses

  1. 1.8

    Yourself, Bhishma, Karna, and Kripa, ever victorious in battle; Ashvatthama, Vikarna, and the son of Somadatta as well.
