दैवीसम्पद्

Divine qualities

Fearlessness, purity, charity, self-control, truthfulness, non-violence, compassion. They lead to liberation (16.5).

निर्भयता, पवित्रता, दान, आत्मसंयम, सत्य, अहिंसा, करुणा। ये मुक्ति की ओर ले जाते हैं (16.5)।

8 verses

  1. 9.13

    But great souls, Partha, who take refuge in the divine nature, worship me with minds turned to nothing else, knowing me as the imperishable source of all beings.

  2. 12.13

    One who bears ill will toward no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free of 'mine' and of ego, the same in pain and pleasure, and forgiving…

  3. 13.7

    Freedom from pride and from pretence, non-violence, patience, straightforwardness, service of the teacher, purity, steadiness and self-restraint…

  4. 16.1

    Fearlessness, a purified inner being, steadiness in the path of knowledge, generosity, self-restraint, sacrifice, study of scripture, austerity and straightforwardness…

  5. 16.2

    …non-violence, truthfulness, freedom from anger, renunciation, calm, not slandering others, compassion for all beings, freedom from greed, gentleness, a sense of shame and steadiness of purpose…

  6. 16.3

    …vigour, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and from excessive pride. These, Bharata, belong to one born with the divine nature.

  7. 16.5

    The divine nature is held to lead to liberation, and the demonic to bondage. Do not grieve, Pandava; you were born with the divine nature.

  8. 16.6

    There are two kinds of beings created in this world: the divine and the demonic. The divine has been described at length; now, Partha, hear from me about the demonic.
