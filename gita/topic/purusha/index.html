Purusha

Purusha

पुरुष

Spirit, the experiencer

The conscious principle that experiences prakriti; both are beginningless (13.19).

वह चेतन तत्त्व जो प्रकृति का अनुभव करता है; दोनों अनादि हैं (13.19)।

10 verses

  1. 8.4

    The physical realm is perishable existence; the divine realm is the cosmic Person; and the lord of sacrifice, best of the embodied, is I myself, here in this body.

  2. 10.12

    You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier: the eternal, divine Person, the first of the gods, unborn and all-pervading.

  3. 11.18

    You are the imperishable, the supreme one to be known; you are the final resting place of this universe. You are the undying guardian of eternal dharma; you are, I believe, the everlasting Person.

  4. 11.38

    You are the first god, the ancient Person; you are the final resting place of this universe. You are the knower and the known and the highest abode. By you this universe is pervaded, O you of infinite forms.

  5. 13.19

    Know that Prakriti and Purusha are both without beginning, and know that the changes of form and the gunas arise from Prakriti.

  6. 13.20

    Prakriti is said to be the cause behind effect, instrument and agency; Purusha is said to be the cause behind the experiencing of pleasure and pain.

  7. 13.21

    Dwelling in Prakriti, Purusha experiences the gunas born of Prakriti; its attachment to the gunas is the cause of its births in good and evil wombs.

  8. 13.23

    One who knows Purusha and Prakriti together with the gunas in this way is not born again, however he may be living.

  9. 15.4

    …one should then seek out that state from which those who reach it never return, thinking: 'I take refuge in that primal Person from whom this ancient flow of creation has streamed forth.'

  10. 15.16

    There are two kinds of persons in this world, the perishable and the imperishable. All beings are the perishable; the unchanging one, standing firm at the core, is called the imperishable.
