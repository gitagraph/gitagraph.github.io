Bhagavad Gita 18.16

Krishna

तत्रैवं सति कर्तारमात्मानं केवलं तु यः ।
पश्यत्यकृतबुद्धित्वान्न स पश्यति दुर्मतिः ॥ १८.१६ ॥

tatraivaṃ sati kartāramātmānaṃ kevalaṃ tu yaḥ ।
paśyatyakṛtabuddhitvānna sa paśyati durmatiḥ ॥

That being so, whoever, through an untrained understanding, sees the pure Self alone as the doer, that dull-minded person does not see truly.

हिन्दीऐसा होने पर भी जो व्यक्ति अपरिपक्व बुद्धि के कारण केवल शुद्ध आत्मा को ही कर्ता मानता है, वह मंदबुद्धि सही नहीं देखता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a team project succeeds or falls apart, it was never you alone: colleagues, tools, effort and plain luck all played a part. Seeing that saves you from both swagger and crushing self-blame.

Krishna says that anyone who, through an untrained understanding, sees the pure Self alone as the doer does not see truly.

Since five causes join in every action (18.14), crediting the ātman by itself with the deed is an error of akṛta-buddhi, an understanding not yet formed. Some read the verse as a warning against taking oneself as the sole agent, others as saying the Self does not act at all; both lead into the next verse.
