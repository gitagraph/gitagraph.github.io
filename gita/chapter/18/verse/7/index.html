Bhagavad Gita 18.7

Krishna

नियतस्य तु संन्यासः कर्मणो नोपपद्यते ।
मोहात्तस्य परित्यागस्तामसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १८.७ ॥

niyatasya tu saṃnyāsaḥ karmaṇo nopapadyate ।
mohāttasya parityāgastāmasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ ॥

It is not right to renounce an action that is one's duty. Giving it up out of delusion is called tamasic relinquishment.

हिन्दीनियत कर्तव्य कर्म का संन्यास करना उचित नहीं है। मोह के कारण उसे छोड़ देना तामस त्याग कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Telling yourself a teenager will 'figure it out' and stepping back from guiding them altogether can feel like letting go. Often it is just confusion dressed up as freedom.

Krishna describes the lowest kind of relinquishment, the tāmasa kind: dropping an action that is one's duty out of delusion.

The key word is niyata, 'required', the work that belongs to one's place and responsibilities. Giving it up because one is confused about what matters is not renunciation at all but moha, delusion, dressed up as letting go, a word Arjuna will use of his own state at the end (18.73).
