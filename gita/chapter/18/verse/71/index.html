Bhagavad Gita 18.71

Krishna

श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च शृणुयादपि यो नरः ।
सोऽपि मुक्तः शुभाँल्लोकान्प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम् ॥ १८.७१ ॥

śraddhāvānanasūyaśca śṛṇuyādapi yo naraḥ ।
so'pi muktaḥ śubhā~llokānprāpnuyātpuṇyakarmaṇām ॥

And a person who simply listens to it with faith and without finding fault, he too will be freed and will reach the auspicious worlds of those whose deeds are pure.

हिन्दीऔर जो मनुष्य श्रद्धा के साथ, दोष देखे बिना इसे केवल सुनेगा, वह भी मुक्त होकर पुण्य कर्म करने वालों के शुभ लोकों को प्राप्त करेगा।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a grandparent shares something learned over a long life, listening openly instead of picking holes in it is how you actually receive it. Fair-minded listening is a gift in itself.

Even someone who only listens to this teaching with faith and without finding fault, Krishna says, will be freed and will reach the auspicious worlds of those whose deeds are pure.

The key word is anasūya, 'free of carping', the opposite of the fault-finder of 18.67. The verses 18.68–71 thus move from teacher to student to listener, each given a place. For a reader, open and fair-minded listening is itself valued.
