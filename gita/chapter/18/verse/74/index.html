Bhagavad Gita 18.74

Sanjaya

इत्यहं वासुदेवस्य पार्थस्य च महात्मनः ।
संवादमिममश्रौषमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ १८.७४ ॥

ityahaṃ vāsudevasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ ।
saṃvādamimamaśrauṣamadbhutaṃ romaharṣaṇam ॥

Thus I heard this wondrous dialogue between Vasudeva and the great-souled Partha, which made my hair stand on end.

हिन्दीइस प्रकार मैंने वासुदेव और महात्मा पार्थ के बीच का यह अद्भुत संवाद सुना, जिससे मेरे रोंगटे खड़े हो गए।

Sanjaya, who has been reporting the battlefield to King Dhritarashtra since the opening verses, closes the dialogue in his own voice: he has heard this exchange between Vasudeva and the great-souled Arjuna, and it made his hair stand on end.

He calls it adbhuta, wondrous, and romaharṣaṇa, 'hair-raising'. The frame of the Gita, a conversation overheard and retold, comes back into view here, and the listener's response becomes part of the text.
