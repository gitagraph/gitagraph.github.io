Bhagavad Gita 18.26

Krishna

मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते ॥ १८.२६ ॥

muktasaṅgo'nahaṃvādī dhṛtyutsāhasamanvitaḥ ।
siddhyasiddhyornirvikāraḥ kartā sāttvika ucyate ॥

A doer who is free of attachment, does not talk of 'I', is full of steadiness and zeal, and is unmoved by success or failure, is called sattvic.

हिन्दीजो कर्ता आसक्ति से मुक्त है, 'मैं' की बात नहीं करता, धैर्य और उत्साह से भरा है, और सफलता या असफलता में विचलित नहीं होता, वह सात्त्विक कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A nurse who turns up on time through dull weeks and stays level after a frantic emergency shows this verse well: steady zeal, without the self-congratulation or the despair.

Krishna turns to the doer and describes the sāttvika kind: free of attachment, not given to talk of 'I', full of steadiness and zeal, and unchanged by success or failure.

The key pair is dhṛti, steadiness, and utsāha, zeal: the calm described here is not indifference, since energy and persistence remain. It is a portrait of someone who works wholeheartedly yet is not undone when things go wrong.
