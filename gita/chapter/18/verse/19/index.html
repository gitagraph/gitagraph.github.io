Bhagavad Gita 18.19

Krishna

ज्ञानं कर्म च कर्ता च त्रिधैव गुणभेदतः ।
प्रोच्यते गुणसंख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यपि ॥ १८.१९ ॥

jñānaṃ karma ca kartā ca tridhaiva guṇabhedataḥ ।
procyate guṇasaṃkhyāne yathāvacchṛṇu tānyapi ॥

Knowledge, action and the doer are each said to be of three kinds, differing according to the gunas, in the analysis of the gunas. Hear about them as they are.

हिन्दीगुणों का विवेचन करने वाले शास्त्र में ज्ञान, कर्म और कर्ता को भी गुणों के भेद से तीन-तीन प्रकार का बताया गया है। उन्हें भी ठीक-ठीक सुनो।

Krishna introduces a sorting of knowledge, action and the doer into three kinds each, according to the guṇas.

The term guṇa-saṃkhyāna, 'the reckoning of the guṇas', is traditionally taken to mean the Sankhya analysis of sattva, rajas and tamas, the three strands said to run through all of nature, already described in chapter 14. What follows (18.20–28) is a set of portraits that can serve as a mirror for noticing which strand shapes one's own way of seeing and working.
