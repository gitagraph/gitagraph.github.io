Bhagavad Gita 18.76

Sanjaya

राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम् ।
केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ १८.७६ ॥

rājansaṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya saṃvādamimamadbhutam ।
keśavārjunayoḥ puṇyaṃ hṛṣyāmi ca muhurmuhuḥ ॥

O King, as I recall again and again this wondrous and sacred dialogue between Keshava and Arjuna, I rejoice over and over.

हिन्दीहे राजन, केशव और अर्जुन के इस अद्भुत और पवित्र संवाद को बार-बार याद करके मैं बार-बार आनंदित होता हूँ।

Sanjaya tells King Dhritarashtra that as he recalls the wondrous, sacred dialogue of Keshava and Arjuna again and again, he rejoices over and over.

The doubled saṃsmṛtya, 'remembering, remembering', is the key: the teaching does not end when it is heard but keeps returning. For a reader, it captures how a text like this tends to work, gaining fresh meaning each time it comes back to mind.
