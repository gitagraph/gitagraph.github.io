Bhagavad Gita 18.10

Krishna

न द्वेष्ट्यकुशलं कर्म कुशले नानुषज्जते ।
त्यागी सत्त्वसमाविष्टो मेधावी छिन्नसंशयः ॥ १८.१० ॥

na dveṣṭyakuśalaṃ karma kuśale nānuṣajjate ।
tyāgī sattvasamāviṣṭo medhāvī chinnasaṃśayaḥ ॥

The relinquisher filled with sattva, wise and free of doubt, neither resents unpleasant work nor clings to pleasant work.

हिन्दीसत्त्वगुण से भरा हुआ, बुद्धिमान और संशय-रहित त्यागी न तो अप्रिय कर्म से द्वेष करता है और न प्रिय कर्म में आसक्त होता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Every job hands you the dull spreadsheet and the exciting launch. Doing the first without grumbling and the second without clinging to it is a quiet sign of a settled mind.

Krishna describes the true relinquisher filled with sattva: such a person neither resents disagreeable work nor clings to agreeable work.

The paired words are akuśala and kuśala, the unwelcome task and the welcome one. Because liking is no longer the reason for acting, the same calm attends both, and doubt has been cut through (chinna-saṃśaya). It pictures someone who does what needs doing without sorting tasks into those they enjoy and those they avoid.
