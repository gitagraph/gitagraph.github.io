Bhagavad Gita 18.65

Krishna

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे ॥ १८.६५ ॥

manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru ।
māmevaiṣyasi satyaṃ te pratijāne priyo'si me ॥

Fix your mind on me, be devoted to me, offer worship to me, bow down to me. You will come to me; I promise you truly, for you are dear to me.

हिन्दीअपना मन मुझमें लगाओ, मेरे भक्त बनो, मेरी पूजा करो और मुझे प्रणाम करो। तुम मुझे ही प्राप्त होगे; यह मैं तुमसे सच्चा वचन देता हूँ, क्योंकि तुम मुझे प्रिय हो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

On a morning when everything feels uncertain, a new city, a new job, a family worry, turning your mind first to what you hold highest and offering it the day's work gives the hours a centre.

Krishna asks Arjuna to fix his mind on him, devote himself, worship and bow down, and promises truly that Arjuna will come to him, for he is dear.

This is the 'most secret' word he promised out of love, echoing the end of chapter 9: man-manāḥ, 'one whose mind is on me', the whole person turning one way. Plainly, it is framed less as a rule than as a personal promise, made because 'you are dear to me'.
