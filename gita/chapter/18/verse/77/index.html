Bhagavad Gita 18.77

Sanjaya

तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः ।
विस्मयो मे महान् राजन्हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः ॥ १८.७७ ॥

tacca saṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya rūpamatyadbhutaṃ hareḥ ।
vismayo me mahān rājanhṛṣyāmi ca punaḥ punaḥ ॥

And as I recall again and again that most wondrous form of Hari, great is my amazement, O King, and I rejoice again and again.

हिन्दीऔर हे राजन, हरि के उस अत्यंत अद्भुत रूप को बार-बार याद करके मुझे बड़ा विस्मय होता है और मैं बार-बार हर्षित होता हूँ।

Sanjaya says that recalling again and again the most wondrous form of Hari fills him with great amazement and he rejoices repeatedly.

The form is most naturally read as the cosmic form of chapter 11, which Sanjaya himself had described to the king. Traditionally, Hari is a name of Vishnu, often explained as 'the one who takes away'. The key word is vismaya, wonder, the feeling Sanjaya carries away from everything he has witnessed.
