Bhagavad Gita 18.5

Krishna

यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यं कार्यमेव तत् ।
यज्ञो दानं तपश्चैव पावनानि मनीषिणाम् ॥ १८.५ ॥

yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyaṃ kāryameva tat ।
yajño dānaṃ tapaścaiva pāvanāni manīṣiṇām ॥

Acts of sacrifice, giving and austerity should not be given up; they ought to be performed, for sacrifice, giving and austerity purify even the wise.

हिन्दीयज्ञ, दान और तप के कर्मों को छोड़ना नहीं चाहिए, उन्हें अवश्य करना चाहिए; क्योंकि यज्ञ, दान और तप बुद्धिमानों को भी पवित्र करने वाले हैं।

In your life

Even if you feel you've grown past it, keep up the small generosities: the monthly donation, an hour helping a neighbour. They keep working on you, however far along you are.

Krishna gives the first part of his verdict: acts of sacrifice, giving and austerity are not to be given up but done.

The key word is pāvana, 'purifying': these acts cleanse even the wise, so they are not meant only for beginners. On what to keep, he sides with the second view of 18.3, and the next verse adds how it should be done. Worship, generosity and self-discipline are treated as work that goes on refining a person, however far along.
