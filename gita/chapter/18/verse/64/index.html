Bhagavad Gita 18.64

Krishna

सर्वगुह्यतमं भूयः शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम् ॥ १८.६४ ॥

sarvaguhyatamaṃ bhūyaḥ śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ ।
iṣṭo'si me dṛḍhamiti tato vakṣyāmi te hitam ॥

Hear once more my supreme word, the most secret of all. You are dearly loved by me, so I will tell you what is for your good.

हिन्दीमेरी सबसे गोपनीय और परम बात फिर से सुनो। तुम मुझे बहुत प्रिय हो, इसलिए मैं तुम्हारे हित की बात कहूँगा।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The most important advice often comes from someone who loves you and says it twice. When a well-wisher repeats something, listen as if it were the first time.

Having left Arjuna free to reflect and choose (18.63), Krishna asks him to hear once more his supreme word, the most secret of all, offered because Arjuna is deeply dear to him.

The key word is hita, 'what is good for you': what follows in 18.65–66 is framed as care, not command. The verse explains the tone of the closing teaching: it is given out of affection, to someone trusted to weigh it.
