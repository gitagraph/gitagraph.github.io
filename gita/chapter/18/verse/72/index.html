Bhagavad Gita 18.72

Krishna

कच्चिदेतच्छ्रुतं पार्थ त्वयैकाग्रेण चेतसा ।
कच्चिदज्ञानसंमोहः प्रनष्टस्ते धनंजय ॥ १८.७२ ॥

kaccidetacchrutaṃ pārtha tvayaikāgreṇa cetasā ।
kaccidajñānasaṃmohaḥ pranaṣṭaste dhanaṃjaya ॥

Partha, have you heard this with a fully focused mind? Dhananjaya, has your delusion born of ignorance been dispelled?

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, क्या तुमने इसे एकाग्र मन से सुना? हे धनंजय, क्या अज्ञान से उत्पन्न तुम्हारा मोह नष्ट हो गया?

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

After a long, honest talk with someone you trust, it's worth asking yourself plainly: did I really hear that, and has my confusion actually cleared?

Krishna ends his teaching with two questions to Arjuna: has he listened with full attention, and has the delusion born of ignorance been dispelled?

The key word is saṃmoha, deep bewilderment, the very state Arjuna admitted to at the start (2.7). After setting out the whole teaching, Krishna asks rather than declares, leaving Arjuna to answer for himself in the next verse.
