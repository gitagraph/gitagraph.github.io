Bhagavad Gita 18.70

Krishna

अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मतिः ॥ १८.७० ॥

adhyeṣyate ca ya imaṃ dharmyaṃ saṃvādamāvayoḥ ।
jñānayajñena tenāhamiṣṭaḥ syāmiti me matiḥ ॥

And whoever studies this sacred dialogue of ours, by him I will have been worshipped through the sacrifice of knowledge; that is my view.

हिन्दीऔर जो हमारे इस धर्ममय संवाद का अध्ययन करेगा, उसके द्वारा मैं ज्ञानयज्ञ से पूजित हो जाऊँगा; ऐसा मेरा मत है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Studying something wise with real attention, even a few lines a day, is itself an offering. Reading slowly and thinking it through counts as much as any ritual.

Whoever studies this sacred dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna, Krishna says, will have worshipped him through the sacrifice of knowledge.

The key term is jñāna-yajña, the offering made through understanding, which 4.33 had already ranked above offerings of material things. The verse treats careful study itself as worship. For readers today, it gives the plain act of reading and thinking through the text a meaning of its own.
