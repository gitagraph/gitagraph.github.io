Bhagavad Gita 18.29

Krishna

बुद्धेर्भेदं धृतेश्चैव गुणतस्त्रिविधं शृणु ।
प्रोच्यमानमशेषेण पृथक्त्वेन धनंजय ॥ १८.२९ ॥

buddherbhedaṃ dhṛteścaiva guṇatastrividhaṃ śṛṇu ।
procyamānamaśeṣeṇa pṛthaktvena dhanaṃjaya ॥

Now hear, Dhananjaya, the threefold division of understanding and of resolve according to the gunas, which I will explain fully and one by one.

हिन्दीहे धनंजय, अब गुणों के अनुसार बुद्धि और धृति के तीन-तीन भेद सुनो, जिन्हें मैं पूरी तरह और अलग-अलग बताऊँगा।

Krishna announces the next part of his analysis: the threefold division of buddhi, understanding, and dhṛti, resolve, according to the guṇas.

Buddhi is the faculty that judges what is right; dhṛti is the steadiness that holds a person to a course once it is chosen. Together they decide whether sound knowledge ever turns into steady action, which is why they are examined next (18.30–35).
