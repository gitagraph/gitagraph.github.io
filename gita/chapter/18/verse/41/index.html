Bhagavad Gita 18.41

Krishna

ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां च परन्तप ।
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि स्वभावप्रभवैर्गुणैः ॥ १८.४१ ॥

brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ śūdrāṇāṃ ca parantapa ।
karmāṇi pravibhaktāni svabhāvaprabhavairguṇaiḥ ॥

The duties of brahmins, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras, scorcher of foes, are divided according to the gunas that arise from their own nature.

हिन्दीहे परंतप, ब्राह्मणों, क्षत्रियों, वैश्यों और शूद्रों के कर्म उनके स्वभाव से उत्पन्न गुणों के अनुसार बाँटे गए हैं।

Krishna applies the gunas to the four social orders, saying their duties are divided according to qualities that arise from each person's own nature.

The key word is svabhāva, one's inborn nature, echoing 4.13, where the four orders are linked to guṇa and karma. Readers differ sharply here: some take the orders as fixed by birth, others as a description of temperament and aptitude. The verse itself grounds the division in nature, not in rank.
