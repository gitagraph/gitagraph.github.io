Bhagavad Gita 18.11

Krishna

न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः ।
यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते ॥ १८.११ ॥

na hi dehabhṛtā śakyaṃ tyaktuṃ karmāṇyaśeṣataḥ ।
yastu karmaphalatyāgī sa tyāgītyabhidhīyate ॥

No embodied being can give up actions completely. But one who gives up the fruits of action is truly called a relinquisher.

हिन्दीदेहधारी के लिए कर्मों को पूरी तरह छोड़ पाना संभव नहीं है। परंतु जो कर्मफल का त्याग करता है, वही सच्चा त्यागी कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Even on a sick day in bed, you still answer the door, eat, decide what can wait; no one can stop acting altogether. What you can set down is the clinging to how it all turns out.

Krishna explains why true relinquishment cannot mean stopping all action: no one who lives in a body can give up action completely.

The key word is dehabhṛt, 'body-bearer', anyone who has a body and so must breathe, eat and act, a point made earlier (3.5, 3.8). So the real relinquisher is the karma-phala-tyāgī, one who gives up the fruits. Letting go is about how we act, not whether we act.
