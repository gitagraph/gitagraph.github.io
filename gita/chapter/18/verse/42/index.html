Bhagavad Gita 18.42

Krishna

शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ १८.४२ ॥

śamo damastapaḥ śaucaṃ kṣāntirārjavameva ca ।
jñānaṃ vijñānamāstikyaṃ brahmakarma svabhāvajam ॥

Calm, self-restraint, austerity, purity, forbearance, straightforwardness, knowledge, realised wisdom and faith in the sacred are the natural work of a brahmin.

हिन्दीशांति, आत्मसंयम, तप, पवित्रता, सहनशीलता, सरलता, ज्ञान, अनुभवजन्य विज्ञान और आस्तिकता — ये ब्राह्मण के स्वभाव से उत्पन्न कर्म हैं।

Krishna lists the qualities natural to a brahmin: calm, self-restraint, austerity, purity, forbearance, straightforwardness, knowledge, realised wisdom and faith in the sacred.

What is notable is that the list is made almost entirely of inner traits rather than rituals or privileges. The key pairing is jñāna and vijñāna, learning and lived understanding. Read today, it describes what a life devoted to thought and teaching would need from the person living it.
