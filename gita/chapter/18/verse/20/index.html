Bhagavad Gita 18.20

Krishna

सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते ।
अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम् ॥ १८.२० ॥

sarvabhūteṣu yenaikaṃ bhāvamavyayamīkṣate ।
avibhaktaṃ vibhakteṣu tajjñānaṃ viddhi sāttvikam ॥

The knowledge by which one sees a single imperishable reality in all beings, undivided among the divided, know that knowledge to be sattvic.

हिन्दीजिस ज्ञान से मनुष्य सभी प्राणियों में एक ही अविनाशी सत्ता को देखता है, जो अलग-अलग में भी अविभक्त है, उस ज्ञान को सात्त्विक जानो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a hospital waiting room full of strangers from every walk of life, seeing the same life in each of them changes how you share the bench, and your patience.

Krishna describes sāttvika knowledge: the vision that sees one imperishable reality in all beings, undivided within what is divided.

The key phrase is avibhaktaṃ vibhakteṣu, 'undivided among the divided': the many forms remain, but they are seen to share a single ground. Traditions differ on whether that unity is one Self in all or a shared divine presence; in either reading it widens concern beyond one's own circle.
