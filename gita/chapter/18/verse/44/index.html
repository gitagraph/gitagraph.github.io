Bhagavad Gita 18.44

Krishna

कृषिगौरक्ष्यवाणिज्यं वैश्यकर्म स्वभावजम् ।
परिचर्यात्मकं कर्म शूद्रस्यापि स्वभावजम् ॥ १८.४४ ॥

kṛṣigaurakṣyavāṇijyaṃ vaiśyakarma svabhāvajam ।
paricaryātmakaṃ karma śūdrasyāpi svabhāvajam ॥

Farming, tending cattle and trade are the natural work of a vaishya; work that consists of service is the natural work of a shudra.

हिन्दीखेती, गोपालन और व्यापार वैश्य के स्वभाव से उत्पन्न कर्म हैं; और सेवा रूपी कर्म शूद्र के स्वभाव से उत्पन्न कर्म है।

Krishna completes the list: farming, cattle-keeping and trade are the natural work of a vaishya, and work that takes the form of service is the natural work of a shudra.

The repeated svabhāvaja, 'born of one's nature', is the thread through all four lists (18.42–44). How this is read depends on the larger question of 18.41; the next verses stress that each kind of work, done well, is itself a path to fulfilment.
