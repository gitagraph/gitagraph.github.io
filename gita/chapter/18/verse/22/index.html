Bhagavad Gita 18.22

Krishna

यत्तु कृत्स्नवदेकस्मिन्कार्ये सक्तमहैतुकम् ।
अतत्त्वार्थवदल्पं च तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १८.२२ ॥

yattu kṛtsnavadekasminkārye saktamahaitukam ।
atattvārthavadalpaṃ ca tattāmasamudāhṛtam ॥

And the knowledge that clings to one single thing as if it were the whole, without reason, without grasp of the truth, and narrow, is declared tamasic.

हिन्दीऔर जो ज्ञान किसी एक ही वस्तु को सब कुछ मानकर उसी में चिपका रहता है, जो बिना तर्क का है, सत्य को नहीं पकड़ता और तुच्छ है, उसे तामस कहा गया है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

One loud quarrel with a neighbour can harden into 'everyone in this building is rude'. That is a single grievance mistaken for the whole picture, never once questioned.

Krishna describes tāmasa knowledge: a narrow view that clings to one single thing as if it were everything, without reason and without grasp of the truth.

The key word is kṛtsnavat, 'as if it were the whole': a part is mistaken for the entire picture. It is a recognisable habit, fixing on one belief, grievance or object and treating it as all there is, while never asking why.
