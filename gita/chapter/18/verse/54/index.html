Bhagavad Gita 18.54

Krishna

ब्रह्मभूतः प्रसन्नात्मा न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।
समः सर्वेषु भूतेषु मद्भक्तिं लभते पराम् ॥ १८.५४ ॥

brahmabhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati ।
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu madbhaktiṃ labhate parām ॥

Having become Brahman, serene in spirit, he neither grieves nor longs. Equal towards all beings, he attains supreme devotion to me.

हिन्दीब्रह्मभाव को प्राप्त होकर, प्रसन्न मन वाला वह न शोक करता है और न कुछ चाहता है। सभी प्राणियों के प्रति समान भाव रखकर वह मेरी परम भक्ति को प्राप्त करता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When you are at peace inside, a colleague's raise doesn't sting and a friend's loss doesn't leave you cold. Equal regard for everyone grows out of that inner calm.

Having become Brahman, serene, free of grief and longing, and equal towards all beings, a person attains supreme devotion to Krishna.

The key word is parā bhakti, supreme devotion, and its placement is striking: devotion comes here after becoming Brahman, not before it. Traditions read this differently, some seeing knowledge as completed by devotion, others seeing devotion as the highest form of knowledge. Either way, the verse refuses to separate the two.
