Chapter 18 · Liberation through RenunciationVerse 12 of 78

Bhagavad Gita 18.12

Krishna

अनिष्टमिष्टं मिश्रं च त्रिविधं कर्मणः फलम् ।
भवत्यत्यागिनां प्रेत्य न तु संन्यासिनां क्वचित् ॥ १८.१२ ॥

aniṣṭamiṣṭaṃ miśraṃ ca trividhaṃ karmaṇaḥ phalam ।
bhavatyatyāgināṃ pretya na tu saṃnyāsināṃ kvacit ॥

The fruit of action is threefold after death for those who have not relinquished: unwanted, wanted and mixed. For those who have renounced, there is none at all.

हिन्दीजो त्यागी नहीं हैं, उन्हें मृत्यु के बाद कर्म का तीन प्रकार का फल मिलता है — अनचाहा, चाहा हुआ और मिला-जुला। पर संन्यासियों को कोई फल नहीं मिलता।

Krishna says that those who have not relinquished meet the threefold fruit of action after death, unwanted, wanted and mixed, while those who have renounced meet none at all.

The key word is pretya, 'having departed', that is, after this life. Here saṃnyāsin names the relinquisher just described (18.11), one who has let go of the fruit, which shows how close the two words of 18.1 have come.
