Bhagavad Gita 18.75

Sanjaya

व्यासप्रसादाच्छ्रुतवानेतद्गुह्यमहं परम् ।
योगं योगेश्वरात्कृष्णात्साक्षात्कथयतः स्वयम् ॥ १८.७५ ॥

vyāsaprasādācchrutavānetadguhyamahaṃ param ।
yogaṃ yogeśvarātkṛṣṇātsākṣātkathayataḥ svayam ॥

By the grace of Vyasa I heard this supreme secret, this yoga, directly from Krishna himself, the lord of yoga, as he spoke it.

हिन्दीव्यास जी की कृपा से मैंने यह परम गोपनीय योग स्वयं योगेश्वर कृष्ण के मुख से कहते हुए प्रत्यक्ष सुना।

Sanjaya says that by the grace of Vyasa he heard this supreme secret, this yoga, directly from Krishna, the lord of yoga, as Krishna himself spoke it.

From the Mahabharata: Vyasa, the sage traditionally credited with the epic, gave Sanjaya the power to see and hear the distant battlefield so he could describe it to the blind king. The key word is sākṣāt, 'directly': Sanjaya stresses that he is no second-hand reporter.
