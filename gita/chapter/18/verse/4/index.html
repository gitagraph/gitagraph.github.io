Bhagavad Gita 18.4

Krishna

निश्चयं शृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम ।
त्यागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः संप्रकीर्तितः ॥ १८.४ ॥

niścayaṃ śṛṇu me tatra tyāge bharatasattama ।
tyāgo hi puruṣavyāghra trividhaḥ saṃprakīrtitaḥ ॥

Now hear my firm conclusion about relinquishment, best of the Bharatas; for relinquishment, tiger among men, is declared to be of three kinds.

हिन्दीहे भरतश्रेष्ठ, अब त्याग के विषय में मेरा निश्चित मत सुनो; क्योंकि हे पुरुषसिंह, त्याग तीन प्रकार का बताया गया है।

Krishna announces that he will now give his own settled view on relinquishment, and says that tyāga is of three kinds.

The key word is niścaya, a firm conclusion: having reported other people's opinions (18.2–3), he now speaks for himself. The threefold division, which he soon links to the three guṇas (18.7–9), sets the pattern for much of the chapter, where knowledge, action, doers, understanding and resolve are all sorted in the same way.
