Bhagavad Gita 18.62

Krishna

तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत ।
तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्तिं स्थानं प्राप्स्यसि शाश्वतम् ॥ १८.६२ ॥

tameva śaraṇaṃ gaccha sarvabhāvena bhārata ।
tatprasādātparāṃ śāntiṃ sthānaṃ prāpsyasi śāśvatam ॥

Take refuge in him alone with your whole being, Bharata. By his grace you will attain supreme peace and the eternal abode.

हिन्दीहे भारत, पूरे भाव से केवल उसी की शरण में जाओ। उसकी कृपा से तुम परम शांति और शाश्वत धाम प्राप्त करोगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When every plan has been made and the rest is out of your hands, giving yourself wholly to what you trust most can bring a peace no plan could.

Krishna urges Arjuna to take refuge in that Lord alone, with his whole being, promising that by his grace he will reach supreme peace and the eternal abode.

The key word is sarva-bhāvena, 'with every part of oneself', not with the intellect alone. Some traditions take 'him' as Krishna himself, others as the indwelling Lord of 18.61 in a more general sense. The call to trust is complete either way.
