Bhagavad Gita 18.58

Krishna

मच्चित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि ।
अथ चेत्त्वमहंकारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्क्ष्यसि ॥ १८.५८ ॥

maccittaḥ sarvadurgāṇi matprasādāttariṣyasi ।
atha cettvamahaṃkārānna śroṣyasi vinaṅkṣyasi ॥

With your mind on me, you will cross every obstacle by my grace. But if, out of ego, you will not listen, you will be lost.

हिन्दीमुझमें चित्त लगाकर तुम मेरी कृपा से सभी कठिनाइयों को पार कर जाओगे। परंतु यदि अहंकार के कारण तुम मेरी बात नहीं सुनोगे, तो नष्ट हो जाओगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a mentor you trust warns you about a plan and pride says 'I alone decide', that is the moment to listen. Many obstacles can be crossed; refusing all help out of ego is the one that sinks you.

Krishna sets out two outcomes for Arjuna: with his mind on Krishna, he will cross every difficulty by grace, but if ego keeps him from listening, he will be lost.

The key word is durga, literally a fort or a hard passage, here every obstacle in the way. The warning is aimed at ahaṃkāra, ego, the 'I alone decide' that had led Arjuna to say he would not fight (2.9).
