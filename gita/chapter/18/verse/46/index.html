Bhagavad Gita 18.46

Krishna

यतः प्रवृत्तिर्भूतानां येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।
स्वकर्मणा तमभ्यर्च्य सिद्धिं विन्दति मानवः ॥ १८.४६ ॥

yataḥ pravṛttirbhūtānāṃ yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ।
svakarmaṇā tamabhyarcya siddhiṃ vindati mānavaḥ ॥

By worshipping, through his own work, the one from whom all beings come forth and by whom all this is pervaded, a person attains perfection.

हिन्दीजिससे सभी प्राणी उत्पन्न होते हैं और जिससे यह सारा जगत व्याप्त है, उसकी अपने कर्म के द्वारा पूजा करके मनुष्य सिद्धि प्राप्त करता है।

In your life

A teacher who treats each class as an offering, not just a period to get through, turns ordinary work into something sacred. Any job can be done in that spirit.

Krishna answers how work leads to perfection: by worshipping, through one's own work, the source from which all beings arise and by which all this is pervaded.

The key word is abhyarcya, 'having worshipped': the work itself becomes the offering, with no separate ritual needed. Plainly, it asks a person to see their daily tasks as a way of honouring what sustains everything, which changes the spirit of the work rather than its content.
