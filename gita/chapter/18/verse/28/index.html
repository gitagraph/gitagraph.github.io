Bhagavad Gita 18.28

Krishna

अयुक्तः प्राकृतः स्तब्धः शठो नैष्कृतिकोऽलसः ।
विषादी दीर्घसूत्री च कर्ता तामस उच्यते ॥ १८.२८ ॥

ayuktaḥ prākṛtaḥ stabdhaḥ śaṭho naiṣkṛtiko'lasaḥ ।
viṣādī dīrghasūtrī ca kartā tāmasa ucyate ॥

A doer who is undisciplined, crude, stubborn, deceitful, malicious, lazy, gloomy and given to delay, is called tamasic.

हिन्दीजो कर्ता असंयमी, असभ्य, अड़ियल, कपटी, दूसरों का अहित करने वाला, आलसी, उदास और टालमटोल करने वाला है, वह तामस कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Putting off an apology to a friend day after day, telling yourself it will keep, is the drift and delay the verse names. The longer it waits, the heavier it gets.

Krishna describes the tāmasa doer: undisciplined, crude, stubborn, deceitful, malicious, lazy, gloomy and given to putting things off.

The last word, dīrgha-sūtrī, literally 'one who draws out a long thread', names the habit of endless delay. The list moves from carelessness through ill will to sheer inertia, and many readers will find it the most familiar portrait of the three: the work that never quite gets started.
