Bhagavad Gita 18.48

Krishna

सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न त्यजेत् ।
सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनाग्निरिवावृताः ॥ १८.४८ ॥

sahajaṃ karma kaunteya sadoṣamapi na tyajet ।
sarvārambhā hi doṣeṇa dhūmenāgnirivāvṛtāḥ ॥

Kaunteya, one should not abandon the work that comes naturally to one, even if it has flaws; for every undertaking is wrapped in flaws, as fire is wrapped in smoke.

हिन्दीहे कौंतेय, अपने स्वभाव से उत्पन्न कर्म को दोषयुक्त होने पर भी नहीं छोड़ना चाहिए; क्योंकि जैसे आग धुएँ से ढकी रहती है, वैसे ही सभी कार्य किसी न किसी दोष से ढके रहते हैं।

Krishna says one should not abandon the work that comes naturally to one, even if it has flaws, since every undertaking is wrapped in flaws as fire is wrapped in smoke.

The key word is sahaja, 'born with one', the work that arises from one's own nature. In everyday terms, no work is spotless, so waiting for a flawless role is not a sound reason to abandon the one that is truly yours.
