Bhagavad Gita 18.6

Krishna

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च ।
कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥ १८.६ ॥

etānyapi tu karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalāni ca ।
kartavyānīti me pārtha niścitaṃ matamuttamam ॥

Yet even these acts should be done after giving up attachment and the fruits. This, Partha, is my settled and highest view.

हिन्दीकिंतु इन कर्मों को भी आसक्ति और फल का त्याग करके ही करना चाहिए। हे पार्थ, यह मेरा निश्चित और उत्तम मत है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Looking after an ageing parent is right to do. Do it fully, without keeping a tally of who among the siblings did more or waiting to be thanked.

Krishna completes his verdict: even sacrifice, giving and austerity must be done after giving up attachment and the wish for their fruits.

He calls this his uttama mata, 'highest view', and it joins the two definitions of 18.2: the acts themselves stay, while what is renounced is saṅga, clinging, and phala, the result. Plainly, it asks for full engagement in worthwhile work while letting go of the personal stake in how it pays off.
