Bhagavad Gita 18.23

Krishna

नियतं सङ्गरहितमरागद्वेषतः कृतम् ।
अफलप्रेप्सुना कर्म यत्तत्सात्त्विकमुच्यते ॥ १८.२३ ॥

niyataṃ saṅgarahitamarāgadveṣataḥ kṛtam ।
aphalaprepsunā karma yattatsāttvikamucyate ॥

An action that is prescribed, done without attachment, without liking or dislike, by one who does not seek its fruit, is called sattvic.

हिन्दीजो कर्म नियत है, आसक्ति रहित है, राग-द्वेष के बिना किया गया है और जिसे फल न चाहने वाले व्यक्ति ने किया है, वह सात्त्विक कहलाता है।

Krishna turns from knowledge to action and describes the sāttvika kind: required work done without attachment, without liking or dislike, by someone who does not seek its fruit.

The key word is a-phala-prepsu, 'one who does not crave the result'. It gathers the chapter's earlier points (18.6, 18.9) into one picture: the work is done because it needs doing, and done steadily whether or not it pleases.
