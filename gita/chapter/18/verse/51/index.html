Bhagavad Gita 18.51

Krishna

बुद्ध्या विशुद्धया युक्तो धृत्यात्मानं नियम्य च ।
शब्दादीन्विषयांस्त्यक्त्वा रागद्वेषौ व्युदस्य च ॥ १८.५१ ॥

buddhyā viśuddhayā yukto dhṛtyātmānaṃ niyamya ca ।
śabdādīnviṣayāṃstyaktvā rāgadveṣau vyudasya ca ॥

Endowed with a purified understanding, firmly restraining himself with resolve, setting aside sound and the other sense objects, and casting off attraction and aversion…

हिन्दीशुद्ध बुद्धि से युक्त होकर, धृति से अपने आप को दृढ़ता से वश में करके, शब्द आदि विषयों को छोड़कर और राग-द्वेष को त्यागकर…

Krishna begins describing the person on the way to Brahman: purified understanding, the self held steady by resolve, sense objects such as sound set aside, attraction and aversion cast off.

The sentence runs on through 18.53. Its two terms, buddhi and dhṛti, are the same understanding and resolve whose sattvic forms were defined in 18.30 and 18.33. The key pair here is rāga-dveṣa, liking and disliking, the two pulls the whole path seeks to quieten.
