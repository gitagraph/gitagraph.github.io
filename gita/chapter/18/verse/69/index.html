Bhagavad Gita 18.69

Krishna

न च तस्मान्मनुष्येषु कश्चिन्मे प्रियकृत्तमः ।
भविता न च मे तस्मादन्यः प्रियतरो भुवि ॥ १८.६९ ॥

na ca tasmānmanuṣyeṣu kaścinme priyakṛttamaḥ ।
bhavitā na ca me tasmādanyaḥ priyataro bhuvi ॥

No one among people does work dearer to me than he, and there will be no one on earth dearer to me than he.

हिन्दीमनुष्यों में उससे बढ़कर मेरा प्रिय कार्य करने वाला कोई नहीं है, और न ही पृथ्वी पर उससे अधिक मुझे कोई प्रिय होगा।

Krishna says that no one among people does work dearer to him than the one who shares this teaching, and none on earth will ever be dearer to him.

The key word is priya-kṛttama, 'the one who does what is most pleasing'. The strong superlatives continue the praise of 18.68, ranking the passing-on of understanding above other acts. For a reader, it gives real weight to the work of explaining clearly to others.
