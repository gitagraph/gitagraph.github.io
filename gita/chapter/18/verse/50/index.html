Bhagavad Gita 18.50

Krishna

सिद्धिं प्राप्तो यथा ब्रह्म तथाप्नोति निबोध मे ।
समासेनैव कौन्तेय निष्ठा ज्ञानस्य या परा ॥ १८.५० ॥

siddhiṃ prāpto yathā brahma tathāpnoti nibodha me ।
samāsenaiva kaunteya niṣṭhā jñānasya yā parā ॥

Learn from me in brief, Kaunteya, how one who has attained that perfection also attains Brahman, the highest culmination of knowledge.

हिन्दीहे कौंतेय, जो इस सिद्धि को पा चुका है, वह ब्रह्म को कैसे प्राप्त होता है, जो ज्ञान की सर्वोच्च अवस्था है, उसे मुझसे संक्षेप में जानो।

Krishna promises to explain briefly how one who has reached that perfection goes on to attain Brahman, the highest culmination of knowledge.

This verse opens the short description that follows in 18.51–53. The key word is niṣṭhā, the final settled state or culmination. In the flow of the chapter, it marks a step beyond doing one's work well: what remains once the work no longer binds.
