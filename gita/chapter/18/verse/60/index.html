Bhagavad Gita 18.60

Krishna

स्वभावजेन कौन्तेय निबद्धः स्वेन कर्मणा ।
कर्तुं नेच्छसि यन्मोहात्करिष्यस्यवशोऽपि तत् ॥ १८.६० ॥

svabhāvajena kaunteya nibaddhaḥ svena karmaṇā ।
kartuṃ necchasi yanmohātkariṣyasyavaśo'pi tat ॥

Kaunteya, bound by your own work born of your nature, you will do, even against your will, what you now refuse to do out of delusion.

हिन्दीहे कौंतेय, जो काम तुम मोह के कारण अभी नहीं करना चाहते, उसे तुम अपने स्वभाव से उत्पन्न कर्म से बँधे होकर अपनी इच्छा के विरुद्ध भी करोगे।

Bound by the work that comes from his own nature, Krishna tells Arjuna, he will end up doing, even helplessly, what delusion now makes him refuse.

The key word is avaśaḥ, 'without control, against one's will'. It restates 18.59 more sharply: the choice is not between fighting and not fighting, but between acting with understanding and being carried along by one's nature, confused and unwilling.
