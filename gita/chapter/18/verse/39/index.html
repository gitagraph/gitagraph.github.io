Bhagavad Gita 18.39

Krishna

यदग्रे चानुबन्धे च सुखं मोहनमात्मनः ।
निद्रालस्यप्रमादोत्थं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १८.३९ ॥

yadagre cānubandhe ca sukhaṃ mohanamātmanaḥ ।
nidrālasyapramādotthaṃ tattāmasamudāhṛtam ॥

The happiness that deludes the self both at the beginning and in its outcome, arising from sleep, sloth and carelessness, is declared tamasic.

हिन्दीजो सुख आरंभ में भी और परिणाम में भी आत्मा को मोह में डालता है, और नींद, आलस्य तथा प्रमाद से उत्पन्न होता है, उसे तामस कहा गया है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

An afternoon nap that stretches into the evening, then a dazed night: pleasure that comes from heaviness deludes you at both ends. Notice when 'rest' is really just drift.

Tamasic happiness, Krishna says, deludes a person from beginning to end and comes from sleep, sloth and carelessness.

Unlike the other two, it never turns: there is no bitter-then-sweet or sweet-then-bitter, only a haze throughout. The key word is pramāda, heedlessness, letting things slide. For a reader, it describes the dull comfort of drifting, of avoiding effort and attention, which feels restful but leaves one no clearer than before.
