Bhagavad Gita 18.52

Krishna

विविक्तसेवी लघ्वाशी यतवाक्कायमानसः ।
ध्यानयोगपरो नित्यं वैराग्यं समुपाश्रितः ॥ १८.५२ ॥

viviktasevī laghvāśī yatavākkāyamānasaḥ ।
dhyānayogaparo nityaṃ vairāgyaṃ samupāśritaḥ ॥

…living in solitude, eating lightly, with speech, body and mind controlled, always devoted to the yoga of meditation, and taking refuge in dispassion…

हिन्दी…एकांत में रहने वाला, थोड़ा भोजन करने वाला, वाणी, शरीर और मन को वश में रखने वाला, सदा ध्यानयोग में लगा हुआ और वैराग्य का आश्रय लेने वाला…

In your life

A weekend alone in a quiet place, eating lightly and speaking little, can show you how much of your restlessness comes from the noise around you. Try it once.

Krishna continues his picture of the seeker on the way to Brahman (18.51–53): such a person lives in solitude, eats lightly, keeps speech, body and mind in check, stays devoted to the yoga of meditation and takes refuge in dispassion.

The key word is vivikta-sevī, one who seeks out quiet, uncrowded places. Many of these practices echo the meditation teaching of chapter 6. Read today, the list is a picture of a simplified life that makes room for inner attention.
