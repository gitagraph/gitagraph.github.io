Bhagavad Gita 18.27

Krishna

रागी कर्मफलप्रेप्सुर्लुब्धो हिंसात्मकोऽशुचिः ।
हर्षशोकान्वितः कर्ता राजसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १८.२७ ॥

rāgī karmaphalaprepsurlubdho hiṃsātmako'śuciḥ ।
harṣaśokānvitaḥ kartā rājasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ ॥

A doer who is driven by passion, eager for the fruits of action, greedy, harmful by nature, impure, and swept by joy and grief, is called rajasic.

हिन्दीजो कर्ता राग से भरा है, कर्मफल का इच्छुक है, लोभी है, स्वभाव से हिंसक है, अपवित्र है और हर्ष-शोक में बह जाता है, वह राजस कहलाता है।

Krishna describes the rājasa doer: driven by passion, eager for the fruits of action, greedy, harmful by nature, impure, and swept between joy and grief.

The key phrase is harṣa-śoka-anvita, 'caught up in elation and dejection': because everything rides on the outcome, each turn of events brings a swing of mood. Read beside 18.26 the contrast is plain: the sāttvika doer is unmoved by success or failure, while this one is ruled by them.
