Bhagavad Gita 2.34

Krishna

अकीर्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम् ।
सम्भावितस्य चाकीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते ॥ २.३४ ॥

akīrtiṃ cāpi bhūtāni kathayiṣyanti te'vyayām ।
sambhāvitasya cākīrtirmaraṇādatiricyate ॥

People will tell of your disgrace forever, and for one who has been honoured, disgrace is worse than death.

हिन्दीलोग सदा तुम्हारी बदनामी की बातें करेंगे, और सम्मानित व्यक्ति के लिए अपयश मृत्यु से भी बढ़कर है।

Krishna warns that people will tell of Arjuna's disgrace forever, and for one who has been honoured, disgrace is worse than death.

The key words are akīrti, dishonour, and avyaya, 'unfading': the story of a warrior who left the field would be retold without end. Calling him sambhāvita, 'honoured', speaks to Arjuna's own standing; for readers, it shows how much weight the epic's world gave to keeping faith with what others rely on you for.
