Bhagavad Gita 2.68

Krishna

तस्माद्यस्य महाबाहो निगृहीतानि सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ २.६८ ॥

tasmādyasya mahābāho nigṛhītāni sarvaśaḥ ।
indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyastasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ॥

Therefore, mighty-armed one, one whose senses are fully held back from their objects has firmly established wisdom.

हिन्दीइसलिए हे महाबाहु, जिसकी इंद्रियाँ विषयों से पूरी तरह रोक ली गई हैं, उसकी बुद्धि स्थिर है।

Krishna draws the conclusion (tasmāt, 'therefore') of the passage on the senses and repeats the refrain heard in 2.57, 2.58 and 2.61: 'his wisdom is firmly established'.

Nigṛhītāni means held in check, and sarvaśaḥ, completely. After the images of the tortoise and the wind-blown boat, this is the summary: steadiness of mind shows less in what a person says than in how well the senses can be reined in.
