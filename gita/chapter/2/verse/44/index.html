Bhagavad Gita 2.44

Krishna

भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम् ।
व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते ॥ २.४४ ॥

bhogaiśvaryaprasaktānāṃ tayāpahṛtacetasām ।
vyavasāyātmikā buddhiḥ samādhau na vidhīyate ॥

Those attached to enjoyment and power, whose minds are carried away by such talk, do not develop the resolute understanding that rests in meditation.

हिन्दीभोग और ऐश्वर्य में आसक्त, जिनका मन ऐसी बातों से बहक गया है, उनमें समाधि में टिकने वाली निश्चयात्मक बुद्धि नहीं बनती।

In your life

Flashy promises of quick riches, from schemes or slick videos, pull the mind toward enjoyment and power. A mind carried off by such talk struggles to settle into steady judgement.

Krishna says that those attached to enjoyment and power, whose minds are apahṛta, carried off, by such talk, do not develop the resolute understanding that rests in samādhi.

This completes the sentence of 2.42–44. Samādhi here means a settled, absorbed state of mind, where the vyavasāyātmikā buddhi of 2.41 would take root. Put simply, a mind busy chasing rewards does not stay still long enough to become clear.
