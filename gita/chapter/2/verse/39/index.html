Bhagavad Gita 2.39

Krishna

एषा तेऽभिहिता सांख्ये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां शृणु ।
बुद्ध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि ॥ २.३९ ॥

eṣā te'bhihitā sāṃkhye buddhiryoge tvimāṃ śṛṇu ।
buddhyā yukto yayā pārtha karmabandhaṃ prahāsyasi ॥

This understanding has been explained to you in terms of Sankhya; now hear it in terms of yoga. Joined with this understanding, Partha, you will throw off the bondage of action.

हिन्दीयह बुद्धि तुम्हें सांख्य के अनुसार बताई गई; अब इसे योग के अनुसार सुनो। हे पार्थ, इस बुद्धि से युक्त होकर तुम कर्म के बंधन से छूट जाओगे।

Krishna marks a turn in the chapter: having explained this understanding in terms of Sankhya, he will now explain it in terms of yoga, which frees one from the bondage of action.

The two key words are sāṃkhya, here meaning understanding through discernment of the self, and yoga, here a disciplined way of acting. The promise is release from karmabandha, the 'bondage of action': the idea that work itself need not tie us down if it is done with the right understanding.
