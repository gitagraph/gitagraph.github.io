Bhagavad Gita 2.56

Krishna

दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः ।
वीतरागभयक्रोधः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते ॥ २.५६ ॥

duḥkheṣvanudvignamanāḥ sukheṣu vigataspṛhaḥ ।
vītarāgabhayakrodhaḥ sthitadhīrmunirucyate ॥

One whose mind is not shaken in sorrow, who does not crave pleasures, who is free from passion, fear and anger — such a sage is called steady of mind.

हिन्दीजिसका मन दुख में विचलित नहीं होता, जो सुखों की लालसा नहीं करता, और जो राग, भय और क्रोध से मुक्त है — ऐसा मुनि स्थिर बुद्धि वाला कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Stuck in traffic and running late, notice the anger and fear rising. You can't move the cars, but you can stay the one who isn't shaken.

Krishna describes the sage of steady mind as one not shaken in sorrow, not craving pleasures, and free of passion, fear and anger.

The key word is sthitadhī, 'one whose understanding stands firm', here called a muni, a quiet, reflective sage, as the answer to Arjuna's question in 2.54 goes on. The test is not an absence of feeling but a mind that sorrow does not knock over and pleasure does not make greedy.
