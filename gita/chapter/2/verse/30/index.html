Bhagavad Gita 2.30

Krishna

देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत ।
तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥ २.३० ॥

dehī nityamavadhyo'yaṃ dehe sarvasya bhārata ।
tasmātsarvāṇi bhūtāni na tvaṃ śocitumarhasi ॥

The self dwelling in every body can never be slain, Bharata. Therefore you should not grieve for any being.

हिन्दीहे भारत, सबके शरीर में रहने वाली यह आत्मा सदा अवध्य है। इसलिए तुम्हें किसी भी प्राणी के लिए शोक नहीं करना चाहिए।

Krishna sums up his case: the dehī, the self dwelling in every body, is avadhya, beyond being slain, so Arjuna should grieve for no being.

This closes the first stretch of his reply, about the self, which began at 2.11. Since what is essential in every person cannot be killed, grief that rests on the idea of their destruction has no ground.
