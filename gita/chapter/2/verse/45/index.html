Bhagavad Gita 2.45

Krishna

त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्त्रैगुण्यो भवार्जुन ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान् ॥ २.४५ ॥

traiguṇyaviṣayā vedā nistraiguṇyo bhavārjuna ।
nirdvandvo nityasattvastho niryogakṣema ātmavān ॥

The Vedas deal with the three gunas; Arjuna, rise above the three gunas. Be free of the pairs of opposites, always rooted in purity, unconcerned with getting and keeping, and centred in the self.

हिन्दीवेद तीन गुणों के विषयों से जुड़े हैं; हे अर्जुन, तुम तीनों गुणों से ऊपर उठो। द्वंद्वों से मुक्त, सदा सत्त्व में स्थित, पाने और बचाने की चिंता से रहित और आत्मा में टिके रहो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If every spare hour goes into tracking savings, policies and everything that might go wrong, the worry about getting and keeping starts to run you. Provide sensibly, then stand on something that doesn't rise and fall with the balance.

Krishna tells Arjuna that the Vedas deal with the three guṇas and asks him to rise above them, free of the pairs of opposites and centred in the self.

The key word is traiguṇya, the realm of the three guṇas, the qualities of nature that make up all changing experience; the Vedic rites operate within it. The rest describes that freedom: not tossed by opposites, unworried about yoga-kṣema, acquiring and keeping, and grounded in oneself rather than in circumstances.
