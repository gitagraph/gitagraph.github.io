Bhagavad Gita 2.7

Arjuna

कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः पृच्छामि त्वां धर्मसम्मूढचेताः ।
यच्छ्रेयः स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम् ॥ २.७ ॥

kārpaṇyadoṣopahatasvabhāvaḥ pṛcchāmi tvāṃ dharmasammūḍhacetāḥ ।
yacchreyaḥ syānniścitaṃ brūhi tanme śiṣyaste'haṃ śādhi māṃ tvāṃ prapannam ॥

My nature is overcome by the flaw of faint-heartedness, and my mind is confused about dharma. I ask you: tell me clearly what is truly good for me. I am your disciple; I have come to you for refuge — guide me.

हिन्दीदीनता के दोष से मेरा स्वभाव दब गया है और धर्म के बारे में मेरा मन भ्रमित है। मैं आपसे पूछता हूँ, जो निश्चित रूप से मेरे लिए कल्याणकारी हो वह मुझे बताइए। मैं आपका शिष्य हूँ, आपकी शरण में आया हूँ, मुझे राह दिखाइए।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Stuck on a big choice, like leaving a stable career, saying to someone you trust, 'I'm confused, help me see what is truly good,' is not weakness. It is how clarity begins.

This is the turning point of the dialogue: Arjuna stops arguing and asks to be taught.

He diagnoses himself honestly: his nature is struck by kārpaṇya, a faint-hearted smallness of spirit, and his mind is confused about dharma, what is right to do. Then he says śiṣyas te 'ham, 'I am your disciple', and asks for śreyas, what is truly good rather than merely pleasant. The rest of the Gita is Krishna's answer to this request.
