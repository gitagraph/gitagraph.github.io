Bhagavad Gita 2.60

Krishna

यततो ह्यपि कौन्तेय पुरुषस्य विपश्चितः ।
इन्द्रियाणि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः ॥ २.६० ॥

yatato hyapi kaunteya puruṣasya vipaścitaḥ ।
indriyāṇi pramāthīni haranti prasabhaṃ manaḥ ॥

For the turbulent senses, son of Kunti, forcibly carry off the mind even of a wise person who is striving.

हिन्दीहे कुंतीपुत्र, ये चंचल इंद्रियाँ प्रयत्न करने वाले बुद्धिमान मनुष्य के मन को भी ज़बरदस्ती खींच ले जाती हैं।

In your life

After weeks of keeping sensible nights, one gripping series can still swallow you till 3 a.m. Don't be shocked; the pull is strong for everyone, so keep the effort going rather than treating it as done.

Krishna warns that the turbulent senses can forcibly carry off the mind even of a wise person who is striving.

The word pramāthīni describes the senses as 'churning', turbulent, and prasabham means 'by force'. The verse is candid rather than discouraging: knowing better is not the same as being in control, so the effort has to be ongoing, not a one-time decision.
