Bhagavad Gita 2.40

Krishna

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते ।
स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात् ॥ २.४० ॥

nehābhikramanāśo'sti pratyavāyo na vidyate ।
svalpamapyasya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt ॥

On this path no effort is lost and no reverse occurs. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.

हिन्दीइस मार्ग में किया गया प्रयास कभी नष्ट नहीं होता और उलटा फल भी नहीं होता। इस धर्म का थोड़ा-सा पालन भी बड़े भय से बचा लेता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If all you can manage is a few minutes a day of doing one task fully, without fretting over how it turns out, that still counts. Nothing sincere here is wasted, and even a little eases a lot of fear.

Krishna reassures Arjuna that on this path of yoga no effort is lost, no reverse occurs, and even a little of it protects from 'great fear'.

The key word is abhikrama, an effort undertaken, and pratyavāya, a reverse caused by doing it imperfectly, does not arise. Commentators often contrast this with ritual, where a slip in procedure could spoil the result; here every sincere step counts.
