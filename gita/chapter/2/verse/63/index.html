Bhagavad Gita 2.63

Krishna

क्रोधाद्भवति संमोहः संमोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः ।
स्मृतिभ्रंशाद्बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति ॥ २.६३ ॥

krodhādbhavati saṃmohaḥ saṃmohātsmṛtivibhramaḥ ।
smṛtibhraṃśādbuddhināśo buddhināśātpraṇaśyati ॥

Anger breeds delusion, and delusion confuses memory; when memory fails, understanding is ruined, and when understanding is ruined, a person is lost.

हिन्दीक्रोध से मोह पैदा होता है, मोह से स्मृति भ्रमित होती है, स्मृति के नष्ट होने से बुद्धि का नाश होता है, और बुद्धि के नाश से मनुष्य का पतन हो जाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a heated argument with your partner, anger makes you forget years of good, and you say what you don't mean. Pausing before that memory clouds over is what protects the relationship.

Krishna completes the chain from desire to ruin: anger breeds delusion, and step by step a person is lost.

Anger brings sammoha, a clouding of judgement; that unsettles smṛti, memory, here the remembrance of what one already knows to be right; with memory gone, buddhi, the power to discern, is ruined; and then the person is lost. It reads like an honest account of how a moment of rage can make someone act against everything they would normally stand for.
