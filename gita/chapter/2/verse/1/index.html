Verse 2.1

Chapter 2 · Sankhya YogaVerse 1 of 72

Bhagavad Gita 2.1

Sanjaya

तं तथा कृपयाविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् ।
विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः ॥ २.१ ॥

taṃ tathā kṛpayāviṣṭamaśrupūrṇākulekṣaṇam ।
viṣīdantamidaṃ vākyamuvāca madhusūdanaḥ ॥

To him, overcome with pity, his eyes troubled and full of tears, sinking in despair, Madhusudana spoke these words.

हिन्दीदया से भरे, आँसुओं से भरी व्याकुल आँखों वाले और शोक में डूबे उस अर्जुन से मधुसूदन ने यह बात कही।

Chapter 2 opens with Sanjaya describing Arjuna as chapter 1 left him: he has put down his bow and sits overwhelmed.

The key word is kṛpā, pity, which has 'entered into' him (āviṣṭa), more like a seizure than a choice. Krishna is called Madhusūdana, traditionally explained as 'slayer of the demon Madhu', a reminder that the one about to speak is himself a fighter. The verse marks the turn from Arjuna's lament to Krishna's teaching.
