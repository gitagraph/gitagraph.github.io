Bhagavad Gita 2.21

Krishna

वेदाविनाशिनं नित्यं य एनमजमव्ययम् ।
कथं स पुरुषः पार्थ कं घातयति हन्ति कम् ॥ २.२१ ॥

vedāvināśinaṃ nityaṃ ya enamajamavyayam ।
kathaṃ sa puruṣaḥ pārtha kaṃ ghātayati hanti kam ॥

Partha, how can a person who knows this self to be indestructible, eternal, unborn and unchanging cause anyone to be killed, or kill anyone?

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, जो मनुष्य इस आत्मा को अविनाशी, नित्य, अजन्मी और अविकारी जानता है, वह किसी को कैसे मारे या किसी से कैसे मरवाए?

Krishna asks how anyone who knows the self to be avināśin, nitya, aja and avyaya (indestructible, eternal, unborn, unchanging) could cause anyone to be killed, or kill anyone.

The verse names ghātayati, 'has someone killed', first, so the question reaches Arjuna's guilt as a commander who sends others to fight as well as a fighter. Both rest on the idea that the self can be destroyed, which the previous verse has just denied.
