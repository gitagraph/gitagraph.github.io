Bhagavad Gita 2.12

Krishna

न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः ।
न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम् ॥ २.१२ ॥

na tvevāhaṃ jātu nāsaṃ na tvaṃ neme janādhipāḥ ।
na caiva na bhaviṣyāmaḥ sarve vayamataḥ param ॥

Never was there a time when I did not exist, nor you, nor these kings; nor will any of us ever cease to be hereafter.

हिन्दीऐसा कोई समय नहीं था जब मैं नहीं था, या तुम नहीं थे, या ये राजा नहीं थे; और न ही आगे कभी ऐसा होगा कि हम सब नहीं रहेंगे।

Krishna gives his first reason why grief is misplaced: there was never a time when he, Arjuna or the kings on the field did not exist, and there never will be.

The key word is jātu, 'ever, at any time'. Some traditions read 'I, you and these kings' as distinct selves, each lasting for ever; others take the plural as belonging to bodies, with one self in all. On either reading, death is not an end.
