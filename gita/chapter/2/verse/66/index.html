Bhagavad Gita 2.66

Krishna

नास्ति बुद्धिरयुक्तस्य न चायुक्तस्य भावना ।
न चाभावयतः शान्तिरशान्तस्य कुतः सुखम् ॥ २.६६ ॥

nāsti buddhirayuktasya na cāyuktasya bhāvanā ।
na cābhāvayataḥ śāntiraśāntasya kutaḥ sukham ॥

The undisciplined have no settled understanding, and no power of contemplation; without contemplation there is no peace, and without peace how can there be happiness?

हिन्दीजो योगयुक्त नहीं है, उसमें निश्चयवाली बुद्धि नहीं होती और न ही ध्यान की शक्ति। बिना ध्यान के शांति नहीं मिलती, और अशांत को सुख कहाँ?

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A mind pulled every way by breaking news, gossip and cravings has no room left to reflect, and without reflection it never settles. Peace rarely arrives in a mind that never sits still.

Krishna says the undisciplined have no settled understanding and no power of contemplation, and so no peace; and without peace, where is happiness?

The ayukta is one not joined in discipline, and bhāvanā is the capacity to hold the mind on something in contemplation. It reads as plain cause and effect: happiness sits at the end of a sequence that begins with a collected mind.
