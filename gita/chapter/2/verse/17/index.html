Bhagavad Gita 2.17

Krishna

अविनाशि तु तद्विद्धि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।
विनाशमव्ययस्यास्य न कश्चित्कर्तुमर्हति ॥ २.१७ ॥

avināśi tu tadviddhi yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ।
vināśamavyayasyāsya na kaścitkartumarhati ॥

Know that what pervades all this is indestructible. No one can bring about the destruction of that which is imperishable.

हिन्दीजान लो कि जिससे यह सारा जगत व्याप्त है, वह अविनाशी है। उस अविनाशी का नाश कोई नहीं कर सकता।

Krishna says that what pervades all this is indestructible, and no one can destroy it.

This is the 'real' of 2.16: that by which everything is tatam, pervaded, which he calls avināśi, indestructible, and avyaya, imperishable. Readers differ on whether 'that' means the individual self, present throughout the body, or the one ultimate reality present in everything. In either reading, Arjuna's arrows cannot reach it, which is the point that matters to him now.
