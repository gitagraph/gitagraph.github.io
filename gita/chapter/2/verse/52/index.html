Bhagavad Gita 2.52

Krishna

यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति ।
तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च ॥ २.५२ ॥

yadā te mohakalilaṃ buddhirvyatitariṣyati ।
tadā gantāsi nirvedaṃ śrotavyasya śrutasya ca ॥

When your understanding crosses beyond the thicket of delusion, you will become indifferent to what you have heard and what is yet to be heard.

हिन्दीजब तुम्हारी बुद्धि मोह के इस दलदल को पार कर लेगी, तब तुम जो सुन चुके हो और जो अभी सुनना बाकी है, दोनों से उदासीन हो जाओगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

After years of chasing every new productivity method, there comes a day you understand how you actually work. From then on, the next viral hack stops tugging at you.

Krishna tells Arjuna that when his understanding crosses beyond the thicket of delusion, he will become indifferent to what he has heard and what is yet to be heard.

The key word is mohakalila, the 'thicket' or murky mass of delusion, the confusion Arjuna is caught in now; beyond it lies nirveda, a calm indifference, especially towards the promises of rewards just criticised. Put plainly, once a person sees clearly, they stop needing new assurances and offers to steer by.
