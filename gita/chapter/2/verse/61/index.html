Bhagavad Gita 2.61

Krishna

तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः ।
वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ २.६१ ॥

tāni sarvāṇi saṃyamya yukta āsīta matparaḥ ।
vaśe hi yasyendriyāṇi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ॥

Restraining them all, one should sit disciplined, intent on me. One whose senses are under control has firm wisdom.

हिन्दीउन सबको वश में करके मनुष्य को योगयुक्त होकर मुझमें मन लगाकर बैठना चाहिए। जिसकी इंद्रियाँ वश में हैं, उसकी बुद्धि स्थिर है।

After admitting how strongly the senses pull, Krishna gives the remedy: gather them all in and sit yukta, disciplined, joined in yoga, and matparaḥ, 'with me as the highest aim'.

In plain terms, restraint comes easier when attention has somewhere worthwhile to rest, not merely something to resist. Some traditions read 'me' here devotionally, as Krishna himself; others read it as the inner Self.
