Bhagavad Gita 2.57

Krishna

यः सर्वत्रानभिस्नेहस्तत्तत्प्राप्य शुभाशुभम् ।
नाभिनन्दति न द्वेष्टि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ २.५७ ॥

yaḥ sarvatrānabhisnehastattatprāpya śubhāśubham ।
nābhinandati na dveṣṭi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā ॥

One who is unattached everywhere, who neither rejoices on meeting good nor resents meeting bad — his wisdom is firmly established.

हिन्दीजो हर जगह आसक्ति से रहित है, जो शुभ मिलने पर न खुश होता है और अशुभ मिलने पर न द्वेष करता है, उसकी बुद्धि स्थिर है।

Krishna names a further mark of steady wisdom: an even response to whatever life brings, neither rejoicing at good nor resenting bad.

The word anabhisnehaḥ means 'without clinging fondness', and it holds sarvatra, everywhere, not just towards a few things. The point is not coldness but a mind that does not swing with each event, which the verse calls established prajñā, wisdom.
