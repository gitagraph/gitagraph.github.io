Bhagavad Gita 2.43

Krishna

कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् ।
क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति ॥ २.४३ ॥

kāmātmānaḥ svargaparā janmakarmaphalapradām ।
kriyāviśeṣabahulāṃ bhogaiśvaryagatiṃ prati ॥

Full of desire and intent on heaven, they speak of many special rites that promise rebirth as the fruit of action and aim at enjoyment and power.

हिन्दीकामनाओं से भरे और स्वर्ग को ही लक्ष्य मानने वाले वे लोग ऐसी अनेक विशेष क्रियाओं की बात करते हैं जो कर्म के फल के रूप में फिर जन्म दिलाती हैं और भोग व ऐश्वर्य की ओर ले जाती हैं।

Krishna describes people full of kāma, desire, and intent on heaven, who speak of many rites aimed at enjoyment and power that bring only rebirth as the fruit of action.

The key phrase is kriyāviśeṣabahula, 'full of many special rites', each promising a particular reward; the sentence continues from 2.42. The underlying point: ritual pursued only for its rewards keeps a person in the same cycle of wanting.
