Bhagavad Gita 2.37

Krishna

हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम् ।
तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः ॥ २.३७ ॥

hato vā prāpsyasi svargaṃ jitvā vā bhokṣyase mahīm ।
tasmāduttiṣṭha kaunteya yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ ॥

Either you are killed and attain heaven, or you win and enjoy the earth. So rise, son of Kunti, resolved to fight.

हिन्दीया तो मारे जाकर स्वर्ग पाओगे, या जीतकर धरती का राज भोगोगे। इसलिए हे कुंतीपुत्र, युद्ध का निश्चय करके खड़े हो जाओ।

In your life

With the small venture you keep talking about, if it fails you learn and if it works you grow. Once you've thought it through, commit fully instead of standing at the edge.

Krishna ends his argument from a warrior's duty with a plain choice: killed, Arjuna attains svarga, heaven; victorious, he enjoys mahī, the earth; so he should rise, resolved to fight.

The key word is kṛtaniścaya, 'having made a firm decision'. What Krishna asks for is not courage alone but an end to wavering, the state in which Arjuna has stood since the first chapter.
