Bhagavad Gita 14.26

Krishna

मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।
स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान्ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ १४.२६ ॥

māṃ ca yo'vyabhicāreṇa bhaktiyogena sevate ।
sa guṇānsamatītyaitānbrahmabhūyāya kalpate ॥

And one who serves me with the yoga of unwavering devotion goes beyond these gunas and becomes fit to become Brahman.

हिन्दीऔर जो अटल भक्ति-योग से मेरी सेवा करता है, वह इन गुणों को पार करके ब्रह्म-भाव को प्राप्त करने योग्य हो जाता है।

In your life

Steady love for what you hold highest can quietly lift you above your moods. You stop being pushed around by each day's weather, without having to fight it.

Krishna answers the last part of Arjuna's question — how one goes beyond the gunas — by naming unwavering devotion to him.

The key word is avyabhicāra, 'not straying', a devotion that does not drift to other objects. The goal is brahma-bhūya, 'becoming Brahman': some traditions read this as realising one's identity with Brahman, others as attaining a pure state akin to Brahman's. Either way, the route given here is undivided devotion.
