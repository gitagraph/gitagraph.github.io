Chapter 14 · The Three GunasVerse 25 of 27

Bhagavad Gita 14.25

Krishna

मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते ॥ १४.२५ ॥

mānāpamānayostulyastulyo mitrāripakṣayoḥ ।
sarvārambhaparityāgī guṇātītaḥ sa ucyate ॥

…who is the same in honour and dishonour, the same toward friend and foe, and has given up every self-driven undertaking — such a one is said to have gone beyond the gunas.

हिन्दी…जो मान और अपमान में समान है, मित्र और शत्रु के पक्ष में समान है, और जिसने अपनी ओर से शुरू किए सब उद्यम छोड़ दिए हैं — वह गुणातीत कहा जाता है।

In your life

Made much of while you held a senior role, forgotten soon after you moved on: both pass. Meet the friend who stood by you and the one who turned away with the same courtesy.

Krishna completes his description of the person beyond the gunas with two more marks — sameness in honour and dishonour and toward friend and foe — and names such a person guṇātīta, 'gone beyond the gunas'.

The phrase sarvārambha-parityāgī, literally 'giving up all undertakings', echoes ārambha, the 'launching of ventures' listed as a sign of rajas (14.12); read in that light, what is given up is self-driven striving, not all activity. Plainly, it describes a life no longer run by ambition and reaction.
