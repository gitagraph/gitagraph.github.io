Bhagavad Gita 14.11

Krishna

सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते ।
ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विवृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥ १४.११ ॥

sarvadvāreṣu dehe'sminprakāśa upajāyate ।
jñānaṃ yadā tadā vidyādvivṛddhaṃ sattvamityuta ॥

When the light of knowledge shines through every gateway of this body, then one should know that sattva has grown strong.

हिन्दीजब इस शरीर के सभी द्वारों में ज्ञान का प्रकाश उत्पन्न होता है, तब जानना चाहिए कि सत्त्व बढ़ा हुआ है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Save big money decisions, a loan or a large purchase, for the hours when your mind feels clear, not for midnight, when you're tired and everything looks urgent.

Krishna gives the first sign by which one can tell that sattva has grown dominant: knowledge shining like light through every gateway of the body.

The 'gateways', dvāra, are the senses and the mind, through which we take in the world. When sattva is strong, what comes through them is seen clearly rather than through a haze. Put simply, clear perception and a lucid mind are how this guna shows itself.
