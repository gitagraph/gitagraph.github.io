Bhagavad Gita 14.18

Krishna

ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः ।
जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥ १४.१८ ॥

ūrdhvaṃ gacchanti sattvasthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ ।
jaghanyaguṇavṛttisthā adho gacchanti tāmasāḥ ॥

Those established in sattva rise upward; the rajasic stay in the middle; the tamasic, sunk in the lowest of the gunas, go downward.

हिन्दीसत्त्व में स्थित लोग ऊपर उठते हैं, राजसी बीच में रहते हैं, और सबसे निचले गुण के स्वभाव में स्थित तामसी नीचे जाते हैं।

In your life

Spend your evenings on what clears the mind and you tend to rise; on what only excites, you stay where you are; on what dulls, you slide. Your habits point you somewhere.

Krishna sums up where each guna leads: those rooted in sattva rise, those in rajas stay in the middle, and those in tamas sink.

'Upward' and 'downward' are often read as higher and lower births, continuing 14.14–15, but they can also be read as the direction a character takes within one life. The word jaghanya, 'lowest', marks tamas as the bottom of the scale. The picture is of three tendencies, each with its own momentum.
