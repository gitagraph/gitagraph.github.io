In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Your small shop gets a glowing review and a harsh one on the same day. If your steadiness rests in yourself rather than in the ratings, neither one decides your mood.

Krishna adds a string of evenness to his portrait of the person beyond the gunas: the same in pain and pleasure, in what is liked and disliked, in blame and praise, and treating a clod, a stone and gold alike.

The key word is svastha, 'standing in oneself'; in ordinary Sanskrit and Hindi it also means 'healthy'. The point is that such a person's balance does not depend on how things or others treat them.
