Bhagavad Gita 14.27

Krishna

ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च ।
शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च ॥ १४.२७ ॥

brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāhamamṛtasyāvyayasya ca ।
śāśvatasya ca dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya ca ॥

For I am the foundation of Brahman — of the immortal and imperishable, of eternal dharma, and of absolute bliss.

हिन्दीक्योंकि मैं ही ब्रह्म का आधार हूँ — अमर और अविनाशी का, सनातन धर्म का, और परम एकांतिक सुख का।

Krishna ends the chapter by calling himself the foundation, pratiṣṭhā, of Brahman — of the immortal and imperishable, of eternal dharma and of absolute happiness.

It explains why devotion to him leads to Brahman (14.26): the goal rests on him. Traditions read this relation differently: some see Krishna here as the form in which formless Brahman is known, others as the higher reality on which Brahman itself rests.
