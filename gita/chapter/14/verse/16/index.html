Bhagavad Gita 14.16

Krishna

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम् ।
रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥ १४.१६ ॥

karmaṇaḥ sukṛtasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṃ nirmalaṃ phalam ।
rajasastu phalaṃ duḥkhamajñānaṃ tamasaḥ phalam ॥

The fruit of good action, it is said, is sattvic and pure; the fruit of rajas is pain, and the fruit of tamas is ignorance.

हिन्दीकहा जाता है कि अच्छे कर्म का फल सात्त्विक और निर्मल होता है; रजोगुण का फल दुःख है और तमोगुण का फल अज्ञान है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Chasing a promotion only to beat a colleague often ends in strain and disappointment, even when you win. Effort driven by craving tends to leave pain behind it.

Krishna names the result each guna produces when it drives action: sattva yields a pure result, rajas yields pain, and tamas yields ignorance.

The word āhuḥ, 'they say', presents this as received teaching rather than a new claim. Rajas producing pain may seem surprising, since it is the guna of effort and ambition; the point is that action done from craving tends to end in strain and disappointment, however much it achieves.
