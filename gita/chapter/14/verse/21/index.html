Chapter 14 · The Three GunasVerse 21 of 27

Bhagavad Gita 14.21

Arjuna

कैर्लिङ्गैस्त्रीन्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो ।
किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ॥ १४.२१ ॥

kairliṅgaistrīnguṇānetānatīto bhavati prabho ।
kimācāraḥ kathaṃ caitāṃstrīnguṇānativartate ॥

By what marks is one known who has gone beyond these three gunas, Lord? How does such a person behave, and how does one pass beyond these three gunas?

हिन्दीहे प्रभो, इन तीन गुणों से परे गया हुआ मनुष्य किन लक्षणों से पहचाना जाता है? उसका आचरण कैसा होता है, और वह इन तीन गुणों से पार कैसे होता है?

Arjuna asks three practical questions about someone who has gone beyond the three gunas: how is such a person recognised, how do they behave, and how does one pass beyond them?

The key word is liṅga, a sign or mark by which something hidden is known. It is a characteristic move for Arjuna, who asked something similar about the person of steady wisdom (2.54). Krishna answers over the remaining verses of the chapter.
