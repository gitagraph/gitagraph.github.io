Bhagavad Gita 14.13

Krishna

अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च ।
तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन ॥ १४.१३ ॥

aprakāśo'pravṛttiśca pramādo moha eva ca ।
tamasyetāni jāyante vivṛddhe kurunandana ॥

Darkness of mind, inertia, carelessness and delusion — these arise when tamas has grown strong, joy of the Kurus.

हिन्दीहे कुरुनंदन, अंधकार, निष्क्रियता, असावधानी और मोह — ये तमोगुण के बढ़ने पर उत्पन्न होते हैं।

Krishna names the signs of tamas grown strong: darkness of mind, inertia, carelessness and delusion.

Each mirrors a sign of the other two gunas: aprakāśa, 'no light', is the opposite of sattva's clarity (14.11), and apravṛtti, 'no drive', is the opposite of rajas's busy activity (14.12). Plainly, tamas is not calm but dullness — the state in which we neither see clearly nor act when action is needed.
