Bhagavad Gita 14.23

Krishna

उदासीनवदासीनो गुणैर्यो न विचाल्यते ।
गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येव योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते ॥ १४.२३ ॥

udāsīnavadāsīno guṇairyo na vicālyate ।
guṇā vartanta ityeva yo'vatiṣṭhati neṅgate ॥

…who sits as if indifferent, unshaken by the gunas, who stays steady knowing simply that it is the gunas that are at work, and does not waver…

हिन्दी…जो उदासीन की तरह बैठा रहता है, गुणों से विचलित नहीं होता, और यह जानकर कि गुण ही अपना काम कर रहे हैं, स्थिर रहता है और डगमगाता नहीं…

In your life

When siblings or in-laws start quarrelling at a family gathering, you can sit like a calm onlooker: not cold, just not pulled into the fray, knowing tempers rise and fall.

Krishna continues describing the person beyond the gunas as someone who stays seated like an onlooker, unshaken by them, knowing that it is only the gunas at work.

Udāsīna literally means 'sitting apart', the stance of a neutral party in a dispute, and the vat, 'as if', matters: this is not coldness but not being pulled into the fray. For a reader, it describes watching one's own changing states without being thrown by them.
