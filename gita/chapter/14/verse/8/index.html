Bhagavad Gita 14.8

Krishna

तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबध्नाति भारत ॥ १४.८ ॥

tamastvajñānajaṃ viddhi mohanaṃ sarvadehinām ।
pramādālasyanidrābhistannibadhnāti bhārata ॥

Know tamas to be born of ignorance, deluding all embodied beings; it binds them, Bharata, through carelessness, sloth and sleep.

हिन्दीहे भारत, तमोगुण को अज्ञान से उत्पन्न और सब देहधारियों को मोह में डालने वाला जानो; वह असावधानी, आलस्य और नींद के द्वारा बाँधता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Snoozing the alarm five times, one more episode at 1 a.m., then a day that starts in a haze: this heaviness, binding through sloth and sleep, is what the Gita calls tamas.

Krishna describes tamas, the third guna, as born of ignorance and binding people through carelessness, sloth and sleep.

It follows his accounts of sattva and rajas (14.6–7). Its root is ajñāna, not-knowing, and its effect is mohana, a fog that confuses every embodied being. In everyday terms it is the heaviness that makes us drift, put things off and stop paying attention.
