Chapter 14 · The Three GunasVerse 10 of 27

Bhagavad Gita 14.10

Krishna

रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥ १४.१० ॥

rajastamaścābhibhūya sattvaṃ bhavati bhārata ।
rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamaścaiva tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajastathā ॥

Sattva prevails by overpowering rajas and tamas, Bharata; rajas prevails by overpowering sattva and tamas; and tamas likewise by overpowering sattva and rajas.

हिन्दीहे भारत, रज और तम को दबाकर सत्त्व बढ़ता है; सत्त्व और तम को दबाकर रज बढ़ता है; और इसी तरह सत्त्व और रज को दबाकर तम बढ़ता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Before calling your child 'lazy', remember that no one is only one thing. The same child can be sharp in the morning and dull by evening; the balance shifts through the day.

Krishna explains that the three gunas are always competing, and whichever one overpowers the other two shapes a person at that moment.

The key word is abhibhūya, 'having overpowered': none of the gunas is ever absent, only pushed into the background. So no one is simply 'a sattvic person' or 'a tamasic person'; the balance shifts through the day and through life, and the next verses describe how to tell which one is on top.
