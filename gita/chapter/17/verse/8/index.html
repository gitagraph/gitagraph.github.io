Bhagavad Gita 17.8

Krishna

आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः ।
रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥ १७.८ ॥

āyuḥsattvabalārogyasukhaprītivivardhanāḥ ।
rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirā hṛdyā āhārāḥ sāttvikapriyāḥ ॥

Foods that increase life, vitality, strength, health, happiness and contentment, and that are tasty, rich, substantial and pleasing to the heart, are dear to sattvic people.

हिन्दीजो भोजन आयु, सत्त्व, बल, आरोग्य, सुख और प्रसन्नता बढ़ाते हैं, और जो रसीले, चिकने, देर तक शक्ति देने वाले और मन को भाने वाले होते हैं, वे सात्त्विक लोगों को प्रिय होते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Notice how you feel an hour after a meal, not only how it tasted in the moment. The Gita describes good food by the strength and contentment it leaves behind.

Krishna describes the food that sattvic people like: food that builds life, vitality, strength, health, happiness and contentment, and that is tasty, rich, substantial and pleasing.

The key word is snigdha, 'rich' or 'smooth', food with some natural moisture or fat rather than dry. The list is about effects as much as ingredients: food is judged by what it does for body and mind over time, not only by how it tastes in the moment.
