Bhagavad Gita 17.25

Krishna

तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः ।
दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ १७.२५ ॥

tadityanabhisandhāya phalaṃ yajñatapaḥkriyāḥ ।
dānakriyāśca vividhāḥ kriyante mokṣakāṅkṣibhiḥ ॥

With the word 'Tat', and without aiming at any reward, those who seek liberation perform the various acts of sacrifice, austerity and charity.

हिन्दी'तत्' शब्द का उच्चारण करके, फल की कोई इच्छा न रखते हुए, मोक्ष चाहने वाले लोग अनेक प्रकार के यज्ञ, तप और दान के कार्य करते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When you give or serve without expecting anything back, even a simple 'this is not for me' at the start shapes the whole act. Intention, set early, carries through.

Krishna explains that people seeking liberation say 'Tat', meaning 'that', and perform their many acts of sacrifice, austerity and charity without aiming at any reward.

The key word is tat, which points to Brahman without any personal claim attached. Saying 'that' rather than 'mine' turns the act away from oneself; in this way the word carries the chapter's repeated theme of acting without craving the fruit.
