Bhagavad Gita 17.20

Krishna

दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ १७.२० ॥

dātavyamiti yaddānaṃ dīyate'nupakāriṇe ।
deśe kāle ca pātre ca taddānaṃ sāttvikaṃ smṛtam ॥

A gift given simply because giving is right, to someone who can do nothing in return, at the proper place and time and to a worthy recipient, is held to be sattvic.

हिन्दीजो दान यह सोचकर दिया जाए कि देना ही उचित है, ऐसे व्यक्ति को जो बदले में कुछ न कर सके, और सही स्थान, सही समय और योग्य पात्र को दिया जाए, वह सात्त्विक दान माना गया है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Helping your house-help's daughter with her college fees, right when she needs it and expecting no favour in return, is giving as the Gita means it: thoughtful, timely, and free of any hidden exchange.

Krishna says that a gift is sattvic when it is given simply because giving is right, to someone who cannot repay it, at a fitting place and time, and to a worthy recipient.

The key word is anupakārin, 'one who does no favour in return'. The point is giving without a hidden exchange, yet with thought: the right occasion and the right person matter, not just generosity in the abstract.
