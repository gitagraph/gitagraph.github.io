Bhagavad Gita 17.23

Krishna

ॐतत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ १७.२३ ॥

oṃtatsaditi nirdeśo brahmaṇastrividhaḥ smṛtaḥ ।
brāhmaṇāstena vedāśca yajñāśca vihitāḥ purā ॥

'Om Tat Sat' is remembered as the threefold designation of Brahman. By it, in ancient times, the brahmins, the Vedas and the sacrifices were ordained.

हिन्दी'ॐ तत् सत्' — इसे ब्रह्म का तीन प्रकार का नाम माना गया है। इसी से प्राचीन काल में ब्राह्मणों, वेदों और यज्ञों का विधान हुआ।

Krishna names 'Om Tat Sat' as the threefold designation of Brahman, the ultimate reality, by which the brahmins, the Vedas and the sacrifices were ordained in ancient times.

This opens the chapter's closing section, which explains each word in turn (17.24–27). The key word is nirdeśa, a name that points toward something rather than defining it. The three words are offered as a way of dedicating any act to what is highest.
