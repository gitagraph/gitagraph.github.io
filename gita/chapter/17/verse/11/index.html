Bhagavad Gita 17.11

Krishna

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥ १७.११ ॥

aphalākāṅkṣibhiryajño vidhidṛṣṭo ya ijyate ।
yaṣṭavyameveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ ॥

Sacrifice offered according to scriptural rule by those who seek no reward, with the mind settled on the thought that it simply ought to be done, is sattvic.

हिन्दीजो यज्ञ फल की इच्छा न रखने वाले लोग शास्त्र की विधि के अनुसार, केवल यह मन में ठानकर करते हैं कि यह करना ही चाहिए, वह सात्त्विक है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Visiting an uncle in hospital because it simply ought to be done, not for thanks or so the family notices, is the quietest kind of offering. Go, sit with him, and expect nothing back.

Krishna says that a sacrifice is sattvic when it is offered by the rules, by people who want nothing back, with the mind settled on the thought that it simply ought to be done.

The key word is yaṣṭavyam, 'it should be offered': the act is done because it is right, not for what it brings. The same test can be applied to any duty today: doing it well, in its proper form, without bargaining for a return.
