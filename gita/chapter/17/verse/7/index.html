Chapter 17 · Three Kinds of FaithVerse 7 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 17.7

Krishna

आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः ।
यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥ १७.७ ॥

āhārastvapi sarvasya trividho bhavati priyaḥ ।
yajñastapastathā dānaṃ teṣāṃ bhedamimaṃ śṛṇu ॥

Even the food each person likes is of three kinds, and so are sacrifice, austerity and charity. Hear how they differ.

हिन्दीहर व्यक्ति को प्रिय भोजन भी तीन प्रकार का होता है, और इसी तरह यज्ञ, तप और दान भी। इनका यह भेद सुनो।

Krishna says that even the food people like comes in three kinds, as do sacrifice, austerity and charity, and he promises to explain how they differ.

This sets out the plan for most of the chapter: food (17.8–10), sacrifice (17.11–13), austerity (17.14–19) and charity (17.20–22). The key word is priya, 'dear' or 'liked': the focus is on what people are drawn to, which shows their temperament as clearly as their beliefs do.
