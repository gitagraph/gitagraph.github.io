Bhagavad Gita 17.19

Krishna

मूढग्राहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः ।
परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १७.१९ ॥

mūḍhagrāheṇātmano yatpīḍayā kriyate tapaḥ ।
parasyotsādanārthaṃ vā tattāmasamudāhṛtam ॥

Austerity undertaken out of a foolish obstinacy, through torment of oneself, or in order to ruin another, is declared tamasic.

हिन्दीजो तप मूर्खतापूर्ण हठ से, अपने को पीड़ा देकर, या किसी दूसरे का नाश करने के लिए किया जाता है, उसे तामसिक कहा गया है।

Krishna says that austerity driven by foolish stubbornness, carried out by tormenting oneself, or aimed at ruining someone else is tamasic.

The key phrase is mūḍha-grāha, a deluded fixation, clinging to an idea without understanding it. Traditionally, stories tell of severe penances undertaken to win power over an enemy, the kind of motive in view here. In the Gita's terms, effort that wounds oneself or targets others is the darkest kind.
