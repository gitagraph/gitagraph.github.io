Bhagavad Gita 17.10

Krishna

यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् ।
उच्छिष्टमपि चामेध्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥ १७.१० ॥

yātayāmaṃ gatarasaṃ pūti paryuṣitaṃ ca yat ।
ucchiṣṭamapi cāmedhyaṃ bhojanaṃ tāmasapriyam ॥

Food that is stale, tasteless, putrid, kept overnight, left over by others, or impure is dear to tamasic people.

हिन्दीजो भोजन बासी, स्वादहीन, सड़ा हुआ, रात भर रखा हुआ, दूसरों का जूठा या अपवित्र हो, वह तामसिक लोगों को प्रिय होता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Eating yesterday's leftovers straight from the fridge, in front of a screen, half-awake, is the dull end of the scale. Notice when meals become something you just get through.

Krishna describes the food tamasic people like: stale, tasteless, rotten, kept overnight, left over by others or impure.

The key word is yāta-yāma, literally 'a watch has passed', food left standing too long after it was cooked. Traditionally, a yāma is a watch of about three hours. What unites the list is neglect: food that has lost its freshness or purity, matching the dullness and inertia of tamas.
