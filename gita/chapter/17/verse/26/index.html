Bhagavad Gita 17.26

Krishna

सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते ।
प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते ॥ १७.२६ ॥

sadbhāve sādhubhāve ca sadityetatprayujyate ।
praśaste karmaṇi tathā sacchabdaḥ pārtha yujyate ॥

The word 'Sat' is used in the sense of reality and of goodness; likewise, Partha, the word 'Sat' is applied to any praiseworthy act.

हिन्दी'सत्' शब्द सत्यता और श्रेष्ठता के अर्थ में प्रयोग किया जाता है; और हे पार्थ, किसी भी प्रशंसनीय कर्म के लिए भी 'सत्' शब्द का प्रयोग होता है।

Krishna explains that the word 'Sat' is used for what is real and for what is good, and is also applied to any praiseworthy act.

The key word is sat, from the verb 'to be': what truly is, and by extension what is right. The verse joins two ideas that English usually keeps apart, reality and goodness, suggesting that a good act is, in this view, in step with what is real.

People: Krishna

Ideas: Om Tat Sat
