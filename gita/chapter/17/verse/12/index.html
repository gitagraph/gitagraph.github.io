Bhagavad Gita 17.12

Krishna

अभिसंधाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् ।
इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १७.१२ ॥

abhisaṃdhāya tu phalaṃ dambhārthamapi caiva yat ।
ijyate bharataśreṣṭha taṃ yajñaṃ viddhi rājasam ॥

But sacrifice performed with an eye on the reward, or merely for show, know that to be rajasic, best of the Bharatas.

हिन्दीपर हे भरतश्रेष्ठ, जो यज्ञ फल को ध्यान में रखकर या केवल दिखावे के लिए किया जाता है, उसे राजसिक जानो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A big festival celebration put on mainly so the neighbours notice is still effort, but of the restless kind. Celebrating for its own sake feels very different afterwards.

Krishna says that a sacrifice performed with an eye on the reward, or merely for show, is rajasic.

The key word is dambha, show or pretence: doing something religious mainly for the impression it makes. Set beside the previous verse, the outward ritual can be exactly the same; what changes its quality is the motive behind it, a calculation of gain or a wish to be seen.
