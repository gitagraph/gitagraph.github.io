Bhagavad Gita 17.27

Krishna

यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते ।
कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिधीयते ॥ १७.२७ ॥

yajñe tapasi dāne ca sthitiḥ saditi cocyate ।
karma caiva tadarthīyaṃ sadityevābhidhīyate ॥

Steadfastness in sacrifice, austerity and charity is also called 'Sat', and any action done for that purpose is likewise named 'Sat'.

हिन्दीयज्ञ, तप और दान में दृढ़ स्थिति को भी 'सत्' कहा जाता है, और उन्हीं के लिए किया गया कर्म भी 'सत्' ही कहलाता है।

In your life

Taking up the tabla at sixty, twenty steady minutes a day mean more than occasional bursts of enthusiasm. The Gita calls that kind of steadfastness 'sat', something real.

Krishna says that steadfastness in sacrifice, austerity and charity is also called 'Sat', and so is any action done for their sake.

The key word is sthiti, steady standing, staying committed rather than dabbling. The phrase tad-arthīya, 'for that purpose', is read by some as 'for the sake of the Supreme' and by others as 'for the sake of sacrifice and the rest'; either way, the purpose gives the work its worth.

People: Krishna
