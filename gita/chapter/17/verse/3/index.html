Bhagavad Gita 17.3

Krishna

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥ १७.३ ॥

sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata ।
śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ ॥

Everyone's faith takes the shape of their inner nature, Bharata. A person is made of faith: whatever one's faith is, that is what one is.

हिन्दीहे भारत, हर व्यक्ति की श्रद्धा उसके अंतःकरण के स्वभाव के अनुसार होती है। मनुष्य श्रद्धा से ही बना है; जिसकी जैसी श्रद्धा होती है, वह वैसा ही होता है।

In your life

Look less at what you say you believe and more at what you trust enough to give your evenings and savings to. Over the years, that trust quietly becomes who you are.

Krishna says that each person's faith matches their inner make-up, and that a person is in effect made of faith: as one's faith is, so one is.

Here sattva is usually taken as one's inner being or disposition, though some read it as the guṇa of that name. The key word is śraddhā-maya, 'made of faith'. Put simply, what we deeply trust and value is not a side detail about us; it shapes who we become.
