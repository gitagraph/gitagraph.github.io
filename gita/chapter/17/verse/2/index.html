Bhagavad Gita 17.2

Krishna

त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।
सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥ १७.२ ॥

trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṃ sā svabhāvajā ।
sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti tāṃ śṛṇu ॥

The faith of embodied beings is of three kinds, born of each one's own nature: sattvic, rajasic and tamasic. Hear about it.

हिन्दीदेहधारियों की श्रद्धा तीन प्रकार की होती है, जो उनके अपने स्वभाव से पैदा होती है — सात्त्विक, राजसिक और तामसिक। उसके बारे में सुनो।

In your life

What you trust most grows out of your own temperament, calm, restless or dull. Looking at what you put your faith in tells you a lot about yourself.

Krishna begins his answer by saying that faith itself comes in three kinds, sattvic, rajasic and tamasic, each born of a person's own nature.

The key word is śraddhā, faith in the sense of trust and deep conviction about what is worth giving oneself to, not merely belief in a doctrine. Rather than ruling on those who set scripture aside, he turns the question round: what matters first is the quality of the faith a person brings.
