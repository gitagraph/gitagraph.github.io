Bhagavad Gita 4.22

Krishna

यदृच्छालाभसंतुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वापि न निबध्यते ॥ ४.२२ ॥

yadṛcchālābhasaṃtuṣṭo dvandvātīto vimatsaraḥ ।
samaḥ siddhāvasiddhau ca kṛtvāpi na nibadhyate ॥

Content with whatever comes by chance, beyond the pairs of opposites, free of envy, and even-minded in success and failure, one is not bound even when acting.

हिन्दीजो अपने आप मिलने वाले लाभ से संतुष्ट रहता है, सुख-दुख जैसे द्वंद्वों से परे है, ईर्ष्या से रहित है और सफलता-असफलता में समान रहता है, वह कर्म करके भी नहीं बँधता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If a college friend now earns twice your salary, keep doing your work without envy. Take the good months as they come and don't let the lean ones define you.

Krishna adds more marks of the person who acts without being bound: content with whatever comes on its own, beyond the pairs of opposites, free of envy, and even-minded in success and failure.

The key word is yadṛcchā, 'chance' or 'what comes unasked': gain is accepted as it arrives, not chased. For a reader, it describes doing one's work while letting outcomes, and comparison with others, lose their grip.
