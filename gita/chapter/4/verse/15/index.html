Bhagavad Gita 4.15

Krishna

एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः ।
कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम् ॥ ४.१५ ॥

evaṃ jñātvā kṛtaṃ karma pūrvairapi mumukṣubhiḥ ।
kuru karmaiva tasmāttvaṃ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraṃ kṛtam ॥

Knowing this, the seekers of freedom of old also acted. So you too should act, as the ancients did long before.

हिन्दीयह जानकर पहले के मोक्ष चाहने वालों ने भी कर्म किए थे। इसलिए तुम भी वैसे ही कर्म करो जैसे पूर्वजों ने बहुत पहले से किए हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Your grandparents worked hard and still knew what not to cling to. You don't need a new philosophy for a new job; you can act as the wise did before you.

Krishna draws the practical conclusion for Arjuna: knowing that action need not bind, the mumukṣus of old, seekers of freedom, still acted, so Arjuna too should act as they did.

The doubled phrase pūrvaiḥ pūrvataram, 'by the ancients, long before', stresses how old this way is. It answers Arjuna's wish to withdraw from the fight: seeking liberation has never required giving up action, only changing how it is done.
