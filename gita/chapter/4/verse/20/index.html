Bhagavad Gita 4.20

Krishna

त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः ।
कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किंचित्करोति सः ॥ ४.२० ॥

tyaktvā karmaphalāsaṅgaṃ nityatṛpto nirāśrayaḥ ।
karmaṇyabhipravṛtto'pi naiva kiṃcitkaroti saḥ ॥

Letting go of attachment to the fruit of action, always content and depending on nothing, such a person does nothing at all even while fully engaged in action.

हिन्दीकर्मफल की आसक्ति छोड़कर, सदा तृप्त और किसी पर आश्रित न रहने वाला व्यक्ति कर्म में पूरी तरह लगा रहकर भी वास्तव में कुछ नहीं करता।

Krishna describes a person who does nothing at all even while fully engaged in action, because attachment to its fruit is gone.

It illustrates his point that the wise see 'inaction in action' (4.18): such a person has dropped karmaphalāsaṅga, attachment to the fruit of action, and is nityatṛpta, always content, and nirāśraya, leaning on nothing outside for security. The work gets no hold on them, because nothing in it is being grasped.
