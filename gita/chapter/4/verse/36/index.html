Bhagavad Gita 4.36

Krishna

अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः ।
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि ॥ ४.३६ ॥

api cedasi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ ।
sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ santariṣyasi ॥

Even if you were the worst of all sinners, you would cross over every wrong by the boat of knowledge alone.

हिन्दीयदि तुम सब पापियों से भी बढ़कर पापी हो, तो भी ज्ञान रूपी नाव से तुम सारे पाप के सागर को पार कर जाओगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you once hurt someone badly, or wasted years you regret, that past doesn't put understanding beyond your reach. Start from where you are; the crossing is still open.

Krishna assures Arjuna that even if he were the worst of all wrongdoers, the boat of knowledge alone would carry him across every wrong.

The image is plava, a raft or boat, which pictures wrongdoing as a flood to be crossed rather than a stain one must first scrub away. It is meant to encourage, not to excuse: no past puts understanding out of reach.
