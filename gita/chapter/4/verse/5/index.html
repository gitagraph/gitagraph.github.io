Verse 4.5

Chapter 4 · Knowledge and Renunciation of ActionVerse 5 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 4.5

Krishna

बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप ॥ ४.५ ॥

bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava cārjuna ।
tānyahaṃ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṃ vettha parantapa ॥

Many births of mine have passed, Arjuna, and many of yours too. I know them all, but you do not, scorcher of foes.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, मेरे और तुम्हारे बहुत से जन्म हो चुके हैं। मैं उन सबको जानता हूँ, पर हे परंतप, तुम नहीं जानते।

Answering Arjuna's objection, Krishna says that both of them have passed through many births; the difference is that Krishna knows all of his, and Arjuna knows none of his.

The key verb is veda, 'I know', set against na vettha, 'you do not know'. The verse takes rebirth for granted, as the Gita does throughout, and moves the question from age to awareness: Krishna's claim rests on knowing what Arjuna does not.
