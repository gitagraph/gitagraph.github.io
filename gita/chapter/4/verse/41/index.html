Chapter 4 · Knowledge and Renunciation of ActionVerse 41 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 4.41

Krishna

योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसंछिन्नसंशयम् ।
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय ॥ ४.४१ ॥

yogasaṃnyastakarmāṇaṃ jñānasaṃchinnasaṃśayam ।
ātmavantaṃ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanaṃjaya ॥

Actions do not bind one who has given up action through yoga, whose doubts are cut away by knowledge, and who is established in the Self, Dhananjaya.

हिन्दीहे धनंजय, जिसने योग के द्वारा कर्मों का त्याग कर दिया है, ज्ञान से जिसके संशय कट गए हैं और जो आत्मा में स्थित है, उसे कर्म नहीं बाँधते।

Krishna draws the threads of chapter 4 together: actions do not bind one who has given up action through yoga, whose doubts are cut away by knowledge, and who is ātmavat, established in the Self.

The striking phrase is yoga-saṃnyasta-karman: action renounced by means of yoga, not by stopping work. Arjuna's next question (5.1) comes straight out of this pairing of renouncing and acting.
