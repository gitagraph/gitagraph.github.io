Bhagavad Gita 4.6

Krishna

अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय संभवाम्यात्ममायया ॥ ४.६ ॥

ajo'pi sannavyayātmā bhūtānāmīśvaro'pi san ।
prakṛtiṃ svāmadhiṣṭhāya saṃbhavāmyātmamāyayā ॥

Though I am unborn and my being never perishes, and though I am the Lord of all beings, I take birth by governing my own nature through my own power of maya.

हिन्दीमैं अजन्मा और अविनाशी स्वरूप वाला हूँ, और सब प्राणियों का ईश्वर भी हूँ; फिर भी अपनी प्रकृति को वश में रखकर अपनी माया से प्रकट होता हूँ।

Krishna explains how one who is unborn can still be born: he is aja, unborn, his being never perishes, and he is Lord of all beings, yet he comes into being by governing his own prakṛti, nature, through ātmamāyā, his own power.

Traditions differ here: some read this māyā as a power of appearance, so the birth only seems to happen; others read it as his own will, so the birth is real but freely taken.
