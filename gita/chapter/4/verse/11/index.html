Bhagavad Gita 4.11

Krishna

ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥ ४.११ ॥

ye yathā māṃ prapadyante tāṃstathaiva bhajāmyaham ।
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ ॥

In whatever way people come to me, in that very way I receive them. Everywhere, Partha, people follow my path.

हिन्दीजो मुझे जिस भाव से भजते हैं, मैं भी उन्हें उसी प्रकार भजता हूँ। हे पार्थ, सब मनुष्य हर तरह से मेरे ही मार्ग पर चलते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In one home, a grandmother lights a lamp, a son sits in silence and a daughter just walks by the sea. Each is reaching in their own way; none needs to correct the others.

Krishna states how he responds to people: in whatever way they approach him, in that same way he meets them, and everywhere people follow his path.

The verb bhaj means to share in or engage with, so the relationship runs both ways. The second line is read in two ways: that every path, knowingly or not, leads to him, or (as the same words mean in 3.23) that people follow the example he sets.
