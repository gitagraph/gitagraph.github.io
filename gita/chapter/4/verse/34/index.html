Bhagavad Gita 4.34

Krishna

तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥ ४.३४ ॥

tadviddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā ।
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadarśinaḥ ॥

Learn it by bowing down, by sincere questioning, and by service. The wise who have seen the truth will teach you that knowledge.

हिन्दीउस ज्ञान को विनम्र प्रणाम करके, बार-बार प्रश्न पूछकर और सेवा करके सीखो। तत्त्व को जानने वाले ज्ञानी तुम्हें उस ज्ञान का उपदेश देंगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Learning a craft, from carpentry to classical music, goes faster when you arrive humble, ask real questions and lend a hand around the workshop. Teachers open up to that.

Krishna tells Arjuna how this knowledge is gained: from those who have seen the truth, approached with humility, real questions and service.

The key word is tattva-darśin, one who has 'seen the reality' rather than only heard about it. The three means work together: praṇipāta, bowing, sets pride aside; paripraśna, searching questions, keeps the learner engaged; and sevā, service, builds the relationship in which teaching can happen.
