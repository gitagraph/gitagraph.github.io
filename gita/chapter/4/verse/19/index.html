Chapter 4 · Knowledge and Renunciation of ActionVerse 19 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 4.19

Krishna

यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसंकल्पवर्जिताः ।
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः ॥ ४.१९ ॥

yasya sarve samārambhāḥ kāmasaṃkalpavarjitāḥ ।
jñānāgnidagdhakarmāṇaṃ tamāhuḥ paṇḍitaṃ budhāḥ ॥

One whose every undertaking is free of desire and selfish intention, whose actions are burned up in the fire of knowledge, is called learned by the wise.

हिन्दीजिसके सब काम कामना और संकल्प से रहित होते हैं और जिसके कर्म ज्ञान की आग में जल गए हैं, उसे ज्ञानीजन पंडित कहते हैं।

People: Krishna

Ideas: Nishkama Karma · Jnana Yoga
