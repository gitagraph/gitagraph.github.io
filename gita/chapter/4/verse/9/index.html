Chapter 4 · Knowledge and Renunciation of ActionVerse 9 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 4.9

Krishna

जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥ ४.९ ॥

janma karma ca me divyamevaṃ yo vetti tattvataḥ ।
tyaktvā dehaṃ punarjanma naiti māmeti so'rjuna ॥

One who truly understands my divine birth and action in this way is not born again on leaving the body, but comes to me, Arjuna.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, जो मेरे दिव्य जन्म और कर्म को इस प्रकार तत्त्व से जान लेता है, वह शरीर छोड़ने पर फिर जन्म नहीं लेता, बल्कि मुझे प्राप्त होता है।

Krishna says that whoever truly understands his divine birth and action is not born again on leaving the body, but comes to him.

The key word is divya, 'divine': having explained why he appears age after age (4.7–8), he presents his birth and action as of the kind described in 4.6, freely taken rather than forced. Knowing this tattvataḥ, 'in its truth', is presented as more than information; it changes the knower's own relation to birth.
