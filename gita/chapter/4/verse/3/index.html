Bhagavad Gita 4.3

Krishna

स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम् ॥ ४.३ ॥

sa evāyaṃ mayā te'dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ ।
bhakto'si me sakhā ceti rahasyaṃ hyetaduttamam ॥

That same ancient yoga I have told you today, because you are my devotee and my friend; this is indeed the highest secret.

हिन्दीवही यह पुरातन योग आज मैंने तुमसे कहा है, क्योंकि तुम मेरे भक्त और मित्र हो। यह सचमुच उत्तम रहस्य है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The most useful advice is often shared only between friends, because it needs trust to be heard properly. If someone opens up to you that way, honour it by actually listening.

Krishna tells Arjuna that this same ancient yoga is what he has taught him today, and gives the reason: Arjuna is his bhakta, devoted to him, and his sakhā, friend.

The teaching is called rahasyam uttamam, 'the highest secret', and here it is opened up within a bond of trust. For readers, the verse frames the whole Gita as a conversation between friends rather than a lecture.
