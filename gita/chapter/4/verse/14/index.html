Bhagavad Gita 4.14

Krishna

न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा ।
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते ॥ ४.१४ ॥

na māṃ karmāṇi limpanti na me karmaphale spṛhā ।
iti māṃ yo'bhijānāti karmabhirna sa badhyate ॥

Actions do not stain me, nor do I long for their fruit. One who knows me in this way is not bound by actions.

हिन्दीकर्म मुझे लिप्त नहीं करते और न ही मुझे कर्मफल की इच्छा है। जो मुझे इस प्रकार जानता है, वह भी कर्मों से नहीं बँधता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A good mentor helps juniors succeed without needing the credit back. Work done that way doesn't cling to you, and watching it helps you learn to work like that yourself.

Krishna explains why his action does not bind him: actions do not stain (limpanti) him, and he has no longing (spṛhā) for their fruit.

The image is of something clinging to a surface, like oil or dye; what lets action leave a mark is the craving for results. The verse then turns to the listener: whoever understands Krishna this way is not bound by action either, because the same principle, acting without craving, is open to anyone.
