Bhagavad Gita 4.8

Krishna

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे ॥ ४.८ ॥

paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṃ vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām ।
dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge ॥

To protect the good, to destroy those who do evil, and to establish righteousness firmly again, I come into being age after age.

हिन्दीसज्जनों की रक्षा करने, दुष्टों का नाश करने और धर्म को फिर से स्थापित करने के लिए मैं हर युग में प्रकट होता हूँ।

Krishna gives the threefold purpose of his coming: paritrāṇa, protection, of sādhus, the good; the destruction of duṣkṛtām, those whose deeds do harm; and dharma-saṃsthāpana, setting right living firmly back in place.

Yuge yuge, 'age after age', presents this as a recurring pattern rather than a single event. For many readers the verse is less a prediction than a reassurance that wrongdoing does not have the last word.
