Bhagavad Gita 4.38

Krishna

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥ ४.३८ ॥

na hi jñānena sadṛśaṃ pavitramiha vidyate ।
tatsvayaṃ yogasaṃsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati ॥

Truly, nothing in this world purifies like knowledge. One who is perfected in yoga finds it in oneself, in time.

हिन्दीइस संसार में ज्ञान के समान पवित्र करने वाला सचमुच कुछ भी नहीं है। योग में सिद्ध हुआ व्यक्ति समय आने पर उसे अपने भीतर स्वयं पा लेता है।

In your life

No amount of new clothes or a fresh haircut cleans the mind like really understanding yourself. That clarity tends to come with time and practice, not all at once.

Krishna says nothing in this world purifies like knowledge, and that one perfected in yoga finds it within, in time.

The key word is pavitra, that which purifies. Two further words stand out: svayam, 'by oneself', and kālena, 'in time'. Knowledge here is not simply handed over; disciplined practice ripens until a person discovers it in their own self, which balances the stress on learning from the wise in 4.34.
