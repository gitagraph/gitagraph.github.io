Bhagavad Gita 4.28

Krishna

द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे ।
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः ॥ ४.२८ ॥

dravyayajñāstapoyajñā yogayajñāstathāpare ।
svādhyāyajñānayajñāśca yatayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ ॥

Some offer wealth as sacrifice, some austerity, some the practice of yoga; and other striving people of firm vows offer study of scripture and knowledge.

हिन्दीकुछ लोग धन से यज्ञ करते हैं, कुछ तप से यज्ञ करते हैं, कुछ योग से यज्ञ करते हैं, और कठोर व्रत वाले कुछ यत्नशील लोग स्वाध्याय और ज्ञान से यज्ञ करते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

One friend gives money to causes, another gives evenings to study, another keeps a hard discipline. Each is an offering of what they have; find the one that fits you and give it sincerely.

Krishna widens the list of sacrifices to whole ways of life: some offer material wealth, some austerity (tapas), some yogic practice, and others, disciplined people of firm vows, offer svādhyāya, the study of scripture, along with knowledge.

Each is called a yajña. For a reader, the point is that giving, self-discipline, practice and study can all be done in the same spirit of offering, with none of them the only true form.
