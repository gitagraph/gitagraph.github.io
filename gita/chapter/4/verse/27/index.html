Bhagavad Gita 4.27

Krishna

सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते ॥ ४.२७ ॥

sarvāṇīndriyakarmāṇi prāṇakarmāṇi cāpare ।
ātmasaṃyamayogāgnau juhvati jñānadīpite ॥

Others offer all the workings of the senses and of the life-breath into the fire of the yoga of self-control, kindled by knowledge.

हिन्दीकुछ दूसरे लोग इंद्रियों के सब कर्मों और प्राणों के सब कर्मों को ज्ञान से प्रज्वलित आत्मसंयम रूपी योग की अग्नि में होम करते हैं।

Krishna names a further sacrifice: offering every working of the senses and of the life-breath into the fire of the 'yoga of self-control', a fire kindled by knowledge.

The key word is prāṇa, the life-breath, the vital energy behind every living activity; here even that is drawn inward. It points to meditative stillness, in which the outward activity of senses and breath settles, and that settling is lit by understanding rather than forced.
