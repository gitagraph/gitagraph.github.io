Bhagavad Gita 4.33

Krishna

श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परन्तप ।
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते ॥ ४.३३ ॥

śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa ।
sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate ॥

Scorcher of foes, the sacrifice of knowledge is better than any sacrifice of material things; for all action, Partha, finds its completion in knowledge.

हिन्दीहे परंतप, द्रव्य से होने वाले यज्ञ से ज्ञानयज्ञ श्रेष्ठ है, क्योंकि हे पार्थ, सारे कर्म अंत में ज्ञान में ही पूरे होते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Buying your father an expensive gift is kind, but sitting with him to understand what he really worries about may give him more. Understanding is the fuller offering.

Krishna now ranks the sacrifices: the sacrifice of knowledge, jñāna-yajña, is better than any sacrifice of material things, because all action finds its completion in knowledge.

The verb parisamāpyate means 'comes to its full end': every practice in the list is heading toward understanding. Read plainly, rituals and gifts have value, but what they are for is a change in how one sees, not the goods offered.
