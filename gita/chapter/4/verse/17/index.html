Chapter 4 · Knowledge and Renunciation of ActionVerse 17 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 4.17

Krishna

कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥ ४.१७ ॥

karmaṇo hyapi boddhavyaṃ boddhavyaṃ ca vikarmaṇaḥ ।
akarmaṇaśca boddhavyaṃ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ ॥

One must understand what action is, understand what wrong action is, and understand what inaction is; the way of action is deep.

हिन्दीकर्म को भी समझना चाहिए, विकर्म (निषिद्ध कर्म) को भी समझना चाहिए और अकर्म को भी समझना चाहिए, क्योंकि कर्म की गति बड़ी गहन है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Staying silent while a friend is mistreated can be a wrong action, and staying busy can be a way of doing nothing. The way of action is subtle; look past how it appears.

Krishna says three things must be understood, karma, vikarma and akarma, because gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ, 'the way of action is deep'.

Karma is action and akarma inaction; vikarma is read by many as wrong or forbidden action, and by others as action of a special or varied kind. The verse prepares the paradox of 4.18. Its lesson is modest: whether someone is truly acting cannot be judged from the outside alone.
