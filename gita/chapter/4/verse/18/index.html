Bhagavad Gita 4.18

Krishna

कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ ४.१८ ॥

karmaṇyakarma yaḥ paśyedakarmaṇi ca karma yaḥ ।
sa buddhimānmanuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsnakarmakṛt ॥

One who sees inaction in action and action in inaction is wise among people; that person is steady in yoga and does every action fully.

हिन्दीजो कर्म में अकर्म और अकर्म में कर्म देखता है, वही मनुष्यों में बुद्धिमान है। वह योगी है और सब कर्मों को पूरी तरह करने वाला है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Someone lying on the sofa replaying an argument all evening is busier inside than a calm nurse on a double shift. Judge your day by what drives you, not by how much you did.

Krishna gives the paradox he has been preparing: the wise person sees inaction in action and action in inaction.

Read in context, someone who acts without craving or the sense of being the doer is, inwardly, not acting, while someone who merely stops may still be busy with ego and choice. Such a person is yukta, steady in yoga, and kṛtsnakarmakṛt, one who does every action fully. The verse asks us to judge action by its inner source.
