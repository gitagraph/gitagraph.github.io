Bhagavad Gita 4.10

Krishna

वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः ॥ ४.१० ॥

vītarāgabhayakrodhā manmayā māmupāśritāḥ ।
bahavo jñānatapasā pūtā madbhāvamāgatāḥ ॥

Free from craving, fear and anger, absorbed in me and taking refuge in me, many, purified by the discipline of knowledge, have reached my state of being.

हिन्दीराग, भय और क्रोध से मुक्त, मुझमें लीन और मेरी शरण में आए हुए बहुत से लोग ज्ञान रूपी तप से पवित्र होकर मेरे स्वरूप को प्राप्त हो चुके हैं।

In your life

Many people have quietly let go of craving, fear and anger and become gentler for it: a strict father who softened, a bitter neighbour who mellowed. It has been done before; it can be done by you.

Krishna says that many people have already reached his own state of being (madbhāva), freed from craving, fear and anger, absorbed in him and relying on him.

The compound jñāna-tapas, 'the discipline of knowledge', names what purified them and suggests that understanding itself is the purifying practice. The verse presents this not as a rare exception but as a path many have already walked, which encourages the listener.
