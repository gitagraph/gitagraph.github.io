Bhagavad Gita 12.20

Krishna

ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।
श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥ १२.२० ॥

ye tu dharmyāmṛtamidaṃ yathoktaṃ paryupāsate ।
śraddadhānā matparamā bhaktāste'tīva me priyāḥ ॥

But those who, full of faith and holding me as supreme, devotedly follow this immortal teaching of dharma just as I have given it — those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.

हिन्दीपरंतु जो श्रद्धा रखते हुए और मुझे ही परम मानते हुए, धर्म से भरे इस अमृत-उपदेश का ठीक वैसे ही पालन करते हैं जैसा मैंने कहा है — वे भक्त मुझे अत्यंत प्रिय हैं।

In your life

You don't need to master every quality in this chapter at once. Following even part of it with faith, a little more patience, a little less hatred, is held especially dear here.

In the last verse of chapter 12, Krishna says that those who follow this teaching with faith, just as he has given it, are exceedingly dear to him.

He calls it dharmyāmṛta, 'a nectar of dharma'. The earlier verses called a person with these qualities 'dear'; here those who take up the teaching with faith are 'exceedingly dear', which, on one reading, opens the ideal to anyone willing to follow it, not only to those who have already reached it.
