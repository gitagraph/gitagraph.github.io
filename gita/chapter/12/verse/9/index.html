Bhagavad Gita 12.9

Krishna

अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनंजय ॥ १२.९ ॥

atha cittaṃ samādhātuṃ na śaknoṣi mayi sthiram ।
abhyāsayogena tato māmicchāptuṃ dhanaṃjaya ॥

If you cannot hold your mind steadily on me, then seek to reach me through the yoga of repeated practice, Dhananjaya.

हिन्दीयदि तुम अपने चित्त को मुझमें स्थिर नहीं रख सकते, तो हे धनंजय, अभ्यास-योग के द्वारा मुझे पाने की इच्छा करो।

In your life

If you can't keep your mind steady for long, start smaller: a few quiet breaths each evening before you walk in the front door, repeated day after day, until coming back becomes the habit.

Krishna offers a gentler alternative: if Arjuna cannot keep his mind steadily on him, he should seek to reach him through abhyāsa-yoga, the discipline of repeated practice.

The idea echoes 6.35, where practice is named as the way to steady a restless mind. Practice here means bringing attention back again and again without expecting it to stay put at first; failing to hold focus is treated as normal, not as a reason to give up.
