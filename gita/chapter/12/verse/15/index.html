Bhagavad Gita 12.15

Krishna

यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १२.१५ ॥

yasmānnodvijate loko lokānnodvijate ca yaḥ ।
harṣāmarṣabhayodvegairmukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ ॥

One who does not disturb the world and is not disturbed by the world, who is free from elation, resentment, fear and agitation — such a person is dear to me.

हिन्दीजिससे संसार को कोई उद्वेग नहीं होता और जो स्वयं संसार से उद्विग्न नहीं होता, जो हर्ष, अमर्ष (असहनशीलता), भय और घबराहट से मुक्त है — वह भी मुझे प्रिय है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a joint family under one roof, try not to be the one whose moods everyone tiptoes around, and don't let every slammed door unsettle you. Peace runs both ways.

Krishna says that one who neither disturbs the world nor is disturbed by it, and is free of elation, resentment, fear and agitation, is also dear to him.

The key verb is udvij, 'to be shaken' or 'to shrink back', and it works in both directions; amarṣa is resentment or impatience at others. The 'also' ties the verse to the refrain running through this portrait. In plain words, it describes a person who is easy to be around and hard to knock off balance.
