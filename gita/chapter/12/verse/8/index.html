Bhagavad Gita 12.8

Krishna

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ १२.८ ॥

mayyeva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṃ niveśaya ।
nivasiṣyasi mayyeva ata ūrdhvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ॥

Set your mind on me alone and let your understanding settle in me; after that you will live in me, without any doubt.

हिन्दीअपना मन केवल मुझमें लगाओ और अपनी बुद्धि मुझमें स्थिर करो; इसके बाद तुम मुझमें ही निवास करोगे, इसमें कोई संदेह नहीं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When you commit to someone, let both your heart and your judgement settle on that choice. A heart that agrees while the head keeps reopening the question stays restless.

Krishna begins a series of graded steps with the highest one: set the mind on him alone, let the understanding settle in him, and Arjuna will live in him without doubt.

The pairing matters: manas is the mind that feels and wanders, buddhi the faculty that judges and decides; both are to be turned the same way. The next verses (12.9–11) offer gentler steps for anyone who finds this too hard.
