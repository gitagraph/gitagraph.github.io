Bhagavad Gita 12.6

Krishna

ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥ १२.६ ॥

ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparāḥ ।
ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate ॥

But those who lay down all their actions in me, hold me as their highest goal, and worship me, meditating on me with undivided yoga…

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Offering your work to something higher can begin with one sentence at your desk: 'This is for you.' It changes how you hold the task without changing the task itself.

Krishna describes the devotees he will rescue: those who lay all their actions in him, hold him as their goal and meditate on him with undivided yoga; the sentence completes in 12.7.

The key word is saṃnyasya, 'having laid down': renunciation here does not mean stopping work but offering it, so that each action is done for something beyond oneself. This is a devotion that fits an active life rather than one of withdrawal.
