Bhagavad Gita 1.3

Duryodhana

पश्यैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्य महतीं चमूम् ।
व्यूढां द्रुपदपुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता ॥ १.३ ॥

paśyaitāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmācārya mahatīṃ camūm ।
vyūḍhāṃ drupadaputreṇa tava śiṣyeṇa dhīmatā ॥

Duryodhana said: Teacher, look at this vast army of the sons of Pandu, arrayed by the son of Drupada, your own gifted pupil.

हिन्दीदुर्योधन ने कहा: आचार्य, पांडु के पुत्रों की इस विशाल सेना को देखिए, जिसे आपके ही बुद्धिमान शिष्य, द्रुपद के पुत्र ने व्यूह में खड़ा किया है।

Duryodhana points his teacher Drona to the vast Pandava army and notes, with an edge, who arranged it: Drona's own pupil, the son of Drupada.

From the Mahabharata: this is Dhrishtadyumna, born to bring about Drona's death, whom Drona taught all the same. The phrase tava śiṣyeṇa dhīmatā, 'your gifted pupil', works as a jab: the skill now ranged against Drona is skill he himself passed on.
