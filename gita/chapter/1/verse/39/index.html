Bhagavad Gita 1.39

Arjuna

कथं न ज्ञेयमस्माभिः पापादस्मान्निवर्तितुम् ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं प्रपश्यद्भिर्जनार्दन ॥ १.३९ ॥

kathaṃ na jñeyamasmābhiḥ pāpādasmānnivartitum ।
kulakṣayakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ prapaśyadbhirjanārdana ॥

why should we, who clearly see the evil of destroying a family, not know enough to turn away from this sin, Janardana?

हिन्दीपर हे जनार्दन, कुल के नाश का दोष साफ़ देखने वाले हम लोग इस पाप से बचना क्यों न समझें?

Arjuna completes his argument: even if the Kauravas cannot see the evil of destroying a family, the Pandavas can, so why should they not know to turn away from this sin?

Prapaśyadbhiḥ, 'by us who see clearly', is the key word: for Arjuna, seeing the harm brings responsibility. Ironically, Krishna will soon question this very clarity, saying in 2.11 that Arjuna speaks wise-sounding words while grieving where no grief is due.
