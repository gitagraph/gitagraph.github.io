Bhagavad Gita 1.45

Arjuna

अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम् ।
यद्राज्यसुखलोभेन हन्तुं स्वजनमुद्यताः ॥ १.४५ ॥

aho bata mahatpāpaṃ kartuṃ vyavasitā vayam ।
yadrājyasukhalobhena hantuṃ svajanamudyatāḥ ॥

Alas, what a great sin we have resolved to commit, setting out to kill our own people out of greed for the pleasures of a kingdom!

हिन्दीहाय, कितने दुख की बात है कि हम एक बड़ा पाप करने पर तुले हुए हैं, जो राज्य के सुख के लोभ में अपने ही लोगों को मारने चले हैं!

Arjuna cries out in dismay that he and his side have resolved on a great wrong: killing their own kin for the comforts of a kingdom.

The exclamation aho bata, 'alas, oh', marks a turn from argument to raw feeling. Note the word lobha, greed: Arjuna now sees his own side's motive in the same light he cast on the Kauravas in 1.38, which is part of what makes his crisis so sharp.
