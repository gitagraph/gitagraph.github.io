Bhagavad Gita 1.28

Arjuna

कृपया परयाविष्टो विषीदन्निदमब्रवीत् ।
दृष्ट्वेमं स्वजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम् ॥ १.२८ ॥

kṛpayā parayāviṣṭo viṣīdannidamabravīt ।
dṛṣṭvemaṃ svajanaṃ kṛṣṇa yuyutsuṃ samupasthitam ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 1.27

…overcome with deep pity and sinking in sorrow, said this. Arjuna said: Krishna, seeing my own people standing here eager to fight,

हिन्दी…गहरी करुणा से भरकर और शोक में डूबकर यह बोले। अर्जुन बोले: हे कृष्ण, युद्ध की इच्छा से सामने खड़े इन अपने ही लोगों को देखकर,

Overcome by the sight of his own people ready to fight, Arjuna, sinking in sorrow, begins to speak to Krishna.

Two words name his state: kṛpā, pity or compassion that has unsettled him, and viṣīdan, 'sinking into despondency'. Traditionally the chapter takes its title from this viṣāda. Apart from Sanjaya's closing verse, the rest of the chapter is Arjuna's own voice, raw and unguarded.
