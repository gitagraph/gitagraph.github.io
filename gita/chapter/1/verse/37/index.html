Chapter 1 · Arjuna's DespondencyVerse 37 of 47

Bhagavad Gita 1.37

Arjuna

तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान् ।
स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव ॥ १.३७ ॥

tasmānnārhā vayaṃ hantuṃ dhārtarāṣṭrānsvabāndhavān ।
svajanaṃ hi kathaṃ hatvā sukhinaḥ syāma mādhava ॥

So it is not right for us to kill the sons of Dhritarashtra, our own kinsmen. How could we be happy after killing our own people, Madhava?

हिन्दीइसलिए अपने ही बंधु धृतराष्ट्र-पुत्रों को मारना हमें शोभा नहीं देता। हे माधव, अपने ही लोगों को मारकर हम कैसे सुखी होंगे?

Arjuna concludes that it is not right for the Pandavas to kill their own kinsmen, for no one could be happy after killing his own people.

The prefix sva-, 'own', runs through the verse in svabāndhavān and svajanam: the argument rests on belonging. Arjuna is weighing the war by its human cost, and the question of happiness afterwards is a real one.
