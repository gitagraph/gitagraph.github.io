Bhagavad Gita 1.43

Arjuna

दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसंकरकारकैः ।
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः ॥ १.४३ ॥

doṣairetaiḥ kulaghnānāṃ varṇasaṃkarakārakaiḥ ।
utsādyante jātidharmāḥ kuladharmāśca śāśvatāḥ ॥

Through these wrongs of the family-destroyers, which cause confusion of the social order, the enduring duties of caste and family are swept away.

हिन्दीवर्णसंकर पैदा करने वाले कुलनाशकों के इन दोषों से सनातन जाति-धर्म और कुल-धर्म उजड़ जाते हैं।

Arjuna sums up his case: the wrongs of those who destroy a family would uproot the long-standing duties of both caste and family.

The key word is varṇasaṃkara, a mixing of the social classes, which for him means the order holding a society together coming apart. These are arguments made in distress; Krishna does not take them up directly and, from Chapter 2, answers on another footing altogether.
