Bhagavad Gita 1.6

Duryodhana

युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान् ।
सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः ॥ १.६ ॥

yudhāmanyuśca vikrānta uttamaujāśca vīryavān ।
saubhadro draupadeyāśca sarva eva mahārathāḥ ॥

the bold Yudhamanyu and the valiant Uttamauja, the son of Subhadra, and the sons of Draupadi — every one of them a great chariot-warrior.

हिन्दीपराक्रमी युधामन्यु और वीर उत्तमौजा, सुभद्रा का पुत्र और द्रौपदी के पुत्र — ये सभी महारथी हैं।

Duryodhana closes his list of Pandava warriors by calling every one a mahāratha.

From the Mahabharata: Yudhamanyu and Uttamauja are Panchala fighters who guard the wheels of Arjuna's chariot; the son of Subhadra is Abhimanyu, Arjuna's young son; and the sons of Draupadi are her five sons, one by each Pandava brother.
