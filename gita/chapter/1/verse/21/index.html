Bhagavad Gita 1.21

Arjuna

हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत ॥ १.२१ ॥

hṛṣīkeśaṃ tadā vākyamidamāha mahīpate ।
senayorubhayormadhye rathaṃ sthāpaya me'cyuta ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 1.20

…and, O king, spoke these words to Hrishikesha. Arjuna said: Achyuta, place my chariot between the two armies,

हिन्दी…और हे राजन, हृषीकेश से यह बात कही। अर्जुन बोले: हे अच्युत, मेरे रथ को दोनों सेनाओं के बीच खड़ा कीजिए,

Arjuna, bow in hand, asks Krishna, his charioteer, to drive into the open ground between the two armies.

He calls Krishna acyuta, 'the unshaken, the one who does not fall', a name that stands in contrast to the wavering Arjuna is about to go through. From the Mahabharata: Krishna had promised not to take up arms in this war and served as Arjuna's driver instead.
