Bhagavad Gita 1.30

Arjuna

गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिदह्यते ।
न च शक्नोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मनः ॥ १.३० ॥

gāṇḍīvaṃ sraṃsate hastāttvakcaiva paridahyate ।
na ca śaknomyavasthātuṃ bhramatīva ca me manaḥ ॥

Gandiva is slipping from my hand, my skin is burning all over; I cannot stand steady, and my mind seems to be reeling.

हिन्दीगांडीव धनुष हाथ से फिसल रहा है, त्वचा जल रही है; मैं खड़ा भी नहीं रह पा रहा और मेरा मन चकरा-सा रहा है।

Arjuna's collapse deepens: his bow Gandiva slips from his hand, his skin burns, he cannot stand steady and his mind reels.

From the Mahabharata: Gandiva is the famous bow he received from the fire god Agni. That Arjuna, the great archer, cannot hold it is the clearest sign of how completely the sight has unmanned him; bhramati, 'whirls', shows his thinking spinning too.
