Bhagavad Gita 1.15

Sanjaya

पाञ्चजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनञ्जयः ।
पौण्ड्रं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः ॥ १.१५ ॥

pāñcajanyaṃ hṛṣīkeśo devadattaṃ dhanañjayaḥ ।
pauṇḍraṃ dadhmau mahāśaṅkhaṃ bhīmakarmā vṛkodaraḥ ॥

Hrishikesha blew Panchajanya, Dhananjaya blew Devadatta, and Vrikodara, doer of fearsome deeds, blew the great conch Paundra.

हिन्दीहृषीकेश (कृष्ण) ने पांचजन्य, धनंजय (अर्जुन) ने देवदत्त, और भयंकर कर्म करने वाले वृकोदर (भीम) ने पौंड्र नाम का महाशंख बजाया।

Sanjaya names the conches of Krishna, Arjuna and Bhima.

Traditionally, Hṛṣīkeśa is read as 'lord of the senses', Dhanañjaya, Arjuna, as 'winner of wealth', and Vṛkodara, Bhima, as 'wolf-bellied'. Krishna's conch is Pāñcajanya, Arjuna's Devadatta, 'god-given', and Bhima's the great Pauṇḍra.
