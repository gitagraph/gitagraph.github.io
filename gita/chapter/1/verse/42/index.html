Chapter 1 · Arjuna's DespondencyVerse 42 of 47

Bhagavad Gita 1.42

Arjuna

संकरो नरकायैव कुलघ्नानां कुलस्य च ।
पतन्ति पितरो ह्येषां लुप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः ॥ १.४२ ॥

saṃkaro narakāyaiva kulaghnānāṃ kulasya ca ।
patanti pitaro hyeṣāṃ luptapiṇḍodakakriyāḥ ॥

This confusion leads both the family and its destroyers to hell, for their ancestors fall, deprived of the offerings of rice and water.

हिन्दीयह संकर कुल के नाशकों और कुल, दोनों को नरक में ले जाता है, क्योंकि पिंड और जल के तर्पण से वंचित होकर उनके पितर गिर जाते हैं।

Arjuna argues that the social confusion he fears from this war would drag both the family and those who destroyed it to hell.

His key term is piṇḍodaka, the rice balls and water offered to ancestors: with no descendants left to offer them, he says, even the forebears would fall. His grief now reaches past the living family to its dead, and Krishna will answer it in Chapter 2 on very different ground.
