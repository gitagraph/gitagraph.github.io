Bhagavad Gita 1.10

Duryodhana

अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् ।
पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषां बलं भीमाभिरक्षितम् ॥ १.१० ॥

aparyāptaṃ tadasmākaṃ balaṃ bhīṣmābhirakṣitam ।
paryāptaṃ tvidameteṣāṃ balaṃ bhīmābhirakṣitam ॥

That strength of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is beyond measure, while their strength, guarded by Bhima, is limited.

हिन्दीभीष्म द्वारा रक्षित हमारी वह सेना अपार है, जबकि भीम द्वारा रक्षित उनकी यह सेना सीमित है।

Duryodhana sets the two armies side by side, calling his own, guarded by Bhishma, beyond measure and the Pandavas', guarded by Bhima, limited.

The key word aparyāpta can mean 'unlimited' or 'insufficient', so readers have long differed: some hear a boast, others a slip that betrays doubt. The site follows the first reading.
