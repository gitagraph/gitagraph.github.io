Bhagavad Gita 1.46

Arjuna

यदि मामप्रतीकारमशस्त्रं शस्त्रपाणयः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत् ॥ १.४६ ॥

yadi māmapratīkāramaśastraṃ śastrapāṇayaḥ ।
dhārtarāṣṭrā raṇe hanyustanme kṣemataraṃ bhavet ॥

It would be better for me if the sons of Dhritarashtra, weapons in hand, killed me in battle unarmed and unresisting.

हिन्दीअगर हाथों में शस्त्र लिए धृतराष्ट्र के पुत्र मुझ निहत्थे और सामना न करने वाले को युद्ध में मार डालें, तो वह मेरे लिए ज़्यादा भला होगा।

Arjuna ends his plea by saying he would rather be killed, unarmed and unresisting, by Dhritarashtra's sons than fight them.

The key word is kṣemataram, 'better for me', literally 'safer' or 'more wholesome': death without resistance now looks to him better than victory won through slaughter. It is the lowest point of his despair, the moment just before he lays down his bow.
