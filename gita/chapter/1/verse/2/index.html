Chapter 1 · Arjuna's DespondencyVerse 2 of 47

Bhagavad Gita 1.2

Sanjaya

दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा ।
आचार्यमुपसंगम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १.२ ॥

dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavānīkaṃ vyūḍhaṃ duryodhanastadā ।
ācāryamupasaṃgamya rājā vacanamabravīt ॥

Seeing the Pandava army drawn up in battle order, King Duryodhana then went up to his teacher and spoke these words.

हिन्दीउस समय पांडवों की सेना को व्यूह में सजी देखकर राजा दुर्योधन अपने आचार्य के पास गए और यह बात कही।

Answering Dhritarashtra's question in 1.1, Sanjaya opens his report: seeing the Pandava army drawn up for battle, King Duryodhana goes straight to his teacher.

From the Mahabharata: this teacher is Drona, who trained both the Kaurava and the Pandava princes in war. The word vyūḍha, 'arrayed', means set out in a formal battle formation. That a king's first move is to seek out his teacher hints at unease behind the title rājā.
