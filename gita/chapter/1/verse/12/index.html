Bhagavad Gita 1.12

Sanjaya

तस्य संजनयन्हर्षं कुरुवृद्धः पितामहः ।
सिंहनादं विनद्योच्चैः शङ्खं दध्मौ प्रतापवान् ॥ १.१२ ॥

tasya saṃjanayanharṣaṃ kuruvṛddhaḥ pitāmahaḥ ।
siṃhanādaṃ vinadyoccaiḥ śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau pratāpavān ॥

Then, to cheer him, the mighty grandsire, eldest of the Kurus, roared like a lion and blew his conch loudly.

हिन्दीतब दुर्योधन का उत्साह बढ़ाने के लिए कुरुवंश के सबसे वृद्ध, प्रतापी पितामह भीष्म ने सिंह की तरह गरजकर ज़ोर से शंख बजाया।

Sanjaya reports that Bhishma, the Kurus' eldest, answered Duryodhana's anxiety with a lion's roar and a loud blast on his conch.

Tasya, 'his', refers to Duryodhana, whose spirits Bhishma means to lift. Notably, the reply comes from Bhishma rather than the teacher Duryodhana addressed, and as sound rather than words.
