Bhagavad Gita 1.11

Duryodhana

अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः ।
भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥ १.११ ॥

ayaneṣu ca sarveṣu yathābhāgamavasthitāḥ ।
bhīṣmamevābhirakṣantu bhavantaḥ sarva eva hi ॥

So all of you, holding your assigned places at every point of entry, must above all protect Bhishma.

हिन्दीइसलिए आप सब लोग सभी मोर्चों पर अपनी-अपनी जगह डटे रहकर हर तरह से भीष्म की ही रक्षा करें।

Duryodhana ends his speech with an order: every commander must hold his post and, above all, protect Bhishma.

Ayana means a path or point of approach, so every line of entry is to be guarded. The doubled eva, 'only, above all', shows how much his whole plan hangs on one aged commander.
