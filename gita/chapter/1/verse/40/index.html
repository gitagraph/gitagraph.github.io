Bhagavad Gita 1.40

Arjuna

कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः ।
धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत ॥ १.४० ॥

kulakṣaye praṇaśyanti kuladharmāḥ sanātanāḥ ।
dharme naṣṭe kulaṃ kṛtsnamadharmo'bhibhavatyuta ॥

When a family is destroyed, its age-old family traditions perish; and when dharma is lost, lawlessness overwhelms the whole family.

हिन्दीकुल का नाश होने पर उसकी सनातन कुल-परंपराएँ नष्ट हो जाती हैं, और धर्म के नष्ट होने पर पूरे कुल पर अधर्म छा जाता है।

Arjuna widens his case from personal grief to social collapse: when a family is destroyed, its age-old traditions perish, and lawlessness overtakes it.

Kuladharma means the duties, rites and customs a family carries from generation to generation, and sanātana, 'age-old', stresses how long they have lasted. His fear is that war wipes out not just people but the ways of life they kept going.
