Bhagavad Gita 1.13

Sanjaya

ततः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च पणवानकगोमुखाः ।
सहसैवाभ्यहन्यन्त स शब्दस्तुमुलोऽभवत् ॥ १.१३ ॥

tataḥ śaṅkhāśca bheryaśca paṇavānakagomukhāḥ ।
sahasaivābhyahanyanta sa śabdastumulo'bhavat ॥

At once conches, kettledrums, tabors, drums and horns blared out together, and the sound was deafening.

हिन्दीइसके बाद शंख, नगाड़े, ढोल, मृदंग और नरसिंघे एक साथ बज उठे, और वह आवाज़ बहुत भयंकर हुई।

After Bhishma's conch, the Kaurava side bursts into sound: conches, kettledrums, tabors, drums and horns all at once.

The word sahasā, 'suddenly, all together', suggests a rush rather than a measured signal, and tumula describes a deafening uproar. The gomukha, literally 'cow-mouth', is a kind of horn.
