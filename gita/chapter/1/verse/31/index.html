Bhagavad Gita 1.31

Arjuna

निमित्तानि च पश्यामि विपरीतानि केशव ।
न च श्रेयोऽनुपश्यामि हत्वा स्वजनमाहवे ॥ १.३१ ॥

nimittāni ca paśyāmi viparītāni keśava ।
na ca śreyo'nupaśyāmi hatvā svajanamāhave ॥

I see ill omens, Keshava, and I see no good coming from killing my own people in battle.

हिन्दीहे केशव, मुझे अपशकुन दिखाई दे रहे हैं, और युद्ध में अपने ही लोगों को मारकर मुझे कोई भलाई नहीं दिखती।

Arjuna says he sees ill omens and can see no good in killing his own people.

The key word is śreyas, the truly good or beneficial, which becomes a thread of the whole dialogue: in 2.7 Arjuna will ask Krishna to tell him what is śreyas for him. Nimitta, 'omen', can also mean 'cause' or 'reason', so some read the line as Arjuna seeing only perverse reasons for this war.
