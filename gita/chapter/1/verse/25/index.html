Bhagavad Gita 1.25

Sanjaya

भीष्मद्रोणप्रमुखतः सर्वेषां च महीक्षिताम् ।
उवाच पार्थ पश्यैतान्समवेतान्कुरूनिति ॥ १.२५ ॥

bhīṣmadroṇapramukhataḥ sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitām ।
uvāca pārtha paśyaitānsamavetānkurūniti ॥

facing Bhishma, Drona and all the rulers of the earth, and said: Partha, see these Kurus assembled here.

हिन्दीभीष्म, द्रोण और सभी राजाओं के सामने, और कहा: हे पार्थ, यहाँ इकट्ठे हुए इन कौरवों को देखो।

Krishna stops the chariot right in front of Bhishma, Drona and the assembled kings, and says only: 'Partha, see these Kurus gathered here.'

Kuru is the family name both sides share, so the phrase quietly points to kinship. Pārtha, 'son of Pritha', recalls Arjuna's mother Kunti. These are Krishna's first words in the Gita, and they simply ask Arjuna to look.
