Bhagavad Gita 1.35

Arjuna

एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि घ्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन ।
अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते ॥ १.३५ ॥

etānna hantumicchāmi ghnato'pi madhusūdana ।
api trailokyarājyasya hetoḥ kiṃ nu mahīkṛte ॥

I do not wish to kill them, Madhusudana, even if they kill me — not even for rule over the three worlds, let alone for this earth.

हिन्दीहे मधुसूदन, चाहे वे मुझे मार डालें, फिर भी मैं इन्हें मारना नहीं चाहता — तीनों लोकों के राज्य के लिए भी नहीं, फिर इस धरती के लिए तो क्या ही।

Arjuna declares he would not kill his kinsmen even if they were killing him, not for rule over the three worlds, let alone for this earth.

Ghnataḥ, 'those who kill', can mean 'even if they kill me' or 'even though they are killers'; the site takes the first. Madhusūdana, traditionally 'slayer of the demon Madhu', is a pointed name to use while refusing to slay.
