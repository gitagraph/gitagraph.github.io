Bhagavad Gita 1.17

Sanjaya

काश्यश्च परमेष्वासः शिखण्डी च महारथः ।
धृष्टद्युम्नो विराटश्च सात्यकिश्चापराजितः ॥ १.१७ ॥

kāśyaśca parameṣvāsaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ ।
dhṛṣṭadyumno virāṭaśca sātyakiścāparājitaḥ ॥

The king of Kashi, a superb archer, Shikhandi the great chariot-warrior, Dhrishtadyumna, Virata, and the unconquered Satyaki;

हिन्दीश्रेष्ठ धनुर्धर काशीराज, महारथी शिखंडी, धृष्टद्युम्न, विराट और कभी न हारने वाले सात्यकि;

Sanjaya lists more Pandava allies sounding their conches.

From the Mahabharata: Shikhandi is Drupada's child, who will later play a part in Bhishma's fall; Dhrishtadyumna, Drupada's son, commands the Pandava army; Virata is the king who sheltered the Pandavas; and Satyaki is the Yuyudhana of 1.4.
