Bhagavad Gita 1.29

Arjuna

सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिशुष्यति ।
वेपथुश्च शरीरे मे रोमहर्षश्च जायते ॥ १.२९ ॥

sīdanti mama gātrāṇi mukhaṃ ca pariśuṣyati ।
vepathuśca śarīre me romaharṣaśca jāyate ॥

my limbs give way and my mouth goes dry; my body trembles and my hair stands on end.

हिन्दीमेरे अंग ढीले पड़ रहे हैं, मुँह सूख रहा है, शरीर काँप रहा है और रोंगटे खड़े हो रहे हैं।

Arjuna describes his body giving way at the sight of his kin: weak limbs, a dry mouth, trembling, hair standing on end.

Romaharṣa, literally 'thrill of the hair', is the bristling that comes with strong feeling. The Gita shows his distress first in the body, before he finds any arguments, which makes the scene very human: shock arrives before reasons do.
