Chapter 15 · The Supreme Person

Chapter 15 · 20 verses

The Supreme Person

पुरुषोत्तमयोग

The world is an ashvattha tree with roots above and branches below; cut it down with the axe of detachment. The individual soul is an eternal fragment of Krishna. Beyond the perishable and the imperishable stands the Supreme Person, Purushottama.

संसार एक अश्वत्थ वृक्ष है, जिसकी जड़ें ऊपर और शाखाएँ नीचे हैं; इसे वैराग्य की कुल्हाड़ी से काटो। जीवात्मा कृष्ण का सनातन अंश है। क्षर और अक्षर से परे परम पुरुष, पुरुषोत्तम, हैं।

  1. 15.1

    They speak of an imperishable ashvattha (sacred fig) tree whose roots point upward and whose branches spread downward. Its leaves are the Vedic hymns, and whoever understands this tree truly knows the Veda.

  2. 15.2

    Its branches reach both downward and upward, fed and swelled by the gunas, with sense-objects as their young shoots. Below, in the world of humans, its roots stretch on and on, binding people through their actions.

  3. 15.3

    Its true form cannot be grasped here, nor its end, nor its beginning, nor what it rests on. Cutting down this firmly rooted ashvattha with the strong axe of non-attachment…

  4. 15.4

    …one should then seek out that state from which those who reach it never return, thinking: 'I take refuge in that primal Person from whom this ancient flow of creation has streamed forth.'

  5. 15.5

    Free of pride and delusion, having overcome the flaw of attachment, always rooted in the Self, with desires fallen away and released from the pairs of opposites called pleasure and pain, the undeluded reach that undying state.

  6. 15.6

    Neither the sun nor the moon nor fire lights up that place. Those who go there do not come back; that is my supreme abode.

  7. 15.7

    A part of my very own self, eternal, becomes the living soul in this world of living beings; it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

  8. 15.8

    Whenever this lord of the body takes up a body or leaves one, it carries these along with it, the way the wind carries scents away from their source.

  9. 15.9

    Presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste and smell, and over the mind as well, it enjoys the objects of the senses.

  10. 15.10

    Whether it is leaving the body, staying in it, or enjoying experiences while joined with the gunas, the deluded do not perceive it; those with the eye of knowledge do.

  11. 15.11

    Yogis who strive see it dwelling within themselves. But those whose minds are unrefined and lack understanding do not see it, even when they make the effort.

  12. 15.12

    The radiance in the sun that lights up the whole world, the radiance in the moon and in fire, know that radiance to be mine.

  13. 15.13

    Entering the earth, I hold up all beings by my power. Becoming Soma, the essence of sap, I nourish every plant.

  14. 15.14

    Becoming the digestive fire Vaishvanara, I live in the bodies of living beings and, joined with the inward and outward breaths, I digest the four kinds of food.

  15. 15.15

    I am seated in the heart of everyone; from me come memory, knowledge and their loss. I alone am what all the Vedas seek to make known; I am the author of Vedanta and the knower of the Vedas.

  16. 15.16

    There are two kinds of persons in this world, the perishable and the imperishable. All beings are the perishable; the unchanging one, standing firm at the core, is called the imperishable.

  17. 15.17

    But there is another, the highest Person, called the Supreme Self, the unchanging Lord who pervades the three worlds and sustains them.

  18. 15.18

    Because I am beyond the perishable and higher even than the imperishable, I am celebrated in the world and in the Veda as Purushottama, the Supreme Person.

  19. 15.19

    Whoever, free of confusion, knows me in this way as the Supreme Person knows everything, Bharata, and worships me with their whole being.

  20. 15.20

    Sinless one, I have now told you this most secret teaching. Understanding it, Bharata, a person becomes truly wise and has done all that needs to be done.
