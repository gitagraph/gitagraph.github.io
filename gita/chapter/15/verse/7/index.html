Bhagavad Gita 15.7

Krishna

ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।
मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥ १५.७ ॥

mamaivāṃśo jīvaloke jīvabhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ ।
manaḥṣaṣṭhānīndriyāṇi prakṛtisthāni karṣati ॥

A part of my very own self, eternal, becomes the living soul in this world of living beings; it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

हिन्दीइस जीव-लोक में जो सनातन जीवात्मा है, वह मेरा ही अंश है। वही प्रकृति में स्थित मन सहित छह इंद्रियों (पाँच इंद्रियाँ और छठा मन) को अपनी ओर खींचती है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

You are a part of something much larger, gathering a mind and senses around you for a while. On a lonely day, that can feel less like smallness and more like belonging.

Krishna says that the eternal living soul in this world is a part of him, and that it draws around itself the mind and the five senses, which belong to Prakriti.

The key word is aṃśa, a 'portion' or 'part'; it follows his description of the supreme abode from which none return. Some traditions read this as a real part, distinct yet dependent, others as only an apparent division of the one. Plainly, each life is linked to its source.
