Bhagavad Gita 15.12

Krishna

यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् ।
यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम् ॥ १५.१२ ॥

yadādityagataṃ tejo jagadbhāsayate'khilam ।
yaccandramasi yaccāgnau tattejo viddhi māmakam ॥

The radiance in the sun that lights up the whole world, the radiance in the moon and in fire, know that radiance to be mine.

हिन्दीसूर्य में जो तेज है और जो पूरे जगत को प्रकाशित करता है, और जो तेज चंद्रमा और अग्नि में है, उस तेज को मेरा ही जानो।

Krishna says that the light of the sun, the moon and fire, which lets us see the whole world, is his own radiance.

The key word is tejas, brilliance or glowing energy. Earlier he said his highest abode needs no sun, moon or fire to light it (15.6); here those same three lights turn out to be his. Put simply, the ordinary brightness we live by every day is offered as a place to notice him.
