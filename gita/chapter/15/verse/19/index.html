Bhagavad Gita 15.19

Krishna

यो मामेवमसंमूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् ।
स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत ॥ १५.१९ ॥

yo māmevamasaṃmūḍho jānāti puruṣottamam ।
sa sarvavidbhajati māṃ sarvabhāvena bhārata ॥

Whoever, free of confusion, knows me in this way as the Supreme Person knows everything, Bharata, and worships me with their whole being.

हिन्दीहे भारत, जो मोह से रहित होकर मुझे इस प्रकार पुरुषोत्तम के रूप में जानता है, वह सब कुछ जानने वाला है और पूरे भाव से मेरी भक्ति करता है।

Krishna says that whoever knows him clearly as the Supreme Person knows everything and worships him with their whole being.

Asaṃmūḍha means 'unconfused', free of the muddle that mistakes the changing for the whole. Sarvabhāvena, 'with every feeling', describes devotion that holds nothing back. Knowing and loving are joined here: clear understanding is presented as leading naturally into wholehearted devotion, not as a rival to it.
