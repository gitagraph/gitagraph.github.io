Bhagavad Gita 15.15

Krishna

सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि संनिविष्टो ।
मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च ।
वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो ।
वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥ १५.१५ ॥

sarvasya cāhaṃ hṛdi saṃniviṣṭo ।
mattaḥ smṛtirjñānamapohanaṃ ca ।
vedaiśca sarvairahameva vedyo ।
vedāntakṛdvedavideva cāham ॥

I am seated in the heart of everyone; from me come memory, knowledge and their loss. I alone am what all the Vedas seek to make known; I am the author of Vedanta and the knower of the Vedas.

हिन्दीमैं सबके हृदय में बसा हूँ; मुझसे ही स्मृति, ज्ञान और उनका लोप होता है। सारे वेदों से जानने योग्य मैं ही हूँ; वेदांत का रचयिता और वेदों का ज्ञाता भी मैं ही हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When an ageing parent starts forgetting names, meet it with patience rather than frustration. The Gita places both remembering and forgetting in a power far larger than any of us.

Krishna says he dwells in every heart, that memory, knowledge and their loss come from him, and that he is what all the Vedas aim to make known.

Apohana means taking away, here the fading or forgetting of what was known. Vedāntakṛt is the 'maker of Vedanta', the Veda's culmination. The verse places him inside the knower as well as in what is known, closing his account of how he is present in the world.
