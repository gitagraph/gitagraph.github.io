Bhagavad Gita 15.9

Krishna

श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च ।
अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते ॥ १५.९ ॥

śrotraṃ cakṣuḥ sparśanaṃ ca rasanaṃ ghrāṇameva ca ।
adhiṣṭhāya manaścāyaṃ viṣayānupasevate ॥

Presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste and smell, and over the mind as well, it enjoys the objects of the senses.

हिन्दीकान, आँख, त्वचा, जीभ और नाक, और साथ ही मन का सहारा लेकर यह जीव विषयों का भोग करता है।

In your life

Enjoying music, a good meal or a cool breeze is how the self meets the world through the senses. Enjoy them fully, but remember who is doing the enjoying.

Krishna explains that the self living in the body experiences the world by presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste, smell and the mind.

The key word is adhiṣṭhāya, 'standing over' or 'taking charge of': the senses are instruments, and the self works through them as a person uses tools. It also sets up the next verse, which says that although the self is present in all this experience, the deluded do not see it.
