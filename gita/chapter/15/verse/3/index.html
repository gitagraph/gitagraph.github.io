Bhagavad Gita 15.3

Krishna

न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते ।
नान्तो न चादिर्न च संप्रतिष्ठा ।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूल- ।
मसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा ॥ १५.३ ॥

na rūpamasyeha tathopalabhyate ।
nānto na cādirna ca saṃpratiṣṭhā ।
aśvatthamenaṃ suvirūḍhamūla- ।
masaṅgaśastreṇa dṛḍhena chittvā ॥

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

After a friendship turns sour, you needn't erase the past or hate the person. Cutting the tie can be as simple, and as hard, as no longer replaying the last argument every night.

Krishna says the tree of worldly existence cannot be seen as it truly is from within — neither its end, nor its beginning, nor what holds it up — and that it must be cut down with the strong axe of asaṅga, non-attachment.

The tree has no visible edges because we stand inside it. Cutting it does not mean destroying the world; it means severing the ties that bind us to it. The sentence completes in 15.4.
