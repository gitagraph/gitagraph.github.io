Bhagavad Gita 15.5

Krishna

निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा ।
अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः ।
द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञै- ।
र्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत् ॥ १५.५ ॥

nirmānamohā jitasaṅgadoṣā ।
adhyātmanityā vinivṛttakāmāḥ ।
dvandvairvimuktāḥ sukhaduḥkhasaṃjñai- ।
rgacchantyamūḍhāḥ padamavyayaṃ tat ॥

Free of pride and delusion, having overcome the flaw of attachment, always rooted in the Self, with desires fallen away and released from the pairs of opposites called pleasure and pain, the undeluded reach that undying state.

हिन्दीजो मान और मोह से मुक्त हैं, जिन्होंने आसक्ति के दोष को जीत लिया है, जो सदा आत्मा में स्थित रहते हैं, जिनकी कामनाएँ मिट चुकी हैं और जो सुख-दुःख नाम के द्वंद्वों से छूट गए हैं, ऐसे ज्ञानी लोग उस अविनाशी पद को पाते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Starting over after losing a job, the old designation can sit on your chest like a weight. Let the pride in it fall away; beginning again is lighter without it.

Krishna lists who reaches the undying state: those free of pride and delusion, who have mastered attachment, stay rooted in the Self, have let desires fall away and are released from the pairs of opposites called pleasure and pain.

The word amūḍha, 'undeluded', sums up the list. Almost every trait here is phrased as something dropped rather than something gained, which fits the image of cutting free from the tree.
