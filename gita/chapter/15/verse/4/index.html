Bhagavad Gita 15.4

Krishna

ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं ।
यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः ।
तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये ।
यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी ॥ १५.४ ॥

tataḥ padaṃ tatparimārgitavyaṃ ।
yasmingatā na nivartanti bhūyaḥ ।
tameva cādyaṃ puruṣaṃ prapadye ।
yataḥ pravṛttiḥ prasṛtā purāṇī ॥

…one should then seek out that state from which those who reach it never return, thinking: 'I take refuge in that primal Person from whom this ancient flow of creation has streamed forth.'

हिन्दी…फिर उस पद की खोज करनी चाहिए, जहाँ पहुँचकर लोग फिर लौटकर नहीं आते, और यह भाव रखना चाहिए: 'मैं उसी आदि पुरुष की शरण लेता हूँ, जिससे यह प्राचीन सृष्टि-प्रवाह निकला है।'

In your life

Once you've dropped a habit that held you, a shopping spree or an old grudge, turn towards something steadier to fill the space. A space left empty tends to call the old pull back.

After the tree of worldly existence is cut down, Krishna says, one should seek the state from which there is no return, in the spirit of taking refuge in the primal Person from whom creation has flowed.

The word parimārgitavya means 'to be searched out', as one tracks down a path. The verse pairs two moves, cutting and seeking: letting go of attachment and turning toward the source of the tree itself.
