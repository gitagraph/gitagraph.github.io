Bhagavad Gita 15.11

Krishna

यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् ।
यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः ॥ १५.११ ॥

yatanto yoginaścainaṃ paśyantyātmanyavasthitam ।
yatanto'pyakṛtātmāno nainaṃ paśyantyacetasaḥ ॥

Yogis who strive see it dwelling within themselves. But those whose minds are unrefined and lack understanding do not see it, even when they make the effort.

हिन्दीप्रयत्न करने वाले योगी इसे अपने भीतर स्थित देखते हैं। पर जिनका अंतःकरण शुद्ध नहीं है और जिनमें विवेक नहीं है, वे प्रयत्न करके भी इसे नहीं देख पाते।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Two colleagues sit through the same leadership course; one changes, one doesn't. Effort counts, but so does arriving prepared: a rested body, a settled mind and a real wish to learn.

Krishna says that striving yogis see the self dwelling within them, but those whose minds are unrefined fail to see it even when they try.

The contrast hinges on akṛtātman, 'one whose self is unformed', an inner life not yet shaped by discipline. Effort alone is not enough; the instrument doing the looking has to be prepared. For a reader, it explains why the same practice can bear fruit for one person and not another.
