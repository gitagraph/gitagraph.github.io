Bhagavad Gita 5.7

Krishna

योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते ॥ ५.७ ॥

yogayukto viśuddhātmā vijitātmā jitendriyaḥ ।
sarvabhūtātmabhūtātmā kurvannapi na lipyate ॥

Steady in yoga, pure in heart, master of mind and senses, whose self has become the self of all beings, such a person is not stained even while acting.

हिन्दीजो योग में स्थित है, जिसका मन शुद्ध है, जिसने अपने मन और इंद्रियों को जीत लिया है और जिसकी आत्मा सब प्राणियों की आत्मा बन गई है, वह कर्म करते हुए भी लिप्त नहीं होता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a competitive office you can still work hard without turning bitter. When a colleague's success feels a little like your own, the rivalry stops leaving its mark on you.

Krishna describes the person steady in yoga who is not stained even while acting: pure within, master of mind and senses, and one whose self has become the self of all beings.

The key compound is sarva-bhūta-ātma-bhūta-ātmā: such a person experiences other lives as continuous with their own. Lipyate, 'is smeared', pictures action leaving a residue; for someone like this, action no longer sticks.
