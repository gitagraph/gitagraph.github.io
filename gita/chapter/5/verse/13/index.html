Bhagavad Gita 5.13

Krishna

सर्वकर्माणि मनसा संन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी ।
नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन् ॥ ५.१३ ॥

sarvakarmāṇi manasā saṃnyasyāste sukhaṃ vaśī ।
navadvāre pure dehī naiva kurvanna kārayan ॥

Having given up all actions with the mind, the self-controlled embodied one dwells at ease in the city of nine gates, neither acting nor causing anything to be done.

हिन्दीमन से सब कर्मों का त्याग करके, अपने को वश में रखने वाला देहधारी नौ द्वारों वाले शरीर रूपी नगर में सुख से रहता है; वह न कुछ करता है और न करवाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

At the end of a long workday, let the office go in your mind before you enter the house. Sitting at ease in your own body, you needn't keep carrying what's already done.

Krishna pictures the body as a city of nine gates in which the embodied one, dehī, lives at ease, having given up all actions 'with the mind'.

Traditionally, the nine gates are the two eyes, two ears, two nostrils, the mouth and the two lower openings. The renunciation here is inner: actions continue, but the claim to be their doer is dropped. In that sense such a person neither acts nor makes others act.
