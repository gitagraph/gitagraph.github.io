Bhagavad Gita 5.28

Krishna

यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥ ५.२८ ॥

yatendriyamanobuddhirmunirmokṣaparāyaṇaḥ ।
vigatecchābhayakrodho yaḥ sadā mukta eva saḥ ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 5.27

…the sage who has mastered the senses, mind and intellect, whose highest aim is liberation, and who is free of longing, fear and anger, is truly free forever.

हिन्दी…जिसने इंद्रियों, मन और बुद्धि को वश में कर लिया है, जिसका परम लक्ष्य मोक्ष है और जो इच्छा, भय और क्रोध से मुक्त है, वह मुनि सदा मुक्त ही है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When fear and anger about a job loss start to fade and you can look at your options calmly, you're freer than you were, even before the new job comes.

Completing the meditation picture begun in 5.27, Krishna says the muni, the sage, who has mastered senses, mind and intellect, who holds mokṣa, liberation, as the highest aim, and who is free of desire, fear and anger, is free forever.

The word sadā, 'always', matters: liberation is described not as an event waiting at the end but as such a person's present condition. Desire, fear and anger name what keeps a mind unsettled.
