Bhagavad Gita 5.16

Krishna

ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः ।
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम् ॥ ५.१६ ॥

jñānena tu tadajñānaṃ yeṣāṃ nāśitamātmanaḥ ।
teṣāmādityavajjñānaṃ prakāśayati tatparam ॥

But for those whose ignorance has been destroyed by knowledge of the Self, that knowledge shines like the sun and reveals the Supreme.

हिन्दीपरंतु जिनका वह अज्ञान आत्मज्ञान से नष्ट हो गया है, उनका ज्ञान सूर्य की तरह उस परम तत्त्व को प्रकाशित कर देता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Sometimes a single honest conversation with an old friend lights up years of confusion about yourself, like sunrise over a dark room. Make space for those conversations.

Krishna turns to the cure for the ignorance named in 5.15: once knowledge of the Self has destroyed it, that knowledge shines ādityavat, 'like the sun', and reveals the highest reality.

The image fits, since the sun does not make what it shows; it only lights up what was already there. In the same way, the verse suggests, the highest truth is not something made but something uncovered once the covering is removed.
