Chapter 5 · Renunciation of ActionVerse 20 of 29

Bhagavad Gita 5.20

Krishna

न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।
स्थिरबुद्धिरसंमूढो ब्रह्मविद्ब्रह्मणि स्थितः ॥ ५.२० ॥

na prahṛṣyetpriyaṃ prāpya nodvijetprāpya cāpriyam ।
sthirabuddhirasaṃmūḍho brahmavidbrahmaṇi sthitaḥ ॥

With steady understanding and free of delusion, the knower of Brahman, established in Brahman, does not rejoice on getting what is pleasant or recoil on meeting what is unpleasant.

हिन्दीस्थिर बुद्धि वाला, मोह से रहित, ब्रह्म को जानने वाला और ब्रह्म में स्थित व्यक्ति प्रिय वस्तु पाकर न फूलता है और अप्रिय पाकर न घबराता है।

Krishna describes the knower of Brahman in everyday terms: such a person neither leaps with joy at what is pleasant nor recoils from what is unpleasant.

The key word is sthirabuddhi, steady understanding, which echoes the portrait of the steady-minded sage in 2.55–72. This is not an absence of feeling but a balance that neither good news nor bad can tip over, because one's footing no longer rests on events themselves.
