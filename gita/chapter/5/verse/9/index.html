Bhagavad Gita 5.9

Krishna

प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन् ॥ ५.९ ॥

pralapanvisṛjangṛhṇannunmiṣannimiṣannapi ।
indriyāṇīndriyārtheṣu vartanta iti dhārayan ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 5.8

…while speaking, letting go, taking hold, opening and closing the eyes, holding firmly that it is only the senses moving among their objects.

हिन्दी…बोलते, छोड़ते, ग्रहण करते, आँखें खोलते और बंद करते हुए भी वह यह निश्चय रखता है कि केवल इंद्रियाँ ही अपने विषयों में बरत रही हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a sharp retort rises in a tense meeting, watch it as the senses and mind reacting rather than as an order from you. That small distance makes the reply your own again.

Finishing the thought begun in 5.8, Krishna explains how the knower can say 'I do nothing' while still talking, releasing, grasping and blinking.

The key phrase is indriyāṇi indriyārtheṣu vartante, 'the senses move among their objects': activity is seen as the faculties doing their natural work. Plainly, this is a shift in where one stands, watching the busy workings of body and senses rather than identifying with each of their motions.
