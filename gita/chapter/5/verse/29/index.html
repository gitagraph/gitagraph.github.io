Bhagavad Gita 5.29

Krishna

भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् ।
सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥ ५.२९ ॥

bhoktāraṃ yajñatapasāṃ sarvalokamaheśvaram ।
suhṛdaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ jñātvā māṃ śāntimṛcchati ॥

Knowing me as the one who receives all sacrifice and austerity, the great Lord of all the worlds and the friend of every being, one attains peace.

हिन्दीमुझे सब यज्ञों और तपों का भोक्ता, सब लोकों का महान ईश्वर और सब प्राणियों का सच्चा मित्र जानकर व्यक्ति शांति को प्राप्त होता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

On a lonely evening in a new city, it can help to remember that whatever you hold highest is called here the friend of every being, and that includes you.

Closing chapter 5, Krishna speaks of himself in the first person: one who knows him as the receiver of sacrifice and austerity, the great Lord of all the worlds and suhṛd, the friend of every being, attains peace.

After many verses on Brahman and inner discipline, the chapter ends on a personal note. Suhṛd means a good-hearted well-wisher, so the highest reality is pictured here as close and kindly rather than distant or frightening.
