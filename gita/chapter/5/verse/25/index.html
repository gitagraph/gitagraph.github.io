Bhagavad Gita 5.25

Krishna

लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।
छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ ५.२५ ॥

labhante brahmanirvāṇamṛṣayaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣāḥ ।
chinnadvaidhā yatātmānaḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ ॥

Seers whose faults are worn away, whose doubts are cut, who are self-controlled and devoted to the welfare of all beings, attain the peace of Brahman.

हिन्दीजिनके पाप नष्ट हो गए हैं, जिनके संशय कट गए हैं, जिन्होंने मन को वश में कर लिया है और जो सब प्राणियों के हित में लगे रहते हैं, वे ऋषि ब्रह्मनिर्वाण पाते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

People who spend their lives caring for others, a nurse, a village teacher, a social worker, often carry a peace you can see. Working for everyone's welfare is part of how it comes.

Krishna names the seers, ṛṣayaḥ, who attain the peace of Brahman: their faults are worn away, their doubts cut through, their minds self-controlled, and they delight in the welfare of all beings.

The phrase sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ stands out, since inner freedom is pictured not as withdrawal from others but as going hand in hand with care for every creature. Chinnadvaidha, literally 'with duality cut', is read as doubts settled or as the sense of division removed.
