Bhagavad Gita 5.19

Krishna

इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः ॥ ५.१९ ॥

ihaiva tairjitaḥ sargo yeṣāṃ sāmye sthitaṃ manaḥ ।
nirdoṣaṃ hi samaṃ brahma tasmādbrahmaṇi te sthitāḥ ॥

Those whose mind rests in equality have conquered the cycle of birth here and now. Brahman is flawless and the same in all, so they are established in Brahman.

हिन्दीजिनका मन समता में स्थित है, उन्होंने यहीं इसी जीवन में संसार को जीत लिया है। ब्रह्म निर्दोष और सबमें समान है, इसलिए वे ब्रह्म में ही स्थित हैं।

In your life

If you can sit in the same office with people you agree and disagree with and keep an even mind, freedom isn't far off. It begins right here, in how you hold people.

Krishna says that those whose minds rest in sāmya, evenness, have overcome sarga, the round of birth and becoming, here and now rather than after death.

The reasoning is brief: Brahman itself is flawless and the same in all, so a mind that has become even is already settled in Brahman. Read simply, freedom is described as a present condition of the mind, reached within this life and not only as a prize after it.
