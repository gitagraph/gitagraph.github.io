Bhagavad Gita 5.15

Krishna

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः ।
अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः ॥ ५.१५ ॥

nādatte kasyacitpāpaṃ na caiva sukṛtaṃ vibhuḥ ।
ajñānenāvṛtaṃ jñānaṃ tena muhyanti jantavaḥ ॥

The all-pervading one takes on no one's sin and no one's good deed either. Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and that is why beings are deluded.

हिन्दीसर्वव्यापी परमात्मा न किसी का पाप लेता है और न किसी का पुण्य। अज्ञान ने ज्ञान को ढक रखा है, इसी से सब जीव मोह में पड़े रहते हैं।

In your life

When a friend can't see what's obvious about a bad relationship, it is often because something is covering it, not because they are foolish. Be patient with what ignorance hides.

Krishna adds that vibhu, the all-pervading one, takes on neither anyone's sin nor anyone's good deed.

Why, then, do beings feel so caught up in merit and blame? Because jñāna, knowledge, is covered by ajñāna, ignorance, and that confusion makes them identify with what they do. The verse treats the problem as one of seeing wrongly, which is why the next verse turns to knowledge as the remedy.
