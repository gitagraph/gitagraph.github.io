In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Before a difficult conversation, take a minute: shut out the noise around you, sit still, and let your breath settle into an even rhythm before you begin to speak.

Krishna sketches the first steps of meditation: outer contacts are 'put outside', the gaze is held between the eyebrows, and prāṇa and apāna, the in-breath and out-breath, are made even as they move within the nostrils.

The sentence is completed in 5.28, and chapter 6 develops the practice in detail. Plainly, this describes settling the senses, the attention and the breath before the inner work of 5.28 can begin.
