Bhagavad Gita 5.18

Krishna

विद्याविनयसंपन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥ ५.१८ ॥

vidyāvinayasaṃpanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini ।
śuni caiva śvapāke ca paṇḍitāḥ samadarśinaḥ ॥

The wise look with an equal eye on a learned and humble Brahmin, a cow, an elephant, a dog and an outcaste who eats dogs.

हिन्दीज्ञानीजन विद्या और विनय से युक्त ब्राह्मण, गाय, हाथी, कुत्ते और चांडाल — सबमें समान दृष्टि रखते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

At a wedding, notice whether you greet the senior officer warmly and barely look at the people serving the food. The wise give everyone in the room the same regard.

Krishna gives a concrete test of wisdom: the wise, paṇḍitāḥ, look with an equal eye on a learned and humble Brahmin, a cow, an elephant, a dog and a śvapāka, an outcaste, literally 'one who cooks dogs'.

The list deliberately runs from the most honoured to the most shunned figures of that society. Samadarśin, 'equal-seeing', does not mean pretending they are all alike; it means seeing the same reality in each and not ranking their worth.
