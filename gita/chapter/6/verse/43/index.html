Bhagavad Gita 6.43

Krishna

तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन ॥ ६.४३ ॥

tatra taṃ buddhisaṃyogaṃ labhate paurvadehikam ।
yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana ॥

There he regains the understanding he had developed in his former body, and from that point, joy of the Kurus, he strives again, even harder, for perfection.

हिन्दीहे कुरुनंदन, वहाँ वह पिछले शरीर में पाई हुई बुद्धि के संस्कार को फिर पा लेता है, और फिर सिद्धि के लिए पहले से अधिक प्रयत्न करता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Going back to a language or a practice you dropped years ago, you'll often find more of it returns than you expected. Pick it up from there and strive again, harder.

Krishna explains why the new birth matters: there the seeker regains the understanding developed in the former body and strives again, even harder, for perfection.

The key phrase is buddhi-saṃyoga paurvadehika, 'the link with understanding from the previous body'. Addressing Arjuna as Kurunandana, 'joy of the Kurus', Krishna is saying that progress is not reset; the seeker resumes roughly where he left off.
