Bhagavad Gita 6.14

Krishna

प्रशान्तात्मा विगतभीर्ब्रह्मचारिव्रते स्थितः ।
मनः संयम्य मच्चित्तो युक्त आसीत मत्परः ॥ ६.१४ ॥

praśāntātmā vigatabhīrbrahmacārivrate sthitaḥ ।
manaḥ saṃyamya maccitto yukta āsīta matparaḥ ॥

Calm within, free of fear, firm in the vow of celibacy, restraining the mind and fixing his thought on me, he should sit joined in yoga, with me as his highest goal.

हिन्दीभीतर से शांत, भयरहित और ब्रह्मचर्य के व्रत में दृढ़ रहकर, मन को संयमित करके और मुझमें चित्त लगाकर, मुझे ही परम लक्ष्य मानता हुआ योगयुक्त होकर बैठे।

Continuing his instructions on how to sit for meditation, Krishna describes the inner state the meditator should bring to the seat: calm, fearless, disciplined, and turned toward him.

Two words carry the verse: maccitta, 'with thought resting on me', and matpara, 'with me as the highest aim'. Posture alone is not the practice here; for a reader today, it suggests that sitting still settles best when attention also has somewhere it truly wants to rest.
