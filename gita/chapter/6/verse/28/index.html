Bhagavad Gita 6.28

Krishna

युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः ।
सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमश्नुते ॥ ६.२८ ॥

yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī vigatakalmaṣaḥ ।
sukhena brahmasaṃsparśamatyantaṃ sukhamaśnute ॥

Constantly joining himself in this way, the yogi, freed of impurity, easily enjoys the boundless happiness of contact with Brahman.

हिन्दीइस प्रकार निरंतर अपने को योग में लगाता हुआ निष्पाप योगी सहज ही ब्रह्म के स्पर्श का असीम सुख भोगता है।

Krishna restates the fruit of steady practice: the yogi, freed of impurity, easily enjoys the boundless happiness of brahma-saṃsparśa, 'contact with Brahman'.

The word sukhena, 'easily', is notable; what began as effort in the earlier verses now happens without strain. The verse closes the description of seated meditation, suggesting that discipline, practised long enough, turns into something natural rather than forced.
