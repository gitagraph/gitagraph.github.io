Bhagavad Gita 6.34

Arjuna

चञ्चलं हि मनः कृष्ण प्रमाथि बलवद्दृढम् ।
तस्याहं निग्रहं मन्ये वायोरिव सुदुष्करम् ॥ ६.३४ ॥

cañcalaṃ hi manaḥ kṛṣṇa pramāthi balavaddṛḍham ।
tasyāhaṃ nigrahaṃ manye vāyoriva suduṣkaram ॥

For the mind is restless, Krishna — turbulent, strong and stubborn. Restraining it seems to me as hard as restraining the wind.

हिन्दीहे कृष्ण, मन बड़ा चंचल है, मथ डालने वाला, बलवान और हठी है। इसे वश में करना मुझे वायु को रोकने जितना कठिन लगता है।

In your life

If you've tried to calm your mind and felt it was like holding the wind, you're in good company; even Arjuna said so. Admitting it is hard is a fair place to start.

Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless, turbulent, strong and stubborn, and restraining it seems as hard as restraining the wind.

It is his reason for doubting that the yoga of evenness could ever hold steady; the striking word is pramāthi, 'churning' or 'agitating', the mind as something that stirs everything up. The verse matters because the Gita lets this honest objection stand in full before Krishna answers it, and he begins by agreeing.
