Bhagavad Gita 6.15

Krishna

युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी नियतमानसः ।
शान्तिं निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति ॥ ६.१५ ॥

yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī niyatamānasaḥ ।
śāntiṃ nirvāṇaparamāṃ matsaṃsthāmadhigacchati ॥

Constantly uniting himself in this way, with his mind under control, the yogi reaches the peace that rests in me and culminates in nirvana.

हिन्दीइस प्रकार निरंतर अपने को योग में लगाते हुए, मन को वश में रखने वाला योगी मुझमें स्थित उस शांति को पाता है जिसकी परिणति निर्वाण है।

Krishna names where this steady practice finally leads: a peace that rests in him and culminates in nirvāṇa.

Nirvāṇa, literally 'blowing out', is used here for the complete stilling of restlessness, and the Gita places that stillness in Krishna himself. Plainly, the verse says meditation is not an end in itself or a brief relief; what it aims at is a lasting quiet that does not depend on the next session going well.
