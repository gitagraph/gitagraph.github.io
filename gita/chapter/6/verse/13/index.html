Bhagavad Gita 6.13

Krishna

समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचलं स्थिरः ।
सम्प्रेक्ष्य नासिकाग्रं स्वं दिशश्चानवलोकयन् ॥ ६.१३ ॥

samaṃ kāyaśirogrīvaṃ dhārayannacalaṃ sthiraḥ ।
samprekṣya nāsikāgraṃ svaṃ diśaścānavalokayan ॥

Holding body, head and neck straight and motionless, staying steady, he should gaze at the tip of his own nose without looking about in any direction.

हिन्दीशरीर, सिर और गर्दन को सीधा और अचल रखकर, स्थिर होकर, अपनी नाक के अग्रभाग पर दृष्टि जमाए और इधर-उधर की दिशाओं में न देखे।

Krishna describes the posture for meditation: body, head and neck held straight, still and steady, the gaze resting on the tip of one's own nose, without looking around in any direction.

The word samprekṣya can mean looking directly at the nose-tip or letting the eyes settle toward it, and readers differ on which is meant. The practical aim is plain: an upright, unmoving body and quiet eyes give a wandering mind fewer things to chase.
