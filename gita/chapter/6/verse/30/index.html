Bhagavad Gita 6.30

Krishna

यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति ।
तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति ॥ ६.३० ॥

yo māṃ paśyati sarvatra sarvaṃ ca mayi paśyati ।
tasyāhaṃ na praṇaśyāmi sa ca me na praṇaśyati ॥

Whoever sees me everywhere and sees everything in me — I am never lost to that person, and that person is never lost to me.

हिन्दीजो मुझे सब जगह देखता है और सब कुछ मुझमें देखता है, उसके लिए मैं कभी ओझल नहीं होता और वह मेरे लिए कभी ओझल नहीं होता।

Krishna now speaks of himself in the same terms as the Self of the previous verse: whoever sees him everywhere and everything in him is never lost to him, nor is he lost to that person.

The key word is praṇaśyati, 'is lost, vanishes from sight'. The bond is described as mutual: a vision that finds the divine in everything leaves no place where one is cut off from it.
