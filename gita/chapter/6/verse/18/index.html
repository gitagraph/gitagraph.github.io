Bhagavad Gita 6.18

Krishna

यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते ।
निःस्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा ॥ ६.१८ ॥

yadā viniyataṃ cittamātmanyevāvatiṣṭhate ।
niḥspṛhaḥ sarvakāmebhyo yukta ityucyate tadā ॥

When the well-disciplined mind settles in the Self alone, free of longing for any object of desire, then a person is called united in yoga.

हिन्दीजब भली-भाँति वश में किया हुआ चित्त केवल आत्मा में ही टिक जाता है और मनुष्य सभी कामनाओं की लालसा से मुक्त हो जाता है, तब वह योगयुक्त कहलाता है।

Krishna defines when a meditator can rightly be called yukta, 'joined in yoga': when the disciplined mind settles in the Self alone and no longer longs for any object of desire.

The key word is niḥspṛha, 'free of longing', describing the person, not just the moment of sitting. For a reader, the test of practice is not how long one sits but whether wanting has loosened its grip.
