Bhagavad Gita 6.5

Krishna

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः ॥ ६.५ ॥

uddharedātmanātmānaṃ nātmānamavasādayet ।
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ ॥

Let a person raise himself by his own self and not let himself sink, for the self alone is one's friend, and the self alone is one's enemy.

हिन्दीमनुष्य अपने द्वारा स्वयं का उद्धार करे, अपने को नीचे न गिरने दे; क्योंकि मनुष्य स्वयं ही अपना मित्र है और स्वयं ही अपना शत्रु।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

After months of illness that knocked your confidence, others can help, but the lifting has to come from you. Talk to yourself the way a good friend would, not an enemy, and take the next small step.

Krishna tells each person to raise themselves by their own self and not let themselves sink, for the self alone is one's friend and one's enemy.

The word ātman runs through the verse in two roles at once: the self that raises and the self that is raised, uddharet meaning 'lift up'. Read plainly, no one else can do this inner work on our behalf, and the way we treat ourselves can either steady us or drag us down.
