Bhagavad Gita 6.4

Krishna

यदा हि नेन्द्रियार्थेषु न कर्मस्वनुषज्जते ।
सर्वसंकल्पसंन्यासी योगारूढस्तदोच्यते ॥ ६.४ ॥

yadā hi nendriyārtheṣu na karmasvanuṣajjate ।
sarvasaṃkalpasaṃnyāsī yogārūḍhastadocyate ॥

When a person no longer clings to the objects of the senses or to actions, and has let go of every self-willed intention, then he is called one who has mounted to yoga.

हिन्दीजब मनुष्य न इंद्रियों के विषयों में आसक्त होता है, न कर्मों में, और सभी संकल्पों को त्याग चुका होता है, तब वह योगारूढ़ कहलाता है।

Krishna defines yogārūḍha, the one who has 'mounted to yoga' named in 6.3: it is a person who no longer clings to sense objects or to actions, and who has let go of every self-willed intention.

The marker is not how much one does or avoids, but whether the mind still hooks onto things. The verb anuṣajjate, 'gets stuck to', pictures attachment as stickiness; the person described has simply stopped sticking.
