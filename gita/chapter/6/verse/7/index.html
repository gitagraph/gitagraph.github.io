Bhagavad Gita 6.7

Krishna

जितात्मनः प्रशान्तस्य परमात्मा समाहितः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु तथा मानापमानयोः ॥ ६.७ ॥

jitātmanaḥ praśāntasya paramātmā samāhitaḥ ।
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu tathā mānāpamānayoḥ ॥

For one who has mastered himself and is deeply at peace, the supreme Self stays steady and collected amid cold and heat, pleasure and pain, honour and dishonour alike.

हिन्दीजिसने स्वयं को जीत लिया है और जो पूर्णतः शांत है, उसका परमात्मा सर्दी-गर्मी, सुख-दुःख और मान-अपमान में भी स्थिर और एकाग्र रहता है।

In your life

A stifling crowded train in summer, a freezing wait at the stop in winter, a seat offered one day and a shove the next: a settled person stays collected through all of it.

Krishna describes one who has mastered himself and is deeply at peace: in cold and heat, pleasure and pain, honour and insult, the paramātmā in such a person stays samāhita, collected and steady.

Readings differ here: some take paramātmā as the supreme Self present in him, others read param ātmā as 'his own self is supremely composed'. Either way, the picture is of a centre that the swings of comfort and reputation no longer throw off balance.
