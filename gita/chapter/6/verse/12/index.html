Bhagavad Gita 6.12

Krishna

तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः ।
उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये ॥ ६.१२ ॥

tatraikāgraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā yatacittendriyakriyaḥ ।
upaviśyāsane yuñjyādyogamātmaviśuddhaye ॥

Seated there, making the mind one-pointed and keeping the workings of thought and senses in check, he should practise yoga to purify himself.

हिन्दीउस आसन पर बैठकर, मन को एकाग्र करके और चित्त तथा इंद्रियों की क्रियाओं को वश में रखकर, आत्मशुद्धि के लिए योग का अभ्यास करे।

Krishna says that, seated on that seat, the practitioner should make the mind ekāgra, one-pointed, keep the activity of thought and senses in check, and practise yoga for ātma-viśuddhi, self-purification.

Ekāgra literally means 'having a single point', attention gathered rather than scattered. The stated aim is worth noticing: not powers or special experiences, but becoming inwardly clearer, the same aim given for unattached action in 5.11.
