Bhagavad Gita 6.9

Krishna

सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु ।
साधुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥ ६.९ ॥

suhṛnmitrāryudāsīnamadhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu ।
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu samabuddhirviśiṣyate ॥

Still higher stands the one who looks with an even mind on well-wishers, friends and foes, the indifferent and the neutral, the hateful and kinsmen, and on the good and the wicked alike.

हिन्दीजो हितैषी, मित्र, शत्रु, उदासीन, मध्यस्थ, द्वेष करने योग्य और संबंधी, तथा सज्जनों और पापियों के प्रति भी समान बुद्धि रखता है, वह श्रेष्ठ है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a family dispute, try treating the relative who backs you and the one who speaks against you with the same fairness. That evenness towards people is rarer, and higher, than calm about things.

Krishna ranks still higher the person whose samabuddhi, even-mindedness, holds toward every kind of person, not just toward stones and gold as in 6.8.

The list is fine-grained: well-wishers and friends, enemies, the indifferent and the neutral, the hostile and relatives, and then the good and the wicked. Evenness toward things is one matter; toward people who help or harm us it is far harder, which is why this stands higher.
