Bhagavad Gita 6.19

Krishna

यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता ।
योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः ॥ ६.१९ ॥

yathā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā ।
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ ॥

"Like a lamp in a windless place that does not flicker" — that is the image used for the yogi of controlled mind who practises union with the Self.

हिन्दी"जैसे वायुरहित स्थान में रखा दीपक नहीं डगमगाता" — यही उपमा उस योगी के लिए दी गई है जिसका चित्त वश में है और जो आत्मा में योग का अभ्यास करता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Waiting outside an operation theatre for someone you love, the mind flickers wildly. Shelter it like a lamp from the wind, gently, one breath or one prayer at a time.

Krishna gives the best-known image of the chapter for a settled mind: a lamp in a windless place whose flame does not flicker.

The key word is nivātastha, 'standing where there is no wind'; the flame is still alive and burning, only undisturbed. The image is quiet but not dead: a meditative mind stays awake and bright, simply no longer pushed about by every gust of thought and feeling.
