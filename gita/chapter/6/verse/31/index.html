Bhagavad Gita 6.31

Krishna

सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः ।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते ॥ ६.३१ ॥

sarvabhūtasthitaṃ yo māṃ bhajatyekatvamāsthitaḥ ।
sarvathā vartamāno'pi sa yogī mayi vartate ॥

The yogi who, grounded in oneness, worships me as dwelling in all beings, lives in me, however he may be living.

हिन्दीजो योगी एकत्व में स्थित होकर सब प्राणियों में बसे हुए मुझे भजता है, वह किसी भी प्रकार रहता हुआ मुझमें ही रहता है।

Krishna adds that the yogi who, grounded in ekatva, 'oneness', worships him as dwelling in all beings lives in him, whatever that yogi's outward way of life.

The phrase sarvathā vartamāno'pi, 'however he may be living', matters: this is not tied to a hermitage or a posture. Readings differ on what 'oneness' means here, unity of all in Krishna or identity with him, but on either reading it is a way of seeing carried into ordinary life.
