Bhagavad Gita 6.40

Krishna

पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।
न हि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति ॥ ६.४० ॥

pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśastasya vidyate ।
na hi kalyāṇakṛtkaściddurgatiṃ tāta gacchati ॥

Partha, there is no ruin for him either in this world or the next; for, my friend, no one who does good ever comes to a bad end.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, उसका न इस लोक में नाश होता है, न परलोक में; क्योंकि हे तात, भलाई करने वाला कोई भी दुर्गति को नहीं जाता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you gave years to a business, a cause or a dream that didn't work out, the good you put into it is not wasted. Sincere effort does not end in ruin.

Krishna assures Arjuna that one who sets out with faith but falls short meets no ruin in this world or the next, for no one who does good comes to a bad end.

He is answering Arjuna's fear that such a person is lost on both sides, like a scattered cloud, and calls him tāta, a tender word used for a son or a dear one. The key term is kalyāṇa-kṛt, 'one who does good': sincere effort is not cancelled because it was left unfinished.
