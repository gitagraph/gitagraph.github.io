Bhagavad Gita 6.37

Arjuna

अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः ।
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति ॥ ६.३७ ॥

ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāccalitamānasaḥ ।
aprāpya yogasaṃsiddhiṃ kāṃ gatiṃ kṛṣṇa gacchati ॥

Krishna, what becomes of the person who has faith but lacks self-control, whose mind slips away from yoga, and who fails to reach its perfection?

हिन्दीहे कृष्ण, जो श्रद्धा से युक्त है पर संयम में ढीला है, जिसका मन योग से हट गया है और जो योग की सिद्धि नहीं पा सका, वह किस गति को जाता है?

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you started a good practice with faith and then slipped, it's natural to wonder whether all of it was wasted. Ask the question honestly; the Gita takes it seriously too.

Arjuna raises a new worry: what becomes of someone who has faith but lacks self-control, whose mind slips away from yoga before reaching its perfection?

The key word is ayati, taken here as 'lacking self-control', though some read it as 'not striving enough'. The question is a very human one, about the sincere beginner who falls short, and it sets up Krishna's reassurance in the verses that follow.
