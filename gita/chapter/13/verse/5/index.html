Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 5 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.5

Krishna

महाभूतान्यहंकारो बुद्धिरव्यक्तमेव च ।
इन्द्रियाणि दशैकं च पञ्च चेन्द्रियगोचराः ॥ १३.५ ॥

mahābhūtānyahaṃkāro buddhiravyaktameva ca ।
indriyāṇi daśaikaṃ ca pañca cendriyagocarāḥ ॥

The great elements, the ego-sense, the intellect and the unmanifest; the ten senses and the one mind, and the five domains of the senses…

हिन्दीपाँच महाभूत, अहंकार, बुद्धि और अव्यक्त प्रकृति; दस इंद्रियाँ और एक मन, तथा इंद्रियों के पाँच विषय…

Krishna begins listing what makes up the field, starting with the basic building blocks of the person and the world; the list continues in 13.6.

Traditionally it is matched with the Sāṅkhya scheme: the unmanifest source (avyakta), intellect (buddhi), the sense of 'I' (ahaṃkāra), the great elements, ten senses of knowing and acting, the mind, and the five sense objects. Notably, mind and intellect are counted in the field, among things that are known, not as the knower.
