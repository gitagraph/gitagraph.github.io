Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 1 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.1

Krishna

इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।
एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः ॥ १३.१ ॥

idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetramityabhidhīyate ।
etadyo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tadvidaḥ ॥

This body, Kaunteya, is called the field; and the one who knows it is called the knower of the field by those who understand these matters.

हिन्दीहे कौंतेय, यह शरीर 'क्षेत्र' कहलाता है; और जो इसे जानता है, उसे इस विषय के जानकार 'क्षेत्रज्ञ' कहते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When an injury keeps you off your feet for weeks, notice that you are the one noticing. The body changes like a field through the seasons; the one who knows it is watching.

Krishna calls the body the kṣetra, the 'field', and the one who knows it the kṣetrajña, the 'knower of the field', opening a new theme: what in us is known, and what knows.

The 'field' here means the body with all that happens in it. In plain words, the body and its changing states are things we can observe, which already suggests that the observer is something other than them; the next verse says the divine is the knower in every field.
