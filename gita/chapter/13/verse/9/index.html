Bhagavad Gita 13.9

Krishna

असक्तिरनभिष्वङ्गः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु ।
नित्यं च समचित्तत्वमिष्टानिष्टोपपत्तिषु ॥ १३.९ ॥

asaktiranabhiṣvaṅgaḥ putradāragṛhādiṣu ।
nityaṃ ca samacittatvamiṣṭāniṣṭopapattiṣu ॥

…non-attachment, not being entangled with son, wife, home and the like, and constant evenness of mind whether what is wanted or unwanted comes about…

हिन्दी…अनासक्ति, पुत्र, पत्नी, घर आदि में लिपटे न रहना, और प्रिय या अप्रिय, जो भी आ पड़े, उसमें सदा मन का समान रहना…

In your life

Love your children fully, but don't tie your peace to their every choice. When they move to another city for college, you can miss them and still stay steady.

Krishna adds to the qualities of knowledge non-attachment, not being entangled with son, wife and home, and constant evenness of mind whether what is wanted or unwanted comes.

Two words are set side by side: asakti, not being attached, and anabhiṣvaṅga, not being wrapped up in something as in a tight embrace. For a reader living with family, the verse need not mean leaving them; it asks that love not become a grip on which one's whole identity depends.
