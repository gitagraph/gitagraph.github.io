Bhagavad Gita 13.22

Krishna

उपद्रष्टानुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः ।
परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः ॥ १३.२२ ॥

upadraṣṭānumantā ca bhartā bhoktā maheśvaraḥ ।
paramātmeti cāpyukto dehe'sminpuruṣaḥ paraḥ ॥

The supreme Purusha in this body is also called the witness, the one who consents, the sustainer, the experiencer, the great Lord and the supreme Self.

हिन्दीइस शरीर में जो परम पुरुष है, उसे साक्षी, अनुमति देने वाला, पालन करने वाला, भोक्ता, महेश्वर और परमात्मा भी कहा जाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Somewhere in you is a witness that watches you scold, laugh and worry without being any of them. On an angry day, step back into that witness for a moment before you speak.

Krishna says the supreme Purusha in this body is also called witness, consenter, sustainer, experiencer, great Lord and supreme Self.

The key words are upadraṣṭā, 'the one who watches from close by', and anumantā, 'the one who gives assent', pointing beyond the Purusha who, living in Prakriti, experiences the gunas. Some traditions take this supreme Purusha as the individual self, others as the divine Lord beside it. Either way, it is a witnessing presence not caught up in what it sees.
