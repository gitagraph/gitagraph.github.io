Verse 13.3

Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 3 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.3

Krishna

तत्क्षेत्रं यच्च यादृक्च यद्विकारि यतश्च यत् ।
स च यो यत्प्रभावश्च तत्समासेन मे शृणु ॥ १३.३ ॥

tatkṣetraṃ yacca yādṛkca yadvikāri yataśca yat ।
sa ca yo yatprabhāvaśca tatsamāsena me śṛṇu ॥

Hear from me in brief what that field is, what it is like, what changes it undergoes and where each thing in it comes from; and who its knower is and what his powers are.

हिन्दीवह क्षेत्र क्या है, कैसा है, उसमें कौन-से विकार होते हैं और कौन-सी चीज़ किससे उत्पन्न होती है; और वह क्षेत्रज्ञ कौन है और उसका क्या प्रभाव है — यह संक्षेप में मुझसे सुनो।

Krishna sets out the plan of his teaching on the field and its knower, promising a brief account of what the field is, its nature, its changes and their sources, and who the knower is and what power it has.

The word vikāra, 'modification', matters: the field is defined by change, while the chapter will describe its knower as untouched by it. Read as an outline, the verse works like a table of contents for what follows.
