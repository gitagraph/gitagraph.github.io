Bhagavad Gita 13.29

Krishna

प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वशः ।
यः पश्यति तथात्मानमकर्तारं स पश्यति ॥ १३.२९ ॥

prakṛtyaiva ca karmāṇi kriyamāṇāni sarvaśaḥ ।
yaḥ paśyati tathātmānamakartāraṃ sa paśyati ॥

One who sees that all actions are carried out entirely by Prakriti alone, and that the Self is not the doer, truly sees.

हिन्दीजो देखता है कि सारे कर्म हर प्रकार से केवल प्रकृति द्वारा ही किए जा रहे हैं, और आत्मा कर्ता नहीं है — वही सच में देखता है।

In your life

When a project you led is cancelled, own the lessons and fix what you can, but don't let 'it failed' harden into 'I am a failure'. Many forces did the work; you are not the sum of the result.

Krishna says that whoever sees all actions as carried out entirely by Prakriti, and the Self as akartā, not the doer, truly sees.

This follows from 13.20, where agency was placed with nature. It does not mean that deeds stop mattering: actions still happen and still have their effects. What drops away is the anxious, possessive 'I did this', which is where, in the Gita's account, bondage takes hold.
