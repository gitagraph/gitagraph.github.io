Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 34 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.34

Krishna

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा ।
भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम् ॥ १३.३४ ॥

kṣetrakṣetrajñayorevamantaraṃ jñānacakṣuṣā ।
bhūtaprakṛtimokṣaṃ ca ye viduryānti te param ॥

Those who see with the eye of knowledge the difference between the field and its knower, and how beings are freed from Prakriti, reach the Supreme.

हिन्दीजो ज्ञान की आँख से क्षेत्र और क्षेत्रज्ञ के अंतर को, और प्राणियों के प्रकृति से छूटने के उपाय को जान लेते हैं, वे परम को प्राप्त होते हैं।

Krishna closes chapter 13 by saying that those who see, with the eye of knowledge, the difference between the field and its knower, and how beings are freed from Prakriti, reach the Supreme.

Jñāna-cakṣus, the eye of knowledge, is a way of seeing rather than a fact learned. The whole chapter has been building toward this one discernment: telling apart what is known from the one who knows. Release, in this view, begins exactly there.
