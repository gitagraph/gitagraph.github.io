Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 30 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.30

Krishna

यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपश्यति ।
तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म संपद्यते तदा ॥ १३.३० ॥

yadā bhūtapṛthagbhāvamekasthamanupaśyati ।
tata eva ca vistāraṃ brahma saṃpadyate tadā ॥

When one perceives the separate existence of beings as resting in the One, and their spreading out as coming from that alone, one then attains Brahman.

हिन्दीजब मनुष्य प्राणियों के अलग-अलग अस्तित्व को एक ही में स्थित देखता है, और उसी एक से उनका विस्तार होते देखता है, तब वह ब्रह्म को प्राप्त हो जाता है।

Krishna says that a person attains Brahman when they see the many separate beings as resting in the One and spreading out from it alone.

Two movements are held together here: pṛthag-bhāva, beings as separate, and vistāra, their unfolding from a single source. The verse does not deny that there are many beings; it changes how the many are seen, so that difference no longer hides the unity behind it.
