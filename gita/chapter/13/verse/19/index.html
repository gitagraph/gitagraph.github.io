Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 19 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.19

Krishna

प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव विद्ध्यनादी उभावपि ।
विकारांश्च गुणांश्चैव विद्धि प्रकृतिसंभवान् ॥ १३.१९ ॥

prakṛtiṃ puruṣaṃ caiva viddhyanādī ubhāvapi ।
vikārāṃśca guṇāṃścaiva viddhi prakṛtisaṃbhavān ॥

Know that Prakriti and Purusha are both without beginning, and know that the changes of form and the gunas arise from Prakriti.

हिन्दीप्रकृति और पुरुष, दोनों को अनादि जानो; और यह भी जानो कि सारे विकार और गुण प्रकृति से उत्पन्न होते हैं।

Krishna restates the field and its knower in new terms, Prakriti and Purusha, and says that both are without beginning.

Prakriti is nature, the source of every vikāra, change of form, and of the three gunas; Purusha is the conscious principle. The pair is also central to Sankhya philosophy, though the Gita uses it in its own way. Calling both beginningless means that neither was produced from the other.
