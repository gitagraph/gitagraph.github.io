Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 6 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.6

Krishna

इच्छा द्वेषः सुखं दुःखं संघातश्चेतना धृतिः ।
एतत्क्षेत्रं समासेन सविकारमुदाहृतम् ॥ १३.६ ॥

icchā dveṣaḥ sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ saṃghātaścetanā dhṛtiḥ ।
etatkṣetraṃ samāsena savikāramudāhṛtam ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 13.5

…desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the bodily aggregate, awareness and steadfastness — this, together with its changes, is the field described in brief.

हिन्दी…इच्छा, द्वेष, सुख, दुःख, शरीर का समूह, चेतना और धैर्य — यही अपने विकारों सहित संक्षेप में बताया गया क्षेत्र है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Envy at a friend's good news arises, grows and fades. Seeing it as something happening in you, not as you, makes it easier to watch without obeying.

Krishna ends his list of what makes up the field with inner states such as desire, aversion, pleasure and pain, and calls the whole 'the field with its changes'.

What stands out is that wanting, disliking and even cetanā, the awareness at work in the mind, belong to the field too: they arise, change and can be observed. The knower, in this scheme, is whatever witnesses all of them without being one of them.
