In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If every weekend is filled with parties and malls, keep an hour alone with no plan and no noise. Some kinds of understanding only come in quiet.

Among the qualities that make up knowledge, Krishna includes unwavering devotion to him through undivided yoga, a liking for solitary places, and no taste for crowds.

The word avyabhicāriṇī means 'not straying', a loyalty that does not drift to other objects. It is notable that bhakti appears inside a list defining jñāna, knowledge, which suggests the Gita does not set the two against each other. The taste for solitude reads more as a temperament than a rule to avoid people.
