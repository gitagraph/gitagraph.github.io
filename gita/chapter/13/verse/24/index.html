Bhagavad Gita 13.24

Krishna

ध्यानेनात्मनि पश्यन्ति केचिदात्मानमात्मना ।
अन्ये सांख्येन योगेन कर्मयोगेन चापरे ॥ १३.२४ ॥

dhyānenātmani paśyanti kecidātmānamātmanā ।
anye sāṃkhyena yogena karmayogena cāpare ॥

Some see the Self within themselves by their own self through meditation; others by the path of Sankhya; and still others by the yoga of action.

हिन्दीकुछ लोग ध्यान के द्वारा अपने भीतर, अपने ही द्वारा आत्मा को देखते हैं; दूसरे सांख्य-योग के द्वारा, और कुछ अन्य कर्मयोग के द्वारा।

Krishna names three ways in which people come to see the Self: through meditation, through Sankhya, and through the yoga of action.

Sāṅkhya here means the path of discernment, sorting what is Self from what is not, as this chapter itself does; karma-yoga is acting without clinging to results. The verse lists rather than ranks: different temperaments arrive by different roads, and the next verse adds one more for those who start from what they have heard.
