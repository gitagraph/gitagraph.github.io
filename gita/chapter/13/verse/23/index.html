Bhagavad Gita 13.23

Krishna

य एवं वेत्ति पुरुषं प्रकृतिं च गुणैः सह ।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि न स भूयोऽभिजायते ॥ १३.२३ ॥

ya evaṃ vetti puruṣaṃ prakṛtiṃ ca guṇaiḥ saha ।
sarvathā vartamāno'pi na sa bhūyo'bhijāyate ॥

One who knows Purusha and Prakriti together with the gunas in this way is not born again, however he may be living.

हिन्दीजो पुरुष और गुणों सहित प्रकृति को इस प्रकार जान लेता है, वह किसी भी प्रकार से जीवन बिताता हुआ भी फिर जन्म नहीं लेता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

You don't need to quit your job or move to the hills to grow wise. Clear understanding can take root right where you are, among bills and school runs.

Krishna says that whoever understands Purusha, and Prakriti with its gunas, in this way is not born again, however they may be living.

The phrase sarvathā vartamāno 'pi, 'living in whatever way', is bold: release is tied to clear understanding, not to giving up a household or a working life. Traditionally, commentators add that real understanding already reshapes conduct, so this is no licence for carelessness, only a statement that outer circumstances do not decide the matter.
