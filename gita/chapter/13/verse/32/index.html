Bhagavad Gita 13.32

Krishna

यथा सर्वगतं सौक्ष्म्यादाकाशं नोपलिप्यते ।
सर्वत्रावस्थितो देहे तथात्मा नोपलिप्यते ॥ १३.३२ ॥

yathā sarvagataṃ saukṣmyādākāśaṃ nopalipyate ।
sarvatrāvasthito dehe tathātmā nopalipyate ॥

Just as all-pervading space is not tainted because of its subtlety, so the Self, present throughout the body, is not tainted.

हिन्दीजैसे सब जगह फैला हुआ आकाश सूक्ष्म होने के कारण किसी से लिप्त नहीं होता, वैसे ही शरीर में सर्वत्र स्थित आत्मा लिप्त नहीं होती।

In your life

After a public embarrassment at work, remember that what holds all your experiences is not stained by them, just as the sky isn't stained by smoke. You can carry the memory without becoming it.

Krishna compares the Self in the body to space: just as all-pervading ākāśa is not tainted because it is so subtle, the Self present throughout the body is not tainted either.

Space holds smoke, dust and perfume without becoming any of them, and nothing can stain it because there is nothing in it to grip. The image suggests that moods and faults fill the body and mind as weather fills the sky, without marking the one in whom they appear.
