Chapter 13 · The Field and its KnowerVerse 33 of 34

Bhagavad Gita 13.33

Krishna

यथा प्रकाशयत्येकः कृत्स्नं लोकमिमं रविः ।
क्षेत्रं क्षेत्री तथा कृत्स्नं प्रकाशयति भारत ॥ १३.३३ ॥

yathā prakāśayatyekaḥ kṛtsnaṃ lokamimaṃ raviḥ ।
kṣetraṃ kṣetrī tathā kṛtsnaṃ prakāśayati bhārata ॥

Just as the one sun lights up this whole world, so the owner of the field lights up the whole field, Bharata.

हिन्दीहे भारत, जैसे एक ही सूर्य इस सारे संसार को प्रकाशित करता है, वैसे ही क्षेत्री (क्षेत्र का स्वामी) सारे क्षेत्र को प्रकाशित करता है।

In your life

Sitting through a tense family meeting, let the anger and hurt in the room simply be seen, the way sunlight shows a messy street without becoming messy itself. The light that shows it stays the same.

Krishna compares the knower of the field to the sun: as the one sun lights up this whole world, the kṣetrī, the owner of the field, lights up the whole field.

The sun shows everything without being changed by what it shows, whether beautiful or ugly. In the same way, awareness reveals every thought, sensation and feeling, and is the reason they can be known at all, while itself remaining one and unaffected.
