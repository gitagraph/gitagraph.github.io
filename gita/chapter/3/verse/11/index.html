Bhagavad Gita 3.11

Krishna

देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥ ३.११ ॥

devānbhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ ।
parasparaṃ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ paramavāpsyatha ॥

"Nourish the gods with this, and let the gods nourish you. Sustaining one another, you will reach the highest good."

हिन्दी"इस यज्ञ से तुम देवताओं को पुष्ट करो और देवता तुम्हें पुष्ट करें। इस तरह एक-दूसरे को पोषित करते हुए तुम परम कल्याण पाओगे।"

In your life

Neighbours who water each other's plants, keep a spare key and check in during a power cut both give and receive. A building runs well when everyone holds up a little of it.

In Prajāpati's words to the first beings, humans and gods are to sustain each other through sacrifice and so reach the highest good together.

The key word is bhāvayata, 'nourish' or 'make to thrive', used of both sides alike. Read today, it describes a relationship of exchange: what we draw from the world around us is meant to be returned, and well-being comes from that circle rather than from taking alone.
