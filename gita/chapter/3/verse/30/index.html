Bhagavad Gita 3.30

Krishna

मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।
निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः ॥ ३.३० ॥

mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā ।
nirāśīrnirmamo bhūtvā yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ ॥

Hand over all your actions to me, with your mind fixed on the Self; free of craving and of the sense of "mine", and with your fever gone, fight.

हिन्दीअपने चित्त को आत्मा में लगाकर सब कर्म मुझे अर्पित कर दो, और आशा तथा ममता से मुक्त होकर, मन का संताप छोड़कर युद्ध करो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When you must tell someone on your team they're being let go, set aside the worry about how you'll look and the craving for it to go smoothly. Then do it plainly and kindly.

Krishna gathers the teaching on action into one direct instruction: hand over every action to him, keep the mind on the Self, and fight.

The verb saṃnyasya, 'laying down', is followed by three conditions: nirāśīḥ, without craving for results; nirmamaḥ, without the sense of 'mine'; and vigatajvaraḥ, with the 'fever' of anxiety gone. Put simply, the task still has to be done, but without the heat of personal stakes that had frozen Arjuna.
