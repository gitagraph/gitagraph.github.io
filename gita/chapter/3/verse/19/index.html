Bhagavad Gita 3.19

Krishna

तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर ।
असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः ॥ ३.१९ ॥

tasmādasaktaḥ satataṃ kāryaṃ karma samācara ।
asakto hyācarankarma paramāpnoti pūruṣaḥ ॥

So always do the work that must be done, without attachment; by acting unattached a person reaches the Supreme.

हिन्दीइसलिए आसक्ति छोड़कर सदा अपना कर्तव्य कर्म अच्छी तरह करते रहो, क्योंकि आसक्ति के बिना कर्म करता हुआ मनुष्य परम को पा लेता है।

In your life

When your careful work goes into a report with someone else's name on top, do the next piece just as well. Work done without clutching at credit leaves you lighter and freer.

Krishna urges Arjuna always to do the work that must be done without attachment, since acting unattached a person reaches param, the highest.

He has just described someone content in the Self, with nothing left to gain by acting (3.17–18), yet his advice is still to act: asaktaḥ, without attachment, doing kāryaṃ karma. Detachment here is not withdrawal from work but freedom from clinging to it. Read plainly, a person can give a task full care without staking their peace on it.
