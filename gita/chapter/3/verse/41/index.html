Bhagavad Gita 3.41

Krishna

तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥ ३.४१ ॥

tasmāttvamindriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha ।
pāpmānaṃ prajahi hyenaṃ jñānavijñānanāśanam ॥

So, best of the Bharatas, first bring the senses under control, and then destroy this evil thing that ruins both knowledge and realisation.

हिन्दीइसलिए हे भरतश्रेष्ठ, तुम पहले इंद्रियों को वश में करो और फिर ज्ञान और विज्ञान को नष्ट करने वाले इस पापी काम को मार डालो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If impulse shopping keeps draining your account, don't wait to fight it at the checkout. Start earlier, with the senses: unsubscribe from the sale alerts and remove the saved card.

Krishna draws the conclusion from 3.40: since desire works through the senses, Arjuna should first bring them under control and then destroy this evil thing.

The word ādau, 'first', sets an order, and pāpman is a strong word for what is harmful in itself. Desire is said to ruin both jñāna and vijñāna, often taken as knowledge learned and knowledge realised in experience. The advice is to begin where the pull is most concrete.
