Bhagavad Gita 3.22

Krishna

न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किंचन ।
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि ॥ ३.२२ ॥

na me pārthāsti kartavyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiṃcana ।
nānavāptamavāptavyaṃ varta eva ca karmaṇi ॥

Partha, there is nothing in the three worlds that I must do, nothing I lack that I need to gain; and yet I go on acting.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, तीनों लोकों में मेरे लिए कोई कर्तव्य नहीं है, और न ही कोई ऐसी वस्तु है जो मुझे पानी हो और न मिली हो; फिर भी मैं कर्म में लगा रहता हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A grandmother who has nothing left to prove still wakes early to help in the kitchen and sit with the children. Doing your part when you no longer need to is the quietest example there is.

Krishna offers himself as the example: there is nothing in the three worlds he must do and nothing left for him to gain, yet he keeps acting.

The key word is kartavya, 'what must be done', which he says does not apply to him. Traditionally, the three worlds are earth, the space between and the heavens. The point follows from 3.20–21: action can come from care for others rather than from need.
