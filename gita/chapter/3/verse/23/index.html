Bhagavad Gita 3.23

Krishna

यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥ ३.२३ ॥

yadi hyahaṃ na varteyaṃ jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ ।
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ ॥

For if I ever stopped acting tirelessly, Partha, people everywhere would follow my path.

हिन्दीक्योंकि हे पार्थ, यदि मैं कभी बिना थके निरंतर कर्म करता न रहूँ, तो सब मनुष्य हर प्रकार से मेरे ही मार्ग का अनुसरण करेंगे।

In your life

If you, the eldest, quietly skip the morning walk with your ageing father, your siblings soon stop too. Keep up the small duties others see you doing.

Krishna explains why he keeps acting: if he ever stopped working tirelessly, people everywhere would follow his example.

The key word is atandrita, 'unwearied, without slackness'. It builds on 3.21, where Krishna said that others copy whatever a leading person does. For anyone whose choices others watch, a parent, a teacher, a leader, the verse points out that example carries further than instruction.
