Bhagavad Gita 3.2

Arjuna

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥ ३.२ ॥

vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṃ mohayasīva me ।
tadekaṃ vada niścitya yena śreyo'hamāpnuyām ॥

Your words seem to pull in two directions, and they leave my understanding confused. Tell me plainly the one path, settled for certain, by which I may reach the highest good.

हिन्दीआपके मिले-जुले से वचनों से मेरी बुद्धि मानो उलझ गई है। इसलिए निश्चय करके मुझे वह एक बात बताइए जिससे मेरा परम कल्याण हो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When two people you trust give you opposite advice about your career, it's fair to say you're confused. Ask them, and yourself, for the one clear next step.

Arjuna asks Krishna to stop giving what sounds like mixed advice and to name the single path, settled for certain, that leads to his highest good.

The key word is vyāmiśra, 'mixed' or 'tangled': praise of understanding alongside calls to fight seemed to him to pull two ways. The twice-repeated iva, 'as if', is worth noticing: it may hint that the confusion lies in his hearing rather than in the teaching.
