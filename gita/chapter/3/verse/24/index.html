Bhagavad Gita 3.24

Krishna

उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् ।
संकरस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः ॥ ३.२४ ॥

utsīdeyurime lokā na kuryāṃ karma cedaham ।
saṃkarasya ca kartā syāmupahanyāmimāḥ prajāḥ ॥

If I did not work, these worlds would fall into ruin; I would be the cause of confusion and would bring these beings to destruction.

हिन्दीयदि मैं कर्म न करूँ तो ये सब लोक नष्ट हो जाएँ। मैं अव्यवस्था का कारण बनूँ और इस सारी प्रजा का नाश करने वाला बनूँ।

Krishna completes his own example: if he did not work, these worlds would fall into ruin, and he would cause confusion and the destruction of beings.

The key word is saṃkara, the same 'confusion' Arjuna feared in 1.41–42 as the result of fighting. Krishna turns the word around: on his account, it is giving up one's work, not doing it, that lets the order of things come apart.
