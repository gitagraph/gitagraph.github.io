Bhagavad Gita 3.1

Arjuna

ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥ ३.१ ॥

jyāyasī cetkarmaṇaste matā buddhirjanārdana ।
tatkiṃ karmaṇi ghore māṃ niyojayasi keśava ॥

Janardana, if you hold that understanding is higher than action, then why, Keshava, do you push me into this terrible deed?

हिन्दीहे जनार्दन, यदि आप कर्म से बुद्धि को श्रेष्ठ मानते हैं, तो हे केशव, मुझे इस भयानक कर्म में क्यों लगा रहे हैं?

Arjuna opens Chapter 3 with a puzzle drawn from Krishna's own words: if understanding is better than action, why push him into a terrible deed?

The key word is buddhi, the settled understanding Krishna praised in 2.49, where mere action was called far inferior to it. Arjuna hears this as ranking thinking above doing, and his question sets the theme of the whole chapter: why act at all?
