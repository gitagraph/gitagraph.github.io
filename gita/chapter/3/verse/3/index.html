Bhagavad Gita 3.3

Krishna

लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।
ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥ ३.३ ॥

loke'smindvividhā niṣṭhā purā proktā mayānagha ।
jñānayogena sāṃkhyānāṃ karmayogena yoginām ॥

Sinless one, long ago I taught that in this world there are two ways of steady practice: the path of knowledge for those given to reflection, and the path of action for those given to yoga.

हिन्दीहे निष्पाप अर्जुन, इस संसार में मैंने पहले दो प्रकार की निष्ठा बताई थी — सांख्य मार्ग वालों के लिए ज्ञानयोग और योगियों के लिए कर्मयोग।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a parent falls ill, one sibling reads every report and another runs to the pharmacy and the bank. Both are real ways of caring; neither is the lesser one.

Krishna answers Arjuna's request for one clear path by saying there have long been two: the path of knowledge for the reflective and the path of action for those who practise yoga.

The key word is niṣṭhā, a settled stance or way of commitment. Purā, 'before', is read by some as pointing back to Chapter 2 and by others as 'from the beginning'. Either way, the two ways are offered as suited to different people.
