Bhagavad Gita 3.33

Krishna

सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि ।
प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति ॥ ३.३३ ॥

sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi ।
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ॥

Even a knowing person acts in keeping with their own nature. All beings follow their nature; what can forcing it achieve?

हिन्दीज्ञानी व्यक्ति भी अपनी प्रकृति के अनुसार ही चेष्टा करता है। सब प्राणी अपनी प्रकृति के अनुसार ही चलते हैं; फिर हठ से दबाने से क्या होगा?

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A quiet child pushed hard to be the life of every party rarely changes for long. Work with their nature, let them shine in their own way, and still help them grow.

Krishna observes that every being, even a knowledgeable person, acts according to its own nature, so forcing that nature down achieves little.

The key words are prakṛti, here one's inborn makeup, and nigraha, forcible restraint. The point is not that effort is useless, since 3.34 goes straight on to say liking and disliking must not rule us, but that lasting change works with a person's nature rather than simply clamping down on it.
