Bhagavad Gita 3.12

Krishna

इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥ ३.१२ ॥

iṣṭānbhogānhi vo devā dāsyante yajñabhāvitāḥ ।
tairdattānapradāyaibhyo yo bhuṅkte stena eva saḥ ॥

"Pleased by sacrifice, the gods will give you the enjoyments you desire." One who enjoys their gifts without offering anything back to them is nothing but a thief.

हिन्दी"यज्ञ से प्रसन्न हुए देवता तुम्हें मनचाहे भोग देंगे।" जो उनके दिए हुए भोगों को उन्हें अर्पित किए बिना स्वयं भोगता है, वह चोर ही है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a shared flat, the person who uses the milk, the Wi-Fi and the clean kitchen but never restocks or scrubs is taking from everyone. Notice where you take more than you return.

The verse finishes Prajāpati's promise that gods pleased by sacrifice will give people what they want, and then calls anyone who enjoys those gifts without giving anything back a thief.

The strong word is stena, 'thief', chosen over milder terms like ungrateful or selfish. Plainly, the verse treats taking without returning not as a private matter but as a kind of theft from a shared order.
