Bhagavad Gita 3.8

Krishna

नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥ ३.८ ॥

niyataṃ kuru karma tvaṃ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ ।
śarīrayātrāpi ca te na prasiddhyedakarmaṇaḥ ॥

Do the work that is yours to do, for action is better than inaction; without acting you could not even keep your body going.

हिन्दीतुम अपना नियत कर्म करो, क्योंकि कर्म न करने से कर्म करना श्रेष्ठ है। बिना कर्म के तो तुम्हारा शरीर-निर्वाह भी नहीं हो सकता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Between jobs, it's tempting to let the days go empty. Keep doing the work that is still yours, the house, the accounts, the applications, the morning walk; acting keeps both body and life going.

Krishna tells Arjuna to do the work that is his to do, since action is better than inaction and even keeping the body going needs it.

This answers Arjuna's question (3.1): if understanding is higher than action, why push him into a terrible deed? The key terms are niyataṃ karma, the work that is yours to do, and śarīrayātrā, the plain upkeep of the body. Plainly, stepping back from work is not automatically more spiritual; the question is how to act, not whether.
