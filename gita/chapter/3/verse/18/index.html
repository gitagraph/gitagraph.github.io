Bhagavad Gita 3.18

Krishna

नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन ।
न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः ॥ ३.१८ ॥

naiva tasya kṛtenārtho nākṛteneha kaścana ।
na cāsya sarvabhūteṣu kaścidarthavyapāśrayaḥ ॥

Such a one gains nothing here by acting and loses nothing by not acting, and depends on no being for any purpose.

हिन्दीऐसे व्यक्ति का इस संसार में न कर्म करने से कोई प्रयोजन है, न कर्म न करने से। और सब प्राणियों में किसी से भी उसका कोई स्वार्थ का संबंध नहीं रहता।

Krishna describes the person content in the Self: nothing is gained for them by acting, nothing lost by not acting, and they depend on no being for anything.

The key term is arthavyapāśraya, relying on others for some purpose. Notice that this says what such a person no longer needs, not that they should stop working; in 3.19 Krishna still tells Arjuna to keep acting, without attachment.
