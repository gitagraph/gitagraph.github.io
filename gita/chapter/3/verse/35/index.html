Bhagavad Gita 3.35

Krishna

श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३.३५ ॥

śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt ।
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ ॥

Better one's own dharma, even done imperfectly, than another's dharma done well. Better to die in one's own dharma; another's dharma brings danger.

हिन्दीअच्छी तरह निभाए गए दूसरे के धर्म से अपना धर्म, कमियों के साथ भी, श्रेष्ठ है। अपने धर्म में मरना भी कल्याणकारी है, जबकि दूसरे का धर्म भय देने वाला है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Your cousin's career abroad may look shinier than your steady work at home. Better to follow your own path, flaws and all, than to live a copy of someone else's.

Krishna says that one's own dharma, even done imperfectly, is better than another's dharma done well, and that even death in one's own dharma is better.

The key word is svadharma, one's own dharma (duty, calling, the way that fits one's nature), set against paradharma, another's; it follows his warning that liking and disliking sit in every sense (3.34). For Arjuna, a warrior, that means not escaping into a renunciant's life; for readers, it suggests an authentic path, clumsily walked, beats a borrowed one.
