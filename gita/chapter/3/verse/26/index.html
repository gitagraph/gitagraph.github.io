Bhagavad Gita 3.26

Krishna

न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥ ३.२६ ॥

na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ karmasaṅginām ।
joṣayetsarvakarmāṇi vidvānyuktaḥ samācaran ॥

The wise should not unsettle the minds of the ignorant who are attached to action. Acting with discipline themselves, they should lead others to take joy in all their work.

हिन्दीज्ञानी को चाहिए कि कर्मों में आसक्त अज्ञानियों की बुद्धि में भ्रम पैदा न करे। स्वयं योग में स्थित रहकर सब कर्म अच्छी तरह करते हुए उनसे भी कर्म करवाए।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If your parents find meaning in a ritual that doesn't speak to you, don't tell them it's pointless. Do your own work steadily and well; that says more than an argument.

Krishna advises the wise not to unsettle people who are attached to their work, but to act well themselves and so lead others to value their tasks.

The key term is buddhibheda, a split or unsettling of someone's understanding. Telling people their work does not matter can leave them with neither their old motive nor any new insight; it is better to show, through one's own steady work, a better way to do it.
