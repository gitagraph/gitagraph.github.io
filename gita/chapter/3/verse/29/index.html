Bhagavad Gita 3.29

Krishna

प्रकृतेर्गुणसंमूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।
तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥ ३.२९ ॥

prakṛterguṇasaṃmūḍhāḥ sajjante guṇakarmasu ।
tānakṛtsnavido mandānkṛtsnavinna vicālayet ॥

Those deluded by the qualities of nature cling to the workings of those qualities. One who knows the whole should not unsettle such slow people who know only in part.

हिन्दीप्रकृति के गुणों से मोहित लोग गुणों और कर्मों में आसक्त रहते हैं। पूर्ण ज्ञान वाले को चाहिए कि ऐसे अधूरी समझ वाले मंदबुद्धि लोगों को विचलित न करे।

Krishna advises those who see the whole picture not to unsettle people who know only part of it.

The pair kṛtsnavid, 'one who knows the whole', and akṛtsnavid, 'one who knows in part', echoes 3.26: people still bound to their work and its rewards should not be shaken out of acting before they can grasp the larger view. For a reader, it is a caution against pressing an insight on others in a way that leaves them only confused or idle.
