Bhagavad Gita 3.16

Krishna

एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति ॥ ३.१६ ॥

evaṃ pravartitaṃ cakraṃ nānuvartayatīha yaḥ ।
aghāyurindriyārāmo moghaṃ pārtha sa jīvati ॥

Partha, whoever in this world does not keep turning the wheel set in motion this way, living in sin and taking delight only in the senses, lives in vain.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, जो व्यक्ति इस लोक में इस प्रकार चलाए गए चक्र के अनुसार नहीं चलता, वह इंद्रियों के सुख में रमने वाला पापमय जीवन जीता है और व्यर्थ ही जीता है।

Krishna closes the passage on sacrifice: whoever does not keep turning the wheel of giving and receiving, and lives only for the senses, lives in vain.

The key image is cakra, a wheel already set in motion, linking action, sacrifice, rain, food and living beings in one cycle. Plainly, the verse asks each person to keep up their part of a larger cycle, and calls a life spent only on one's own pleasure a wasted one.
