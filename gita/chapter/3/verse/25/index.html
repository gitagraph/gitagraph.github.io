Bhagavad Gita 3.25

Krishna

सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत ।
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसंग्रहम् ॥ ३.२५ ॥

saktāḥ karmaṇyavidvāṃso yathā kurvanti bhārata ।
kuryādvidvāṃstathāsaktaścikīrṣurlokasaṃgraham ॥

Bharata, just as the unwise act out of attachment to their work, the wise should act without attachment, wishing to hold the world together.

हिन्दीहे भारत, जैसे अज्ञानी लोग कर्म में आसक्त होकर कर्म करते हैं, वैसे ही ज्ञानी को लोकसंग्रह की इच्छा से, आसक्ति के बिना, कर्म करना चाहिए।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Organising the office annual day, some colleagues work hard because they want their way. Work just as hard, but for the occasion and for keeping everyone together, not for winning.

Krishna asks the wise to work as hard as the unwise do, but without their attachment, wanting only to hold the world together.

The contrast turns on two words, sakta, attached, and asakta, unattached: the effort is the same, only the motive changes. In plain terms, the wise are not those who do less, but those who do the same work for others rather than for what it brings them.
