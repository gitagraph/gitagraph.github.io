Bhagavad Gita 9.31

Krishna

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति ।
कौन्तेय प्रति जानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥ ९.३१ ॥

kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati ।
kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati ॥

He quickly becomes righteous and attains lasting peace. Declare it boldly, Kaunteya: my devotee never perishes.

हिन्दीवह शीघ्र ही धर्मात्मा बन जाता है और स्थायी शांति प्राप्त करता है। हे कौंतेय, तुम निश्चयपूर्वक घोषणा करो कि मेरा भक्त कभी नष्ट नहीं होता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you've been stuck in a habit you're ashamed of, don't wait to feel worthy before turning around. The wholehearted turn is itself the start, and things can mend sooner than you fear.

Krishna says that even a person of very bad conduct who turns to him wholeheartedly quickly becomes righteous and finds lasting peace, and asks Arjuna to declare that his devotee never perishes.

The key word is dharmātmā, a person whose nature has come into line with dharma, and pratijānīhi means 'declare it' (some read 'know it for certain'). Plainly put, a person's past is not their final verdict; once the direction changes, change can come quickly.
