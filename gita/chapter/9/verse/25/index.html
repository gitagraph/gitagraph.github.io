Bhagavad Gita 9.25

Krishna

यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः ।
भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम् ॥ ९.२५ ॥

yānti devavratā devānpitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ ।
bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino'pi mām ॥

Those devoted to the gods go to the gods, those devoted to the ancestors go to the ancestors, those who worship spirits go to the spirits, and those who worship me come to me.

हिन्दीदेवताओं का व्रत रखने वाले देवताओं को प्राप्त होते हैं, पितरों का व्रत रखने वाले पितरों को, भूत-प्रेतों को पूजने वाले भूतों को, और मेरी पूजा करने वाले मुझे ही प्राप्त होते हैं।

Krishna states a plain rule: people reach whatever they devote themselves to, whether gods, ancestors or spirits, and those who worship him reach him.

The verb yānti, 'they go', is repeated four times to make the pattern unmistakable. Vrata here means a vow or chosen observance. The underlying idea is that what a person gives themselves to shapes where they end up, which is why the object of devotion matters.
