Bhagavad Gita 9.19

Krishna

तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च ।
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥ ९.१९ ॥

tapāmyahamahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca ।
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna ॥

I give heat; I hold back the rain and send it down. I am immortality and death as well, being and non-being, Arjuna.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, मैं ही तपता हूँ, मैं ही वर्षा को रोकता और बरसाता हूँ। मैं ही अमरता और मृत्यु हूँ, और सत् तथा असत् भी मैं ही हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Rain that saves one farmer floods another field. Life brings both gift and loss from the same source; seeing that can soften the bitterness when it is your turn for the hard one.

Krishna says he is the one who gives heat, who holds back the rain and sends it down, who is immortality and death, and both sat and asat.

Sat and asat are usually rendered 'being' and 'non-being'; some commentators take them as the manifest and the unmanifest, or as effect and cause. The verse deliberately pairs opposites, rain and drought, deathlessness and death: nothing, not even what people fear, falls outside him.
