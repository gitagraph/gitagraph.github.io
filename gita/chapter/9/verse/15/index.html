Bhagavad Gita 9.15

Krishna

ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते ।
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ९.१५ ॥

jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate ।
ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham ॥

Others worship me by offering the sacrifice of knowledge, seeing me as one, as distinct, and in many ways as the one whose faces are everywhere.

हिन्दीकुछ दूसरे लोग ज्ञान-यज्ञ के द्वारा मेरी उपासना करते हैं; वे मुझे एक रूप में, अलग-अलग रूप में और अनेक प्रकार से, सब ओर मुख वाले के रूप में पूजते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In one family, one person finds meaning through prayer and another through reading and reflection. Both can be sincere; neither needs to convert the other at the dinner table.

Krishna names another path beside devotion: some worship him through jñāna-yajña, the sacrifice of knowledge, in which understanding itself is the offering.

They approach him as one, as distinct, and in many ways, as the one whose faces are everywhere. Traditionally the three are often read as seeing oneself as identical with him, worshipping him as other, and seeing him in all forms; commentators differ on whether these are three kinds of seeker or one seeker's stages.
