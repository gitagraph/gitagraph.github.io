Bhagavad Gita 9.3

Krishna

अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप ।
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि ॥ ९.३ ॥

aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa ।
aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani ॥

People who have no faith in this teaching, Parantapa, do not reach me; they turn back onto the road of death and rebirth.

हिन्दीहे परंतप, जिन लोगों को इस धर्म पर श्रद्धा नहीं होती, वे मुझे प्राप्त न करके मृत्यु और जन्म-मरण के मार्ग पर लौट आते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Learning to swim as an adult, you have to trust the coach enough to let go of the edge. Without that trust you keep turning back to where you started.

Krishna says that people who lack faith in this teaching do not reach him and instead turn back onto the road of death and rebirth.

Having just called it the royal knowledge (9.1–2), he names what it asks for: śraddhā, a trust willing to take a teaching seriously before it is proven. The verse reads less as a threat than as a description: without that openness, life keeps circling the same well-worn road.
