Bhagavad Gita 9.26

Krishna

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥ ९.२६ ॥

patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati ।
tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ ॥

Whoever offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or some water, that offering of love from a pure-hearted person I accept.

हिन्दीजो कोई भक्ति से मुझे पत्ता, फूल, फल या जल अर्पित करता है, उस शुद्ध मन वाले के प्रेम से दिए उस उपहार को मैं स्वीकार करता हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you can't afford a grand gift for a friend's wedding, a small one given with real warmth is enough. What reaches the other person is the love in it, not the price.

Krishna says that whatever is offered to him with devotion, a leaf, a flower, a fruit or a little water, he accepts.

The key word is bhakti, loving devotion, and the verb aśnāmi literally says 'I eat', taking the gift as truly received. In plain terms, what counts is the heart of the giver, not the cost of the gift, so no one is too poor to offer.
