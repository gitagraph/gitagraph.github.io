Bhagavad Gita 9.21

Krishna

ते तं भुक्त्वा स्वर्गलोकं विशालं ।
क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति ।
एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्ना ।
गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते ॥ ९.२१ ॥

te taṃ bhuktvā svargalokaṃ viśālaṃ ।
kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṃ viśanti ।
evaṃ trayīdharmamanuprapannā ।
gatāgataṃ kāmakāmā labhante ॥

Having enjoyed that vast heavenly world, they return to the world of mortals once their merit is spent. In this way, following the ritual dharma of the three Vedas and craving pleasures, they keep going and coming.

हिन्दीउस विशाल स्वर्गलोक को भोगकर, पुण्य समाप्त होने पर वे फिर मनुष्य लोक में आ जाते हैं। इस प्रकार तीनों वेदों के कर्मकांड का पालन करने वाले, भोगों की कामना करने वाले लोग बार-बार आते-जाते रहते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The thrill of a big purchase, a new car or a lavish holiday, runs out like a balance being spent, and you're back to wanting the next one. Notice the loop before you chase it again.

Krishna completes the picture of 9.20: once their merit is used up, those who enjoyed heaven return to the world of mortals, and so ritual followers driven by desire keep going and coming.

The key phrase is gatāgata, 'gone and come', a back-and-forth with no end in sight. Heaven appears here as a reward that is spent and runs out, which sets up the contrast in 9.22, where he himself provides for those who rely on him.
