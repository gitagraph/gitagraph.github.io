Bhagavad Gita 9.22

Krishna

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥ ९.२२ ॥

ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate ।
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham ॥

But those who think of me alone and worship me with no other thought, ever devoted to me: I myself take on their welfare, bringing them what they need and keeping safe what they have.

हिन्दीपरंतु जो लोग अनन्य भाव से मेरा चिंतन करते हुए मेरी उपासना करते हैं, उन सदा मुझसे जुड़े हुए भक्तों को जो नहीं मिला वह मैं दिलाता हूँ और जो मिला है उसकी रक्षा मैं स्वयं करता हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When you've done all you can on a visa application for your family, the waiting can fill every hour. Hand what is beyond you to whatever you trust most, and give your full attention to today's work.

Krishna promises that for those who think of him alone and worship him with no other thought, he himself takes on their yoga-kṣema.

Yoga is gaining what one lacks and kṣema keeping safe what one has; he sets these people against those who use ritual to win heaven and return once their merit runs out. Some traditions take this as everyday welfare, others as progress toward liberation. Either way, the divine is pictured taking on the care of one who turns to it wholly.
