Bhagavad Gita 9.16

Krishna

अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् ।
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम् ॥ ९.१६ ॥

ahaṃ kraturahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāhamahamauṣadham ।
mantro'hamahamevājyamahamagnirahaṃ hutam ॥

I am the ritual and I am the sacrifice; I am the offering to the ancestors and the healing herb; I am the sacred chant, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire and I am the act of offering.

हिन्दीमैं ही क्रतु (वैदिक अनुष्ठान) हूँ, मैं ही यज्ञ हूँ, मैं ही पितरों को अर्पित स्वधा हूँ, मैं ही औषधि हूँ; मैं ही मंत्र, मैं ही घी, मैं ही अग्नि और मैं ही आहुति हूँ।

Krishna names himself in every part of a Vedic fire ritual: the rite, the sacrifice, the offering to the ancestors, the herb, the chant, the clarified butter, the fire and the act of offering itself.

Svadhā is the call spoken with offerings to the ancestors, and kratu a prescribed Vedic rite. The list leaves nothing of the ritual outside him, from the materials to the flame and the gesture of pouring.
