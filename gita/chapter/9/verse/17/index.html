Bhagavad Gita 9.17

Krishna

पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः ।
वेद्यं पवित्रमोंकार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च ॥ ९.१७ ॥

pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ ।
vedyaṃ pavitramoṃkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca ॥

I am the father of this world, its mother, its upholder and its grandsire. I am what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om, and the Rig, Sama and Yajur Vedas as well.

हिन्दीमैं इस जगत का पिता, माता, धारण करने वाला और पितामह हूँ। जानने योग्य वस्तु, पवित्र करने वाला, ओंकार, तथा ऋग्वेद, सामवेद और यजुर्वेद भी मैं ही हूँ।

Krishna calls himself the father, mother, upholder and grandsire of this world, and also what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om and the Rig, Sama and Yajur Vedas.

Dhātṛ, 'upholder' or 'arranger', and pitāmaha, 'grandsire', stretch the family image to the source behind every source. Read plainly, the verse places care and knowledge in one reality: what nurtures the world is also what is most worth knowing in it.

People: Krishna
