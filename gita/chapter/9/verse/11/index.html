Bhagavad Gita 9.11

Krishna

अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।
परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम् ॥ ९.११ ॥

avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam ।
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram ॥

Deluded people look down on me when I take on a human body, not knowing my higher nature as the great Lord of all beings.

हिन्दीमूढ़ लोग मनुष्य शरीर धारण करने पर मेरी अवहेलना करते हैं, क्योंकि वे सब प्राणियों के महान ईश्वर के रूप में मेरे परम भाव को नहीं जानते।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

It's easy to dismiss the quiet neighbour who seems ordinary in every way. Before writing anyone off, remember how little the outside shows of who a person really is.

Krishna says that deluded people look down on him because he appears in a human body, not knowing his higher nature as the great Lord of beings.

The contrast is between mānuṣī tanu, a human body, and his para bhāva, his highest state of being. Having just described himself as the ground of all worlds, he notes how easily that is missed: the verse names the habit of judging by the surface and dismissing what looks ordinary.
