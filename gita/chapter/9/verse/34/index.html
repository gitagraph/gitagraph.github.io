Bhagavad Gita 9.34

Krishna

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ ९.३४ ॥

manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru ।
māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ ॥

Fix your mind on me, be devoted to me, offer worship to me and bow down to me. Joining yourself to me in this way, with me as your highest aim, you will come to me.

हिन्दीअपना मन मुझमें लगाओ, मेरे भक्त बनो, मेरी पूजा करो और मुझे प्रणाम करो। इस प्रकार अपने को मुझसे जोड़कर, मुझे ही परम लक्ष्य मानकर, तुम मुझे ही प्राप्त होगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Start the day by bringing your mind, effort and gratitude to whatever you hold highest, even for a moment. A day that begins turned in one direction tends to stay closer to it.

Closing chapter 9, Krishna sums up its teaching in four short commands: fix your mind on him, be devoted to him, worship him and bow to him, and so, with him as the highest aim, come to him.

Mat-parāyaṇa means 'having me as the final resort'. The first line returns almost word for word in 18.65, which marks it as one of the Gita's central statements of devotion.
