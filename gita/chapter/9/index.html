Chapter 9 · The Royal Secret

Chapter 9 · 34 verses

The Royal Secret

राजविद्याराजगुह्ययोग

The most confidential knowledge: everything rests in Krishna, yet he is not contained in anything. Even a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water offered with devotion is accepted. He carries the welfare of those who worship him with undivided mind.

सबसे गोपनीय ज्ञान: सब कुछ कृष्ण में स्थित है, फिर भी वे किसी में सीमित नहीं। भक्ति से अर्पित एक पत्ता, फूल, फल या जल भी वे स्वीकार करते हैं। जो अनन्य मन से उन्हें भजते हैं, उनका योगक्षेम वे स्वयं वहन करते हैं।

  1. 9.1

    Because you do not find fault, I will now tell you the most hidden knowledge of all, together with its practical realisation; once you know it, you will be set free from what is harmful.

  2. 9.2

    This is the king of all knowledge and the king of all secrets, supremely purifying. It can be known by direct experience, it is in keeping with dharma, it is very easy to practise, and it never perishes.

  3. 9.3

    People who have no faith in this teaching, Parantapa, do not reach me; they turn back onto the road of death and rebirth.

  4. 9.4

    This whole universe is pervaded by me in my unmanifest form. All beings rest in me, but I do not rest in them.

  5. 9.5

    And yet beings do not rest in me either: see my divine power. My Self sustains all beings and brings them into existence, yet it does not dwell in them.

  6. 9.6

    Just as the great wind, moving everywhere, always remains within space, understand that in the same way all beings remain within me.

  7. 9.7

    At the end of a cosmic age, Kaunteya, all beings pass back into my own nature; at the start of the next age I send them forth again.

  8. 9.8

    Taking hold of my own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, which is helpless under the sway of nature.

  9. 9.9

    Those actions do not bind me, Dhananjaya, for I sit among them as one who is indifferent, unattached to those deeds.

  10. 9.10

    With me as overseer, nature gives birth to all that moves and all that is still; for this reason, Kaunteya, the world keeps turning.

  11. 9.11

    Deluded people look down on me when I take on a human body, not knowing my higher nature as the great Lord of all beings.

  12. 9.12

    Their hopes are empty, their actions futile and their knowledge useless; senseless, they cling to the deluding nature of fiends and demons.

  13. 9.13

    But great souls, Partha, who take refuge in the divine nature, worship me with minds turned to nothing else, knowing me as the imperishable source of all beings.

  14. 9.14

    Always singing my glory, striving with firm resolve, and bowing to me in devotion, they worship me ever united with me.

  15. 9.15

    Others worship me by offering the sacrifice of knowledge, seeing me as one, as distinct, and in many ways as the one whose faces are everywhere.

  16. 9.16

    I am the ritual and I am the sacrifice; I am the offering to the ancestors and the healing herb; I am the sacred chant, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire and I am the act of offering.

  17. 9.17

    I am the father of this world, its mother, its upholder and its grandsire. I am what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om, and the Rig, Sama and Yajur Vedas as well.

  18. 9.18

    I am the goal, the sustainer, the master and the witness; the home, the refuge and the friend; the origin and the dissolution, the ground, the storehouse and the seed that never decays.

  19. 9.19

    I give heat; I hold back the rain and send it down. I am immortality and death as well, being and non-being, Arjuna.

  20. 9.20

    Those versed in the three Vedas, who drink the soma and are cleansed of sin, worship me through sacrifices and pray for a path to heaven. Reaching the holy world of the king of the gods through their merit, they enjoy the divine pleasures of the gods in heaven.

  21. 9.21

    Having enjoyed that vast heavenly world, they return to the world of mortals once their merit is spent. In this way, following the ritual dharma of the three Vedas and craving pleasures, they keep going and coming.

  22. 9.22

    But those who think of me alone and worship me with no other thought, ever devoted to me: I myself take on their welfare, bringing them what they need and keeping safe what they have.

  23. 9.23

    Even those who are devoted to other gods and worship them with faith, Kaunteya, are in fact worshipping me alone, though not in the proper way.

  24. 9.24

    For I am the enjoyer and the lord of every sacrifice. But they do not know me as I truly am, and so they fall back.

  25. 9.25

    Those devoted to the gods go to the gods, those devoted to the ancestors go to the ancestors, those who worship spirits go to the spirits, and those who worship me come to me.

  26. 9.26

    Whoever offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or some water, that offering of love from a pure-hearted person I accept.

  27. 9.27

    Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in the fire, whatever you give and whatever discipline you practise, Kaunteya, do it as an offering to me.

  28. 9.28

    In this way you will be freed from the bonds of action and their good and bad results. With your mind set firm in the yoga of renunciation, you will be liberated and come to me.

  29. 9.29

    I am the same toward all beings; no one is hateful to me and no one is my favourite. But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I am in them.

  30. 9.30

    Even if a person of very bad conduct worships me with undivided devotion, he should be regarded as good, for he has made the right resolve.

  31. 9.31

    He quickly becomes righteous and attains lasting peace. Declare it boldly, Kaunteya: my devotee never perishes.

  32. 9.32

    For, Partha, those who take refuge in me, even those of lowly birth, women, Vaishyas and Shudras too, reach the highest goal.

  33. 9.33

    How much more so, then, pure Brahmins and devoted royal sages! Having come into this passing and joyless world, worship me.

  34. 9.34

    Fix your mind on me, be devoted to me, offer worship to me and bow down to me. Joining yourself to me in this way, with me as your highest aim, you will come to me.
