Bhagavad Gita 10.12

Arjuna

परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् ।
पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम् ॥ १०.१२ ॥

paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma pavitraṃ paramaṃ bhavān ।
puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyamādidevamajaṃ vibhum ॥

You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier: the eternal, divine Person, the first of the gods, unborn and all-pervading.

हिन्दीआप परम ब्रह्म, परम धाम और परम पवित्र हैं। आप सनातन दिव्य पुरुष, देवताओं के भी आदि देव, अजन्मा और सर्वव्यापी हैं।

Arjuna, moved by what he has heard, answers in a hymn of praise, calling Krishna the supreme Brahman, the supreme abode and the supreme purifier.

The praise runs into the next verse, where he says the sages have long described him this way. The key word is vibhu, 'all-pervading', present everywhere. Here the chapter turns: Arjuna now accepts the teaching and wants to hear it in detail.
