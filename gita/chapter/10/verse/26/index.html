Bhagavad Gita 10.26

Krishna

अश्वत्थः सर्ववृक्षाणां देवर्षीणां च नारदः ।
गन्धर्वाणां चित्ररथः सिद्धानां कपिलो मुनिः ॥ १०.२६ ॥

aśvatthaḥ sarvavṛkṣāṇāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ ca nāradaḥ ।
gandharvāṇāṃ citrarathaḥ siddhānāṃ kapilo muniḥ ॥

Among all trees I am the sacred fig; among the divine sages, Narada; among the Gandharvas, Chitraratha; and among the perfected ones, the sage Kapila.

हिन्दीसब वृक्षों में मैं पीपल हूँ, देवर्षियों में नारद हूँ, गंधर्वों में चित्ररथ हूँ और सिद्धों में कपिल मुनि हूँ।

Krishna names his glories among trees and seers: he is the aśvattha, the sacred fig, among trees; Narada among divine sages; Chitraratha among Gandharvas; and Kapila among the perfected.

The aśvattha returns in 15.1–3 as the image of the world itself, an upside-down tree. Traditionally, Chitraratha is a king of the celestial musicians, and Kapila is honoured as the founding teacher of Sāṅkhya. Narada also appears in 10.13, among the sages Arjuna cites.
