Bhagavad Gita 10.19

Krishna

हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।
प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे ॥ १०.१९ ॥

hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ ।
prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha nāstyanto vistarasya me ॥

Very well, best of the Kurus, I will tell you of my divine glories, but only the chief ones, for there is no end to my extent.

हिन्दीअच्छा, हे कुरुश्रेष्ठ, मैं तुम्हें अपनी दिव्य विभूतियाँ बताऊँगा, पर केवल मुख्य-मुख्य, क्योंकि मेरे विस्तार का कोई अंत नहीं है।

Krishna agrees to tell Arjuna of his divine vibhūtis, but says he will name only the chief ones, since his extent has no end.

The key word is prādhānyataḥ, 'by prominence': each item that follows is the foremost of its kind, not part of a complete inventory. This sets how to read the long list ahead: the examples are signposts pointing to a presence that runs through everything, not boundaries drawn around it.
