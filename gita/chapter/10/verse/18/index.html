Bhagavad Gita 10.18

Arjuna

विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन ।
भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि शृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम् ॥ १०.१८ ॥

vistareṇātmano yogaṃ vibhūtiṃ ca janārdana ।
bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptirhi śṛṇvato nāsti me'mṛtam ॥

Tell me again in detail, Janardana, of your power and your glories, for I never tire of hearing your nectar-like words.

हिन्दीहे जनार्दन, अपनी योग-शक्ति और विभूतियों को फिर से विस्तार से बताइए, क्योंकि आपके अमृत जैसे वचन सुनते हुए मेरा मन भरता ही नहीं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a grandparent's stories never tire you however often you hear them, that's love speaking. Let yourself enjoy hearing again what is dear to you.

Arjuna asks Krishna to tell again, in detail, of his yoga and his vibhūtis, because he never tires of hearing them.

The key word is amṛta, 'nectar', literally 'deathless': the words are so sweet to him that tṛpti, the sense of having had enough, never comes. The verse shows a listener whose questions come from delight rather than doubt, and it closes Arjuna's request before Krishna begins his list.
