Bhagavad Gita 10.24

Krishna

पुरोधसां च मुख्यं मां विद्धि पार्थ बृहस्पतिम् ।
सेनानीनामहं स्कन्दः सरसामस्मि सागरः ॥ १०.२४ ॥

purodhasāṃ ca mukhyaṃ māṃ viddhi pārtha bṛhaspatim ।
senānīnāmahaṃ skandaḥ sarasāmasmi sāgaraḥ ॥

Know me, Partha, as Brihaspati, chief of household priests. Among army commanders I am Skanda, and among bodies of water, the ocean.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, पुरोहितों में मुख्य बृहस्पति मुझे ही जानो। सेनापतियों में मैं स्कंद हूँ और जलाशयों में समुद्र हूँ।

Krishna tells Arjuna to know him as Brihaspati, chief of household priests, and says that among army commanders he is Skanda and among waters the ocean.

Traditionally, Brihaspati is the priest and teacher of the gods and Skanda leads their army. The key word is purodhas, the priest 'placed in front', who stands before a household in its rites. Speaking to a warrior, Krishna sets learning and leadership in battle side by side as places where he appears.
