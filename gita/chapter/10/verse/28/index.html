Bhagavad Gita 10.28

Krishna

आयुधानामहं वज्रं धेनूनामस्मि कामधुक् ।
प्रजनश्चास्मि कन्दर्पः सर्पाणामस्मि वासुकिः ॥ १०.२८ ॥

āyudhānāmahaṃ vajraṃ dhenūnāmasmi kāmadhuk ।
prajanaścāsmi kandarpaḥ sarpāṇāmasmi vāsukiḥ ॥

Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows, the wish-fulfilling Kamadhenu. I am Kandarpa, the god of love, who brings forth offspring, and among serpents I am Vasuki.

हिन्दीशस्त्रों में मैं वज्र हूँ, गायों में कामधेनु हूँ, संतान उत्पन्न करने वाला कामदेव मैं हूँ और सर्पों में वासुकि हूँ।

Krishna says that among weapons he is the thunderbolt, among cows Kamadhenu, among serpents Vasuki, and that he is Kandarpa, the god of love, who brings forth offspring.

The key word is prajana, 'begetting': desire is named here as the force that continues life. This matches 7.11, where he says he is desire that does not go against dharma. Traditionally, the thunderbolt is Indra's weapon and Vasuki the serpent used as the rope when the ocean was churned.
