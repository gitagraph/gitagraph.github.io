Bhagavad Gita 10.17

Arjuna

कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् ।
केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया ॥ १०.१७ ॥

kathaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yogiṃstvāṃ sadā paricintayan ।
keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyo'si bhagavanmayā ॥

How am I to know you, Yogi, while constantly meditating on you? In which of your many forms, Lord, should I think of you?

हिन्दीहे योगी, निरंतर आपका चिंतन करते हुए मैं आपको कैसे जानूँ? हे भगवन, किन-किन भावों में मैं आपका ध्यान करूँ?

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you want to keep what you hold highest in mind through a busy day, it helps to know where to look: in a face, a place, a piece of music. Ask yourself which form steadies you.

Arjuna explains why he asks: he wants to know how to know Krishna while meditating on him constantly, and in which forms or states to hold him in mind.

The key word is bhāva, here a form or way of being in which the divine can be pictured. He calls Krishna yogin, a master of yoga. It is a practical question: a reality that pervades everything is hard to keep in attention without something particular to rest on.
