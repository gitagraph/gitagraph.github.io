Bhagavad Gita 10.30

Krishna

प्रह्लादश्चास्मि दैत्यानां कालः कलयतामहम् ।
मृगाणां च मृगेन्द्रोऽहं वैनतेयश्च पक्षिणाम् ॥ १०.३० ॥

prahlādaścāsmi daityānāṃ kālaḥ kalayatāmaham ।
mṛgāṇāṃ ca mṛgendro'haṃ vainateyaśca pakṣiṇām ॥

Among the Daityas I am Prahlada; among those who count, I am Time; among beasts, the lion; and among birds, Garuda, son of Vinata.

हिन्दीदैत्यों में मैं प्रह्लाद हूँ, गिनती करने वालों में काल हूँ, पशुओं में सिंह हूँ और पक्षियों में विनता का पुत्र गरुड़ हूँ।

Krishna says that among the Daityas he is Prahlada, among those who count he is Time, among beasts the lion, and among birds Garuda, son of Vinata.

Traditionally, Prahlada is the devoted son of a demon king, which shows that excellence can appear even among the gods' opponents. The key word is kāla, Time, set among kalayatām, 'those who reckon', a play on the same sound; Krishna names himself Time again in 10.33, and in 11.32 as the destroyer of worlds.
