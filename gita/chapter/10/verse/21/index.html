Bhagavad Gita 10.21

Krishna

आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंशुमान् ।
मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी ॥ १०.२१ ॥

ādityānāmahaṃ viṣṇurjyotiṣāṃ raviraṃśumān ।
marīcirmarutāmasmi nakṣatrāṇāmahaṃ śaśī ॥

Among the Adityas I am Vishnu; among lights, the radiant sun; among the Maruts I am Marichi; and among the stars, the moon.

हिन्दीआदित्यों में मैं विष्णु हूँ, प्रकाश देने वालों में किरणों वाला सूर्य हूँ, मरुतों में मरीचि हूँ और नक्षत्रों में चंद्रमा हूँ।

Krishna names the first of his glories in the outer world: among the Adityas he is Vishnu, among lights the sun, among the Maruts Marichi, and among the stars the moon.

Traditionally, the Adityas are the sons of Aditi and the Maruts are storm gods. The pattern 'X among Y' sets the method for the rest of the chapter: in each class he points to its brightest or foremost member as the place where he is most easily seen.
