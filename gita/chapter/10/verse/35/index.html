Bhagavad Gita 10.35

Krishna

बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् ।
मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥ १०.३५ ॥

bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnāṃ gāyatrī chandasāmaham ।
māsānāṃ mārgaśīrṣo'hamṛtūnāṃ kusumākaraḥ ॥

Among Sama hymns I am the Brihat Saman; among metres, the Gayatri; among months, Margashirsha; and among seasons, flower-bearing spring.

हिन्दीसामगानों में मैं बृहत्साम हूँ, छंदों में गायत्री हूँ, महीनों में मार्गशीर्ष हूँ और ऋतुओं में फूलों वाली वसंत ऋतु हूँ।

Krishna picks out the most prized chant, metre, month and season as places where he shows himself.

The Brihat Saman is a chant of the Sama Veda; Gayatri is the metre of the best-known Vedic prayer. Margashirsha falls around November–December, and traditionally it was once counted as the first month of the year. Kusumākara, 'store of flowers', is a name for spring. Each time, the choice is the one people valued most.
