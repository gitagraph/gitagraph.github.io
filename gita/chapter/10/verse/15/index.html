Bhagavad Gita 10.15

Arjuna

स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम ।
भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते ॥ १०.१५ ॥

svayamevātmanātmānaṃ vettha tvaṃ puruṣottama ।
bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate ॥

Only you know yourself through yourself, Supreme Person, source of beings, lord of beings, god of gods, master of the world.

हिन्दीहे पुरुषोत्तम, हे प्राणियों को उत्पन्न करने वाले, हे प्राणियों के स्वामी, हे देवों के देव, हे जगत के स्वामी, आप स्वयं ही अपने आप से अपने को जानते हैं।

Arjuna tells Krishna that he alone knows himself, through himself, and heaps up titles of address: Supreme Person, source of beings, lord of beings, god of gods, master of the world.

The key phrase is svayam ātmanā ātmānam, 'yourself, by yourself, yourself': there is no outside standpoint from which he could be known. The string of names is praise, but also the reason for the request that follows: only the one who knows can explain.
