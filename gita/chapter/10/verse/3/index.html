Chapter 10 · Divine GloriesVerse 3 of 42

Bhagavad Gita 10.3

Krishna

यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् ।
असंमूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ १०.३ ॥

yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram ।
asaṃmūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ॥

Whoever knows me as unborn and without beginning, the great Lord of the worlds, is free of delusion among mortals and is released from all sins.

हिन्दीजो मुझे अजन्मा, अनादि और सब लोकों का महान ईश्वर जानता है, वह मनुष्यों में मोह से रहित होकर सब पापों से मुक्त हो जाता है।

Krishna says that knowing him as unborn, beginningless and the great Lord of the worlds frees a person from delusion and from all sins.

It follows his claim (10.2) that even gods and great sages do not know his origin. The key word is asaṃmūḍha, 'undeluded': someone no longer confused about what is ultimate. Plainly, the verse ties release from wrongdoing to seeing clearly what one's life finally rests on.
