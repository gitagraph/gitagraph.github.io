Bhagavad Gita 10.23

Krishna

रुद्राणां शंकरश्चास्मि वित्तेशो यक्षरक्षसाम् ।
वसूनां पावकश्चास्मि मेरुः शिखरिणामहम् ॥ १०.२३ ॥

rudrāṇāṃ śaṃkaraścāsmi vitteśo yakṣarakṣasām ।
vasūnāṃ pāvakaścāsmi meruḥ śikhariṇāmaham ॥

Among the Rudras I am Shankara; among the Yakshas and Rakshasas, Kubera, lord of wealth; among the Vasus I am fire; and among mountain peaks, Meru.

हिन्दीरुद्रों में मैं शंकर हूँ, यक्षों और राक्षसों में धन का स्वामी कुबेर हूँ, वसुओं में अग्नि हूँ और शिखर वाले पर्वतों में मेरु हूँ।

Krishna names four more glories: among the Rudras he is Shankara, among the Yakshas and Rakshasas Kubera, among the Vasus fire, and among peaks Meru.

Traditionally, the Rudras are a group of eleven storm-and-wild deities, the Vasus a group of eight elemental gods, Kubera is the guardian of wealth, and Meru the mountain at the centre of the world. The key word is pāvaka, 'the purifier', a name for fire that says what fire does.
