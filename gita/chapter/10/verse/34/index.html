Bhagavad Gita 10.34

Krishna

मृत्युः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् ।
कीर्तिः श्रीर्वाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेधा धृतिः क्षमा ॥ १०.३४ ॥

mṛtyuḥ sarvaharaścāhamudbhavaśca bhaviṣyatām ।
kīrtiḥ śrīrvākca nārīṇāṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā ॥

I am death that carries off everything, and the birth of all that is yet to be. Among feminine qualities I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forbearance.

हिन्दीसबको हर लेने वाली मृत्यु मैं हूँ और आगे होने वालों की उत्पत्ति भी मैं हूँ। स्त्रियों के गुणों में मैं कीर्ति, श्री, वाणी, स्मृति, मेधा, धैर्य और क्षमा हूँ।

Krishna names himself as death that carries off everything and as the birth of all that is yet to be, and then as seven qualities counted among the feminine.

The seven are kīrti (fame), śrī (prosperity), vāk (speech), smṛti (memory), medhā (intelligence), dhṛti (steadfastness) and kṣamā (forbearance). Nārīṇām literally means 'of women'; all seven words are feminine in Sanskrit, and traditionally they were also personified as goddesses, which is the usual explanation of the grouping.

People: Krishna
