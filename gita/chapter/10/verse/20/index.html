Bhagavad Gita 10.20

Krishna

अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः ।
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥ १०.२० ॥

ahamātmā guḍākeśa sarvabhūtāśayasthitaḥ ।
ahamādiśca madhyaṃ ca bhūtānāmanta eva ca ॥

I am the Self, Gudakesha, seated in the heart of every being. I am the beginning, the middle and the end of all beings.

हिन्दीहे गुडाकेश, मैं सब प्राणियों के हृदय में बसी आत्मा हूँ। मैं ही सब प्राणियों का आदि, मध्य और अंत हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

On a crowded commute, the stranger who elbows past you carries in their heart the same Self that is in yours. Remembering that won't make the train roomier, but it can soften the irritation.

Krishna opens his list of glories not with a god or a mountain but with the deepest one: he is the Self seated in the heart of every being, and the beginning, middle and end of all beings.

The key word is āśaya, the inner 'resting place', rendered here as the heart. Starting here frames all that follows: the outward examples point back to what is already within each being. Traditionally, Gudakesha means 'one who has mastered sleep'.
