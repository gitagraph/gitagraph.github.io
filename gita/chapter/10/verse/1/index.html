Bhagavad Gita 10.1

Krishna

भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥ १०.१ ॥

bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ ।
yatte'haṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā ॥

Listen once more, mighty-armed one, to my supreme word, which I will speak to you because you delight in it and because I wish you well.

हिन्दीहे महाबाहो, मेरे परम वचन को एक बार फिर सुनो, जिसे मैं तुम्हारे हित की इच्छा से कहूँगा, क्योंकि तुम उसे सुनकर प्रसन्न होते हो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When a grandparent tells you the same story again, it's because you once listened gladly and they wish you well. Hear it that way, and you may catch something you missed the first time.

Opening chapter 10, Krishna, without being asked, offers to speak his supreme word once more, because Arjuna delights in hearing it and because he wishes him well.

Two motives are named: prīyamāṇa, Arjuna's pleasure in listening, and hita-kāmyā, Krishna's wish for his good. The verse frames the teaching as something given out of care to a willing listener, setting a warm tone for the account of his glories that follows.
