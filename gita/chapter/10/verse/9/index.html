Bhagavad Gita 10.9

Krishna

मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् ।
कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च ॥ १०.९ ॥

maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam ।
kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca ॥

With their minds on me and their lives given to me, enlightening one another and always speaking of me, they are content and full of joy.

हिन्दीउनका चित्त मुझमें लगा रहता है, उनके प्राण मुझे अर्पित रहते हैं; वे एक-दूसरे को मेरा बोध कराते हुए और सदा मेरी ही चर्चा करते हुए संतुष्ट और आनंदित रहते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The friendships that keep you steady are often the ones where you talk about what really matters and help each other see more clearly. Make room for those conversations, not only for small talk.

Krishna describes how those devoted to him live: minds on him, lives given to him, speaking of him with one another.

The key phrase is bodhayantaḥ parasparam, 'enlightening one another': devotion here is not solitary but lived in conversation. The verse ends with two words of joy, tuṣyanti and ramanti, 'they are content' and 'they delight', pointing to the quiet satisfaction of talking about what one cares about most.
