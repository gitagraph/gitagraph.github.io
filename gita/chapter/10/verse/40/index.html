Bhagavad Gita 10.40

Krishna

नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परन्तप ।
एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया ॥ १०.४० ॥

nānto'sti mama divyānāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ parantapa ।
eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūtervistaro mayā ॥

There is no end to my divine glories, Parantapa. What I have said is only a brief indication of their extent.

हिन्दीहे परंतप, मेरी दिव्य विभूतियों का कोई अंत नहीं है। मैंने तो अपनी विभूतियों के विस्तार का यह केवल संक्षेप में वर्णन किया है।

Krishna tells Arjuna that his divine glories have no end, so the long list just given is only a sample.

Arjuna had asked to hear them in detail (10.18); the reply is that a full account is impossible. The key word is uddeśataḥ, 'by way of indication': pointing, not counting. The examples were meant to train the eye to recognise excellence, not to make an inventory that could ever be finished.
