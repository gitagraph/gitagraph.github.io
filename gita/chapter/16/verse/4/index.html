Bhagavad Gita 16.4

Krishna

दम्भो दर्पोऽभिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च ।
अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ संपदमासुरीम् ॥ १६.४ ॥

dambho darpo'bhimānaśca krodhaḥ pāruṣyameva ca ।
ajñānaṃ cābhijātasya pārtha saṃpadamāsurīm ॥

Hypocrisy, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance, Partha, belong to one born with the demonic nature.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, पाखंड, घमंड, अभिमान, क्रोध, कठोरता और अज्ञान — ये आसुरी स्वभाव लेकर जन्मे व्यक्ति के लक्षण हैं।

In your life

Snapping at the newest person on the team, then calling it 'just being direct': harshness and pride rarely look ugly from the inside. Noticing them is where changing them begins.

Krishna lists the marks of the demonic nature: hypocrisy, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance.

Āsurī comes from asura, traditionally the rivals of the gods, but here it describes a disposition of character. The list is short beside the divine one (16.1–3), and each item is a familiar human failing. Read plainly, it describes what anyone can become when self-importance takes charge.
