Bhagavad Gita 16.13

Krishna

इदमद्य मया लब्धमिमं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् ।
इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम् ॥ १६.१३ ॥

idamadya mayā labdhamimaṃ prāpsye manoratham ।
idamastīdamapi me bhaviṣyati punardhanam ॥

'This I have gained today; that wish I will fulfil next. This wealth is mine, and more will be mine again.'

हिन्दी'आज मैंने यह पा लिया, अब वह इच्छा पूरी करूँगा। यह धन मेरे पास है, और आगे और भी धन मेरा होगा।'

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

'This much I have, next year I'll have that': the mental shopping list rarely ends. Catching yourself in it is the moment to ask whether you are enjoying what is already here.

Krishna gives voice to the inner talk of the demonic person: 'This I gained today, that wish I will fulfil next; this wealth is mine, and more will come.'

Manoratha, 'chariot of the mind', is a wish or daydream that carries one forward. The verse opens a short monologue (16.13–15) whose tone is the point: every thought is about having and getting more. It is a voice many people can recognise.
