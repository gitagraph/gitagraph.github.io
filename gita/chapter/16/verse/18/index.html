Bhagavad Gita 16.18

Krishna

अहंकारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः ।
मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥ १६.१८ ॥

ahaṃkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ ।
māmātmaparadeheṣu pradviṣanto'bhyasūyakāḥ ॥

Clinging to ego, force, arrogance, lust and anger, these envious people hate me, who dwell in their own bodies and in the bodies of others.

हिन्दीअहंकार, बल, घमंड, काम और क्रोध का सहारा लेकर, ये दूसरों से जलने वाले लोग अपने और दूसरों के शरीर में स्थित मुझसे द्वेष करते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

In a long quarrel with a neighbour over parking or noise, remember that the same life moving in you moves in them. Contempt aimed at them quietly sours you too.

Krishna says the demonic, relying on ego, force, arrogance, lust and anger, hate him, who dwells in their own bodies and in the bodies of others.

Abhyasūyaka means one who resents others, finding fault with their good. The striking phrase is ātmaparadeheṣu, 'in one's own and others' bodies': hostility to others is hostility to what lives in everyone, oneself included. Contempt for others thus turns into a kind of self-harm.
