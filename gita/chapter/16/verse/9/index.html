Bhagavad Gita 16.9

Krishna

एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः ।
प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः ॥ १६.९ ॥

etāṃ dṛṣṭimavaṣṭabhya naṣṭātmāno'lpabuddhayaḥ ।
prabhavantyugrakarmāṇaḥ kṣayāya jagato'hitāḥ ॥

Clinging to this view, these lost souls of small understanding and cruel deeds rise up as enemies of the world, bent on its destruction.

हिन्दीइस दृष्टि को पकड़कर, जिनकी आत्मा खो चुकी है और बुद्धि छोटी है, ऐसे क्रूर कर्म करने वाले लोग जगत के शत्रु बनकर उसके विनाश के लिए उठ खड़े होते हैं।

Krishna says that those who cling to this view, lost and of small understanding, turn to cruel deeds and rise up as enemies of the world, bent on its ruin.

Naṣṭātman, 'one whose self is lost', describes someone cut off from their own inner ground. The verse ties belief to conduct: a world seen as baseless and driven only by desire invites treating others as obstacles. Ugrakarman, 'of cruel deeds', marks that turn to harm.

People: Krishna

Ideas: Asuri Sampad
