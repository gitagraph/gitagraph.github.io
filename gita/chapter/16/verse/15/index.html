Bhagavad Gita 16.15

Krishna

आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया ।
यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः ॥ १६.१५ ॥

āḍhyo'bhijanavānasmi ko'nyo'sti sadṛśo mayā ।
yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣya ityajñānavimohitāḥ ॥

'I am rich and well born; who else is my equal? I will perform sacrifices, I will give gifts, I will enjoy myself.' So they speak, led astray by ignorance.

हिन्दी'मैं धनवान हूँ, ऊँचे कुल का हूँ; मेरे बराबर और कौन है? मैं यज्ञ करूँगा, दान दूँगा और मौज करूँगा।' अज्ञान से भ्रमित होकर वे ऐसा कहते हैं।

In your life

Planning a wedding, ask of each big expense whether it serves the couple or is there so relatives say nobody has done it better. Giving and celebrating done for display lose their point.

Krishna completes the boastful monologue of the demonic person: 'I am rich and well born; who else is my equal? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will enjoy myself.'

Abhijanavān means 'of high family'. Even sacrifice and giving, counted among the divine qualities in 16.1, appear here as display, done to be seen. Krishna names the source plainly: ajñānavimohita, 'led astray by ignorance'.

People: Krishna
