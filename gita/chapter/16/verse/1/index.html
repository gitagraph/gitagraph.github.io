Bhagavad Gita 16.1

Krishna

अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥ १६.१ ॥

abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhirjñānayogavyavasthitiḥ ।
dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam ॥

Fearlessness, a purified inner being, steadiness in the path of knowledge, generosity, self-restraint, sacrifice, study of scripture, austerity and straightforwardness…

हिन्दीनिर्भयता, अंतःकरण की शुद्धि, ज्ञान-योग में दृढ़ता, दान, इंद्रियों पर संयम, यज्ञ, शास्त्रों का अध्ययन, तप और सरलता…

In your life

When you spot a mistake in your own work, tell your team early and plainly instead of hoping nobody notices. That small act of fearlessness and straightforwardness is where the 'divine wealth' of this list begins.

Krishna begins listing the qualities of the divine nature, starting with fearlessness, inner purity and steadiness on the path of knowledge.

The list runs across 16.1–3 and is called daivī sampad, 'divine wealth' (16.3). It mixes inner states, such as sattvasaṃśuddhi, a cleansed inner being, with outward practices like giving, sacrifice and study. Read as a whole, it sketches a character rather than a checklist: unafraid, honest, generous and disciplined.
