Bhagavad Gita 16.14

Krishna

असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि ।
ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी ॥ १६.१४ ॥

asau mayā hataḥ śatrurhaniṣye cāparānapi ।
īśvaro'hamahaṃ bhogī siddho'haṃ balavānsukhī ॥

'That enemy I have killed, and I will kill the others too. I am the master, I am the enjoyer; I am successful, strong and happy.'

हिन्दी'उस शत्रु को मैंने मार डाला, और दूसरों को भी मारूँगा। मैं ही स्वामी हूँ, मैं ही भोगने वाला हूँ, मैं सफल, बलवान और सुखी हूँ।'

In your life

After beating a rival to a deal, 'I'm the best, I'm unstoppable' feels wonderful for a day. Notice how much of that is pride talking, and how fragile it is.

In Krishna's portrait, the demonic person's inner talk turns to boasts of power: 'That enemy I have killed, the others I will kill; I am the master, the enjoyer, successful, strong and happy.'

Īśvara, 'lord', is the very title given to the Supreme in 15.17, now claimed by the ego for itself. The repeated aham, 'I', carries the verse. Possession has become domination, with the self placed at the centre of everything.
