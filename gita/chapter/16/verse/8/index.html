Bhagavad Gita 16.8

Krishna

असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् ।
अपरस्परसंभूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥ १६.८ ॥

asatyamapratiṣṭhaṃ te jagadāhuranīśvaram ।
aparasparasaṃbhūtaṃ kimanyatkāmahaitukam ॥

They say the world is without truth, without foundation and without God; that it has come about through the union of male and female and nothing else, with desire as its only cause.

हिन्दीवे कहते हैं कि यह जगत असत्य है, इसका कोई आधार नहीं है और कोई ईश्वर नहीं है; यह केवल स्त्री-पुरुष के मेल से पैदा हुआ है, और काम के सिवा इसका कोई कारण नहीं है।

Krishna reports the worldview of the demonic: the world has no truth, no foundation and no God, and arises from desire alone.

Anīśvara means 'without a Lord'. The compound aparasparasaṃbhūta is read here as 'born of the union of male and female'; some commentators parse it instead as 'not arising in any ordered causal sequence'. Either way, the view described leaves nothing above appetite to guide a life.
