Chapter 16 · Divine and Demonic NaturesVerse 5 of 24

Bhagavad Gita 16.5

Krishna

दैवी संपद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता ।
मा शुचः संपदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥ १६.५ ॥

daivī saṃpadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā ।
mā śucaḥ saṃpadaṃ daivīmabhijāto'si pāṇḍava ॥

The divine nature is held to lead to liberation, and the demonic to bondage. Do not grieve, Pandava; you were born with the divine nature.

हिन्दीदैवी स्वभाव मुक्ति की ओर ले जाता है और आसुरी स्वभाव बंधन की ओर, ऐसा माना गया है। हे पांडव, शोक मत करो, तुम दैवी स्वभाव लेकर जन्मे हो।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If you snapped at your child today and now feel you are simply a bad parent, set that grief down. One harsh moment doesn't define your nature; apologise, and start again from the better self you know is there.

Krishna says the divine nature leads to liberation and the demonic to bondage, then reassures Arjuna that he was born with the divine one.

Vimokṣa is release and nibandha is binding. Mā śucaḥ, 'do not grieve', meets a worry Arjuna might feel on hearing such a list, and echoes the opening of the teaching in 2.11, which also began with grief. The tone is encouragement: the description is a mirror held up, not a sentence passed.
