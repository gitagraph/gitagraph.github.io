Chapter 16 · Divine and Demonic NaturesVerse 19 of 24

Bhagavad Gita 16.19

Krishna

तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् ।
क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥ १६.१९ ॥

tānahaṃ dviṣataḥ krūrānsaṃsāreṣu narādhamān ।
kṣipāmyajasramaśubhānāsurīṣveva yoniṣu ॥

These hateful, cruel, inauspicious people, the lowest of humans, I hurl again and again into demonic wombs within the cycles of birth and death.

हिन्दीऐसे द्वेष करने वाले, क्रूर और अशुभ, मनुष्यों में सबसे नीच लोगों को मैं संसार में बार-बार आसुरी योनियों में ही डालता हूँ।

Krishna says that he casts these hateful, cruel people, the lowest of humans, again and again into demonic births within the cycle of rebirth.

Yoni here means womb or birth, the kind of life one is born into, and ajasram means 'ceaselessly'. Many readers take this as Krishna describing how the moral order works, each nature leading to births that match it, rather than as personal punishment; readings differ.
