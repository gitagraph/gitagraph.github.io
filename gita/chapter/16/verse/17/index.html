Bhagavad Gita 16.17

Krishna

आत्मसंभाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः ।
यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १६.१७ ॥

ātmasaṃbhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhanamānamadānvitāḥ ।
yajante nāmayajñaiste dambhenāvidhipūrvakam ॥

Self-admiring, stubborn, drunk on wealth and status, they perform sacrifices in name only, out of show and without following the prescribed rules.

हिन्दीअपने को ही बड़ा मानने वाले, अकड़े हुए, धन और मान के नशे में चूर, वे दिखावे के लिए, विधि-विधान को छोड़कर केवल नाम के यज्ञ करते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If a donation or a prayer doesn't feel complete until it's posted online, it may be turning into a ritual in name only. Try doing the next one quietly and notice how it feels.

Krishna says the demonic, self-admiring, stubborn and drunk on wealth and status, perform sacrifices in name only, for show and without proper method.

Nāmayajña, 'sacrifice in name', is ritual that keeps the label and loses the meaning. Dambha, hypocrisy, returns from 16.4 and 16.10. The point reaches well beyond ritual: any religious or generous act can be hollowed out when its real aim is to be admired.
