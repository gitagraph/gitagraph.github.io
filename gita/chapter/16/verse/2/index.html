Bhagavad Gita 16.2

Krishna

अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् ।
दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम् ॥ १६.२ ॥

ahiṃsā satyamakrodhastyāgaḥ śāntirapaiśunam ।
dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam ॥

…non-violence, truthfulness, freedom from anger, renunciation, calm, not slandering others, compassion for all beings, freedom from greed, gentleness, a sense of shame and steadiness of purpose…

हिन्दी…अहिंसा, सत्य, क्रोध न करना, त्याग, शांति, किसी की चुगली न करना, सब प्राणियों पर दया, लोभ न होना, कोमलता, लज्जा और चंचलता का अभाव…

Krishna continues the list of divine qualities with non-violence, truthfulness, calm, compassion for all beings and gentleness.

Apaiśuna means not speaking ill of others behind their backs, and hrī is a healthy sense of shame at doing wrong. Most items here concern how we treat others: harming no one, not slandering, not grasping. The last, acāpala, is steadiness of purpose, not being pulled about by every passing impulse.
