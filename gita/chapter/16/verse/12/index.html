Bhagavad Gita 16.12

Krishna

आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः ।
ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान् ॥ १६.१२ ॥

āśāpāśaśatairbaddhāḥ kāmakrodhaparāyaṇāḥ ।
īhante kāmabhogārthamanyāyenārthasañcayān ॥

…bound by hundreds of ropes of hope, given over to lust and anger, they strive to pile up wealth by unjust means for the sake of enjoying their desires.

हिन्दी…आशा के सैकड़ों फंदों में बँधे हुए, काम और क्रोध के वश में होकर, वे विषय-भोग के लिए अन्याय से धन इकट्ठा करने की कोशिश करते हैं।

Krishna says such people, bound by hundreds of ropes of hope and ruled by lust and anger, pile up wealth by unjust means to feed their desires.

Āśāpāśa, 'the noose of hope', pictures expectations as cords that tie a person down. Anyāyena, 'unjustly', shows where this leads: the need to satisfy desire overrides fairness. The verse completes the sentence begun in 16.11.
