Bhagavad Gita 16.10

Krishna

काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः ।
मोहाद्गृहीत्वासद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः ॥ १६.१० ॥

kāmamāśritya duṣpūraṃ dambhamānamadānvitāḥ ।
mohādgṛhītvāsadgrāhānpravartante'śucivratāḥ ॥

Giving themselves to desire that can never be filled, full of hypocrisy, pride and intoxication, they seize false notions out of delusion and pursue their aims with impure resolves.

हिन्दीकभी पूरी न होने वाली कामना का सहारा लेकर, पाखंड, मान और मद से भरे हुए, वे मोहवश गलत धारणाएँ पकड़ लेते हैं और अपवित्र संकल्पों के साथ काम में लगे रहते हैं।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The salary hike you longed for last year already feels ordinary, and the next number is calling. Desire grows the more it is fed; seeing that is the first step off the treadmill.

Krishna describes the demonic as clinging to desire that can never be filled, swollen with hypocrisy, pride and intoxication.

Duṣpūra means 'hard to fill', desire that grows the more it is fed. Asadgrāha are false fixations, wrong notions seized out of delusion, and aśucivrata are impure resolves. Plainly, the verse pictures a life organised around appetite, with beliefs and plans bending to serve it.
