Chapter 7 · 30 verses

Knowledge and Realisation

ज्ञानविज्ञानयोग

Krishna describes his two natures: a lower eightfold prakriti (earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, ego) and a higher nature that is life itself. His maya, made of the three gunas, is hard to cross, except for those who take refuge in him. Four kinds of people worship him: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of gain, and the wise.

कृष्ण अपनी दो प्रकृतियों का वर्णन करते हैं: आठ प्रकार की अपरा प्रकृति (पृथ्वी, जल, अग्नि, वायु, आकाश, मन, बुद्धि, अहंकार) और परा प्रकृति, जो स्वयं जीवन है। तीन गुणों से बनी उनकी माया को पार करना कठिन है, सिवाय उनके जो उनकी शरण लेते हैं। चार प्रकार के लोग उन्हें भजते हैं: आर्त, जिज्ञासु, अर्थार्थी और ज्ञानी।

  1. 7.1

    Listen, Partha, to how — with your mind attached to me, practising yoga and taking refuge in me — you will come to know me fully and without doubt.

  2. 7.2

    I will explain to you in full this knowledge together with its realisation; once you know it, nothing further remains to be known here.

  3. 7.3

    Among thousands of people, hardly one strives for perfection; and even among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows me as I truly am.

  4. 7.4

    Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and the sense of 'I' — this is my nature, divided eightfold.

  5. 7.5

    This is my lower nature. But know, mighty-armed one, that beyond it I have another, higher nature — the living principle by which this world is upheld.

  6. 7.6

    Understand that all beings have these two as their source. I am the origin of the whole world and also its dissolution.

  7. 7.7

    Dhananjaya, there is nothing whatever higher than me. All this is strung on me, like a row of gems on a thread.

  8. 7.8

    Son of Kunti, I am the taste in water, the light in the moon and the sun, the syllable Om in all the Vedas, the sound in space, and the vigour in human beings.

  9. 7.9

    I am the pure fragrance in the earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

  10. 7.10

    Partha, know me as the eternal seed of all beings. I am the intelligence of the intelligent and the splendour of the splendid.

  11. 7.11

    In the strong I am strength free of craving and attachment; and in beings, best of the Bharatas, I am desire that does not go against dharma.

  12. 7.12

    Whatever states of being there are — sattvic, rajasic or tamasic — know that they come from me alone. Yet I am not in them; they are in me.

  13. 7.13

    Deluded by these states made of the three gunas, this whole world fails to recognise me, who stand beyond them, imperishable.

  14. 7.14

    This divine maya of mine, made of the gunas, is very hard to get past; but those who take refuge in me alone cross beyond it.

  15. 7.15

    Evildoers, the deluded and the lowest of people do not turn to me — their understanding stolen by maya, they have taken on a demonic disposition.

  16. 7.16

    Four kinds of virtuous people worship me, Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise, best of the Bharatas.

  17. 7.17

    Of these the wise one, ever united and devoted to the One alone, stands out. For I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and the wise one is dear to me.

  18. 7.18

    All of these are noble, but the wise one I consider my very Self; for, steadfast in mind, he is anchored in me alone as the highest goal.

  19. 7.19

    Only after many births does the one who truly knows take refuge in me, seeing that Vasudeva is all there is. A great soul like that is hard indeed to find.

  20. 7.20

    Those whose understanding has been carried off by this or that desire turn to other gods, following one observance or another, driven by their own nature.

  21. 7.21

    Whatever form a devotee wishes to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his unwavering.

  22. 7.22

    Endowed with that faith he seeks to worship that form, and from it he obtains his desires — yet those are in truth granted by me alone.

  23. 7.23

    But the fruit gained by those of small understanding is perishable. Worshippers of the gods go to the gods; my devotees come to me.

  24. 7.24

    The undiscerning think of me, the unmanifest, as having taken on a manifest form, not knowing my higher being, which is imperishable and unsurpassed.

  25. 7.25

    Veiled by my yogamaya, I am not revealed to everyone. This deluded world does not recognise me as unborn and imperishable.

  26. 7.26

    Arjuna, I know the beings of the past, the present and the future; but no one knows me.

  27. 7.27

    Through the delusion of opposites that arises from liking and aversion, Bharata, all beings fall into bewilderment at birth, scorcher of foes.

  28. 7.28

    But people of virtuous deeds whose wrongdoing has come to an end are freed from the delusion of opposites and worship me with firm resolve.

  29. 7.29

    Those who take refuge in me and strive for release from old age and death come to know that Brahman, the whole of the inner self, and all action.

  30. 7.30

    Those who know me together with the physical realm, the divine realm and the lord of sacrifice — they, with minds steadfast, know me even at the moment of departure.
