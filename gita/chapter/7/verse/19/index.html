Bhagavad Gita 7.19

Krishna

बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः ॥ ७.१९ ॥

bahūnāṃ janmanāmante jñānavānmāṃ prapadyate ।
vāsudevaḥ sarvamiti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ ॥

Only after many births does the one who truly knows take refuge in me, seeing that Vasudeva is all there is. A great soul like that is hard indeed to find.

हिन्दीअनेक जन्मों के अंत में ज्ञानवान मनुष्य यह जानकर मेरी शरण लेता है कि सब कुछ वासुदेव ही है। ऐसा महात्मा अत्यंत दुर्लभ है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Some understandings take decades to arrive: that your parents did their best, that everyone is carrying something. When one finally lands, recognise how rare and hard-won it is.

Krishna says that only at the end of many births does the one who truly knows take refuge in him, seeing that Vāsudeva is everything — and such a great soul is very rare.

From the Mahabharata: Vāsudeva, 'son of Vasudeva', is a name of Krishna; traditionally, it is also read as 'the one who dwells in all'. The phrase vāsudevaḥ sarvam, 'Vasudeva is all', sums up the chapter's picture of everything strung on one thread (7.7).
