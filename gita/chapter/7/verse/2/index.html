Chapter 7 · Knowledge and RealisationVerse 2 of 30

Bhagavad Gita 7.2

Krishna

ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते ॥ ७.२ ॥

jñānaṃ te'haṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ ।
yajjñātvā neha bhūyo'nyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate ॥

I will explain to you in full this knowledge together with its realisation; once you know it, nothing further remains to be known here.

हिन्दीमैं तुम्हें यह ज्ञान उसके अनुभव सहित पूरा-पूरा बताऊँगा, जिसे जान लेने के बाद यहाँ और कुछ जानने योग्य शेष नहीं रहता।

Krishna promises to explain this knowledge in full, together with its realisation, so that once it is known nothing further remains to be known.

The pair is jñāna, 'knowledge', and vijñāna, here 'realisation'. Some traditions take vijñāna as direct experience of what is taught, others as detailed knowledge of the manifest world in all its variety. Either way, what is promised is more than information: knowledge complete enough to leave no remainder.
