Bhagavad Gita 7.7

Krishna

मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किंचिदस्ति धनंजय ।
मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥ ७.७ ॥

mattaḥ parataraṃ nānyatkiṃcidasti dhanaṃjaya ।
mayi sarvamidaṃ protaṃ sūtre maṇigaṇā iva ॥

Dhananjaya, there is nothing whatever higher than me. All this is strung on me, like a row of gems on a thread.

हिन्दीहे धनंजय, मुझसे बढ़कर दूसरा कुछ भी नहीं है। यह सब कुछ मुझमें वैसे ही पिरोया हुआ है जैसे धागे में मणियों के दाने।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A busy week looks like loose beads: meetings, chores, a school pickup. Look for the thread running through them, whatever you hold highest, and the scattered days start to hang together as one life.

Krishna declares that nothing at all stands higher than him, and that everything is strung on him like gems on a thread.

The image turns on sūtra, 'thread': the gems are what one sees, while the thread running through them is hidden yet holds the string together. The comparison suggests a unity that does not erase variety — each gem stays distinct, but none would stay in place without what passes through it.
