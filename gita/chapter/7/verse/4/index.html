Bhagavad Gita 7.4

Krishna

भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च ।
अहंकार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा ॥ ७.४ ॥

bhūmirāpo'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca ।
ahaṃkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā ॥

Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and the sense of 'I' — this is my nature, divided eightfold.

हिन्दीपृथ्वी, जल, अग्नि, वायु, आकाश, मन, बुद्धि और अहंकार — यह मेरी आठ भागों में बँटी हुई प्रकृति है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When pride flares because a sibling teased you at a family dinner, recall that the verse counts the ego as part of changing nature, like earth or water. It is something you have, not who you are.

Krishna begins describing his nature by listing the eight parts of what the next verse calls his lower nature.

Five are physical — earth, water, fire, air and space — and three are inner: manas (mind), buddhi (intellect) and ahaṃkāra, the sense of 'I'. The striking point is that thought and ego are counted alongside matter, as part of the same changing nature rather than as the true self.
