Bhagavad Gita 7.10

Krishna

बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् ।
बुद्धिर्बुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ॥ ७.१० ॥

bījaṃ māṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ viddhi pārtha sanātanam ।
buddhirbuddhimatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham ॥

Partha, know me as the eternal seed of all beings. I am the intelligence of the intelligent and the splendour of the splendid.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, मुझे सब प्राणियों का सनातन बीज जानो। मैं बुद्धिमानों की बुद्धि और तेजस्वियों का तेज हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When your child shows a spark, for drawing or for numbers, see it as something shining through them rather than a trophy for you. Nurture it gently; it was never yours to own.

Krishna calls himself the eternal seed of all beings, and the intelligence of the intelligent and the splendour of the splendid.

The key word is bījam, 'seed': a seed is where a plant comes from, and sanātana, 'eternal', marks this one as never exhausted. The second line turns to human excellence: whatever understanding or brilliance a person shows is presented as drawn from that same source.
