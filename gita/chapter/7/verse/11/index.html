Bhagavad Gita 7.11

Krishna

बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ ॥ ७.११ ॥

balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam ।
dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo'smi bharatarṣabha ॥

In the strong I am strength free of craving and attachment; and in beings, best of the Bharatas, I am desire that does not go against dharma.

हिन्दीमैं बलवानों का कामना और आसक्ति से रहित बल हूँ; और हे भरतश्रेष्ठ, प्राणियों में धर्म के अनुकूल काम मैं हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Wanting a bigger flat or a raise is not wrong in itself. The test is whether the wanting stays within what is fair: honest money, no corners cut, no one trampled on the way.

Krishna names two more presences, each with a condition: the strength of the strong that is free of craving and attachment, and desire in beings that does not go against dharma.

The key phrase is dharmāviruddha, 'not opposed to dharma'. The verse does not condemn strength or desire as such; it draws the line at how they are held — strength without craving, desire within what is right.
