Chapter 7 · Knowledge and RealisationVerse 26 of 30

Bhagavad Gita 7.26

Krishna

वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन ।
भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन ॥ ७.२६ ॥

vedāhaṃ samatītāni vartamānāni cārjuna ।
bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni māṃ tu veda na kaścana ॥

Arjuna, I know the beings of the past, the present and the future; but no one knows me.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, मैं भूतकाल, वर्तमान और भविष्य के सभी प्राणियों को जानता हूँ, परंतु मुझे कोई नहीं जानता।

Krishna says he knows all beings of the past, present and future, yet no one knows him.

The key word is veda, 'I know', set against na kaścana veda, 'no one knows'. The verse completes a run on his being unrecognised (7.24–26): the knowing runs one way. It also recalls 4.5, where Krishna told Arjuna that he knows all the past births of both of them, while Arjuna does not.
