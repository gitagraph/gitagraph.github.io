Bhagavad Gita 7.5

Krishna

अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् ।
जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं धार्यते जगत् ॥ ७.५ ॥

apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām ।
jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat ॥

This is my lower nature. But know, mighty-armed one, that beyond it I have another, higher nature — the living principle by which this world is upheld.

हिन्दीयह मेरी अपरा प्रकृति है। हे महाबाहु, इससे भिन्न मेरी एक परा प्रकृति जानो, जो जीवरूप है और जिससे यह सारा जगत धारण किया जाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

The flat, the furniture and even your moods are part of changing nature. Under them is the life that makes you you; don't mistake the house for the one who lives in it.

Krishna calls the eightfold nature of elements, mind and ego his lower nature, and points to a higher one: the living principle by which the world is upheld.

The Sanskrit is parā prakṛti, described as jīvabhūtā, 'become the living being' — the conscious life in creatures, which some traditions read as the individual selves taken together. On one side stand body, mind and ego; on the other, the life that knows and animates them — and Krishna calls both his own.
