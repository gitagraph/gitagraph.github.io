Chapter 7 · Knowledge and RealisationVerse 15 of 30

Bhagavad Gita 7.15

Krishna

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः ।
माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः ॥ ७.१५ ॥

na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ ।
māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ ॥

Evildoers, the deluded and the lowest of people do not turn to me — their understanding stolen by maya, they have taken on a demonic disposition.

हिन्दीदुष्कर्म करने वाले, मूढ़ और नीच मनुष्य मेरी शरण नहीं आते; माया ने उनका ज्ञान हर लिया है और वे आसुरी स्वभाव का आश्रय लिए रहते हैं।

In your life

When someone keeps doing harm and refuses to look inward, it is often because something has taken over their judgement. Keep your distance where you must, and don't let their darkness become yours.

Krishna describes those who do not turn to him: wrongdoers, the deluded and the lowest of people, whose understanding māyā has carried off and who have taken on an āsura disposition.

Āsura, 'demonic', names a set of traits that the Gita details in chapter 16 — chiefly arrogance and self-will — rather than a race of beings. The word apahṛtajñāna, 'knowledge carried off', frames this as something lost, not only something chosen.
