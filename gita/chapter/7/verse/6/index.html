Bhagavad Gita 7.6

Krishna

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय ।
अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा ॥ ७.६ ॥

etadyonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya ।
ahaṃ kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayastathā ॥

Understand that all beings have these two as their source. I am the origin of the whole world and also its dissolution.

हिन्दीसमझ लो कि सभी प्राणी इन्हीं दोनों प्रकृतियों से उत्पन्न होते हैं। मैं ही संपूर्ण जगत की उत्पत्ति हूँ और उसका प्रलय भी।

Krishna concludes that all beings arise from these two natures together, and that he himself is where the whole world begins and where it dissolves.

The word yoni, 'womb' or 'source', in etadyonīni frames the two natures as a joint origin. Prabhava, coming forth, and pralaya, dissolution, are the two ends of the cycle; placing himself at both, Krishna presents himself not as one thing in the world but as its ground.
