Bhagavad Gita 7.1

Krishna

मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः ।
असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु ॥ ७.१ ॥

mayyāsaktamanāḥ pārtha yogaṃ yuñjanmadāśrayaḥ ।
asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu ॥

Listen, Partha, to how — with your mind attached to me, practising yoga and taking refuge in me — you will come to know me fully and without doubt.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, सुनो कि मुझमें आसक्त मन से, मेरा आश्रय लेकर योग का अभ्यास करते हुए तुम मुझे पूर्ण रूप से और बिना संशय के कैसे जानोगे।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Getting to know anyone deeply, a spouse, an old friend, takes attention, practice and some trust. Understanding the deepest things in life asks for the same three.

Krishna opens a new teaching: how Arjuna, with his mind fixed on him and relying on him, can come to know him completely and without doubt.

The previous chapter ended by praising the yogi who worships Krishna with faith (6.47); this one takes up what such a person comes to know. The key word is samagram, 'entire': not a partial glimpse but the whole picture, which the coming verses begin to lay out.
