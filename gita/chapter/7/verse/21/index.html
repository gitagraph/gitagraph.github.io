Bhagavad Gita 7.21

Krishna

यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति ।
तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम् ॥ ७.२१ ॥

yo yo yāṃ yāṃ tanuṃ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārcitumicchati ।
tasya tasyācalāṃ śraddhāṃ tāmeva vidadhāmyaham ॥

Whatever form a devotee wishes to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his unwavering.

हिन्दीजो-जो भक्त जिस-जिस रूप की श्रद्धा से पूजा करना चाहता है, उसी-उसी की उस श्रद्धा को मैं अटल कर देता हूँ।

In your life

Your neighbour's way of praying may look nothing like yours. The verse honours sincere faith in whatever form it takes, so give theirs the respect you would want for your own.

Krishna says that whatever form a devotee chooses to worship with faith, he himself makes that faith steady.

The key word is śraddhā, 'faith' or 'trust', and the striking point is acalā, 'unwavering': Krishna does not redirect such worshippers but strengthens the trust they already have. The verse treats sincere faith in any form as something he supports, while the next verses go on to compare where such paths lead.
