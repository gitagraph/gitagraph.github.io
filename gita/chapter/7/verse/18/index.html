Bhagavad Gita 7.18

Krishna

उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम् ।
आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम् ॥ ७.१८ ॥

udārāḥ sarva evaite jñānī tvātmaiva me matam ।
āsthitaḥ sa hi yuktātmā māmevānuttamāṃ gatim ॥

All of these are noble, but the wise one I consider my very Self; for, steadfast in mind, he is anchored in me alone as the highest goal.

हिन्दीये सभी उदार हैं, पर ज्ञानी को तो मैं अपना ही स्वरूप मानता हूँ; क्योंकि वह स्थिर मन वाला मुझ सर्वोत्तम गति में ही टिका हुआ है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Some people turn to faith for help, some out of curiosity, some for wealth; all are welcome. The one who seeks understanding for its own sake is simply closest to the heart of it.

Krishna calls all four kinds of worshippers noble, but says he regards the wise one as his very self, because that person rests in him alone as the highest goal.

The key words are udāra, 'generous' or 'noble', given to everyone, and ātmā eva, 'the very self', reserved for the wise. Traditions differ here: some read it as the wise one being one with Krishna, others as being as close to him as his own self.
