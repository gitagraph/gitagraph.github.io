Bhagavad Gita 7.16

Krishna

चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥ ७.१६ ॥

caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino'rjuna ।
ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha ॥

Four kinds of virtuous people worship me, Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise, best of the Bharatas.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, चार प्रकार के पुण्यात्मा लोग मुझे भजते हैं — हे भरतश्रेष्ठ, दुःखी, जिज्ञासु, धन चाहने वाला और ज्ञानी।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Many people first turn to prayer when a job is lost or money runs out. That is not a lesser beginning; the verse counts even the distressed among the good.

Krishna sorts the people who do turn to him into four kinds: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise.

They are all called sukṛtin, 'doers of good', so the verse is generous: even worship that starts from trouble or from wanting something counts. The four words name motives — ārta, someone in pain; jijñāsu, someone who wants to understand; arthārthī, someone after a goal or wealth; jñānī, someone who already knows.
