Bhagavad Gita 7.17

Krishna

तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते ।
प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रियः ॥ ७.१७ ॥

teṣāṃ jñānī nityayukta ekabhaktirviśiṣyate ।
priyo hi jñānino'tyarthamahaṃ sa ca mama priyaḥ ॥

Of these the wise one, ever united and devoted to the One alone, stands out. For I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and the wise one is dear to me.

हिन्दीइनमें सदा मुझमें लगा हुआ, एक ही में भक्ति रखने वाला ज्ञानी श्रेष्ठ है। क्योंकि ज्ञानी को मैं अत्यंत प्रिय हूँ और वह मुझे प्रिय है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Drop in on an old friend not because you need a favour, just to see how they are. A bond that wants nothing but the other person is the kind both sides hold dear.

Of the four kinds of worshippers, Krishna singles out the wise one, ever united and devoted to the One alone, and says the love between them runs both ways.

Two words carry it: nityayukta, 'always joined', and ekabhakti, 'devotion with a single focus'. The others turn to him for something; the wise one wants nothing from him but him. The verse describes a relationship, not a reward — each is dear to the other.
