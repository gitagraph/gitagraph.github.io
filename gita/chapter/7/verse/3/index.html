Bhagavad Gita 7.3

Krishna

मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये ।
यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ॥ ७.३ ॥

manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye ।
yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ ॥

Among thousands of people, hardly one strives for perfection; and even among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows me as I truly am.

हिन्दीहजारों मनुष्यों में कोई एक ही सिद्धि के लिए प्रयत्न करता है, और उन प्रयत्न करने वाले सिद्धों में भी कोई एक ही मुझे तत्त्व से जानता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Plenty of people say they'll write a book someday; few begin, and fewer stay long enough to finish a draft. If you're one of the few still at your desk, don't wait for company.

Krishna stresses how rare it is to know him truly: among thousands, hardly one strives for perfection, and among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows him in truth.

The key word is tattvataḥ, 'in truth' or 'as it really is', from tattva, the real nature of a thing. In context, the verse underlines how rare the knowledge about to be given is, and that striving is only a first step.
