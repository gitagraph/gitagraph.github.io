Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 23 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.23

Krishna

यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।
प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ ॥ ८.२३ ॥

yatra kāle tvanāvṛttimāvṛttiṃ caiva yoginaḥ ।
prayātā yānti taṃ kālaṃ vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha ॥

Now, best of the Bharatas, I will tell you the times at which yogis who depart either do not return or do return.

हिन्दीहे भरतश्रेष्ठ, अब मैं तुम्हें वह समय बताऊँगा जिसमें शरीर छोड़कर गए योगी लौटकर नहीं आते, और जिसमें लौटकर आते हैं।

Krishna introduces a teaching about the times at which departing yogis either return to rebirth or do not, which he sets out in 8.24–26.

The key pair is anāvṛtti, 'no return', and āvṛtti, 'return'. Some read kāla, 'time', literally, as the hour or season of death; others, following older Upanishadic teaching, take it as a path marked by stages. Either way, the chapter's question about the end is now framed as two possible directions.
