Verse 8.28

Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 28 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.28

Krishna

वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव ।
दानेषु यत् पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम् ।
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा ।
योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥ ८.२८ ॥

vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapaḥsu caiva ।
dāneṣu yat puṇyaphalaṃ pradiṣṭam ।
atyeti tatsarvamidaṃ viditvā ।
yogī paraṃ sthānamupaiti cādyam ॥

Whatever reward of merit is promised for study of the Vedas, for sacrifices, austerities and gifts — the yogi who knows this goes beyond all of it and reaches the supreme, primal abode.

हिन्दीवेदों के अध्ययन, यज्ञों, तपों और दानों के लिए जो पुण्यफल बताया गया है, इसे जानने वाला योगी उन सबको पार कर जाता है और परम आदि धाम को प्राप्त होता है।

Krishna closes Chapter 8 by saying that the yogi who understands this teaching goes beyond every reward promised for Vedic study, sacrifice, austerity and giving, and reaches the supreme, primal abode.

The key word is puṇya-phala, the 'fruit of merit' such good works are said to earn. The Gita does not dismiss these practices; it ranks their rewards, like the heavens of 8.16 and 8.25, below a goal that does not run out.
