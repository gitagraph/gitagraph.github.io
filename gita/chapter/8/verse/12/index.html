Bhagavad Gita 8.12

Krishna

सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुध्य च ।
मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥ ८.१२ ॥

sarvadvārāṇi saṃyamya mano hṛdi nirudhya ca ।
mūrdhnyādhāyātmanaḥ prāṇamāsthito yogadhāraṇām ॥

Closing all the gates of the body, confining the mind in the heart, placing his life-breath at the crown of the head, and holding firm in yogic concentration...

हिन्दीशरीर के सब द्वारों को रोककर, मन को हृदय में स्थिर करके, अपने प्राण को मस्तक में स्थापित करके और योग की धारणा में दृढ़ रहकर…

Krishna describes the yogi's inner posture at the time of departure: closing all the body's gates, holding the mind in the heart and drawing the life-breath to the crown of the head, in a sentence that continues into 8.13.

Traditionally, the 'gates' are read as the senses, through which attention usually flows outward. The key term is yoga-dhāraṇā, steady yogic concentration. The picture is of attention gathered wholly inward instead of scattered.
