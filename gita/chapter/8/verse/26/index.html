Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 26 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.26

Krishna

शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययावर्तते पुनः ॥ ८.२६ ॥

śuklakṛṣṇe gatī hyete jagataḥ śāśvate mate ।
ekayā yātyanāvṛttimanyayāvartate punaḥ ॥

These two paths of the world, the bright and the dark, are held to be eternal. By one a person goes and does not return; by the other he comes back again.

हिन्दीजगत के ये दो मार्ग, शुक्ल और कृष्ण, सनातन माने गए हैं। एक से जाने वाला लौटकर नहीं आता, और दूसरे से जाने वाला फिर लौट आता है।

Krishna sums up the two routes as the bright and the dark paths of the world, both held to be eternal: one leads to no return, the other back to birth.

The key words are śukla and kṛṣṇa, 'bright' and 'dark', and śāśvata, 'eternal'. The word mate, 'held to be', marks this as an accepted teaching rather than a new claim. Plainly, the verse reduces the imagery of 8.24–25 to one contrast: a journey that ends and one that comes round again.
