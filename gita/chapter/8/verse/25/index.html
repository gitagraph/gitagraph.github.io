Bhagavad Gita 8.25

Krishna

धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतिर्योगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥ ८.२५ ॥

dhūmo rātristathā kṛṣṇaḥ ṣaṇmāsā dakṣiṇāyanam ।
tatra cāndramasaṃ jyotiryogī prāpya nivartate ॥

Smoke, night, the dark half of the month, the six months of the sun's southern course — taking this path, the yogi reaches the light of the moon and returns.

हिन्दीधुआँ, रात्रि, कृष्ण पक्ष और दक्षिणायन के छह महीने — इस मार्ग से जाने वाला योगी चंद्रमा की ज्योति पाकर लौट आता है।

Krishna describes the second, dark path, by which a yogi reaches the light of the moon and then returns to birth.

Its markers mirror the bright path: dhūma, smoke; night; the kṛṣṇa, dark, half of the month; and dakṣiṇāyana, the six months of the sun's southern course. Traditionally, the moon's light stands for a heavenly world earned by good deeds, enjoyed for a time and then left. The traveller is still called a yogi: this is a worthy path, only not a final one.
