Bhagavad Gita 8.24

Krishna

अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।
तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥ ८.२४ ॥

agnirjyotirahaḥ śuklaḥ ṣaṇmāsā uttarāyaṇam ।
tatra prayātā gacchanti brahma brahmavido janāḥ ॥

Fire, light, day, the bright half of the month, the six months of the sun's northern course — departing by this path, those who know Brahman go to Brahman.

हिन्दीअग्नि, ज्योति, दिन, शुक्ल पक्ष और उत्तरायण के छह महीने — इस मार्ग से जाने वाले ब्रह्मवेत्ता लोग ब्रह्म को प्राप्त होते हैं।

Krishna describes the bright path by which those who know Brahman go to Brahman.

It is named through a chain of luminous markers: agni, fire; jyotis, light; day; the śukla, bright, half of the lunar month; and uttarāyaṇa, the six months of the sun's northern course. Traditionally, these are read either as the actual time of death or as stages on a path of light, each watched over by a presiding power. Either way, the imagery links the final goal with ever-growing light.
