Bhagavad Gita 8.22

Krishna

पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया ।
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ ८.२२ ॥

puruṣaḥ sa paraḥ pārtha bhaktyā labhyastvananyayā ।
yasyāntaḥsthāni bhūtāni yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ॥

That supreme Person, Partha, within whom all beings dwell and by whom all this is pervaded, can be reached through undivided devotion.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, वह परम पुरुष, जिसके भीतर सब प्राणी स्थित हैं और जिससे यह सारा जगत व्याप्त है, अनन्य भक्ति से ही प्राप्त होता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Undivided attention is rare. Whether it is a friend in trouble or your own evening prayer, giving it your whole mind, even briefly, reaches further than half-attention ever does.

Krishna names the way to that highest goal: the supreme Person, within whom all beings dwell and by whom all this is pervaded, is reached through ananya bhakti, devotion that has no other object.

The verse holds two images together: everything lies within this Person, and this Person runs through everything. After the cosmic scale of 8.17–21, the means offered is close and personal, a single undivided turning of the heart.
