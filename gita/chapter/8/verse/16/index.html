Bhagavad Gita 8.16

Krishna

आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥ ८.१६ ॥

ābrahmabhuvanāllokāḥ punarāvartino'rjuna ।
māmupetya tu kaunteya punarjanma na vidyate ॥

Arjuna, all worlds, up to the realm of Brahma, are subject to return; but having come to me, son of Kunti, there is no further birth.

हिन्दीहे अर्जुन, ब्रह्मलोक तक के सभी लोक फिर लौटकर आने वाले हैं; परंतु हे कुंतीपुत्र, मुझे प्राप्त होने पर फिर जन्म नहीं होता।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Even the best job, the best house and the best holiday come round and go again. Enjoy them, but don't build your whole peace on any place you can be moved out of.

Krishna draws a sharp line between every world, even the highest, and reaching him: all worlds up to Brahma's realm are punar-āvartin, 'bound to return', but for one who comes to him there is no further birth.

Brahma here is the creator god, not Brahman, the ultimate. The verse means that even the finest heavens are temporary stations within the cycle of birth and death; only the goal beyond them, in the Gita's terms, is final.
