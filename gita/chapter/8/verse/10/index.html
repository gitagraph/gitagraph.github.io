Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 10 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.10

Krishna

प्रयाणकाले मनसाचलेन ।
भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक् ।
स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥ ८.१० ॥

prayāṇakāle manasācalena ।
bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva ।
bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samyak ।
sa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam ॥

↑ The sentence begins in 8.9

...at the time of departure, with an unwavering mind, filled with devotion and by the strength of yoga, drawing the life-breath fully between the eyebrows — he reaches that supreme divine Person.

हिन्दी…वह मृत्यु के समय अचल मन से, भक्ति से युक्त होकर और योगबल से प्राण को भौंहों के बीच भली-भाँति स्थापित करके, उस परम दिव्य पुरुष को प्राप्त होता है।

Completing the sentence begun in 8.9, Krishna says that one who holds that Person in mind at the time of departure, with an unmoving mind, reaches him.

Two strands meet here: bhakti, devotion, and yoga-bala, the strength that yogic training gives, seen in drawing the prāṇa, the life-breath, fully to the point between the eyebrows. The verse joins feeling and discipline rather than setting them against each other; the physical detail describes a practice of concentration at the very end.
