Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 21 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.21

Krishna

अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ ८.२१ ॥

avyakto'kṣara ityuktastamāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim ।
yaṃ prāpya na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama ॥

This unmanifest is called the imperishable, and it is said to be the supreme goal. Those who reach it do not return; that is my highest abode.

हिन्दीवह अव्यक्त अक्षर कहलाता है और उसी को परम गति कहते हैं। जिसे पाकर लोग लौटकर नहीं आते, वही मेरा परम धाम है।

Krishna names the deeper unmanifest of 8.20: it is called akṣara, the imperishable, the supreme goal from which those who reach it do not return, and it is his own highest dwelling, dhāma.

The verse ties together terms used across the chapter, akṣara from 8.3 and 8.11, paramā gati from 8.13 and no return from 8.15–16, and identifies them with Krishna's own abode. What looked like several goals turns out to be one.
