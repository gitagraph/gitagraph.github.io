Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 1 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.1

Arjuna

किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥ ८.१ ॥

kiṃ tadbrahma kimadhyātmaṃ kiṃ karma puruṣottama ।
adhibhūtaṃ ca kiṃ proktamadhidaivaṃ kimucyate ॥

Purushottama, what is that Brahman? What is the inner self? What is action? What is called the physical realm, and what is said to be the divine realm?

हिन्दीहे पुरुषोत्तम, वह ब्रह्म क्या है? अध्यात्म क्या है? कर्म क्या है? अधिभूत किसे कहा गया है और अधिदैव किसे कहते हैं?

Arjuna opens Chapter 8 by asking Krishna to define terms left unexplained at the close of Chapter 7 (7.29–30): Brahman, adhyātma (the inner self), karma (action), adhibhūta (the physical realm) and adhidaiva (the divine realm).

A sixth, adhiyajña, follows in 8.2. Arjuna addresses him as Puruṣottama, 'highest of persons'. For a reader, the questions work as a map of the chapter: what is ultimate, what the self is, and how the world and its powers relate to it.
