Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 3 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.3

Krishna

अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥ ८.३ ॥

akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo'dhyātmamucyate ।
bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ ॥

Brahman is the imperishable, the supreme. Its presence as one's own being is called the inner self. The creative outpouring that brings beings into existence is called action.

हिन्दीब्रह्म परम अक्षर है। प्रत्येक प्राणी में अपने स्वरूप के रूप में उसकी उपस्थिति अध्यात्म कहलाती है। प्राणियों को अस्तित्व में लाने वाला सृजनकारी विसर्ग कर्म कहलाता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Your job, your role at home and your title are activities you do. Underneath them is who you are; knowing the difference makes losing any one of them less frightening.

Krishna begins his answers: Brahman is the imperishable, the supreme; adhyātma is its presence as each being's own nature; and karma is the creative outpouring that brings beings into existence.

Akṣara means 'that which does not decay', and svabhāva means 'one's own being'. Karma here is not everyday work but visarga, 'sending forth'. Some traditions read svabhāva as the individual self, others as nature in a wider sense; visarga is also read as the ritual offering that sustains creation.
