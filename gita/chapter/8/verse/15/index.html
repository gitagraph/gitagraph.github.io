Verse 8.15

Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 15 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.15

Krishna

मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।
नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः ॥ ८.१५ ॥

māmupetya punarjanma duḥkhālayamaśāśvatam ।
nāpnuvanti mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ ॥

Having come to me, these great souls, who have reached the highest perfection, do not return to rebirth, that impermanent home of sorrow.

हिन्दीमुझे प्राप्त होकर ये महात्मा, जो परम सिद्धि को पहुँच चुके हैं, दुःखों के घर और नश्वर पुनर्जन्म को फिर नहीं पाते।

Krishna states what reaching him means: the mahātmās, great souls who have attained the highest perfection, do not return to rebirth.

Rebirth is described in two words: duḥkhālaya, 'a home of sorrow', and aśāśvata, 'impermanent'. In the Gita's view, any condition that keeps ending and beginning again cannot give lasting rest, and the goal Krishna names is the one that does not end. The verse prepares the contrast with even the highest worlds in 8.16.
