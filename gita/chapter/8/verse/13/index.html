Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 13 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.13

Krishna

ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ८.१३ ॥

omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanmāmanusmaran ।
yaḥ prayāti tyajandehaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim ॥

...uttering the single syllable Om, which is Brahman, and remembering me, whoever departs, leaving the body, reaches the supreme goal.

हिन्दी…जो ब्रह्मरूप एक अक्षर ॐ का उच्चारण करता हुआ और मुझे स्मरण करता हुआ शरीर त्यागकर जाता है, वह परम गति को प्राप्त होता है।

Completing the sentence begun in 8.12, Krishna says that whoever departs uttering Om and remembering him reaches paramā gati, the supreme goal.

Om is called ekākṣara brahma, 'the one-syllable Brahman': a single sound taken to stand for the ultimate itself. Voice and memory work together here, the syllable spoken while Krishna is remembered. For a reader, it shows the Gita using a sound as a support for attention, a way of gathering the mind onto one point.
