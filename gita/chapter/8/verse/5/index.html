Verse 8.5

Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 5 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.5

Krishna

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥ ८.५ ॥

antakāle ca māmeva smaranmuktvā kalevaram ।
yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ ॥

Whoever leaves the body at the time of death remembering me alone attains my being; of this there is no doubt.

हिन्दीजो अंत समय में केवल मुझे ही स्मरण करता हुआ शरीर छोड़कर जाता है, वह मेरे स्वरूप को प्राप्त होता है; इसमें कोई संदेह नहीं।

In your life

When you sit beside a grandparent in their last days, help them hold on to what they love most: a favourite prayer, a song, a familiar voice. The verse says what fills the mind at the end matters.

Krishna answers Arjuna's question about the time of death: whoever leaves the body remembering him alone reaches his very being, without doubt.

The key word is smaran, 'remembering', and the goal is mad-bhāva, 'my state of being'. Kalevara is a plain word for the body, here something simply set down. The verse introduces the chapter's central idea, that what fills the mind at the end matters, and the following verses explain why that last thought depends on a whole life.
