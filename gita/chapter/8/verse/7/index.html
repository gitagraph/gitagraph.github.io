Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 7 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.7

Krishna

तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयः ॥ ८.७ ॥

tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca ।
mayyarpitamanobuddhirmāmevaiṣyasyasaṃśayaḥ ॥

So at all times remember me, and fight. With mind and intellect offered to me, you will surely come to me.

हिन्दीइसलिए हर समय मुझे स्मरण करो और युद्ध भी करो। मुझमें मन और बुद्धि अर्पित करके तुम निःसंदेह मुझे ही प्राप्त होगे।

In your life

Keep what you hold highest in mind while you work, not instead of working. A bus driver on a long night route can carry that remembrance and still give the road everything.

Drawing a practical conclusion from 8.5–6, Krishna tells Arjuna to remember him at all times and, at the same time, to fight.

The two commands are joined by a simple ca, 'and': remembrance is not a retreat from duty but something carried into it. The phrase arpita-mano-buddhiḥ means with mind and intellect 'offered' to him. Plainly, it describes a steady inner orientation kept alive in the middle of demanding work, not apart from it.
