Chapter 8 · The Imperishable BrahmanVerse 2 of 28

Bhagavad Gita 8.2

Arjuna

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥ ८.२ ॥

adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko'tra dehe'sminmadhusūdana ।
prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo'si niyatātmabhiḥ ॥

Madhusudana, who is the lord of sacrifice here in this body, and how? And how are you to be known at the time of death by those who have mastered themselves?

हिन्दीहे मधुसूदन, इस शरीर में अधियज्ञ कौन है और कैसे है? और संयमी लोग मृत्यु के समय आपको कैसे जान पाते हैं?

Arjuna completes his questions by asking who the adhiyajña, the lord of sacrifice, is within this body and in what way, and how self-mastered people can know Krishna at the time of death.

That last question, also prompted by 7.30, sets the theme of the chapter: what a person holds in mind at the end. The key word niyatātman means one whose self is held in check. Traditionally, the epithet Madhusūdana means 'slayer of the demon Madhu'.
