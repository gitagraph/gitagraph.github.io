Bhagavad Gita 8.9

Krishna

कविं पुराणमनुशासितारमणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूपमादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥ ८.९ ॥

kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāramaṇoraṇīyāṃsamanusmaredyaḥ ।
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpamādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt ॥

One who meditates on the seer, the ancient one, the ruler, subtler than the subtlest, the sustainer of all, of inconceivable form, radiant as the sun and beyond all darkness...

हिन्दीजो सर्वज्ञ, सनातन, सबके शासक, सूक्ष्म से भी सूक्ष्म, सबके धारण करने वाले, अचिंत्य रूप वाले, सूर्य के समान प्रकाशमान और अंधकार से परे उस पुरुष का स्मरण करता है…

Krishna begins describing the supreme Person to meditate on: the ancient seer and ruler, subtler than the subtlest, sustainer of all, beyond thought, radiant as the sun beyond darkness; the sentence runs on into 8.10.

The list pairs opposites: the ruler of all is also aṇor aṇīyān, 'subtler than the subtlest'; the one of acintya-rūpa, 'inconceivable form', is still āditya-varṇa, 'sun-coloured'. Kavi, 'seer', and purāṇa, 'ancient', add wisdom and age. Together the images suggest something vast yet intimate.
