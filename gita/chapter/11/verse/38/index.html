Bhagavad Gita 11.38

Arjuna

त्वमादिदेवः पुरुषः पुराण- ।
स्त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।
वेत्तासि वेद्यं च परं च धाम ।
त्वया ततं विश्वमनन्तरूप ॥ ११.३८ ॥

tvamādidevaḥ puruṣaḥ purāṇa- ।
stvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam ।
vettāsi vedyaṃ ca paraṃ ca dhāma ।
tvayā tataṃ viśvamanantarūpa ॥

You are the first god, the ancient Person; you are the final resting place of this universe. You are the knower and the known and the highest abode. By you this universe is pervaded, O you of infinite forms.

हिन्दीआप आदि देव और पुराण पुरुष हैं, आप ही इस विश्व के परम आश्रय हैं। आप ही जानने वाले हैं, जानने योग्य हैं और परम धाम हैं। हे अनंत रूपों वाले, यह सारा विश्व आपसे व्याप्त है।

Arjuna praises Krishna as the first god and the ancient Person, the final resting place of the universe, and both the knower and what is to be known.

Nidhāna is a place where something is laid down and kept safe; here it is where the universe comes to rest. By calling him both vettā, 'the knower', and vedya, 'what is to be known', Arjuna leaves nothing outside him, not even the act of knowing.
