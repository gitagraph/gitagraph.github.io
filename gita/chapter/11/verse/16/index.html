Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 16 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.16

Arjuna

अनेकबाहूदरवक्त्रनेत्रं ।
पश्यामि त्वां सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् ।
नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिं ।
पश्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप ॥ ११.१६ ॥

anekabāhūdaravaktranetraṃ ।
paśyāmi tvāṃ sarvato'nantarūpam ।
nāntaṃ na madhyaṃ na punastavādiṃ ।
paśyāmi viśveśvara viśvarūpa ॥

I see you everywhere, endless in form, with countless arms, bellies, mouths and eyes. Lord of the universe, whose form is the universe, I see no end of you, no middle and no beginning.

हिन्दीमैं आपको सब ओर अनंत रूपों वाला, अनगिनत भुजाओं, पेटों, मुखों और नेत्रों वाला देखता हूँ। हे विश्वेश्वर, हे विश्वरूप, मुझे आपका न अंत दिखता है, न मध्य और न ही आदि।

Arjuna tells Krishna that wherever he looks he sees countless arms, bellies, mouths and eyes, and can find no end, middle or beginning to him.

He calls Krishna viśvarūpa, 'whose form is the universe', the word from which this vision traditionally takes its name. The order 'no end, no middle, no beginning' runs backwards, as if he searches from the edges inward and never reaches a starting point.
