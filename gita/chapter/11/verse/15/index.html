Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 15 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.15

Arjuna

पश्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे ।
सर्वांस्तथा भूतविशेषसंघान् ।
ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थ- ।
मृषींश्च सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान् ॥ ११.१५ ॥

paśyāmi devāṃstava deva dehe ।
sarvāṃstathā bhūtaviśeṣasaṃghān ।
brahmāṇamīśaṃ kamalāsanastha- ।
mṛṣīṃśca sarvānuragāṃśca divyān ॥

In your body, O God, I see all the gods and the hosts of every kind of being: Brahma, the lord, seated on the lotus, all the sages and the divine serpents.

हिन्दीहे देव, मैं आपके शरीर में सब देवताओं और अनेक प्रकार के प्राणियों के समूहों को देखता हूँ; कमल के आसन पर बैठे स्वामी ब्रह्मा को, सब ऋषियों को और दिव्य सर्पों को भी।

Arjuna begins describing the vision himself: within Krishna's body he sees all the gods, hosts of every kind of being, Brahma on his lotus seat, all the sages and the divine serpents.

Here īśa, 'lord', is taken as a title of Brahma; some traditions read it instead as Shiva, so that both are named. Traditionally Brahma, the creator, is pictured seated on a lotus. Hearing Arjuna's own words makes the vision immediate.
