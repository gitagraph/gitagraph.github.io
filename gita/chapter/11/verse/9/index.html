Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 9 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.9

Sanjaya

एवमुक्त्वा ततो राजन्महायोगेश्वरो हरिः ।
दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम् ॥ ११.९ ॥

evamuktvā tato rājanmahāyogeśvaro hariḥ ।
darśayāmāsa pārthāya paramaṃ rūpamaiśvaram ॥

Having spoken thus, O King, Hari, the great lord of yoga, then revealed to Partha his supreme, sovereign form.

हिन्दीहे राजन, ऐसा कहकर महायोगेश्वर हरि ने तब पार्थ को अपना परम ईश्वरीय रूप दिखाया।

Sanjaya tells King Dhritarashtra that, after these words, Krishna revealed his supreme sovereign form to Arjuna.

The narration returns to its frame: from here Sanjaya describes the vision himself before Arjuna speaks again. He calls Krishna Hari and mahāyogeśvara, 'great lord of yoga', echoing Arjuna's own 'master of yoga' in 11.4. From the Mahabharata: Sanjaya can see all this because Vyasa granted him divine sight.
