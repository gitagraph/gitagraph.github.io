Bhagavad Gita 11.1

Arjuna

मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् ।
यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥ ११.१ ॥

madanugrahāya paramaṃ guhyamadhyātmasaṃjñitam ।
yattvayoktaṃ vacastena moho'yaṃ vigato mama ॥

Out of kindness to me you have spoken this supreme secret teaching about the Self, and by your words this confusion of mine is gone.

हिन्दीमुझ पर कृपा करने के लिए आपने आत्मा के विषय में जो परम गोपनीय उपदेश दिया, उन वचनों से मेरा यह मोह दूर हो गया है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

When an elder sibling patiently talks you through a decision you'd been stuck on for weeks, tell them plainly that the confusion has lifted. Gratitude said aloud closes the loop.

Opening chapter 11, Arjuna thanks Krishna for the teaching so far and says that his confusion has lifted.

Moha, 'bewilderment', names the state he was in at the start (2.7), unable to see what was right. Adhyātma, 'concerning the self', names what the teaching was about. His relief is real, but the next verses show he now wants more than words: having heard, he wants to see.
