Bhagavad Gita 11.27

Arjuna

वक्त्राणि ते त्वरमाणा विशन्ति ।
दंष्ट्राकरालानि भयानकानि ।
केचिद्विलग्ना दशनान्तरेषु ।
संदृश्यन्ते चूर्णितैरुत्तमाङ्गैः ॥ ११.२७ ॥

vaktrāṇi te tvaramāṇā viśanti ।
daṃṣṭrākarālāni bhayānakāni ।
kecidvilagnā daśanāntareṣu ।
saṃdṛśyante cūrṇitairuttamāṅgaiḥ ॥

are rushing into your fearful mouths, terrible with tusks. Some are seen caught between your teeth, their heads crushed to powder.

हिन्दीबड़ी तेज़ी से आपके दाढ़ों से विकराल, भयानक मुखों में प्रवेश कर रहे हैं। कुछ आपके दाँतों के बीच फँसे हुए दिख रहे हैं, जिनके सिर चूर-चूर हो गए हैं।

Arjuna sees the warriors of both armies rushing into the cosmic form's fearsome mouths, some caught between its teeth with their heads crushed, completing the sentence begun in 11.26.

The word tvaramāṇāḥ, 'hurrying', matters: no one is dragged; they rush in. In the story, Arjuna is being shown the outcome of the war before it is fought, which prepares Krishna's statement in 11.32–34 that these warriors are already slain.
