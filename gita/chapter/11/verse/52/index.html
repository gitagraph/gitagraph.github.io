Verse 11.52

Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 52 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.52

Krishna

सुदुर्दर्शमिदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम ।
देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ११.५२ ॥

sudurdarśamidaṃ rūpaṃ dṛṣṭavānasi yanmama ।
devā apyasya rūpasya nityaṃ darśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ ॥

This form of mine that you have seen is very hard to see. Even the gods always long for a sight of it.

हिन्दीमेरा यह जो रूप तुमने देखा है, उसके दर्शन बहुत दुर्लभ हैं। देवता भी सदा इस रूप के दर्शन की इच्छा रखते हैं।

Krishna tells Arjuna that the form he has just been shown is very hard to see, so rare that even the gods keep longing for a glimpse of it.

The key word is sudurdarśa, 'extremely hard to behold' (su 'very' + dur 'hard' + darśa 'seeing'). Commentators differ on whether 'this form' is the cosmic form of the chapter or the gentle form Krishna has just resumed (11.50–51); either way, the verse marks Arjuna's vision as a rare gift.
