Bhagavad Gita 11.26

Arjuna

अमी च त्वां धृतराष्ट्रस्य पुत्राः ।
सर्वे सहैवावनिपालसंघैः ।
भीष्मो द्रोणः सूतपुत्रस्तथासौ ।
सहास्मदीयैरपि योधमुख्यैः ॥ ११.२६ ॥

amī ca tvāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāḥ ।
sarve sahaivāvanipālasaṃghaiḥ ।
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ sūtaputrastathāsau ।
sahāsmadīyairapi yodhamukhyaiḥ ॥

And all these sons of Dhritarashtra, along with the hosts of kings, Bhishma, Drona and that charioteer's son Karna too, together with the chief warriors on our side as well,

हिन्दीऔर ये सब धृतराष्ट्र के पुत्र, राजाओं के समूहों के साथ, भीष्म, द्रोण और वह सूतपुत्र कर्ण भी, हमारे पक्ष के प्रमुख योद्धाओं के साथ,

Arjuna begins to describe the warriors of both armies being drawn into the cosmic form, in a sentence that finishes in the next verse.

He names Dhritarashtra's sons, Bhishma, Drona and 'that sūtaputra', the charioteer's son. From the Mahabharata: Bhishma is the Kuru elder, Drona the teacher of both sides, and the sūtaputra is Karna, raised by a charioteer. What chills Arjuna is that the leading warriors of his own side are there too.
