Bhagavad Gita 11.40

Arjuna

नमः पुरस्तादथ पृष्ठतस्ते ।
नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व ।
अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वं ।
सर्वं समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः ॥ ११.४० ॥

namaḥ purastādatha pṛṣṭhataste ।
namo'stu te sarvata eva sarva ।
anantavīryāmitavikramastvaṃ ।
sarvaṃ samāpnoṣi tato'si sarvaḥ ॥

Salutations to you in front and behind; salutations to you on every side, you who are all. Your strength is infinite and your might beyond measure; you pervade everything, and so you are everything.

हिन्दीआपको आगे से और पीछे से नमस्कार है; हे सर्वस्वरूप, आपको सब ओर से नमस्कार है। आपकी शक्ति अनंत और पराक्रम अपार है; आप सबमें व्याप्त हैं, इसलिए आप ही सब कुछ हैं।

Arjuna bows to Krishna from every direction, then gives his reason: since Krishna pervades everything, he is everything.

The verse plays on sarva, 'all': Krishna is addressed as sarva, saluted sarvataḥ, 'from all sides', said to pervade sarvam, and finally called sarvaḥ. The logic is simple: if nothing lies outside him, there is no direction in which a bow would not be toward him.
