Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 35 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.35

Sanjaya

एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं केशवस्य ।
कृताञ्जलिर्वेपमानः किरीटी ।
नमस्कृत्वा भूय एवाह कृष्णं ।
सगद्गदं भीतभीतः प्रणम्य ॥ ११.३५ ॥

etacchrutvā vacanaṃ keśavasya ।
kṛtāñjalirvepamānaḥ kirīṭī ।
namaskṛtvā bhūya evāha kṛṣṇaṃ ।
sagadgadaṃ bhītabhītaḥ praṇamya ॥

Hearing these words of Keshava, the crowned Arjuna, trembling, with joined palms, bowed down; and, bowing again, overwhelmed with fear, he spoke once more to Krishna in a faltering voice.

हिन्दीकेशव के ये वचन सुनकर मुकुटधारी अर्जुन ने काँपते हुए हाथ जोड़कर नमस्कार किया, और बहुत डरे हुए फिर से प्रणाम करके गद्गद वाणी में कृष्ण से बोले।

Sanjaya, narrating to Dhritarashtra, describes how Arjuna responds to Krishna's words: trembling, palms joined, he bows again and again and speaks in a choked voice.

Kirīṭin, 'the crowned one', is Arjuna's title (from the Mahabharata: the crown was a gift from Indra). The doubled word bhītabhītaḥ, 'deeply afraid', shows how far even this fearless archer has been shaken by the vision.
