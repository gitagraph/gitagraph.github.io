Bhagavad Gita 11.21

Arjuna

अमी हि त्वां सुरसंघा विशन्ति ।
केचिद्भीताः प्राञ्जलयो गृणन्ति ।
स्वस्तीत्युक्त्वा महर्षिसिद्धसंघाः ।
स्तुवन्ति त्वां स्तुतिभिः पुष्कलाभिः ॥ ११.२१ ॥

amī hi tvāṃ surasaṃghā viśanti ।
kecidbhītāḥ prāñjalayo gṛṇanti ।
svastītyuktvā maharṣisiddhasaṃghāḥ ।
stuvanti tvāṃ stutibhiḥ puṣkalābhiḥ ॥

Hosts of gods are entering into you; some, afraid, praise you with joined palms. Hosts of great sages and perfected ones cry 'May all be well!' and praise you with abundant hymns.

हिन्दीदेवताओं के ये समूह आपमें प्रवेश कर रहे हैं; कुछ डरकर हाथ जोड़े आपकी स्तुति कर रहे हैं। महर्षियों और सिद्धों के समूह 'कल्याण हो' कहकर अनेक सुंदर स्तोत्रों से आपकी स्तुति कर रहे हैं।

Arjuna, gazing at the cosmic form Krishna has revealed, describes the heavenly beings around it: some enter it, while others praise it in fear.

The word svasti, 'may it be well', is the blessing the great sages and perfected ones call out as they sing their hymns. For readers, the scene holds two reactions side by side, awe-filled praise and plain fear, and both run through the rest of Arjuna's vision.
