Bhagavad Gita 11.13

Sanjaya

तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा ।
अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा ॥ ११.१३ ॥

tatraikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ pravibhaktamanekadhā ।
apaśyaddevadevasya śarīre pāṇḍavastadā ॥

There, in the body of the god of gods, the Pandava then saw the entire universe, divided in many ways, gathered together in one place.

हिन्दीतब पांडुपुत्र अर्जुन ने वहाँ देवों के देव के शरीर में अनेक भागों में बँटे हुए सारे जगत को एक ही जगह स्थित देखा।

Sanjaya reports that Arjuna saw the whole universe, in all its many divisions, gathered in one place in the body of the god of gods.

This fulfils Krishna's invitation in 11.7 and repeats its word ekastha, 'standing in one'. The pairing of pravibhakta, 'divided in many ways', with ekastha is the heart of the verse: nothing loses its separateness, yet all of it is seen as a single whole.
