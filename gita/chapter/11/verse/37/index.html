Bhagavad Gita 11.37

Arjuna

कस्माच्च ते न नमेरन्महात्मन् ।
गरीयसे ब्रह्मणोऽप्यादिकर्त्रे ।
अनन्त देवेश जगन्निवास ।
त्वमक्षरं सदसत्तत्परं यत् ॥ ११.३७ ॥

kasmācca te na nameranmahātman ।
garīyase brahmaṇo'pyādikartre ।
ananta deveśa jagannivāsa ।
tvamakṣaraṃ sadasattatparaṃ yat ॥

And why should they not bow to you, great soul, who are greater even than Brahma and are the first creator? Infinite one, lord of gods, refuge of the world, you are the imperishable: being and non-being, and what lies beyond both.

हिन्दीहे महात्मन, वे आपको नमस्कार क्यों न करें, आप तो ब्रह्मा से भी बड़े और आदि सृष्टिकर्ता हैं। हे अनंत, हे देवेश, हे जगन्निवास, आप ही अक्षर हैं; सत् और असत् भी आप हैं, और जो उन दोनों से परे है वह भी आप ही हैं।

In his hymn of praise, Arjuna asks why beings should not bow to one who is greater even than Brahma and is the first creator, and calls Krishna akṣara, the imperishable.

The closing phrase sad-asat tat-paraṃ yat is read in two ways: the translation here takes it as 'being and non-being, and what lies beyond both'; others read 'the imperishable that is beyond being and non-being'. Either way, Arjuna places Krishna above every category he can name.
