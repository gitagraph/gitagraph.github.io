Bhagavad Gita 11.34

Krishna

द्रोणं च भीष्मं च जयद्रथं च ।
कर्णं तथान्यानपि योधवीरान् ।
मया हतांस्त्वं जहि मा व्यथिष्ठा ।
युध्यस्व जेतासि रणे सपत्नान् ॥ ११.३४ ॥

droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca jayadrathaṃ ca ।
karṇaṃ tathānyānapi yodhavīrān ।
mayā hatāṃstvaṃ jahi mā vyathiṣṭhā ।
yudhyasva jetāsi raṇe sapatnān ॥

Drona and Bhishma, Jayadratha and Karna, and the other heroic warriors too, already slain by me, you must kill. Do not be troubled. Fight: you will conquer your rivals in battle.

हिन्दीद्रोण, भीष्म, जयद्रथ, कर्ण और दूसरे वीर योद्धाओं को भी, जो मेरे द्वारा मारे जा चुके हैं, तुम मारो। व्याकुल मत हो। युद्ध करो, तुम रण में शत्रुओं को जीतोगे।

Krishna names the great warriors Arjuna dreads facing, and repeats that they are already slain by him, so Arjuna should fight without distress.

From the Mahabharata: Drona was Arjuna's teacher, Bhishma the revered grandsire of the family, Jayadratha a king fighting for the Kauravas, and Karna his great rival as an archer. The phrase mā vyathiṣṭhāḥ, 'do not be troubled', answers the grief of chapter 1, and the verse ends with an assurance: jetāsi, 'you will conquer'.

People: Krishna · Drona · Bhishma · Jayadratha · Karna
