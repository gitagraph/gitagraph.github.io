Bhagavad Gita 11.42

Arjuna

यच्चावहासार्थमसत्कृतोऽसि ।
विहारशय्यासनभोजनेषु ।
एकोऽथवाप्यच्युत तत्समक्षं ।
तत्क्षामये त्वामहमप्रमेयम् ॥ ११.४२ ॥

yaccāvahāsārthamasatkṛto'si ।
vihāraśayyāsanabhojaneṣu ।
eko'thavāpyacyuta tatsamakṣaṃ ।
tatkṣāmaye tvāmahamaprameyam ॥

and whatever disrespect I showed you in jest, while playing, resting, sitting or eating, alone or in front of others, Achyuta, for all that I beg forgiveness of you, the immeasurable one.

हिन्दीऔर हे अच्युत, हँसी-मज़ाक में खेलते, सोते, बैठते या खाते समय, अकेले में या दूसरों के सामने, मैंने आपका जो अनादर किया, उस सबके लिए मैं आप अपार प्रभु से क्षमा माँगता हूँ।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

If years of teasing at the dinner table have hurt your spouse more than you realised, a plain, specific apology helps more than a promise to do better. Ask to be forgiven for the small things too.

Arjuna asks Krishna's forgiveness for any disrespect he showed him in jest, at play, resting, sitting or eating, alone or in company, closing the apology begun in 11.41.

The verb kṣāmaye, 'I ask you to forgive', is the heart of the verse. Traditionally, Acyuta means 'one who never slips or falls'. The list of everyday settings shows how ordinary their friendship had been, and why the vision makes Arjuna look back on it differently.
