Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 5 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.5

Krishna

पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च ॥ ११.५ ॥

paśya me pārtha rūpāṇi śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ ।
nānāvidhāni divyāni nānāvarṇākṛtīni ca ॥

Behold, Partha, my forms by the hundreds and thousands, of many kinds, divine, of many colours and shapes.

हिन्दीहे पार्थ, मेरे सैकड़ों और हज़ारों रूपों को देखो, जो अनेक प्रकार के, दिव्य और अनेक रंगों तथा आकारों वाले हैं।

Krishna grants Arjuna's request and tells him to behold his forms in their hundreds and thousands.

Arjuna asked for one form, but Krishna speaks of rūpāṇi, 'forms' in the plural, of many kinds, colours and shapes. Paśya, 'see' or 'behold', becomes the repeated word of these verses. The scale tells the reader at once that the vision will not be a single figure but a multitude.
