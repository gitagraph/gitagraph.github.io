Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 23 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.23

Arjuna

रूपं महत्ते बहुवक्त्रनेत्रं ।
महाबाहो बहुबाहूरुपादम् ।
बहूदरं बहुदंष्ट्राकरालं ।
दृष्ट्वा लोकाः प्रव्यथितास्तथाहम् ॥ ११.२३ ॥

rūpaṃ mahatte bahuvaktranetraṃ ।
mahābāho bahubāhūrupādam ।
bahūdaraṃ bahudaṃṣṭrākarālaṃ ।
dṛṣṭvā lokāḥ pravyathitāstathāham ॥

Seeing your immense form, mighty-armed one, with its many mouths and eyes, many arms, thighs and feet, many bellies, and terrifying with many tusks, the worlds are shaken, and so am I.

हिन्दीहे महाबाहो, आपके अनेक मुखों और नेत्रों वाले, अनेक भुजाओं, जाँघों और पैरों वाले, अनेक पेटों वाले और अनेक दाढ़ों के कारण विकराल इस विशाल रूप को देखकर सब लोक व्याकुल हैं, और मैं भी।

Arjuna's wonder turns to alarm as he describes Krishna's vast cosmic form's countless mouths, eyes, limbs and bellies, and its terrifying tusks.

The word repeated through the verse is bahu, 'many': no single face or body can be held in view. He admits that the worlds are shaken, and he with them, so the vision that came as a gift is now becoming hard for him to bear.
