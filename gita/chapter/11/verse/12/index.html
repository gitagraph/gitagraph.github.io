Bhagavad Gita 11.12

Sanjaya

दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता ।
यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥ ११.१२ ॥

divi sūryasahasrasya bhavedyugapadutthitā ।
yadi bhāḥ sadṛśī sā syādbhāsastasya mahātmanaḥ ॥

If the light of a thousand suns were to blaze up in the sky all at once, that might resemble the radiance of that great being.

हिन्दीयदि आकाश में हज़ार सूर्यों का प्रकाश एक साथ उदय हो जाए, तो वह शायद उस महात्मा के प्रकाश के समान हो।

Sanjaya compares the radiance of the form to a thousand suns blazing up together in the sky, and says only that this might come close.

The grammar is careful: yadi … syāt, 'if … it might be', an image offered as a guess rather than a measure. Bhās means light or radiance, and mahātman, 'great being', refers to Krishna in this form. The line's force lies in admitting that even its brightest comparison may fall short.
