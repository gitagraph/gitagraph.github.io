Bhagavad Gita 11.41

Arjuna

सखेति मत्वा प्रसभं यदुक्तं ।
हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखेति ।
अजानता महिमानं तवेदं ।
मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि ॥ ११.४१ ॥

sakheti matvā prasabhaṃ yaduktaṃ ।
he kṛṣṇa he yādava he sakheti ।
ajānatā mahimānaṃ tavedaṃ ।
mayā pramādātpraṇayena vāpi ॥

Thinking of you as a friend, I rashly called out 'O Krishna, O Yadava, O friend!', not knowing this greatness of yours, whether out of carelessness or out of affection.

हिन्दीआपको अपना मित्र मानकर, आपकी इस महिमा को न जानते हुए, मैंने लापरवाही से या प्रेम के कारण बिना सोचे 'हे कृष्ण, हे यादव, हे सखा' कहकर जो कुछ कहा,

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

It's easy, out of habit or affection, to treat a close friend so casually for years that you stop seeing how remarkable they are. Noticing it one day is not too late.

Having seen Krishna's true greatness, Arjuna is struck by how casually he has treated him as a friend, and begins an apology that runs into 11.42.

He recalls calling out 'Krishna', 'Yadava', 'friend' without a second thought. Two causes are named: pramāda, heedlessness, and praṇaya, affection. The verse captures a familiar feeling: realising that someone close to us was far more than we had taken them to be.
