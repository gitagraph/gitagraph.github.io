Bhagavad Gita 11.25

Arjuna

दंष्ट्राकरालानि च ते मुखानि ।
दृष्ट्वैव कालानलसन्निभानि ।
दिशो न जाने न लभे च शर्म ।
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ११.२५ ॥

daṃṣṭrākarālāni ca te mukhāni ।
dṛṣṭvaiva kālānalasannibhāni ।
diśo na jāne na labhe ca śarma ।
prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa ॥

Seeing your mouths, terrible with tusks and like the fire at the end of time, I lose all sense of direction and find no peace. Be gracious, lord of gods, refuge of the world.

हिन्दीदाढ़ों से विकराल और प्रलय की अग्नि जैसे आपके मुखों को देखते ही मुझे दिशाओं का ज्ञान नहीं रहा और कहीं सुख नहीं मिल रहा। हे देवेश, हे जगन्निवास, प्रसन्न होइए।

Overwhelmed by mouths that look like the fire that ends the world, Arjuna says he has lost his bearings and begs Krishna to be gracious.

Kālānala is literally 'the fire of time', the blaze imagined at the dissolution of the universe. His plea prasīda, 'be gracious, be kind', comes again in 11.31 and 11.45, and it shows a man asking no longer for more revelation but for relief.
