Bhagavad Gita 11.53

Krishna

नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया ।
शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा ॥ ११.५३ ॥

nāhaṃ vedairna tapasā na dānena na cejyayā ।
śakya evaṃvidho draṣṭuṃ dṛṣṭavānasi māṃ yathā ॥

Not through the Vedas, not through austerity, not through charity and not through sacrifice can I be seen in the way you have seen me.

हिन्दीजिस रूप में तुमने मुझे देखा है, उस रूप में मुझे न वेदों से, न तप से, न दान से और न यज्ञ से देखा जा सकता है।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

Some closeness can't be bought with gifts or earned by ritual alone; it comes through love that keeps showing up. That is as true of a friendship as of what you hold highest.

Krishna says that the sight Arjuna has just had cannot be won through the Vedas, austerity, charity or sacrifice.

These four were the familiar religious means: study of scripture, tapas (self-discipline), dāna (giving) and ijyā (ritual worship). The verse does not call them worthless; it says only that this particular seeing is not something they can produce, and so prepares the answer given in the next verse (11.54).
