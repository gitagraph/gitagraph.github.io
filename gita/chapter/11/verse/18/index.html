Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 18 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.18

Arjuna

त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यं ।
त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।
त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता ।
सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे ॥ ११.१८ ॥

tvamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ veditavyaṃ ।
tvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam ।
tvamavyayaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā ।
sanātanastvaṃ puruṣo mato me ॥

You are the imperishable, the supreme one to be known; you are the final resting place of this universe. You are the undying guardian of eternal dharma; you are, I believe, the everlasting Person.

हिन्दीआप ही जानने योग्य परम अक्षर हैं, आप ही इस विश्व के परम आश्रय हैं। आप अविनाशी और सनातन धर्म के रक्षक हैं; मेरे मत में आप ही सनातन पुरुष हैं।

Pausing in his description, Arjuna states what he now understands Krishna to be: the imperishable, the final resting place of the universe, guardian of eternal dharma and the everlasting Person.

Akṣara, 'imperishable', is the word used for the highest reality in 8.3. Nidhāna means a resting place or treasury where all things are finally held. The closing mato me, 'in my view', keeps it personal: Arjuna's conviction, born of what he sees.
