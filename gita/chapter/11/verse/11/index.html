Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 11 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.11

Sanjaya

दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् ।
सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ११.११ ॥

divyamālyāmbaradharaṃ divyagandhānulepanam ।
sarvāścaryamayaṃ devamanantaṃ viśvatomukham ॥

He wore divine garlands and garments and was anointed with divine fragrances. That god was all wonder, boundless, with faces turned in every direction.

हिन्दीवह दिव्य मालाएँ और वस्त्र धारण किए हुए था और दिव्य सुगंधों का लेप लगाए था। वह देव सब प्रकार के आश्चर्यों से भरा, अनंत और सब ओर मुख वाला था।

Sanjaya goes on describing the form: divinely garlanded, robed and perfumed, full of every wonder, endless and facing every way.

Garlands, garments and fragrant paste are the honours given to a deity in worship, so the vision appears already adorned. Viśvatomukha, 'facing everywhere', repeats Krishna's self-description in 10.33. Ananta, 'without end', prepares Arjuna's cry that he can find no beginning, middle or end (11.16).
