Bhagavad Gita 11.17

Arjuna

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च ।
तेजोराशिं सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम् ।
पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ता- ।
द्दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम् ॥ ११.१७ ॥

kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakriṇaṃ ca ।
tejorāśiṃ sarvato dīptimantam ।
paśyāmi tvāṃ durnirīkṣyaṃ samantā- ।
ddīptānalārkadyutimaprameyam ॥

I see you wearing a crown, bearing a mace and a discus, a mass of light shining on every side, hard to look at, blazing all round like burning fire and the sun, beyond all measure.

हिन्दीमैं आपको मुकुट, गदा और चक्र धारण किए, सब ओर चमकते तेज के पुंज के रूप में देखता हूँ; जलती अग्नि और सूर्य जैसी प्रभा वाले, अपार, जिन्हें चारों ओर से देख पाना कठिन है।

Arjuna sees the form wearing a crown and bearing a mace and a discus, set within a mass of light too bright to look at.

Traditionally these are the emblems of Vishnu, so something recognisable sits inside the overwhelming. Durnirīkṣya, 'hard to gaze upon', and aprameya, 'beyond measure', name the two limits Arjuna meets: his eyes cannot hold the brightness, and his mind cannot hold the size.
