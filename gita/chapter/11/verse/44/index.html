Bhagavad Gita 11.44

Arjuna

तस्मात्प्रणम्य प्रणिधाय कायं ।
प्रसादये त्वामहमीशमीड्यम् ।
पितेव पुत्रस्य सखेव सख्युः ।
प्रियः प्रियायार्हसि देव सोढुम् ॥ ११.४४ ॥

tasmātpraṇamya praṇidhāya kāyaṃ ।
prasādaye tvāmahamīśamīḍyam ।
piteva putrasya sakheva sakhyuḥ ।
priyaḥ priyāyārhasi deva soḍhum ॥

So, bowing down and laying my body at your feet, I seek your grace, adorable Lord. As a father bears with his son, a friend with a friend, a lover with the beloved, please bear with me, O God.

हिन्दीइसलिए मैं शरीर को आपके चरणों में रखकर प्रणाम करता हूँ और आप स्तुति के योग्य ईश्वर से प्रसन्न होने की प्रार्थना करता हूँ। हे देव, जैसे पिता पुत्र के, मित्र मित्र के और प्रिय अपनी प्रिया के अपराध सह लेता है, वैसे ही आप मेरे अपराध सह लीजिए।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

After a hard argument with your father, you can ask him to bear with you the way a parent bears with a child. And when he asks the same of you, give the patience you hoped for.

Arjuna prostrates himself and asks Krishna to bear with him as a father bears with a son, a friend with a friend, and a lover with the beloved.

The verb prasādaye, 'I seek to win your favour', echoes his earlier plea prasīda (11.25, 11.31). The three pairs are telling: Arjuna is not asking a distant ruler to overlook an offence, but asking for the patience that people who love each other already show.
