Bhagavad Gita 11.24

Arjuna

नभःस्पृशं दीप्तमनेकवर्णं ।
व्यात्ताननं दीप्तविशालनेत्रम् ।
दृष्ट्वा हि त्वां प्रव्यथितान्तरात्मा ।
धृतिं न विन्दामि शमं च विष्णो ॥ ११.२४ ॥

nabhaḥspṛśaṃ dīptamanekavarṇaṃ ।
vyāttānanaṃ dīptaviśālanetram ।
dṛṣṭvā hi tvāṃ pravyathitāntarātmā ।
dhṛtiṃ na vindāmi śamaṃ ca viṣṇo ॥

Seeing you touching the sky, blazing, of many colours, with mouths gaping and huge eyes aflame, my heart shakes within me, Vishnu, and I find neither courage nor calm.

हिन्दीहे विष्णो, आपको आकाश को छूते हुए, जगमगाते, अनेक रंगों वाले, खुले मुखों और विशाल जलते नेत्रों वाले देखकर मेरा अंतःकरण काँप उठा है, और मुझे न धैर्य मिल रहा है, न शांति।

In your life

When a loved one's diagnosis leaves you shaken, you don't have to pretend to be steady. Even Arjuna, a seasoned warrior, admitted he had lost both his courage and his calm; saying so is no failure.

Arjuna confesses that the blazing, sky-high form, with gaping mouths and fiery eyes, has unsettled him to the core.

He names two things he has lost: dhṛti, the steadiness that holds a person firm, and śama, inner calm. He calls Krishna Viṣṇu, a name that returns in 11.30. The verse shows that even a seasoned warrior can lose his footing before something too vast to take in.
