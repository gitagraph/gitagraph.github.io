Bhagavad Gita 11.45

Arjuna

अदृष्टपूर्वं हृषितोऽस्मि दृष्ट्वा ।
भयेन च प्रव्यथितं मनो मे ।
तदेव मे दर्शय देव रूपं ।
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ११.४५ ॥

adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ hṛṣito'smi dṛṣṭvā ।
bhayena ca pravyathitaṃ mano me ।
tadeva me darśaya deva rūpaṃ ।
prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa ॥

I am thrilled to have seen what was never seen before, yet my mind is shaken with fear. Show me your former form, O God; be gracious, lord of gods, refuge of the world.

हिन्दीपहले कभी न देखा गया रूप देखकर मैं हर्षित हूँ, पर भय से मेरा मन व्याकुल भी है। हे देव, मुझे अपना वही पहले वाला रूप दिखाइए; हे देवेश, हे जगन्निवास, प्रसन्न होइए।

In your life · a reflection by Gitagraph

A move to a new city can thrill and frighten you at once; both feelings are real. It's fine to reach for something familiar, a known voice or a home-cooked meal, while you find your feet.

Arjuna admits to two feelings at once, a thrill at seeing what was never seen before and a mind shaken by fear, and asks Krishna to show his former form again.

The key phrase is adṛṣṭapūrvam, 'never seen before'. His request is not ingratitude: the vision has given him what he asked for in 11.3–4, and now he needs the familiar face he can bear to look at.
