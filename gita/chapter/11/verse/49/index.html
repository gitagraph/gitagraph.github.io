Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 49 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.49

Krishna

मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो ।
दृष्ट्वा रूपं घोरमीदृङ्ममेदम् ।
व्यपेतभीः प्रीतमनाः पुनस्त्वं ।
तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपश्य ॥ ११.४९ ॥

mā te vyathā mā ca vimūḍhabhāvo ।
dṛṣṭvā rūpaṃ ghoramīdṛṅmamedam ।
vyapetabhīḥ prītamanāḥ punastvaṃ ।
tadeva me rūpamidaṃ prapaśya ॥

Do not be afraid and do not be bewildered at seeing this terrible form of mine. Free from fear and glad at heart, look once more upon that familiar form of mine.

हिन्दीमेरे इस भयंकर रूप को देखकर तुम न व्याकुल हो और न मोहित हो। भय छोड़कर और प्रसन्न मन से तुम फिर मेरे उसी परिचित रूप को देखो।

Krishna tells Arjuna not to be troubled or bewildered by the terrible form, and to look once more, free of fear and glad at heart, on his familiar form.

Two states are named and dismissed: vyathā, distress, and vimūḍhabhāva, bewilderment. The contrast between ghoram, 'terrible', and prītamanāḥ, 'glad at heart', marks the turn of the chapter, from a vision that overwhelmed Arjuna to a presence he can rest in again.
