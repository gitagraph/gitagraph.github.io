Bhagavad Gita 11.6

Krishna

पश्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा ।
बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत ॥ ११.६ ॥

paśyādityānvasūnrudrānaśvinau marutastathā ।
bahūnyadṛṣṭapūrvāṇi paśyāścaryāṇi bhārata ॥

Behold the Adityas, the Vasus, the Rudras, the two Ashvins and the Maruts as well. Behold many wonders never seen before, Bharata.

हिन्दीहे भारत, आदित्यों, वसुओं, रुद्रों, दोनों अश्विनीकुमारों और मरुतों को देखो। बहुत-से ऐसे आश्चर्य देखो जो पहले कभी नहीं देखे गए।

Krishna tells Arjuna to see within him the great groups of Vedic gods, and many wonders no one has seen before.

Traditionally the Adityas are twelve solar deities, the Vasus eight, the Rudras eleven, the Ashvins twin healers and the Maruts storm gods; the Gita has already named several of them in 10.21–23. Adṛṣṭapūrva, 'never seen before', signals that Arjuna is about to witness something without precedent.
