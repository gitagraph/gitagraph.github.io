Chapter 11 · The Cosmic FormVerse 43 of 55

Bhagavad Gita 11.43

Arjuna

पितासि लोकस्य चराचरस्य ।
त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान् ।
न त्वत्समोऽस्त्यभ्यधिकः कुतोऽन्यो ।
लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभाव ॥ ११.४३ ॥

pitāsi lokasya carācarasya ।
tvamasya pūjyaśca gururgarīyān ।
na tvatsamo'styabhyadhikaḥ kuto'nyo ।
lokatraye'pyapratimaprabhāva ॥

You are the father of this world, of all that moves and is still; you are its object of worship and its most honoured teacher. There is none equal to you in the three worlds; how could there be anyone greater, O you of incomparable power?

हिन्दीआप इस चर-अचर जगत के पिता हैं, इसके पूज्य हैं और सबसे बड़े गुरु हैं। हे अतुलनीय प्रभाव वाले, तीनों लोकों में आपके समान भी कोई नहीं है, तो आपसे बड़ा कोई कैसे हो सकता है?

Arjuna praises Krishna as the father, the object of worship and the greatest teacher of the whole world, moving and unmoving, with no equal anywhere.

The key word is apratimaprabhāva, 'one whose power has no match' (a-pratima, 'without a likeness'). The argument climbs a step: if no one in the three worlds is even equal to him, no one can be greater. It also explains Arjuna's apology: he had treated as a peer someone who has none.
