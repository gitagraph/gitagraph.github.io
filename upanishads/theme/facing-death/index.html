Facing death

For when loss, illness or the thought of dying is close.

  • Katha Upanishad · 1.1–1.2Nachiketa and Death

    Given to Death by his angry father, a boy asks what happens after we die.

    For someone young weighing what is worth wanting, or anyone wondering what outlasts death.
  • Chandogya Upanishad · 8.7–8.12Indra, Virochana and the reflection in water

    Two kings look into a pan of water. One goes home satisfied; the other keeps asking.

    For anyone who has looked in a mirror and wondered who is doing the looking.
  • Brihadaranyaka Upanishad · 1.3.28From what is not to what is

    Three lines murmured during an ancient chant, and the Upanishad's own key to what they mean.

    For anyone who has recited these three lines at school or at home and wondered where they come from.
