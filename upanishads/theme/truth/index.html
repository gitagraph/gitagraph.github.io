Truth and honesty

People who said the true thing when it cost them.

  • Chandogya Upanishad · 4.4Satyakama tells the truth

    A boy who does not know his father tells a stranger exactly that, and is accepted.

    For a student starting somewhere new, or anyone who has wondered whether to tell the plain truth about themselves.
  • Taittiriya Upanishad · 1.11The teacher's farewell

    A teacher's parting words to a student about to go out into the world.

    For a student finishing school or college and stepping into a life of their own.
  • Prashna Upanishad · 1.1–1.5, 6.1–6.8Six seekers come to Pippalada

    Six learned men bring their questions to a sage. He tells them to wait a year.

    For anyone starting out with a new teacher, coach or mentor, and eager for answers.
