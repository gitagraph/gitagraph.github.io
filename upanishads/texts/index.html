The Upanishads

The Upanishads

  • Katha Upanishad: Krishna Yajurveda. A boy goes to Death and will not leave without an answer. Verse, in two parts of three vallis each.
  • Chandogya Upanishad: Samaveda. Eight chapters of teachers and students: Uddalaka and his son, Satyakama, Narada, Indra and Prajapati.
  • Brihadaranyaka Upanishad: Shukla Yajurveda. The 'great forest teaching': Yajnavalkya with Maitreyi, Gargi and King Janaka.
  • Kena Upanishad: Samaveda. 'By whom?' asks who moves the mind, and tells of the gods and the mysterious Yaksha.
  • Taittiriya Upanishad: Krishna Yajurveda. A teacher's farewell to his students, and Bhrigu's search from food to bliss.
  • Mundaka Upanishad: Atharvaveda. A householder asks what, once known, makes everything known; and two birds sit on one tree.
  • Prashna Upanishad: Atharvaveda. Six seekers come to the sage Pippalada, and each asks one question.
  • Isha Upanishad: Shukla Yajurveda. The shortest principal Upanishad: eighteen verses on living in a world that is not yours to own.
  • Mandukya Upanishad: Atharvaveda. Om, and the four states: waking, dream, deep sleep and the fourth.
