The inner light

The inner light

The light by which we see when sun and moon are gone.

  • Kena Upanishad · 3–4The Yaksha, the gods and Uma

    The fire god boasts he can burn everything. A single blade of grass will not burn.

    For anyone who has wondered where their own talent and strength really come from.
  • Brihadaranyaka Upanishad · 1.3.28From what is not to what is

    Three lines murmured during an ancient chant, and the Upanishad's own key to what they mean.

    For anyone who has recited these three lines at school or at home and wondered where they come from.
  • Brihadaranyaka Upanishad · 4.3.1–6Janaka asks: what is a person's light?

    The sun sets, the moon sets, the fire dies, the voices stop. What light is left?

    For anyone who has sat in a dark room and wondered how they still know they are there.
