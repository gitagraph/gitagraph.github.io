Gautama Haridrumata

The teacher who accepted Satyakama because he told the truth.

  • Chandogya Upanishad · 4.4Satyakama tells the truth

    A boy who does not know his father tells a stranger exactly that, and is accepted.

    For a student starting somewhere new, or anyone who has wondered whether to tell the plain truth about themselves.
  • Chandogya Upanishad · 4.5–4.9Satyakama's four teachers

    Alone with a herd for years, a young man learns from a bull, fire and birds.

    For anyone who has learned as much from quiet, ordinary hours as from any classroom.
