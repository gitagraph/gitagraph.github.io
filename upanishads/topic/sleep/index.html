Sleep and dream

What dreams and deep sleep show about the self.

  • Chandogya Upanishad · 8.7–8.12Indra, Virochana and the reflection in water

    Two kings look into a pan of water. One goes home satisfied; the other keeps asking.

    For anyone who has looked in a mirror and wondered who is doing the looking.
  • Chandogya Upanishad · 4.1–4.3Raikva, the man with the cart

    A famously generous king overhears two swans say a man with a cart outshines him.

    For anyone who has done well in life and quietly wonders what it all adds up to.
  • Brihadaranyaka Upanishad · 2.1Proud Balaki and King Ajatashatru

    A scholar comes to teach a king, runs out of answers, and learns from a sleeper.

    For someone proud of their degree, and curious about what it still leaves out.
  • Mandukya Upanishad · 1–12Om and the four states

    Waking, dreaming, dreamless sleep: the shortest Upanishad points to what stays through all three.

    For anyone who has wondered where "I" go in deep, dreamless sleep.
