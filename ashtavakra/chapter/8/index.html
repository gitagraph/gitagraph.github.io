Bondage and Freedom

तदा बन्धो यदा चित्तम्

The shortest definition in the text. Bondage is when the mind wants something, grieves, grabs or lets go, is pleased or angry. Freedom is when it does none of this. Where there is an 'I', there is bondage; where there is no 'I', there is freedom.

इस ग्रंथ की सबसे छोटी परिभाषा। बंधन तब है जब मन कुछ चाहता है, शोक करता है, पकड़ता या छोड़ता है, प्रसन्न या क्रोधित होता है। मुक्ति तब है जब वह इनमें से कुछ नहीं करता। जहाँ 'मैं' है वहाँ बंधन है; जहाँ 'मैं' नहीं वहाँ मुक्ति है।

  1. 8.1

    It is bondage when the mind wants something or grieves over something, lets go of something or grabs something, is pleased by something or angered by something.

  2. 8.2

    It is liberation when the mind does not want or grieve, does not let go or grab, is not pleased or angered.

  3. 8.3

    It is bondage when the mind clings to any of the things it sees. It is liberation when the mind clings to nothing it sees.

  4. 8.4

    When there is no 'I', there is liberation; when there is 'I', there is bondage. Seeing this, lightly and without effort, neither take hold of anything nor push anything away.
