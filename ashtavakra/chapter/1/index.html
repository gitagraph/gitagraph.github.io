Chapter 1 · The Witness

Chapter 1 · 20 verses

The Witness

साक्षी चिद्रूपः

Janaka asks three questions: how is knowledge gained, how does liberation come, how is dispassion reached? Ashtavakra answers at once, without preliminaries. You are not earth, water, fire, air or space, not a caste or a stage of life. You are the witness of all this, pure awareness. Stop believing you are the doer, rest in awareness, and be happy now.

जनक तीन प्रश्न पूछते हैं: ज्ञान कैसे मिलता है, मुक्ति कैसे होती है, वैराग्य कैसे आता है? अष्टावक्र बिना किसी भूमिका के तुरंत उत्तर देते हैं। तुम न पृथ्वी हो, न जल, न अग्नि, न वायु, न आकाश; न किसी वर्ण के हो, न किसी आश्रम के। तुम इन सबके साक्षी, शुद्ध चैतन्य हो। 'मैं करने वाला हूँ' यह मान्यता छोड़ो, चैतन्य में विश्राम करो और अभी सुखी हो जाओ।

  1. 1.1

    How does one gain knowledge? How will liberation come? And how is dispassion reached? Tell me this, master.

  2. 1.2

    If you want liberation, child, drop the objects of the senses as you would poison. Take up patience, honesty, kindness, contentment and truth as you would nectar.

  3. 1.3

    You are not earth, nor water, nor fire, nor air, nor space. To be free, know yourself as the Self, the witness of all these, whose nature is consciousness.

  4. 1.4

    If you set the body apart and rest in consciousness, you will this very moment be happy, at peace, and free from bondage.

  5. 1.5

    You are not a Brahmin or any other caste, you belong to no stage of life, and you are not an object of the senses. Unattached and formless, you are the witness of all. Be happy.

  6. 1.6

    Right and wrong, pleasure and pain belong to the mind, not to you, all-pervading one. You are neither the doer nor the enjoyer; you have always been free.

  7. 1.7

    You are the one seer of everything, and you are always essentially free. Your only bondage is this: you see the seer as something other than yourself.

  8. 1.8

    You have been bitten by the great black snake of the ego that says, 'I am the doer.' Drink the nectar of the conviction 'I am not the doer,' and be happy.

  9. 1.9

    With the fire of the certainty 'I am the one, pure awareness,' burn down the dense forest of ignorance. Free from sorrow, be happy.

  10. 1.10

    That in which this world appears, imagined like a snake on a rope, that awareness, bliss beyond all bliss, is what you are. Live happily.

  11. 1.11

    One who thinks himself free is free, and one who thinks himself bound is bound. The saying holds true here: as you think, so you become.

  12. 1.12

    The Self is the witness: all-pervading, complete, one, free, consciousness itself, without action, unattached, without desire, at peace. Only through delusion does it seem caught up in worldly life.

  13. 1.13

    Let go of the illusion that you are a reflected self, and of every sense of outer and inner. Contemplate the Self as unchanging, non-dual awareness.

  14. 1.14

    For a long time, my child, you have been bound by the noose of identifying with the body. Cut it with the sword of the knowledge 'I am awareness,' and be happy.

  15. 1.15

    You are unattached, actionless, self-luminous and without stain. Your bondage is just this: that you keep practising samadhi.

  16. 1.16

    This whole world is pervaded by you and truly woven into you. You are by nature pure awareness. Do not fall into smallness of mind.

  17. 1.17

    Be free of expectation, unchanging, full, cool at heart, of unfathomable understanding and undisturbed, with your only inclination toward pure consciousness.

  18. 1.18

    Know that whatever has form is unreal, and the formless is unmoving. Through this teaching of the truth, you will not be born into the world again.

  19. 1.19

    Just as a mirror is both inside and all around the image seen in it, so the Supreme Lord is within this body and all around it.

  20. 1.20

    Just as the one all-pervading space is both outside and inside a pot, so the eternal, unbroken Brahman is in all the multitude of beings.
