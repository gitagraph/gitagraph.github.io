Chapter 16 · Forget Everything

Chapter 16 · 11 verses

Forget Everything

विशेषोपदेशः

You may hear and recite many scriptures, but you will not be at ease until you forget everything. Effort is the root of suffering. Even if Shiva, Vishnu or Brahma were your teacher, you would find no rest without letting all of it go.

तुम अनेक शास्त्र सुन और दोहरा सकते हो, पर जब तक सब कुछ नहीं भूलते, तब तक सुखी नहीं होगे। प्रयास ही दुख की जड़ है। चाहे शिव, विष्णु या ब्रह्मा तुम्हारे गुरु हों, सब कुछ छोड़े बिना विश्राम नहीं मिलेगा।

  1. 16.1

    You may recite or listen to many scriptures again and again, child, yet you will not rest in yourself until you forget everything.

  2. 16.2

    Enjoy pleasures, do your work, or practise samadhi, knowing one; still, your mind will long above all for the state where every hope has fallen away.

  3. 16.3

    Everyone is miserable because of effort, yet no one understands this. Through this one teaching alone, the fortunate one reaches release.

  4. 16.4

    Happiness belongs to that master of idleness who finds even opening and closing his eyes a bother, and to no one else.

  5. 16.5

    When the mind is free of opposites like 'this is done, this is not done', it no longer cares for duty, wealth, pleasure or liberation.

  6. 16.6

    The renunciant hates sense objects; the passionate person is greedy for them. But one who neither grasps nor lets go is neither renunciant nor passionate.

  7. 16.7

    As long as craving lives, the soil in which want of discernment grows, so long will the sense of rejecting and accepting remain, the sprout of the tree of worldly life.

  8. 16.8

    Activity breeds attachment, and withdrawal breeds aversion. The wise person, free of opposites like a child, simply stays as he is.

  9. 16.9

    The passionate person wants to leave worldly life to escape sorrow. But one free of passion is free of sorrow and is not troubled even while in the world.

  10. 16.10

    One who takes pride even in liberation and still claims the body as his own is neither a knower nor a yogi; he simply has his share of suffering.

  11. 16.11

    Even if Shiva, Vishnu or lotus-born Brahma were your teacher, you would not rest in yourself until you forget everything.
