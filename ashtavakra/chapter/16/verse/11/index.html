Ashtavakra Gita 16.11

Ashtavakra

हरो यद्युपदेष्टा ते हरिः कमलजोऽपि वा ।
तथापि न तव स्वास्थ्यं सर्वविस्मरणादृते ॥ १६.११ ॥

haro yadyupadeṣṭā te hariḥ kamalajo'pi vā ।
tathāpi na tava svāsthyaṃ sarvavismaraṇādṛte ॥

Even if Shiva, Vishnu or lotus-born Brahma were your teacher, you would not rest in yourself until you forget everything.

हिन्दीचाहे शिव, विष्णु या कमल से जन्मे ब्रह्मा भी तुम्हारे उपदेशक हों, फिर भी सब कुछ भूले बिना तुम अपने आप में स्थिर नहीं हो पाओगे।

People: Ashtavakra · Shiva (Hara) · Vishnu (Hari) · Brahma

Ideas: Teacher and scripture · Stillness and rest
