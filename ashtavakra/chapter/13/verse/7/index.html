Chapter 13 · I Live at EaseVerse 7 of 7

Ashtavakra Gita 13.7

Janaka

सुखादिरूपा नियमं भावेष्वालोक्य भूरिशः ।
शुभाशुभे विहायास्मादहमासे यथासुखम् ॥ १३.७ ॥

sukhādirūpā niyamaṃ bhāveṣvālokya bhūriśaḥ ।
śubhāśubhe vihāyāsmādahamāse yathāsukham ॥

Having seen again and again how pleasure and the like come and go without any rule among things, I let go of good and bad, and live at ease, as it suits me.

हिन्दीबार-बार देखकर कि वस्तुओं में सुख आदि का कोई नियम नहीं, शुभ और अशुभ दोनों को छोड़कर मैं मौज से, सहज सुख में रहता हूँ।

People: Janaka

Ideas: Equanimity · Dispassion · Happiness and ease
