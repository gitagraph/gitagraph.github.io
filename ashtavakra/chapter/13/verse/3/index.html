Chapter 13 · I Live at EaseVerse 3 of 7

Ashtavakra Gita 13.3

Janaka

कृतं किमपि नैव स्याद् इति संचिन्त्य तत्त्वतः ।
यदा यत्कर्तुमायाति तत् कृत्वासे यथासुखम् ॥ १३.३ ॥

kṛtaṃ kimapi naiva syād iti saṃcintya tattvataḥ ।
yadā yatkartumāyāti tat kṛtvāse yathāsukham ॥

Seeing truly that nothing is ever really done, I do whatever comes to be done when it comes, and live at ease, as it suits me.

हिन्दीयह भली-भाँति समझकर कि वास्तव में कुछ भी किया नहीं जाता, जब जो करने को आता है उसे करके मैं मौज से, सहज सुख में रहता हूँ।

People: Janaka

Ideas: Not the doer · Action and inaction · Effortlessness · Happiness and ease
