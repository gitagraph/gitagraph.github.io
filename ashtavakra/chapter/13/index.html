Chapter 13 · 7 verses

I Live at Ease

अहमासे यथासुखम्

The ease of having nothing is rare even in a loincloth. Janaka has dropped both taking and giving up. Whether he sleeps or strives, sits or walks, nothing is gained or lost, and so, he says, 'I live at ease.'

कुछ न होने का सुख लंगोटी में भी दुर्लभ है। जनक ने लेना और छोड़ना दोनों छोड़ दिए हैं। वे सोएँ या प्रयास करें, बैठें या चलें, न कुछ मिलता है न खोता है, इसलिए वे कहते हैं, 'मैं सुख से रहता हूँ।'

  1. 13.1

    The ease that comes from owning nothing is hard to find even for one who wears only a loincloth. So, letting go of both giving up and taking, I live at ease, as it suits me.

  2. 13.2

    Somewhere the body gets weary, somewhere the tongue gets tired, somewhere the mind. Leaving all that behind, I rest happily in life's true aim.

  3. 13.3

    Seeing truly that nothing is ever really done, I do whatever comes to be done when it comes, and live at ease, as it suits me.

  4. 13.4

    Yogis who identify with the body insist on action or on inaction. Free of both union and separation, I live at ease, as it suits me.

  5. 13.5

    I gain nothing and lose nothing by standing, walking or lying down; so whether I stand, walk or sleep, I live at ease, as it suits me.

  6. 13.6

    I lose nothing by sleeping and gain nothing by striving. So, letting go of both loss and elation, I live at ease, as it suits me.

  7. 13.7

    Having seen again and again how pleasure and the like come and go without any rule among things, I let go of good and bad, and live at ease, as it suits me.
