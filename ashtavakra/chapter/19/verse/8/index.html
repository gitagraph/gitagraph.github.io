Ashtavakra Gita 19.8

Janaka

अलं त्रिवर्गकथया योगस्य कथयाप्यलं ।
अलं विज्ञानकथया विश्रान्तस्य ममात्मनि ॥ १९.८ ॥

alaṃ trivargakathayā yogasya kathayāpyalaṃ ।
alaṃ vijñānakathayā viśrāntasya mamātmani ॥

Enough of talk about the three aims of life, enough of talk about yoga, enough even of talk about knowledge, for me who rests in the Self.

हिन्दीधर्म, अर्थ, काम की चर्चा बस हो, योग की चर्चा भी बस हो, और ज्ञान की चर्चा भी बस हो — मेरे लिए, जो आत्मा में विश्राम कर रहा हूँ।

People: Janaka

Ideas: Wealth, pleasure, duty · Meditation and samadhi · Knowledge · Stillness and rest
