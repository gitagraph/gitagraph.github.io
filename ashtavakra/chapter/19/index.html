Chapter 19 · Rest in the Self

Chapter 19 · 8 verses

Rest in the Self

आत्मविश्रान्तिः

Janaka answers his teacher. With the tongs of knowledge he has pulled the thorn of opinions from his heart. Now, standing in his own glory, he asks: where is duty, where pleasure, where past and future, where near and far?

जनक अपने गुरु को उत्तर देते हैं। ज्ञान की चिमटी से उन्होंने हृदय से मतों का काँटा निकाल दिया है। अब अपनी महिमा में स्थित होकर वे पूछते हैं: कहाँ धर्म, कहाँ भोग, कहाँ भूत और भविष्य, कहाँ निकट और दूर?

  1. 19.1

    With the tongs of the knowledge of truth, I have pulled out from deep within my heart the thorn of countless opinions.

  2. 19.2

    Where is duty, where is pleasure, where is wealth, where is discernment? Where is duality, and where is non-duality, for me who abides in my own glory?

  3. 19.3

    Where is the past, where is the future, where even the present? Where is space, and where is eternity, for me who abides in my own glory?

  4. 19.4

    Where is the Self, where is the not-Self, where is good, where is bad? Where is thought, and where is no-thought, for me who abides in my own glory?

  5. 19.5

    Where is dreaming, where is deep sleep, where is waking? Where is the fourth state, or even fear, for me who abides in my own glory?

  6. 19.6

    Where is far, where is near, where is outside, where is inside? Where is gross, and where is subtle, for me who abides in my own glory?

  7. 19.7

    Where is death, where is life, where are the worlds, and where are worldly affairs? Where is dissolution, and where is samadhi, for me who abides in my own glory?

  8. 19.8

    Enough of talk about the three aims of life, enough of talk about yoga, enough even of talk about knowledge, for me who rests in the Self.
