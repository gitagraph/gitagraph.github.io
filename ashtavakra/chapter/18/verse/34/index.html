Ashtavakra Gita 18.34

Ashtavakra

अप्रयत्नात् प्रयत्नाद् वा मूढो नाप्नोति निर्वृतिं ।
तत्त्वनिश्चयमात्रेण प्राज्ञो भवति निर्वृतः ॥ १८.३४ ॥

aprayatnāt prayatnād vā mūḍho nāpnoti nirvṛtiṃ ।
tattvaniścayamātreṇa prājño bhavati nirvṛtaḥ ॥

Neither by effort nor by giving up effort does the deluded one find peace. The wise one is at peace simply by being certain of the truth.

हिन्दीमूढ़ न प्रयत्न से शांति पाता है, न प्रयत्न छोड़ने से। ज्ञानी केवल तत्त्व के निश्चय से ही शांत हो जाता है।

People: Ashtavakra

Ideas: The deluded one · Knowledge · Stillness and rest · Effortlessness
