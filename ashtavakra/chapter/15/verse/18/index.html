Ashtavakra Gita 15.18

Ashtavakra

एक एव भवांभोधावासीदस्ति भविष्यति ।
न ते बन्धोऽस्ति मोक्षो वा कृतकृत्यः सुखं चर ॥ १५.१८ ॥

eka eva bhavāṃbhodhāvāsīdasti bhaviṣyati ।
na te bandho'sti mokṣo vā kṛtakṛtyaḥ sukhaṃ cara ॥

In the ocean of existence there was, is and will be only one. You have no bondage and no liberation. Your work is done; go about happily.

हिन्दीसंसार-सागर में एक ही था, एक ही है और एक ही रहेगा। तुम्हारा न बंधन है न मोक्ष; तुम कृतकृत्य हो, सुख से विचरो।

People: Ashtavakra

Ideas: Oneness · Bondage · Liberation · The ocean and its waves
