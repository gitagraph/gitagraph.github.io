Chapter 15 · 20 verses

You Are That

तत्त्वोपदेशः

Ashtavakra returns to teaching, more freely now. A person of pure mind gets it from a hint; another spends a lifetime asking. You are the ocean in which the world rises and falls. Trust this, child, do not be confused. Give up even meditation; hold nothing in the heart.

अष्टावक्र फिर उपदेश देते हैं, अब और खुलकर। शुद्ध मन वाला एक संकेत से समझ जाता है; दूसरा जीवन भर पूछता रहता है। तुम वह सागर हो जिसमें संसार उठता और डूबता है। इस पर विश्वास करो, वत्स, भ्रम में मत पड़ो। ध्यान भी छोड़ दो; मन में कुछ भी मत रखो।

  1. 15.1

    A person of pure understanding reaches life's goal even from a passing word of instruction, while another may keep inquiring all his life and still stay confused about it.

  2. 15.2

    Liberation is losing the taste for sense objects; bondage is relishing them. That is all there is to know. Now do as you wish.

  3. 15.3

    This knowledge of truth turns an eloquent, clever, hard-striving person into one who is silent, still and idle. That is why those hungry for enjoyment avoid it.

  4. 15.4

    You are not the body, nor is the body yours; you are neither the doer nor the enjoyer. You are consciousness itself, ever the witness, needing nothing. Go about happily.

  5. 15.5

    Attraction and aversion belong to the mind, and the mind is never yours. You are free of thought, pure awareness, unchanging. Go about happily.

  6. 15.6

    Knowing the Self in all beings and all beings in the Self, be free of 'I' and 'mine', and be happy.

  7. 15.7

    That in which this world shimmers like waves on the sea, that is what you are, without doubt. You who are consciousness itself, let the fever go.

  8. 15.8

    Have faith, child, have faith; do not be confused about this, my dear. You are the Self, knowledge itself, the Lord, beyond nature (prakriti).

  9. 15.9

    The body, wrapped in the qualities (gunas), stays, comes and goes. The Self neither goes nor comes. Why grieve over it?

  10. 15.10

    Let the body last until the end of the age, or let it go this very day. What gain or loss is there for you, who are pure consciousness?

  11. 15.11

    In you, the endless great ocean, let the wave of the world rise or fall by its own nature. You neither gain nor lose.

  12. 15.12

    Child, you are pure consciousness; this world is not separate from you. So who could reject or accept anything, and how, and where?

  13. 15.13

    In you, the one, the imperishable, the peaceful, the stainless sky of consciousness, where is birth, where is action, where is even the ego?

  14. 15.14

    Whatever you see, it is you alone appearing there. Do bracelets, armlets and anklets shine apart from the gold?

  15. 15.15

    Give up dividing things into 'this I am' and 'this I am not'. Knowing for certain that all is the Self, let go of intentions and be happy.

  16. 15.16

    The world exists only through your own ignorance; in truth you alone are. Apart from you there is no one, bound to worldly life or free of it.

  17. 15.17

    One who knows for certain that this world is mere illusion and nothing at all is freed of latent desires; he is sheer radiance and comes to rest as though he were nothing.

  18. 15.18

    In the ocean of existence there was, is and will be only one. You have no bondage and no liberation. Your work is done; go about happily.

  19. 15.19

    You who are made of consciousness, do not stir up your mind with wanting and doubting. Grow calm and rest happily in your own Self, which is bliss itself.

  20. 15.20

    Give up meditation altogether; hold nothing in your heart. You are the Self, already free. What will you achieve by pondering?
