Four Ways to Dissolve

लयं व्रज

Four short verses, each ending with the same advice: 'dissolve, just like this.' Whether you see the world as a bubble on the sea, a snake in a rope, or pleasure and pain as passing, let the separate self melt back into what it always was.

चार छोटे श्लोक, हर एक का अंत एक ही सलाह से: 'बस ऐसे ही विलीन हो जाओ।' चाहे संसार को सागर का बुलबुला देखो, रस्सी में साँप, या सुख-दुख को आने-जाने वाला, अलग 'मैं' को उसमें घुल जाने दो जो वह सदा से था।

  1. 5.1

    You are tied to nothing at all. Being pure, what would you renounce? Letting this bundle of body and mind fall apart, just so, let yourself dissolve.

  2. 5.2

    The universe rises from you like a bubble from the sea. Knowing the Self to be one, just so, let yourself dissolve.

  3. 5.3

    Though it is right before your eyes, the universe has no real substance, and so it does not exist in you, the stainless one, any more than the snake in a rope. Just so, let yourself dissolve.

  4. 5.4

    Same in pain and pleasure, full, equal in hope and despair, equal in life and death — being so, just so, let yourself dissolve.
