Chapter 20 · Free While Living

Chapter 20 · 14 verses

Free While Living

जीवन्मुक्तिः

The last chapter is a long series of 'where is…?'. Where are the elements, the body, the mind? Where is bondage, where liberation? Where is the teacher, the scripture, the disciple? The text ends with Janaka saying that nothing at all arises for him any more.

अंतिम अध्याय 'कहाँ है…?' की एक लंबी शृंखला है। कहाँ तत्व, कहाँ शरीर, कहाँ मन? कहाँ बंधन, कहाँ मुक्ति? कहाँ गुरु, कहाँ शास्त्र, कहाँ शिष्य? ग्रंथ जनक के इस कथन के साथ समाप्त होता है कि अब उनके लिए कुछ भी नहीं उठता।

  1. 20.1

    Where are the elements, where is the body, where are the senses, where is the mind? Where is emptiness, and where is despair, in my own stainless nature?

  2. 20.2

    Where is scripture, where is knowledge of the Self, where is a mind free of objects? Where is contentment, and where is freedom from craving, for me who am always beyond the pairs of opposites?

  3. 20.3

    Where is knowledge, where is ignorance, where is 'I', where is 'this', where is 'mine'? Where is bondage, where is liberation? How can my own nature have any form?

  4. 20.4

    Where are the past actions now bearing fruit, where is liberation while living, and where is that final freedom after the body falls, for me who am always without distinctions?

  5. 20.5

    Where is the doer, where is the enjoyer, where is actionless shining? Where is direct realisation, and where is its fruit, for me who am always without any nature of my own?

  6. 20.6

    Where is the world, where is the seeker of liberation, where is the yogi, where is the knower? Where is the bound, and where is the free, for me in my own non-dual nature?

  7. 20.7

    Where is creation, where is destruction, where is the goal, where is the means? Where is the seeker, and where is attainment, for me in my own non-dual nature?

  8. 20.8

    Where is the knower, where is the means of knowing, where is the known, where is knowing itself? Where is something, and where is nothing, for me who am always pure?

  9. 20.9

    Where is distraction, where is one-pointedness, where is dullness, where is delusion? Where is joy, and where is sorrow, for me who am always actionless?

  10. 20.10

    Where is everyday life, and where is ultimate truth? Where is happiness, and where is sorrow, for me who am always beyond thought?

  11. 20.11

    Where is illusion, where is worldly life, where is attachment, where is detachment? Where is the individual soul, and where is that Brahman, for me who am always pure?

  12. 20.12

    Where is activity, where is withdrawal, where is liberation, where is bondage, for me who am always unchanging, undivided and at rest in myself?

  13. 20.13

    Where is instruction, where is scripture, where is the disciple, where is the teacher? Where is any goal of life, for me who am free of all limits and auspicious?

  14. 20.14

    Where is existence, where is non-existence, where is oneness, where is twoness? What more is there to say? Nothing at all arises for me.
