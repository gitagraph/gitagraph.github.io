Chapter 14 · 4 verses

The Quiet Heart

शान्तिः

Four verses from one whose thinking has worn away. He sleeps as if awake and wakes as if asleep. Desire is gone, so where is wealth, where are friends, where the robbers of the senses? Inwardly empty, outwardly he may look like anyone.

चार श्लोक, उसके जिसके विचार घिस कर मिट गए हैं। वह सोते हुए भी जागा-सा है और जागते हुए भी सोया-सा। इच्छा चली गई, तो कहाँ धन, कहाँ मित्र, कहाँ इंद्रियों के लुटेरे? भीतर से खाली, बाहर से वह किसी और जैसा ही दिख सकता है।

  1. 14.1

    One whose mind is by nature empty, who thinks of things only by oversight, who is as if awake even in sleep — his memories of worldly life have worn away.

  2. 14.2

    Where now is wealth, where are friends, where are the thieves that are my senses' objects? Where is scripture, where is knowledge, now that my wanting has melted away?

  3. 14.3

    Now that I have known the witness, the supreme Self and the Lord, and expect nothing from either bondage or freedom, I have no worry about liberation.

  4. 14.4

    He is empty of thoughts within and moves freely without, seeming like someone confused; only those like him can know the states he passes through.
