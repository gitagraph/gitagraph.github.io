Ashtavakra Gita 14.2

Janaka

क्व धनानि क्व मित्राणि क्व मे विषयदस्यवः ।
क्व शास्त्रं क्व च विज्ञानं यदा मे गलिता स्पृहा ॥ १४.२ ॥

kva dhanāni kva mitrāṇi kva me viṣayadasyavaḥ ।
kva śāstraṃ kva ca vijñānaṃ yadā me galitā spṛhā ॥

Where now is wealth, where are friends, where are the thieves that are my senses' objects? Where is scripture, where is knowledge, now that my wanting has melted away?

हिन्दीजब मेरी इच्छा ही गल गई, तो कहाँ धन, कहाँ मित्र, कहाँ विषय रूपी लुटेरे? कहाँ शास्त्र और कहाँ ज्ञान?

People: Janaka

Ideas: Desire · Wealth, pleasure, duty · Teacher and scripture
