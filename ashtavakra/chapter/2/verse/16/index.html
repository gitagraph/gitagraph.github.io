Ashtavakra Gita 2.16

Janaka

द्वैतमूलमहो दुःखं नान्यत्तस्याऽस्ति भेषजं ।
दृश्यमेतन् मृषा सर्वं एकोऽहं चिद्रसोमलः ॥ २.१६ ॥

dvaitamūlamaho duḥkhaṃ nānyattasyā'sti bheṣajaṃ ।
dṛśyametan mṛṣā sarvaṃ eko'haṃ cidrasomalaḥ ॥

Oh! Duality is the root of suffering, and there is no other cure for it than this: all that is seen is false, and I am one, pure, the very essence of consciousness.

हिन्दीअहो! दुःख की जड़ द्वैत है। इसकी कोई और दवा नहीं, सिवाय यह जानने के कि यह सारा दृश्य मिथ्या है और मैं एक, निर्मल चैतन्य-रस हूँ।

People: Janaka

Ideas: Fear and sorrow · Oneness · The world as appearance · Pure awareness
