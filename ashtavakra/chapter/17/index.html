Chapter 17 · 20 verses

The One Who Knows

तत्त्वज्ञस्वरूपम्

A portrait of the one who knows the truth. Such a person is content alone, wants neither enjoyment nor liberation, sees, hears, touches and eats without craving, and treats praise and blame, man and woman, fortune and loss alike. Such a person is rare in the world.

सत्य जानने वाले का चित्र। ऐसा व्यक्ति अकेले में संतुष्ट रहता है, न भोग चाहता है न मुक्ति, देखता, सुनता, छूता और खाता है पर लालसा के बिना, और प्रशंसा-निंदा, पुरुष-स्त्री, संपत्ति-विपत्ति को समान देखता है। ऐसा व्यक्ति संसार में दुर्लभ है।

  1. 17.1

    One who is content, with senses clear, and always delights in being alone, has won the fruit of knowledge and the fruit of yoga practice as well.

  2. 17.2

    Ah, the knower of truth is never troubled in this world, for he alone fills this whole sphere of the universe.

  3. 17.3

    No sense objects ever delight one who rejoices in the Self, just as neem leaves do not please an elephant that loves sallakī leaves.

  4. 17.4

    One who does not cling to pleasures already enjoyed and does not crave those not yet enjoyed is rare in this world.

  5. 17.5

    In this world we see people hungry for enjoyment and people hungry for liberation, but rare indeed is the great-souled one who wants neither.

  6. 17.6

    Only a rare, open-hearted person has no sense of rejecting or accepting duty, wealth, pleasure, liberation, life or death.

  7. 17.7

    He neither wishes for the world to dissolve nor resents its going on. So the blessed one lives happily on whatever livelihood comes.

  8. 17.8

    Fulfilled by this knowledge, his thinking mind dissolved, the accomplished one lives happily, seeing, hearing, touching, smelling and eating.

  9. 17.9

    For one in whom the ocean of worldly life has dried up, the gaze is empty, actions have no purpose, the senses are idle; there is neither craving nor aversion.

  10. 17.10

    He is neither awake nor asleep, neither opens his eyes nor closes them. Ah, what a supreme state the free mind abides in!

  11. 17.11

    The free one is seen everywhere at ease, everywhere pure in heart; released from all latent desires, he shines wherever he is.

  12. 17.12

    Seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, taking, speaking, walking, yet free of wanting and not wanting, the great-souled one is truly free.

  13. 17.13

    He neither blames nor praises, is neither elated nor angry, neither gives nor takes. The free one has no taste for anything anywhere.

  14. 17.14

    Whether he sees a woman full of love or death drawing near, the great-souled one stays steady in mind and at ease. He is truly free.

  15. 17.15

    In pleasure and pain, in man and woman, in good fortune and bad, the wise one sees no difference, for he sees everything with an equal eye.

  16. 17.16

    In one whose wandering through worldly life is over, there is no violence and no pity, no arrogance and no meekness, no amazement and no agitation.

  17. 17.17

    The free one neither hates sense objects nor is greedy for them. With a mind always unattached, he takes what comes and what does not come alike.

  18. 17.18

    With an empty mind, as if established in complete aloneness (kaivalya), he does not even know the notions of focused and unfocused, good and bad.

  19. 17.19

    Without 'mine', without 'I', certain that nothing is, with every hope melted away within, he does nothing even while acting.

  20. 17.20

    One whose mind has melted away reaches a state beyond words, free of the mind's display, delusion, dreaming and dullness.
