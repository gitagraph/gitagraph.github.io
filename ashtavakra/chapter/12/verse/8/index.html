Ashtavakra Gita 12.8

Janaka

एवमेव कृतं येन स कृतार्थो भवेदसौ ।
एवमेव स्वभावो यः स कृतार्थो भवेदसौ ॥ १२.८ ॥

evameva kṛtaṃ yena sa kṛtārtho bhavedasau ।
evameva svabhāvo yaḥ sa kṛtārtho bhavedasau ॥

Whoever has done just this has fulfilled the purpose of life. Whoever is just so by nature has fulfilled the purpose of life.

हिन्दीजिसने ठीक ऐसा ही कर लिया है, वह कृतार्थ है; जिसका स्वभाव ही ऐसा है, वह कृतार्थ है।

People: Janaka

Ideas: The free one · Effortlessness
