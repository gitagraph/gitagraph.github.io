Ashtavakra Gita 4.1

Janaka

हन्तात्मज्ञस्य धीरस्य खेलतो भोगलीलया ।
न हि संसारवाहीकैर्मूढैः सह समानता ॥ ४.१ ॥

hantātmajñasya dhīrasya khelato bhogalīlayā ।
na hi saṃsāravāhīkairmūḍhaiḥ saha samānatā ॥

Ah, the wise one who knows the Self plays the game of life's enjoyments, and bears no likeness at all to the deluded, who are hauled along like beasts of burden by the world.

हिन्दीअहो, आत्मा को जानने वाला धीर पुरुष भोगों का खेल खेलता है; संसार का बोझ ढोने वाले मूढ़ों से उसकी कोई समानता नहीं।

People: Janaka

Ideas: The free one · The deluded one · Effortlessness
