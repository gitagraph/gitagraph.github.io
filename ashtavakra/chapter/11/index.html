Chapter 11 · Wisdom

Chapter 11 · 8 verses

Wisdom

ज्ञानम्

Everything that happens arises from its own nature. Knowing this, the wise one is not troubled. Pleasure and pain, birth and death come by destiny; seeing that nothing is done by 'me', one is at peace, free of desire, like empty space.

जो कुछ होता है, अपने स्वभाव से होता है। यह जानकर ज्ञानी विचलित नहीं होता। सुख-दुख, जन्म-मृत्यु भाग्य से आते हैं; यह देखकर कि 'मैं' कुछ नहीं करता, व्यक्ति शांत, इच्छा-रहित, खाली आकाश जैसा हो जाता है।

  1. 11.1

    One who knows for certain that existence, non-existence and change all arise from the nature of things becomes free of change and distress, and easily finds peace.

  2. 11.2

    One who knows for certain that God is the maker of everything and there is no other here has all his hopes melted away within; at peace, he clings to nothing anywhere.

  3. 11.3

    One who knows for certain that misfortune and good fortune come in their time by destiny alone stays content, his senses at rest; he neither longs nor grieves.

  4. 11.4

    One who knows for certain that pleasure and pain, birth and death come by destiny alone sees nothing to be achieved and makes no strain; even while acting he is not stained.

  5. 11.5

    One who knows for certain that suffering in this world comes from anxious thinking and nothing else is freed of it — happy, at peace, his wants fallen away everywhere.

  6. 11.6

    One who knows for certain, 'I am not the body, the body is not mine, I am awareness,' is as if he has reached absolute aloneness; he does not recall what was done or left undone.

  7. 11.7

    One who knows for certain, 'From Brahma down to a blade of grass, it is all I alone,' is free of thought, pure and at peace, untroubled by what is gained or not gained.

  8. 11.8

    One who knows for certain that this universe, with all its many wonders, is nothing at all becomes free of latent desires; pure shining awareness, he rests in peace as if nothing were there.
