Chapter 10 · 8 verses

Letting Go of Desire

उपशमः

Drop pleasure, wealth and even religious duty, which are their cause. Wives, friends, land and riches are a dream that lasts a few days. Desire alone is the world; give it up and you are free.

भोग, धन और उनके कारण धर्म को भी छोड़ दो। पत्नी, मित्र, भूमि और धन कुछ दिनों का सपना हैं। इच्छा ही संसार है; उसे छोड़ दो तो तुम मुक्त हो।

  1. 10.1

    Put away desire, which is your enemy, and wealth, which is full of trouble, and even duty, which is the root of both. Be indifferent to all of them.

  2. 10.2

    See friends, lands, money, houses, wives, inheritances and all such riches as a dream or a magic show, lasting three days or five.

  3. 10.3

    Wherever there is craving, know that the world of becoming is right there. Taking firm hold of mature dispassion, be free of craving and be happy.

  4. 10.4

    Bondage is made of craving alone, and its ending is called liberation. Simply by not clinging to the world, one finds contentment again and again.

  5. 10.5

    You are the one pure consciousness; the world is inert and unreal. Even ignorance is nothing at all. So what is left for you to want to know?

  6. 10.6

    Kingdoms, sons, wives, bodies and pleasures have been lost to you birth after birth, even though you clung to them.

  7. 10.7

    Enough of wealth, of pleasure, and even of good deeds. In the wilderness of the world, the mind never found rest through any of them.

  8. 10.8

    For how many lives have you not toiled with body, mind and speech at painful, exhausting work? At least now, let it come to rest.
