Chapter 10 · Letting Go of DesireVerse 1 of 8

Ashtavakra Gita 10.1

Ashtavakra

विहाय वैरिणं काममर्थं चानर्थसंकुलं ।
धर्ममप्येतयोर्हेतुं सर्वत्रानादरं कुरु ॥ १०.१ ॥

vihāya vairiṇaṃ kāmamarthaṃ cānarthasaṃkulaṃ ।
dharmamapyetayorhetuṃ sarvatrānādaraṃ kuru ॥

Put away desire, which is your enemy, and wealth, which is full of trouble, and even duty, which is the root of both. Be indifferent to all of them.

हिन्दीशत्रु रूपी काम को, अनर्थों से भरे अर्थ को, और इन दोनों के कारण धर्म को भी छोड़कर, सब के प्रति उदासीन हो जाओ।

People: Ashtavakra

Ideas: Wealth, pleasure, duty · Desire · Dispassion
