Ashtavakra Gita 10.3

Ashtavakra

यत्र यत्र भवेत्तृष्णा संसारं विद्धि तत्र वै ।
प्रौढवैराग्यमाश्रित्य वीततृष्णः सुखी भव ॥ १०.३ ॥

yatra yatra bhavettṛṣṇā saṃsāraṃ viddhi tatra vai ।
prauḍhavairāgyamāśritya vītatṛṣṇaḥ sukhī bhava ॥

Wherever there is craving, know that the world of becoming is right there. Taking firm hold of mature dispassion, be free of craving and be happy.

हिन्दीजहाँ-जहाँ तृष्णा है, वहीं संसार जानो। दृढ़ वैराग्य का आश्रय लेकर तृष्णा से मुक्त हो जाओ और सुखी रहो।

People: Ashtavakra

Ideas: Desire · Dispassion · Happiness and ease
