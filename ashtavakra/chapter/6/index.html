Chapter 6 · 4 verses

Boundless as Space

आकाशवदनन्तोऽहम्

Four verses of pure statement: I am like space, the world like a pot; I am the ocean, the world its waves; I am the mother-of-pearl, the world its silver glint; I am in all beings and all beings are in me. Knowing this, there is nothing to give up, nothing to take, nothing to dissolve.

चार श्लोक, सीधे कथन: मैं आकाश जैसा हूँ, संसार घड़े जैसा; मैं सागर हूँ, संसार उसकी लहरें; मैं सीप हूँ, संसार उसकी चाँदी जैसी चमक; मैं सब प्राणियों में हूँ और सब प्राणी मुझमें। यह जान लेने पर न कुछ छोड़ना है, न पकड़ना, न विलीन करना।

  1. 6.1

    I am boundless like space; the world of nature is like a pot. This is knowledge, and so there is nothing to give up, nothing to take hold of, nothing to dissolve.

  2. 6.2

    I am like the great ocean; this spread of the world is like its waves. This is knowledge, and so there is nothing to give up, nothing to take hold of, nothing to dissolve.

  3. 6.3

    I am like the mother-of-pearl; the imagined universe is like the silver seen in it. This is knowledge, and so there is nothing to give up, nothing to take hold of, nothing to dissolve.

  4. 6.4

    I am in all beings, and all beings are in me. This is knowledge, and so there is nothing to give up, nothing to take hold of, nothing to dissolve.
