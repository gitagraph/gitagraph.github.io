Vishnu (Hari)

हरि

The preserver god

Named once, with Shiva and Brahma: even Hari as your teacher could not make you whole without forgetting everything.

एक बार शिव और ब्रह्मा के साथ नाम आता है: स्वयं हरि भी गुरु हों, तो भी सब भूले बिना तुम पूर्ण नहीं होगे।

1 verses

  1. 16.11

    Even if Shiva, Vishnu or lotus-born Brahma were your teacher, you would not rest in yourself until you forget everything.
