Not the doer

Not the doer

अकर्तृत्वम्

akartṛtva

'You are not the doer, not the enjoyer.' The belief 'I am the doer' is the black snake whose bite the nectar of 'I am not the doer' cures.

'तुम न करने वाले हो, न भोगने वाले।' 'मैं कर्ता हूँ' वह काला साँप है जिसके डसे का इलाज 'मैं कर्ता नहीं' का अमृत है।

17 verses

  1. 1.6

    Right and wrong, pleasure and pain belong to the mind, not to you, all-pervading one. You are neither the doer nor the enjoyer; you have always been free.

  2. 1.8

    You have been bitten by the great black snake of the ego that says, 'I am the doer.' Drink the nectar of the conviction 'I am not the doer,' and be happy.

  3. 4.3

    For one who knows That, merit and sin never touch the inner self, just as smoke, though it seems to, never truly clings to the sky.

  4. 11.4

    One who knows for certain that pleasure and pain, birth and death come by destiny alone sees nothing to be achieved and makes no strain; even while acting he is not stained.

  5. 11.6

    One who knows for certain, 'I am not the body, the body is not mine, I am awareness,' is as if he has reached absolute aloneness; he does not recall what was done or left undone.

  6. 13.3

    Seeing truly that nothing is ever really done, I do whatever comes to be done when it comes, and live at ease, as it suits me.

  7. 15.4

    You are not the body, nor is the body yours; you are neither the doer nor the enjoyer. You are consciousness itself, ever the witness, needing nothing. Go about happily.

  8. 17.19

    Without 'mine', without 'I', certain that nothing is, with every hope melted away within, he does nothing even while acting.

  9. 18.19

    The awakened one, beyond being and non-being, content and free of latent desire, does nothing at all, though in the world's eyes he acts.

  10. 18.25

    "This action was done by the body, not by me, who am pure by nature." One who keeps to this understanding does nothing, even while acting.

  11. 18.29

    One who carries ego within is acting even when he does nothing. For the wise one free of ego, nothing is done and nothing is left undone.

  12. 18.30

    The mind of the free one shines: not agitated and not gratified, not acting, without stirring, without hopes, its doubts gone.

  13. 18.51

    When one recognises one's own Self as neither the doer nor the enjoyer, all the movements of the mind simply wear away.

  14. 18.64

    The sage who goes about all undertakings without desire, like a child, is pure; no action clings to him, even as it is being done.

  15. 18.77

    One whose actions have fallen away through knowledge, though in the world's eyes he still acts, finds no occasion to do or to say anything at all.

  16. 18.90

    Knowing, he does not know; seeing, he does not see; speaking, he does not speak. Who could this be but one free of all craving?

  17. 20.5

    Where is the doer, where is the enjoyer, where is actionless shining? Where is direct realisation, and where is its fruit, for me who am always without any nature of my own?
