Teacher and scripture

उपदेशः

guru, śāstra, upadeśa

Instruction, scripture and the teacher-student relation, which the final chapter dissolves: 'where is the teacher, where the disciple?'

उपदेश, शास्त्र और गुरु-शिष्य संबंध, जिन्हें अंतिम अध्याय विलीन कर देता है: 'कहाँ गुरु, कहाँ शिष्य?'

14 verses

  1. 1.1

    How does one gain knowledge? How will liberation come? And how is dispassion reached? Tell me this, master.

  2. 1.18

    Know that whatever has form is unreal, and the formless is unmoving. Through this teaching of the truth, you will not be born into the world again.

  3. 9.5

    Seeing how the great sages, the saints and the yogis all hold different views, what person would not grow weary of it all and become calm?

  4. 9.6

    Is he not a true guru who has fully known the nature of consciousness, and who, through dispassion, evenness and right reasoning, carries one across the round of birth and death?

  5. 14.2

    Where now is wealth, where are friends, where are the thieves that are my senses' objects? Where is scripture, where is knowledge, now that my wanting has melted away?

  6. 15.1

    A person of pure understanding reaches life's goal even from a passing word of instruction, while another may keep inquiring all his life and still stay confused about it.

  7. 16.1

    You may recite or listen to many scriptures again and again, child, yet you will not rest in yourself until you forget everything.

  8. 16.3

    Everyone is miserable because of effort, yet no one understands this. Through this one teaching alone, the fortunate one reaches release.

  9. 16.11

    Even if Shiva, Vishnu or lotus-born Brahma were your teacher, you would not rest in yourself until you forget everything.

  10. 18.32

    Hearing the truth as it really is, a dull person becomes bewildered; while some who are not deluded draw inward and seem like fools.

  11. 18.48

    Just by hearing the truth, his mind becomes pure and untroubled. He sees no proper conduct, no improper conduct, and no indifference either.

  12. 18.76

    The dull one, even after hearing that truth, does not give up his delusion. Outwardly he strains to be free of thought, but inwardly he hungers for objects.

  13. 20.2

    Where is scripture, where is knowledge of the Self, where is a mind free of objects? Where is contentment, and where is freedom from craving, for me who am always beyond the pairs of opposites?

  14. 20.13

    Where is instruction, where is scripture, where is the disciple, where is the teacher? Where is any goal of life, for me who am free of all limits and auspicious?
