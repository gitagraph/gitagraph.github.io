The witness

The witness

साक्षी

sākṣī, draṣṭā

The one who sees everything and is touched by none of it. Ashtavakra's first answer: you are the witness.

वह जो सब देखता है और किसी से छुआ नहीं जाता। अष्टावक्र का पहला उत्तर: तुम साक्षी हो।

9 verses

  1. 1.3

    You are not earth, nor water, nor fire, nor air, nor space. To be free, know yourself as the Self, the witness of all these, whose nature is consciousness.

  2. 1.5

    You are not a Brahmin or any other caste, you belong to no stage of life, and you are not something the eyes can see. Unattached and formless, you are the witness of all. Be happy.

  3. 1.7

    You are the one seer of everything, and you are always essentially free. Your only bondage is this: you see the seer as something other than yourself.

  4. 1.12

    The Self is the witness: all-pervading, complete, one, free, consciousness itself, without action, unattached, without desire, at peace. Only through delusion does it seem caught up in worldly life.

  5. 3.10

    The great-hearted one watches his own body act as if it were someone else's. How could praise or blame disturb him?

  6. 14.3

    Now that I have known the witness, the supreme Self and the Lord, and have no hope left of bondage or freedom, I have no worry about liberation.

  7. 15.4

    You are not the body, nor is the body yours; you are neither the doer nor the enjoyer. You are consciousness itself, ever the witness, needing nothing. Go about happily.

  8. 18.40

    How can one who leans on what is seen ever see the Self? The wise do not see this or that; they see the imperishable Self.

  9. 18.94

    Asleep, he is not in deep sleep; dreaming, he is not lying down; awake, he is not awake. The wise one is content at every step.
