Bondage

Bondage

बन्धः

bandha

Bondage is only the belief that you are bound, the mind's wanting and grasping, and seeing anything other than yourself.

बंधन केवल यह मान्यता है कि तुम बँधे हो, मन का चाहना और पकड़ना, और अपने से अलग कुछ देखना।

21 verses

  1. 1.7

    You are the one seer of everything, and you are always essentially free. Your only bondage is this: you see the seer as something other than yourself.

  2. 1.11

    One who thinks himself free is free, and one who thinks himself bound is bound. The saying holds true here: as you think, so you become.

  3. 1.14

    For a long time, my child, you have been bound by the noose of identifying with the body. Cut it with the sword of the knowledge 'I am awareness,' and be happy.

  4. 1.15

    You are unattached, actionless, self-luminous and without stain. Your bondage is just this: that you keep practising samadhi.

  5. 2.18

    For me there is neither bondage nor liberation. The illusion has lost its ground and come to rest. Oh! The world rests in me, and yet in truth it does not rest in me.

  6. 2.20

    The body, heaven and hell, bondage and liberation, and fear too: all this is mere imagining. What have I, who am consciousness, to do with any of it?

  7. 2.22

    I am not the body, nor is the body mine; I am not the individual soul, I am consciousness. My only bondage was this: the craving for life.

  8. 8.1

    It is bondage when the mind wants something or grieves over something, lets go of something or grabs something, is pleased by something or angered by something.

  9. 8.3

    It is bondage when the mind clings to any of the things it sees. It is liberation when the mind clings to nothing it sees.

  10. 8.4

    When there is no 'I', there is liberation; when there is 'I', there is bondage. Seeing this, lightly and without effort, neither take hold of anything nor push anything away.

  11. 10.4

    Bondage is made of craving alone, and its ending is called liberation. Simply by not clinging to the world, one finds contentment again and again.

  12. 14.3

    Now that I have known the witness, the supreme Self and the Lord, and expect nothing from either bondage or freedom, I have no worry about liberation.

  13. 15.2

    Liberation is losing the taste for sense objects; bondage is relishing them. That is all there is to know. Now do as you wish.

  14. 15.18

    In the ocean of existence there was, is and will be only one. You have no bondage and no liberation. Your work is done; go about happily.

  15. 16.7

    As long as craving lives, the soil in which want of discernment grows, so long will the sense of rejecting and accepting remain, the sprout of the tree of worldly life.

  16. 18.38

    Without real ground and restless to grasp, the deluded keep feeding samsara. The wise have cut this root of all misery at its root.

  17. 18.57

    The feeling that something must be done is itself samsara. The wise do not see it, for their very form is emptiness: formless, unchanging and untouched by ill.

  18. 18.72

    For one who shines as the infinite and does not see nature at all, where is bondage, where liberation, where joy, where sorrow?

  19. 20.3

    Where is knowledge, where is ignorance, where is 'I', where is 'this', where is 'mine'? Where is bondage, where is liberation? How can my own nature have any form?

  20. 20.6

    Where is the world, where is the seeker of liberation, where is the yogi, where is the knower? Where is the bound, and where is the free, for me in my own non-dual nature?

  21. 20.12

    Where is activity, where is withdrawal, where is liberation, where is bondage, for me who am always unchanging, undivided and at rest in myself?
