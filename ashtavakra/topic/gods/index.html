Indra and the gods

इन्द्रादयो देवाः

The gods

Indra and the other gods appear as those who long for the state the knower already rests in, and who cannot match it.

इंद्र और अन्य देवता उन के रूप में आते हैं जो उस अवस्था की कामना करते हैं जिसमें ज्ञानी पहले से विश्राम करता है।

2 verses

  1. 4.2

    The state that Indra and all the gods long for and pine after — amazing, the yogi established there feels no thrill of joy.

  2. 18.54

    Seeing and honoring a learned Vedic priest, a deity, a holy place, a woman, a king or a loved one, the wise one feels no craving in his heart.
