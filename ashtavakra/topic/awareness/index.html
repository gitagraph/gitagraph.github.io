Pure awareness

Pure awareness

चित्

cit, bodha, caitanya

Consciousness itself, not the contents of consciousness. Ashtavakra calls you 'awareness alone' (cinmātra).

स्वयं चेतना, चेतना की विषय-वस्तु नहीं। अष्टावक्र तुम्हें 'केवल चैतन्य' (चिन्मात्र) कहते हैं।

33 verses

  1. 1.3

    You are not earth, nor water, nor fire, nor air, nor space. To be free, know yourself as the Self, the witness of all these, whose nature is consciousness.

  2. 1.4

    If you set the body apart and rest in consciousness, you will this very moment be happy, at peace, and free from bondage.

  3. 1.9

    With the fire of the certainty 'I am the one, pure awareness,' burn down the dense forest of ignorance. Free from sorrow, be happy.

  4. 1.10

    That in which this world appears, imagined like a snake on a rope, that awareness, bliss beyond all bliss, is what you are. Live happily.

  5. 1.13

    Let go of the illusion that you are a reflected self, and of every sense of outer and inner. Contemplate the Self as unchanging, non-dual awareness.

  6. 1.17

    Be free of expectation, unchanging, full, cool at heart, of unfathomable understanding and undisturbed, with your only inclination toward pure consciousness.

  7. 2.1

    Oh! I am stainless, at peace, pure awareness, beyond nature. All this time I have simply been fooled by delusion.

  8. 2.2

    As I alone light up this body, so I light up the world. So either the whole world is mine, or nothing at all is.

  9. 2.8

    Light is my very nature; I am nothing other than that. When the world shines, it is only I who shine.

  10. 2.16

    Oh! Duality is the root of suffering, and there is no other cure for it than this: all that is seen is false, and I am one, pure, the very essence of consciousness.

  11. 2.17

    I am pure awareness; through ignorance I imagined limitations on myself. Reflecting on this constantly, I abide beyond all thought.

  12. 2.19

    It is now certain that this world, body included, is nothing, and that the Self is pure consciousness alone. So on what could imagination now rest?

  13. 2.22

    I am not the body, nor is the body mine; I am not the individual soul, I am consciousness. My only bondage was this: the craving for life.

  14. 3.4

    Even after hearing that the Self is pure consciousness and supremely beautiful, a person who is deeply attached to sex falls into impurity.

  15. 7.5

    Ah, I am pure consciousness alone, and the world is like a magician's show. So how, and where, could the idea of rejecting or accepting anything arise for me?

  16. 9.6

    Is he not a true guru who has fully known the nature of consciousness, and who, through dispassion, evenness and right reasoning, carries one across the round of birth and death?

  17. 10.5

    You are the one pure consciousness; the world is inert and unreal. Even ignorance is nothing at all. So what is left for you to want to know?

  18. 11.6

    One who knows for certain, 'I am not the body, the body is not mine, I am awareness,' is as if he has reached absolute aloneness; he does not recall what was done or left undone.

  19. 11.8

    One who knows for certain that this universe, with all its many wonders, is nothing at all becomes free of latent desires; pure shining awareness, he rests in peace as if nothing were there.

  20. 15.4

    You are not the body, nor is the body yours; you are neither the doer nor the enjoyer. You are consciousness itself, ever the witness, needing nothing. Go about happily.

  21. 15.5

    Attraction and aversion belong to the mind, and the mind is never yours. You are free of thought, pure awareness, unchanging. Go about happily.

  22. 15.7

    That in which this world shimmers like waves on the sea, that is what you are, without doubt. You who are consciousness itself, let the fever go.

  23. 15.10

    Let the body last until the end of the age, or let it go this very day. What gain or loss is there for you, who are pure consciousness?

  24. 15.12

    Child, you are pure consciousness; this world is not separate from you. So who could reject or accept anything, and how, and where?

  25. 15.13

    In you, the one, the imperishable, the peaceful, the stainless sky of consciousness, where is birth, where is action, where is even the ego?

  26. 18.1

    Salutations to that peaceful radiance, whose nature is bliss alone, with the dawning of whose knowledge delusion at once becomes like a dream.

  27. 18.69

    The dual world, from mahat onward, is just an unfolding of names. Once that is let go, what is left to do for one who is pure awareness?

  28. 18.70

    Certain that all this is a product of illusion and that nothing truly is, the pure one, whose light cannot be made an object, comes to rest by his very nature.

  29. 18.71

    For one whose nature is pure radiance and who sees no world of objects, where is rule, where dispassion, where renunciation, where even calm?

  30. 18.72

    For one who shines as the infinite and does not see nature at all, where is bondage, where liberation, where joy, where sorrow?

  31. 18.94

    Asleep, he is not in deep sleep; in dream, he is not asleep; awake, he is not awake. The wise one is content at every step.

  32. 19.5

    Where is dreaming, where is deep sleep, where is waking? Where is the fourth state, or even fear, for me who abides in my own glory?

  33. 20.5

    Where is the doer, where is the enjoyer, where is actionless shining? Where is direct realisation, and where is its fruit, for me who am always without any nature of my own?
