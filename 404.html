The Bhagavad Gita

Gita for all

For someone opening the Gita for the first time, and for someone who has read it many times.

  • New to it? Start at Chapter 1 and read the story verse by verse in simple Hindi or English.
  • Know it well? Jump to any shloka, read the Sanskrit beside the translation, or follow one person or idea through all the verses.
  • Curious? Tap Karna to read every scene he is in, or tap dharma to read every verse that teaches it.

The translations are new and written to be easy to read. Suggestions and corrections are welcome. · About this project →
