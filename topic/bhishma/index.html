Bhishma

Bhishma

भीष्म

Kaurava commander

Grand-uncle to both sides. His conch blast opens the battle (1.12). Arjuna cannot bear to fight him.

दोनों पक्षों के पितामह। उनका शंखनाद युद्ध का आरंभ करता है (1.12)। अर्जुन उनसे लड़ने की बात सह नहीं पाते।

8 verses

  1. 1.8

    Yourself, Bhishma, Karna, and Kripa, ever victorious in battle; Ashvatthama, Vikarna, and the son of Somadatta as well.

  2. 1.10

    That strength of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is beyond measure, while their strength, guarded by Bhima, is limited.

  3. 1.11

    So all of you, holding your assigned places at every point of entry, must above all protect Bhishma.

  4. 1.12

    Then, to cheer him, the mighty grandsire, eldest of the Kurus, roared like a lion and blew his conch loudly.

  5. 1.25

    facing Bhishma, Drona and all the rulers of the earth, and said: Partha, see these Kurus assembled here.

  6. 2.4

    Madhusudana, slayer of enemies, how can I fight back with arrows in battle against Bhishma and Drona, who deserve my reverence?

  7. 11.26

    And all these sons of Dhritarashtra, along with the hosts of kings, Bhishma, Drona and that charioteer's son too, together with the chief warriors on our side as well,

  8. 11.34

    Drona and Bhishma, Jayadratha and Karna, and the other heroic warriors too, already slain by me, you must kill. Do not be troubled. Fight: you will conquer your rivals in battle.
