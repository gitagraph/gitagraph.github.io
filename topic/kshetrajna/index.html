Kshetrajna

क्षेत्रज्ञ

Knower of the field

The witness who knows the field. Krishna is the knower in all fields (13.2).

क्षेत्र का साक्षी, जो उसे जानता है। सभी क्षेत्रों में क्षेत्रज्ञ कृष्ण हैं (13.2)।

6 verses

  1. 13.2

    This body, Kaunteya, is called the field; and the one who knows it is called the knower of the field by those who understand these matters.

  2. 13.3

    Know me too as the knower of the field in every field, Bharata. In my view, true knowledge is the knowledge of the field and its knower.

  3. 13.4

    Hear from me in brief what that field is, what it is like, what changes it undergoes and where each thing in it comes from; and who its knower is and what his powers are.

  4. 13.27

    Whatever being comes into existence, still or moving, know, best of the Bharatas, that it arises from the union of the field and the knower of the field.

  5. 13.34

    Just as the one sun lights up this whole world, so the owner of the field lights up the whole field, Bharata.

  6. 13.35

    Those who see with the eye of knowledge the difference between the field and its knower, and how beings are freed from Prakriti, reach the Supreme.
