Bhagavad Gita 6.24

Krishna

सङ्कल्पप्रभवान्कामांस्त्यक्त्वा सर्वानशेषतः ।
मनसैवेन्द्रियग्रामं विनियम्य समन्ततः ॥ ६.२४ ॥

saṅkalpaprabhavānkāmāṃstyaktvā sarvānaśeṣataḥ ।
manasaivendriyagrāmaṃ viniyamya samantataḥ ॥

Giving up entirely all desires that spring from mental intention, and reining in the whole crowd of the senses from every side by the mind alone...
