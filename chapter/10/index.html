Chapter 10 · 42 verses

Divine Glories

विभूतियोग

Krishna lists his vibhutis: among lights he is the sun, among rivers the Ganga, among warriors Rama, among the Pandavas Arjuna. Whatever is glorious, powerful or beautiful comes from a spark of his splendour.

कृष्ण अपनी विभूतियाँ गिनाते हैं: ज्योतियों में वे सूर्य हैं, नदियों में गंगा, शस्त्रधारियों में राम, पांडवों में अर्जुन। जो कुछ भी तेजस्वी, शक्तिशाली या सुंदर है, वह उनके तेज के एक अंश से उत्पन्न है।

  1. 10.1

    Listen once more, mighty-armed one, to my supreme word, which I will speak to you because you delight in it and because I wish you well.

  2. 10.2

    Neither the hosts of gods nor the great sages know my origin, for I am the source of the gods and of the great sages in every way.

  3. 10.3

    Whoever knows me as unborn and without beginning, the great Lord of the worlds, is free of delusion among mortals and is released from all sins.

  4. 10.4

    Understanding, knowledge, freedom from confusion, patience, truthfulness, self-control and calm; pleasure and pain, coming into being and ceasing to be, fear and fearlessness;

  5. 10.5

    non-violence, evenness, contentment, austerity, generosity, fame and disgrace: all these varied states of beings arise from me alone.

  6. 10.6

    The seven great sages of old and the four Manus too were born of my mind and share my nature; from them came all the creatures of this world.

  7. 10.7

    Whoever truly knows this splendour and power of mine is joined to me by unwavering yoga; of this there is no doubt.

  8. 10.8

    I am the origin of everything, and from me everything proceeds. Knowing this, the wise worship me, filled with love.

  9. 10.9

    With their minds on me and their lives given to me, enlightening one another and always speaking of me, they are content and full of joy.

  10. 10.10

    To those who are always devoted and worship me with love, I give the yoga of understanding by which they come to me.

  11. 10.11

    Out of compassion for them alone, dwelling in their hearts, I destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge.

  12. 10.12

    You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier: the eternal, divine Person, the first of the gods, unborn and all-pervading.

  13. 10.13

    So all the sages describe you, and so do the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa; and now you yourself are telling me.

  14. 10.14

    All that you tell me, Keshava, I accept as true. Indeed, Lord, neither the gods nor the demons understand your manifestation.

  15. 10.15

    Only you know yourself through yourself, Supreme Person, source of beings, lord of beings, god of gods, master of the world.

  16. 10.16

    Please tell me fully of your divine glories, the manifestations through which you pervade these worlds and abide in them.

  17. 10.17

    How am I to know you, Yogi, while constantly meditating on you? In which of your many forms, Lord, should I think of you?

  18. 10.18

    Tell me again in detail, Janardana, of your power and your glories, for I never tire of hearing your nectar-like words.

  19. 10.19

    Very well, best of the Kurus, I will tell you of my divine glories, but only the chief ones, for there is no end to my extent.

  20. 10.20

    I am the Self, Gudakesha, seated in the heart of every being. I am the beginning, the middle and the end of all beings.

  21. 10.21

    Among the Adityas I am Vishnu; among lights, the radiant sun; among the Maruts I am Marichi; and among the stars, the moon.

  22. 10.22

    Among the Vedas I am the Sama Veda; among the gods I am Indra; among the senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am consciousness.

  23. 10.23

    Among the Rudras I am Shankara; among the Yakshas and Rakshasas, Kubera, lord of wealth; among the Vasus I am fire; and among mountain peaks, Meru.

  24. 10.24

    Know me, Partha, as Brihaspati, chief of household priests. Among army commanders I am Skanda, and among bodies of water, the ocean.

  25. 10.25

    Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among utterances, the single syllable Om. Among sacrifices I am the sacrifice of silent repetition, and among things that stand firm, the Himalaya.

  26. 10.26

    Among all trees I am the sacred fig; among the divine sages, Narada; among the Gandharvas, Chitraratha; and among the perfected ones, the sage Kapila.

  27. 10.27

    Among horses know me as Uchchaihshravas, born of the churning for nectar; among lordly elephants, Airavata; and among men, the king.

  28. 10.28

    Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows, the wish-fulfilling Kamadhenu. I am Kandarpa, the god of love, who brings forth offspring, and among serpents I am Vasuki.

  29. 10.29

    Among the Nagas I am Ananta; among the beings of the waters, Varuna; among the ancestors, Aryaman; and among those who restrain, I am Yama.

  30. 10.30

    Among the Daityas I am Prahlada; among those who count, I am Time; among beasts, the lion; and among birds, Garuda, son of Vinata.

  31. 10.31

    Among purifiers I am the wind; among those who bear weapons, Rama; among sea creatures, the makara; and among rivers, the Ganga.

  32. 10.32

    Of all creations I am the beginning, the end and the middle too, Arjuna. Among kinds of knowledge I am knowledge of the Self, and in those who debate, I am the reasoning that seeks truth.

  33. 10.33

    Among letters I am the letter A, and among compounds, the copulative compound. I alone am unending Time, and I am the sustainer whose face is turned everywhere.

  34. 10.34

    I am death that carries off everything, and the birth of all that is yet to be. Among feminine qualities I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forbearance.

  35. 10.35

    Among Sama hymns I am the Brihat Saman; among metres, the Gayatri; among months, Margashirsha; and among seasons, flower-bearing spring.

  36. 10.36

    Among cheats I am gambling; I am the brilliance of the brilliant. I am victory, I am resolve, and I am the goodness of the good.

  37. 10.37

    Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas, Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; and among poets, the poet Ushana.

  38. 10.38

    Among those who punish I am the rod of authority; among those who seek victory, sound policy. Among secrets I am silence, and in the wise I am wisdom.

  39. 10.39

    Whatever is the seed of all beings, that too am I, Arjuna. There is no creature, moving or still, that could exist without me.

  40. 10.40

    There is no end to my divine glories, Parantapa. What I have said is only a brief indication of their extent.

  41. 10.41

    Whatever being has glory, beauty or great strength, know that it springs from just a fragment of my splendour.

  42. 10.42

    But what use is all this detail to you, Arjuna? I stand supporting this entire universe with a single fraction of myself.
